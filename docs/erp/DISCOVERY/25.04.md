# Part 25.04 discovery — Client & Consultant Portal

Date 2026-11-20. Part 25.01 is in progress. Parts 11.02 and 22.04 are ready for review and await formal acceptance. A read-only query found no G1 or G2 record for development company `MAIN`. The brief limits implementation to a thin adapter while prerequisites are outstanding. | Requirement | Decision | Existing owner and pending integration |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 Client dashboard | Extend | Site Progress supplies S-curves, milestones and an internal Client Progress Report. Reuse `prs_periods`, the progress service and the approved report projections. Project and site scope must be combined with approval to share each photo, issue or report. |
| FR-02 Consultant worklists | Extend | `engg/rfis.js` serves `engg_rfis` and `engg_rfi_routing` under `/api/v1/engg` and drives the RFI follow-up sweep. Reuse their workflows and route each review to an identified external party. |
| FR-03 RA bill certification | Extend | `bill/cert.js` owns `bill_certifications`, client certification events and the internal certification screens. Reuse its partial and disputed quantity rules and its downstream invoice and receivable handoffs. |
| FR-04 Snags and handover | Extend | `qa/snags.js` owns the snag list, decision history and overdue reminders. Part 26.02 is listed as not started. The handover dossier review depends on its owning service. |
| FR-05 Audit and watermarks | Reuse | Part 25.01 uses the shared audit trail and issues a session-bound signed ticket for a clean, issued document revision shared to an account. Downloads are watermarked. Mutations in the future worklists must be audited within their owning transactions. |
| BR-01 Explicit sharing and hidden internal comments | Reuse | Part 25.01 document shares already bind a revision to one account. Transaction routes still need record assignment or explicit sharing, plus external field projections that exclude internal comments. A project or site grant alone grants no access to a business record. |
| §5 Database | Extend | The adapter reads existing `ptl_accounts`, `ptl_sessions`, `mdm_partner_contacts`, `proj_stakeholders`, `org_projects` and `org_sites`. Seed one default-off flag. Future ownership and decision records belong with their domain services. |
| §6 Services and APIs | Extend | Resolve a live, immutable client or consultant project grant inside the portal service. Future external endpoints need owning service checks, version validation, idempotency, transactional events and route policy declarations. |
| §7 and §7A Screens | Extend | Reuse the portal shell for branding, MFA, themes, language and text size. The seven business pages and phone verification await the prerequisite decisions and referenced UI standard. |
| §8 Roles and access | Reuse | Existing `X-CLI` and `X-CON` identities can come from MDM contacts or project stakeholders. The adapter checks the active project and any assigned site against the account’s company and current grant. |
| §9 Approvals | Reuse | Drawings, RFIs, submittals, inspection requests and bill certification continue through existing workflows. The adapter adds no business approval or configuration write. |
| §10 Dashboards, reports and exports | Reuse | Use approved progress data, the dashboard framework, Project 360 and shared export services with the same record-level sharing and field masks as the portal views. |
| §11 Events and integrations | Reuse | Route decisions through the owning services in 11.01, 11.02, 11.03, 18.02, 22.01, 22.03, 22.04, 18.06 and 26.02. Publish or consume their existing domain events for updates. |

The local ERP standards, role catalogue and approval reference are available. The referenced master UI/mobile standard and verification report were unavailable in the workspace.

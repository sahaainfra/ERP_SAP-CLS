# Discovery 11.04: EPC engineering deliverables and vendor document review

Inspected: the drawing register and the drawing revisions (11.01), the RFI and submittal services (11.02, 11.03) in the module `engg`, the workflow engine with resolvers and evidence providers (04.01), the parameter engine, the list and export engines, the integrity registry, the project master (10.01) and the role templates (DESIGN, PLAN, PM, CM, QS). No deliverable, comment-sheet, vendor-document or hold table existed; purchase orders (15.05), the vendor portal (25.02), project progress (14.05) and expediting (15.07) do not exist yet.

| Requirement | Decision | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 deliverable register with number, title, discipline, type, package, weight and planned and actual dates for IFR, IFA, IFC and as-built | New | `engg_deliverables` (tables are `engg_*`, not `epc_*`); an optional link to a drawing of 11.01 and to a document of 04.10; ten disciplines for EPC work. |
| FR-02 weighted engineering progress by deliverable and milestone, rolled up by discipline and package, S-curve against plan | New | milestone weights are a company parameter `engg.progress_weights` (IFR 50 %, IFA 70 %, IFC 100 %); the roll-up is the sum of weight times progress over the sum of weight, worked out on the server; the S-curve gives planned and actual progress at each month end. |
| FR-03 client comment resolution sheet per deliverable revision | New | `engg_crs`: comments per revision with a response, closed when the client accepts, reopened when not. |
| FR-04 vendor document requirements per PO with due dates and review codes; vendor submissions via the portal | New | `engg_vdr_items` with the PO as a reference (no PO table yet); the submission is recorded by the design coordinator until the portal (25.02); the review code is proposed and approved (`ENGG_VDR_REVIEW`); C and D start another cycle. |
| FR-05 engineering holds with impact on procurement and construction | New | `engg_holds` and events `engg.hold.raised` and `engg.hold.cleared` carrying the PO and activity for expediting (15.07). |
| BR-01 progress from milestone weights; only approved status changes count | New | IFR, IFA and as-built are recorded by the people who manage the deliverables, the date cannot be in the future and the order is kept; IFC counts only after the release is approved (discipline lead, engineering manager, project manager). |
| Output: engineering progress to project progress (14.05), holds to expediting (15.07) | New | events `engg.progress.updated` (with the project figure), `engg.hold.raised`, `engg.hold.cleared`, `engg.deliverable.released`, `engg.vdr.reviewed`. |
| Permissions | New | `engg.deliverable.*` and `engg.vdr_item.*` (10 keys each). |
| Shared services | Reuse | workflow, parameters, lists and exports, audit, integrity registry, i18n, feature flags, navigation. |

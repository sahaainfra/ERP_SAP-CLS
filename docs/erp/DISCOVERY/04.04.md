# Discovery note 04.04

Inspected (searched: control, engine, exceptions, pcs_check_results, pcs_control_points, pcs_exceptions, pcs_reason_codes, points, protocol): there is no protocol or control engine and no `pcs_*` table. What exists and was reused: the workflow engine (04.01) with the matrix, limits, escalation and SLA (04.02) and the inbox and evidence framework (04.03); the reason picker and the table `ui_reason_codes` of 03.04 (cancel, reject, return); the file service with entity registry (01.05); the audit trail, event outbox, parameters, feature flags, the list and saved-view framework, the export writers, and the dashboard widget framework. The "controls" of 04.02 (`modules/eng/controls.js`) are the limits, delegation and SLA services and are not related.

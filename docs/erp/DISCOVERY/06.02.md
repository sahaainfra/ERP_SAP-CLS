
# Discovery note 06.02

- `core_uoms` / `core_uom_conversions` and the conversion service of the calculation engine (01.06), with defect D-042 pointing to this part;

- the language preference in `ui_user_preferences` and `iam_user_profiles` (03.09, 02.03);
- the governance framework of 06.01 (change requests, worklist, workflow);
- the Excel engine (04.13);
- the IFSC format check of 06.01 (`FORMATS.IFSC`).

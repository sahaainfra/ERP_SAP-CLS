# Part 14.01 discovery: Work fronts, daily work plans and work authorisation

Date: 2026-11-22. New module `site` (M14), flag `ff.site.p1401`. Depends on 13.04 (approved weekly plans and their daily breakdown, read through `planOf(db).lookahead.dailyPlan`), 12.03 (the budget check, called through `budOf(db).ledger.check` with the person's own right `bud.check.use`) and 07.03 (crews of a site and their members, deployments). Reads the activities, quantities, locations and material/plant norms of 13.01 and 13.02, the drawing register and its IFC revisions (11.01) and the constraint log (13.04). Provides `site.wa.approved`, `site.wa.closed`, `site.daily_plan.issued` and `GET /api/v1/site/work-authorisations/active` to material issue (16.04), plant (17.03), attendance (07.05), the DPR (14.02) and progress (14.05).

Searched for `daily`, `fronts`, `plans`, `site_daily_plans`, `site_wa_resources`, `site_work_authorisations`, `site_work_fronts`, `work authorisation`, `PENDING_WA`: there was no `site` module and no work authorisation. What exists: the navigation group `g-site` ("Site", seeded in 03.05/03.06 and used by 04.15 for field pages), which this part reuses; attendance punches with their site and deployment (07.05) but no PENDING_WA flag or WA adapter; the control engine (04.04) with modes OFF/OBSERVE/WARN/ENFORCE and exceptions; the workflow engine with conditional steps (`when`); the budget check and commitment ledger (12.03). Material issue (16.04), plant logs (17.03), the DPR (14.02), measurement (22.01), permits (19.02) and inspection requests (18.02) are not built.

Decisions taken here:

- **One module `site`.** The prompt names `site_*` tables; the module is new and owns them. The nav group `g-site` already existed and is reused, not re-created.
- **The chain is CM, then PM when escalated.** "SE → CM" is read as: the site engineer raises and sends, the construction manager approves. A creator never approves (the engine's rule); a CM who raises a WA is approved by another CM.
- **The tolerances are thresholds of CP-SITE-02** (`usePct`, `planPct`, `closePct`), so an administrator changes them where the control is configured.

Prerequisites 13.04 and 12.03 are Ready for review; 07.03 is Ready for review. None of their tables is written by this part.

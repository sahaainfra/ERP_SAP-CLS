## Decisions

| Requirement | Existing | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 Daily work done | 22.02 apportioning | **Extend** the 22.02 service with `recon.executedLines` (dated, per site, with planned quantity), so the DPR→BOQ apportioning is not copied. **New** valuation: quantity × contract rate (`amountOf` of 22.04), and × budget cost rate of the approved budget for the internal margin view, masked without `bi.execution.cost`. |
| FR-02 WIP and billed gap | 22.02 chain | **Reuse** `recon.chainRows`: WIP = executed not certified × rate, billed gap = executed not billed × rate, balance = revised not executed × rate. Project level only; the chain holds the current certification, not as at the day (D-775). |
| FR-03 Snapshots and site comparison | — | **New** `dei_daily`: one row per project, site and day plus a project row. It is recomputed when a DPR is approved (`site.dpr.approved` consumer), by a nightly job at 23:45 IST, and on demand. Publishes `bi.execution.snapshot_captured`, and `executionOf(db).series` serves MIS (27.07) and forecasting. |
| FR-04 RFIs, hindrances, labour and plant | DPR, RFI, hindrance registers | **New** counts per day. Labour and plant are compared with the plan only through the planned value of the DPR lines; there is no resource plan per day (D-776). |
| BR-01 Approved DPRs only | — | Only `site_dprs.status = 'APPROVED'` lines are valued. |
| §9 Configuration | — | The part has no settings of its own, so no maker-checker flow is needed. |
| §8 Roles | Role templates | **New** permissions. `bi.execution.view` and `bi.execution.export`: PM, PD, DIR, QS, PCMGR. `bi.execution.cost`: DIR, PD, PCMGR. `bi.execution.run`: DIR, PCMGR. Site rows are scoped by site; project rows by project. |

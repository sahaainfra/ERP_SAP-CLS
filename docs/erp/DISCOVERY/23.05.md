## Requirement by requirement

| Requirement | Decision | What |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 receivables from tax invoices and other income | **New** consumer + **Reuse** 22.04 link | `ar_invoices` from `bill.invoice.issued` (once per invoice; the client from the contract, the due date from the event or the contract's payment days, the interest rate on delay from the payment terms when given), linked back with `linkReceivable`; other income typed, approved by the accounts manager (`FIN_AR_INVOICE`), posted |
| FR-02 receipts, deductions, allocation | **New** | `ar_receipts` with TDS, GST-TDS, retention and other deductions; allocation oldest first or by hand (`ar_allocations`); posted when entered; the bank statement match is 23.06's (D-719) |

| FR-04 credit notes with GST | **New** workflow `FIN_AR_CREDIT_NOTE` | GST at the invoice's rate and supply; the IRN due date for an e-invoicing company; posted; accounts manager, CFO above ₹5 lakh |
| FR-05 collection follow-up | **New** | `ar_followups`; the worklist by days overdue with an SLA colour; promises; the daily escalation of a broken promise; the dunning letter |
| FR-06 ageing, DSO, statements | **New** | in SQL; DSO over the last 90 days' sales (parameter) |

Decisions taken here:

- **The chain is PM then COMMGR.** The prompt's "PLAN → PM → COMMGR" is read as: the planner prepares and sends, the project manager reviews, the commercial manager approves. The planner holds no approval.
- **An analysis belongs to a delay that is not decided.** Approved, the impact is locked with the classification; a new analysis is made on a revision.
- **Delay days and impact days are two figures.** Delay days are the period of the event; impact days are what the analysis shows on the completion. The claims read both.

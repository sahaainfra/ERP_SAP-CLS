## Requirement by requirement

| Requirement | Decision | What |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 tax resolver | **New** (over **Reuse** of 06.02 masters) | `resolve` / `rateOn` in `fin/gst.js`: supplier and recipient by GSTIN or state, place of supply (given, the property's state for works contract s.12(3), the recipient, export 96), B2B / B2C / SEZ / export / import / deemed export, with or without payment, reverse charge (given or from the HSN master); the rate of the dated master on the s.14 time-of-supply date; `POST /fin/gst/resolve`; registered as the platform's tax engine (`platform/tax`) |
| FR-02 GSTIN validation | **Reuse** shared check + **New** portal status | `gst_gstin_checks`; `POST /fin/gst/gstin-check`; the company's GSTINs on the settings page |
| FR-03 e-invoice | **New** + **Extend** 22.04 / 23.05 by event | `gst_einvoices`; IRP schema 1.1 from the tax invoice or credit note, checked against the resolver; GSP connector (sandbox provider and `gsp_http`); IRN, ack, signed QR handed back to the document; retries with back-off; cancellation within 24 h; the IRN-due worklist (day 20 and 25, the 30-day window blocks); print with the QR |
| FR-04 e-way bill | **New** + **Extend** 16.05 | `gst_ewaybills`: from a tax invoice, a transfer or a concrete delivery; Part A/B, vehicle update, extension (8-hour window, 360-day cap), cancellation (24 h); validity by distance; expiry alerts; the transfer dispatch takes the generated number |
| FR-05 compliance log, sandbox | **New** | `gst_compliance_log`; the sandbox provider plays the IRP and the e-way bill system |
| BR-01 deterministic, never hard-coded | **New** | rates only from the dated masters; integer arithmetic (BigInt) half up; same input, same lines (tested) |
| Case-insensitive invoice numbers | **New** | unique index on `upper(document_no)` per supplier GSTIN, year and type (22.04's own numbers are already unique without case) |
| §7 pages | **New** | e-Invoice & e-Way Bill Register (List report, pill tabs, the IRN-due worklist), e-Way Bills (same page for stores), GST Settings (Configuration, versions, maker-checker) |

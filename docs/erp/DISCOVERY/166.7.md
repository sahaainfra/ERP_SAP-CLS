## 2. Findings reproduced on `main`

| # | Finding | Evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-01 | No document flow: a purchase order, GRN or MR shows its own fields; its predecessors and successors are only reference numbers | `before-po-list-*`, `before-grn-list-*`, `before-mr-list-*` (no "Document flow" anywhere) | **New** `platform/docflow` (server registry + providers reading the link columns above) and a Document Flow panel |
| F-02 | No group-by on a list (R18) | the List Report has no grouping | **Extend** the List Report: group-by for lists that declare natural groups |
| F-03 | On a phone the filter bar is inline above the cards | `before-*-mobile.png` | **Extend**: a filter sheet (bottom sheet) on a phone, with the same views |
| F-04 | The key facts of a record are written in each page; no registry | e.g. `PurchaseOrdersPage.jsx` `po-figures` | **New** `objectFacts` registry (one place per entity), used by the Document Flow nodes and the record headers of this part |
| F-05 | 358 page files draw tables of their own; the crawler T-01 can only hold the List Report pages to the standard | `grep` | **Record**: the List Report pages are checked; the others are listed in D-694 for their modules |

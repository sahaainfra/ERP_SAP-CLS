# Part 12.02 discovery: Budget revisions, transfers and contingency drawdown

Date: 2026-11-14. Module `bud`, flag `ff.bud.p1202`. Depends on 12.01 and 10.06; used by 21.05.

## What exists and what is decided
| Requirement | Decision | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 revision request with before/after, total and margin impact | New (`bud_changes`, `bud_change_lines`, `POST /changes/preview`) | Margin uses the 12.01 margin rule (`budgetBase.margin`). |
| FR-02 transfer between lines, same total | New | BR-01 net to zero; contingency and reserve are not transferred (drawdown instead). |
| FR-03 contingency drawdown linked to a risk | New, reads `proj_risks` (10.06) | Only risks (not opportunities) that are open or occurred; no write to the risk register. |
| FR-04 draft revision when a variation is approved | New consumer, adapter | 21.05 is not built; event `ctr.variation.approved` agreed (ADR-452, D-459). |
| FR-05 version history with comparison; revised budget current | Extend 12.01 | 12.01 versions (`bud_budgets.version_no`, SUPERSEDED) reused; a change makes a new approved version; `GET /compare`. |
| BR-01 | New | Net zero; drawdown not above the contingency in force less waiting drawdowns. |
| BR-02 bands by % of budget | Workflow engine (04.01) | `BUD_CHANGE` PCMGR → PD → DIR (> 5 %); `BUD_DRAWDOWN` PM → PD. |
| Approval-controlled lifecycle, amend | Reuse of the 12.01 pattern | Amend raises a rejected or cancelled change again. |
| Permissions `bud.change.*` | New | PCMGR and PM request; PD and DIR approve; PCMGR approves the first step of revisions, PM of drawdowns. |

## Prerequisites
12.01 is Ready for review (not yet accepted); 10.06 is Ready for review. Both are used through their tables and services only. Changing 12.01: the event of a revision draft is renamed `bud.budget.revision_started`, and `bud.budget.revised` now means a new version came into force (from either part).

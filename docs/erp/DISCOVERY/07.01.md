# Discovery note 07.01

Inspected (searched: employee, employment, hr_employee_banks, hr_employee_contacts, hr_employee_education, hr_employee_history, hr_employee_statutory, hr_employees, master, records, designation, department, branch, cost centre, employee_code): no employee master existed. What exists:
- the **organisation master of 02.02** (companies, branches, departments, designations with a grade and a level, projects, sites, cost centres, effective-dated assignments);
- **user management of 02.03**: user accounts with a profile that already carries an `employee_code` (unique per company) and `employee_id` (empty), the deactivation wizard (blockers, successor, sessions ended) and the user creation service with invitations;
- the formats (PAN, IFSC, mobile, PIN), name similarity and masking of 06.01, the states and banks of 06.02, the vault of 04.07 for encryption at rest;
- the workflow engine, approval matrix, record registry, evidence panels, files, collaboration tabs, List Report with exports, Excel engine, notifications, parameters, widgets and the job scheduler;
- the numbering service of 01.03 (no series for employees, so the adapter below);
- **not present**: the role "HR executive" (the catalogue 06 has HRMGR and PAY only), the modules for attendance, leave, assets and training (07.05 to 07.07, M19), the skills master (07.10), payroll (M24).

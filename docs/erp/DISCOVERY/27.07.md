## Decisions

| Requirement | Existing | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 Checklist with owners and dependencies | Owner records listed above | **New**: eight mandatory steps read live from the owners: DPRs, attendance, material reconciliation (16.10 `missing`), measurements, CVR, costs posted, forecast, finance closed. Each step has an owner role, dependencies, a detail and a drill link. |
| FR-02 Project MIS | Owner records | **New** pack: progress (approved period, SPI/CPI), cost vs budget (approved forecast), CVR, billing and collections, cash, resources, quality and HSE, risks, look-ahead. It is frozen in the record when the close is sent, and exported as PDF, XLSX and CSV. |
| FR-03 Budget Master | `fc_forecast_lines` + cost codes and heads | **Reuse** the approved 23.11 forecast: budget, committed, actual, EAC and variance by cost head, frozen and locked with the close. |
| FR-04 Monthly lock | — | **New** shared guard `platform/locks` (`projectPeriodLocks`, `assertProjectPeriodOpen`). The close registers as a lock source; the DPR service (14.02) calls the guard on create, update, submit and amend. Publishes `bi.monthly_close.locked`. Other project modules → D-777. |
| BR-01 Lock only after all mandatory steps | — | Checked on submit and again on approval (the approval rolls back if a step reopened). |
| §6 Standard endpoints | — | list, get, create, update, delete, submit, withdraw, cancel, amend, history, print, export. A bulk import of closes has no business use, so it is not offered (D-778). |
| §9 Chain QS → PM → PD | Workflow engine | **Reuse** `workflowTypes.register` (`BI_MONTHLY_CLOSE`). The QS step applies when someone other than a QS prepared the close; distinct approvers; return, reject and resubmit; approval from the shared inbox, including on mobile. |
| §8 Permissions | Role templates | **New** `bi.monthly_close.{view,create,edit,delete,submit,approve,cancel,amend,print,export}` for QS, PM, PCMGR, PD and DIR, scoped by project. |
| §7 Board | Card, KPI and status components | **New** board: drag to the next stage (start, send) on a desktop, with server validation and undo; a pill column switcher and card menu on a phone (one shared CSS rule, `.ui-board`). |
| D-775 (from 27.06) | Targets 27.07 | **Kept open**. Dated certification facts are not part of this close; the frozen MIS records the certification at the time the close is sent. Re-targeted to 27.10 with that reason. |

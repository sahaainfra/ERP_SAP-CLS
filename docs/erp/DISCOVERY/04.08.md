Inspected (searched: initiated, management, routing, senior, task, tsk_assignees, tsk_checklist_items, tsk_dependencies, tsk_routing_matrix, tsk_tasks): no `tsk_*` table existed. What did: the workflow engine's own tasks (`wf_tasks`, approvals only, 04.01) with their SLA, reminders and escalation along the path of 04.02; the delegation table (04.02); the RACI of 04.05 with `resolve()`; the notification engine (04.06) and its `notify()`; the "My Work" sources (03.07); the file service with registered entities (01.05); the department head (`org_departments.head_user_id`).

# Part 13.03 discovery: Baselines, programme revisions and MS Project / Primavera exchange

Date: 2026-11-19. Module `plan` (extended), flag `ff.plan.p1303`. Depends on 13.02; used by 10.01 (control accounts), 14.05 (DPR), 13.05 (earned value) and 21.06 (delay analysis) through the event `plan.baseline.approved` and `GET /api/v1/plan/baselines/current`.

Searched for `baseline`, `plan_baselines`, `plan_imports`, `MSP`, `Primavera`, `xer`, `DCMA`, `variance`, `exchange`, `snapshot`: no baseline of a schedule, no file exchange and no schedule quality check existed. `plan_schedules.version_type` allows only `WORKING` and the Gantt contract of 13.02 carries an empty `baseline` field: both were left for this part. What exists and is used: the schedule core of 13.01 (activities, links, calendars, the CPM pass, the lock of an approved schedule, `findCycle`), the resources of 13.02 (`plan_activity_resources`, the resource key), the item master (`mdm_items`), the WBS (`proj_wbs`), the approval engine, the list, export (XLSX, PDF, CSV), numbering, audit, permission, scope, event and notification services.

| Requirement | Decision | Notes |

Decisions taken here:

- **Approval chain.** `PLAN_BASELINE`: step 1 the project manager, step 2 the project director; distinct approvers; the engine never gives the originator a task on their own document.
- **The current baseline is the latest approved one.** There is no action to make an older baseline current again: a programme goes back only by a new revision with its reason and approval, so the history of what was current when is the approval history itself.
- **The file comes as the body of the request** (`application/octet-stream`, at most 16 MB), as the translation import of 03.06 does. The files platform accepts no `.xml` or `.xer` today, so `plan_imports.file_id` stays empty and the row keeps the name, size and SHA-256 of the file; the parsed content is kept until the import is applied or discarded. Logged as DEF-13.03-01.
- **Durations and lags are whole working days** in this schedule; a file gives hours. They are divided by the hours of a day of the file and rounded, with a warning that counts them.
- **Constraints.** The schedule knows "start no earlier than" and "finish no later than". A harder constraint of a file is carried as the nearer of the two and reported; the others are left out and reported.
- **The activity code in MS Project.** MS Project has no activity code. The writer puts it in the text field Text1 with the alias "Activity code" (and in the WBS field of the task); the reader offers that field, the WBS code, the row number or the unique id.
- **Calendars are matched, not created.** A calendar of a file is matched to a calendar in force by its code or name; what is not matched works on the calendar of the schedule, and a difference in working days over the programme is reported. A calendar is still drafted and put in force by two people on the Calendars page (13.01).
- **No XML library.** The reader is a small one of its own: elements and text only, no document type, no entity declaration, a depth and size limit.

Prerequisite 13.02 is Ready for review and is used through its services; none of its tables is altered.

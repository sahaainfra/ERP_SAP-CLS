# Part 22.06 discovery: Vendor Invoices, Three-Way/Multi-Way Matching & Invoice Hub

Date: 2026-11-21. Depends on 15.05 (purchase orders), 16.03 (goods receipts), 15.06 (service entries), 17.06 (hire bills), 22.05 (subcontractor bills), 22.03 (client bills). Search terms checked (invoice, invoices, matching, multi, three, vendor, vinv_invoice_lines, vinv_vendor_invoices).

| Requirement | Decision | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 vendor invoice registration: vendor, GSTIN, number/date, lines, taxes, attachments; duplicate detection; GSTIN and IRN validation | New (tables named `bill_vendor_invoices`, `bill_vendor_invoice_lines`; spec `vinv_*`) | `vinv.js`; channel MANUAL, PORTAL, EMAIL or OCR; the duplicate (vendor, invoice number, financial year) is refused with the document it duplicates and by a unique index; GSTIN checked by format and against the GSTINs of the vendor and of the order; IRN checked by format (a portal check is 23.07) |
| FR-02 matching: quantity, rate, tax, totals; two-way for services against service entries and hire bills | New, reads 15.05, 16.03, 15.06, 17.06 | invoice quantity <= accepted less returned less already invoiced (tolerance `bill.vinv.qty_tol_pct`); rate <= order rate (`bill.vinv.rate_tol_pct`); tax against the order GST percentage and the total against the lines (`bill.vinv.abs_tol_paise`); a service invoice against the amount left on the approved service entry, a hire invoice against the hire bill and its GST |
| FR-03 exceptions workflow: price, quantity, missing GRN, tax mismatch; resolve by debit note, PO amendment or rejection | New | exceptions listed with the line; `resolve` by the accounts or procurement manager (debit note with the excess computed, amendment then matched again, rejection); or the chain over the exception with a reason: accounts manager, procurement manager for a price exception, CFO above `bill.vinv.cfo_threshold_paise` |
| FR-04 matched invoices to AP (23.03); blocked invoices visible with reasons | Event and adapter | `ap.invoice.matched` (with the ITC lines for 23.08) on approval; `vendorInvoices.linkPayable` refuses a blocked or unapproved invoice; the quantity invoiced is recorded on the order line through the adapter `invoicing.record` |
| FR-05 invoice hub of client, subcontractor and vendor invoices with status, ageing and drill-down | New | `hub.js`: stage per kind, KPI by kind, ageing, my pending actions, rows linked to the source; only the kinds the person may see; payment and collection stages come with 23.x |
| BR-01 payment not allowed for unmatched or blocked invoices; tolerances configurable | New | `paymentAllowed`; parameters as above |
| BR-02 the recorder of the goods receipt does not approve (SoD-04) | Reuse rule SOD-04 | checked at approval against the receipts of the order: the approval is refused and rolls back |

# Part 16.06 discovery: Physical Verification, Adjustments, Scrap & Disposal

Date: 2026-11-12. Depends on 16.01 (stock engine, adjustments `inv_adjustments`, `qty_inspection` hold) and uses the gate pass of 16.02 and the issue/transfer freeze rules of 16.04 and 16.05.

## Findings
| Requirement | Decision | Evidence in the repository |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 count plans (full and cycle by ABC class) | New | 16.01 has stock adjustments but no count. New `inv_count_plans` and `inv_count_lines`; the ABC class is worked out from the stock value (top 70% A, next 20% B, rest C); the cycle is A 30, B 90, C 180 days and a full count 365 days. |
| FR-02 blind count, recount beyond tolerance | New | A counter sees item and bin, never the book quantity (the sheet and the plan view hide it); a difference beyond `inv.count.tolerance_pct` (2%) is recounted by someone else. |
| FR-03 variance by quantity, value and per cent; reason for each | New | `GET /inv/count-variance`; the reviewer gives a reason for every difference before the adjustment is raised. |
| FR-04 adjustment by value band, SoD | Reuse 16.01 adjustment, new workflow | A count adjustment is a 16.01 `inv_adjustments` row (`source_type = inv_count_plan`) approved through `INV_ADJ_BAND`: store manager; procurement manager above ₹ 50,000; CFO above ₹ 5 lakh. SoD-09: nobody who counted or recounted approves, at any step (new `beforeApprove` workflow hook). |
| FR-05 scrap register and disposal (sale, auction, write-off) | New | `inv_scrap_entries`, `inv_scrap_disposals`, `inv_count_lines`; sale to an active registered dealer through the store manager, procurement manager and CFO; write-off by value band; stock leaves at the average rate; gate pass `createForDisposal`. |
| FR-06 expiry of shelf-life stock | New | Expiry from the manufacturing date on the approved GRN line plus the item shelf life; daily job `inv.count_expiry_daily` alerts once per item and day. |
| BR-01 the counter does not approve their own adjustment; BR-02 counted items do not move | New | `beforeApprove` guard; count-window freeze in `stock.postOne`. |
| Pages (plans, blind count on the phone, variance, scrap and expiry) | New | Four pages under the Stores group. |

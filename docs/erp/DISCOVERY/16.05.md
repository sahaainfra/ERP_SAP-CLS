# Part 16.05 discovery: Inter-Store Transfers & In-Transit Stock

Date: 2026-11-12. Depends on 16.01 (stock engine; `qty_in_transit` on the balances, left at zero by D-541) and uses the gate pass of 16.02.

## Findings
| Requirement | Decision | Evidence in the repository |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 transfer request | New | 16.01 has `movements.transfer`, an immediate store-to-store move with no request, approval or transit. It is kept for corrections. New `inv_transfers` and `inv_transfer_lines`; an approved material request (`proc_material_requests`, 15.01) pre-fills the lines. |
| FR-02 dispatch with gate pass, vehicle, e-way bill, challan | New, reuse 16.02 | `gate.passes.createForTransfer` makes an approved non-returnable pass; e-way bill by threshold or between states (the tax engine 23.07 does not exist, D-562). |
| FR-03 receipt, damages and shortages, resolution workflow | New | Receipt in parts; `inv_transfer_discrepancies`; write-off by `INV_TRANSFER_DISC`. |
| FR-04 in-transit report and ageing | New | `GET /inv/in-transit`; daily job. |
| FR-05 inter-project valuation at book rate, inter-state treatment | New | The destination project owns the received entry; `costTransfer` in `inv.transfer.received`; inter-state is a flag, GST is D-562. |
| BR-01 leave at dispatch into transit, enter at receipt, discrepancies held | New | ADR-579, ADR-580. |
| Workflow transfer CM, both PMs between projects; write-off STOREMGR then PROCMGR | Reuse the engine | `workflowTypes.register` `INV_TRANSFER`, `INV_TRANSFER_DISC`. |
| Roles SK, CM, STOREMGR, PM | Extend templates | `PERMISSIONS_16_05`, `ADDED_16_05`. |
| Pages (list and object, request, mobile receive, in-transit report) | New | Four pages under the Stores group. |

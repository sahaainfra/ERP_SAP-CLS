# Part 22.07 discovery: Retention, Security Deposit & Advance Recovery Ledgers

Date: 2026-11-21. Depends on 22.04 (client certification), 22.05 (subcontractor bills), 21.02 (contracts, bank guarantees, advances). Search terms checked (retention, security, deposit, advance, recovery, ledgers, release).

| Requirement | Decision | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 retention ledger per contract and work order: deducted per bill, released, balance | New (table named `bill_retention_ledger`) | `ledger.js`; entries are posted from the events `bill.client.certified` (retention) and `bill.sub.approved` (retention and security deposit), once for each bill (unique index on source, kind and type); the balance is kept on every entry; `retention.summary` per contract (receivable) or work order (payable) |
| FR-02 advances received and given, recovery schedule (percent of bill or fixed), interest, recovered and outstanding, linked bank guarantees | New (tables `bill_advances`, `bill_advance_recoveries`), reads 21.02 | advance with rule PCT_OF_BILL or FIXED, simple interest by days, recovery rows per bill; advances received are synced to the contract advances (`advances.apply`); the guarantee is chosen from the contract guarantees |
| FR-03 release requests with checklist (DLP completion, no pending defects, BG substitution) and approval | New (table `bill_releases`) | automatic checklist: balance, end of the defects liability period (contract revised completion plus DLP months, or the work order end), no open NCRs, a bank guarantee in place for a release against a guarantee; a failing required check blocks submission unless a reason (5+ characters) is given; chain COMMGR, PM, CFO; approval posts RELEASED to the ledger |
| FR-04 ageing and expected release dates for the cash forecast | New | buckets OVERDUE, 30 days, 90 days, later, not known; `expectedCash(companyId)` for 23.13; export to CSV |
| BR-01 recovery per bill is computed from the rule and cannot exceed the outstanding | New | `recoveryOf` caps the principal at the outstanding; a bill spreads its recovery over the active approved advances, first come first served; replays of the same bill are ignored |

## Requirement by requirement

| Requirement | Decision | What |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 proposal from due bills, advances, statutory payments; filters; cash | **New** + **Reuse** | the candidates: approved bills with something left and no hold (23.03 `holdsOf`), MSME and overdue first, filters by due date, project, partner, MSME; approved advances; on-account lines; the book balance of the bank account (cash position 23.13 not built: D-716; statutory payments are 23.10's challans: D-714) |

| FR-04 advice to the vendor | **New** + **Reuse** 04.07 | e-mail through the gateway with the bills paid and their TDS; PDF advice (WhatsApp and portal: D-717) |

## Data
New tables `fin_bank_accounts`, `pmt_proposals`, `pmt_proposal_lines`, `pmt_payments` (migration `20261218000340_pmt_payments`); no pre-existing table changed. Payments are created only by the release of an approved proposal (no API creates or edits them).

## Not in this part
Employees (expense claims) are not paid here yet: their payable is not posted (D-712) and their bank numbers are HR's (D-713).

# Discovery note 04.02

Inspected (searched: approval, authority, delegation, limits, matrix, rules, wf_authority_limits, wf_delegations, wf_escalation_paths, wf_matrix, wf_matrix_rules): the workflow engine of 04.01 (accepted, pushed) already has definitions, instances, tasks, delegation of a single task, reminders and one escalation to the administrators after a grace period, but its chain is the fixed steps of a definition, its due time is plain hours, and a delegation only moves one task. Calendars (01.03) exist: `core.calendar.week_off_days` and the holidays, with `isWorkingDay`. There is no leave module (07.07 later). The change-request engine of 01.03 is the older maker-checker; the matrix uses the new engine for its own changes. The reference bands are in `docs/erp/07_APPROVAL_WORKFLOW_REFERENCE.md` section B.

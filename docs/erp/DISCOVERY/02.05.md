# Discovery note 02.05

Inspected: after part 02.04 permissions are computed per request by the role engine, roles and scoped assignments exist, and every sensitive change goes through the interim approval engine, which already refuses the requester as approver (D-022). There was no segregation-of-duties rule, no way to see who holds conflicting duties, no time-boxed elevated access (the Super Admin role is a normal two-person assignment, D-090), no access review and no check for accounts nobody uses (searched: sod, segregation, elevation, break-glass, access_review, dormant, last_login). `iam_users.last_login_at` exists from 02.01. The documents most of the standard rules protect (purchase, payment, journal, payroll, measurement, stock) belong to later parts.

| Requirement | Existing | Decision | Where |
|---|---|---|---|

## Decisions

| Requirement | Existing | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 Builder with drivers and magnitudes per project / portfolio | — | **New** `scn_scenarios` and ten drivers with validated ranges. Scenarios are per project; a portfolio roll-up is not built (D-781, 28.03). |
| FR-02 EAC and margin | `etcOf`, forecast lines | **Reuse** on a copy. A price, wage or quantity driver changes remaining quantity × rate of the matching lines by its percent (T-01); breakdown, scope and delay overhead add their cost; margin = revenue (+ scope revenue) − EAC. |
| FR-02 Completion date (schedule copy recalculation) | `calculate` | **Reuse**: the CPM of 13.01 on a copy with the delays added to the activities named (else the last open critical activity); the working days lost × overhead per day go into the cost. |
| FR-02 13-week cash | `weekly` | **Reuse** on a copy: the project's collections shifted by the weeks given, the extra cost spread over the horizon, the weeks under a floor listed. |
| FR-03 Compare, save, share, convert | Tasks | **New** compare (2–6 scenarios, chart and table, export); save draft / saved; share with named people (notified); a task or a forecast assumption (a task plus the `bi.scenario.assumption_proposed` event; the forecast changes only through its own approval). |
| BR-01 Never modify live records | — | The engine only reads; a fingerprint test proves the forecast, schedule and cash forecast rows are unchanged. |
| Roles | Role templates | **New** `bi.scenario.{view,create,edit,delete,share,convert,export}` for DIR, CFO, PD and PCMGR, scoped by project; only the author changes, shares or deletes a scenario. |
| Maker-checker | — | The part adds no configuration (no settings, rules or templates): nothing to approve. Every create, change, share, action and export is audited. |

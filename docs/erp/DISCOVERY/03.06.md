# Discovery note 03.06

Inspected (searched: capabilities, device, layouts, responsive, manifest, serviceWorker, BarcodeDetector, getUserMedia, geolocation, PushManager, viewport, breakpoint): the client has no manifest, icons or service worker, so it cannot be installed or opened offline. Device access exists only inside form fields (`GpsField`, `PhotoField`, `QrScanField`, `SignaturePad` of 03.02): a position with its accuracy, a photo from the camera or gallery, a code read with `BarcodeDetector`, typed or from a photo. There is no shared device layer, no image compression, no EXIF handling, no push subscription, no QR resolver, no field home or scanner page. The shell of 03.05 hides the sidebar behind a menu button below 768 px; breakpoint tokens exist (600/900/1200/1600) but not the 360/768/1024/1280/1920 rules of this part. The List Report already draws cards on a phone (03.03); dialogs are centred boxes; the offline banner of 03.04 exists. The content security policy is `default-src 'self'` (a same-origin service worker and manifest are allowed).

Prerequisite: 03.05 accepted (committed `ea6219d`, pushed). The offline engine (04.15), notification service (04.06), Messages (05.01), attendance (07.05), DPR (14.02), GRN (16.03) and inspections (18.02) are later parts: this part provides the device capabilities they will call and shows their tiles when they register pages; it does not simulate them.

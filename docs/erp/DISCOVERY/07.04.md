# Discovery note 07.04

Inspected (searched: att_policies, att_policy_assignments, att_rosters, att_shifts, attendance, calendars, policies, shifts, work): no attendance table existed. What exists and is reused:
- the **holiday calendars of 01.03** (`core_holiday_calendars`, `core_holidays`, `holidaysOn`: company, state and site calendars) and the weekly-off parameter `core.calendar.week_off_days`; the financial periods of `core_periods` are accounting periods and are not the attendance period;
- the employee master (07.01), **deployments and crews (07.03)** (where a person is on a date, and in which crew), the permission engine, the workflow engine and matrix, the List Report and export service, notifications, the object-type registry;
- not present: punches and geofence attendance (07.05, 07.06), leave (07.07), payroll (24.02, 24.03), a PDF engine.

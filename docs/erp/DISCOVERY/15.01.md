# Part 15.01 discovery: Material Requests (Site Indents) & Purchase Requisitions

Date: 2026-11-29. New module `proc`, flag `ff.proc.p1501`. Depends on 14.01 (work authorisations, read in place), 12.03 (the budget check and the commitment ledger, called through their service) and 06.03 (the item master and the item selector). Searched `indents`, `material`, `proc_material_requests`, `proc_mr_lines`, `proc_pr_lines`, `proc_requisitions`, `purchase`, `requests`, `requisitions`, `site`: no procurement tables or code existed; the navigation group `g-procurement` and the sample requests of the platform demonstrations (`SampleRequestsPage`, the sample document of 04.01) were the only traces, and are untouched. 16.01 (stock), 15.02 (MRP), 11.03 (rate contracts, approved makes) and 15.04 (orders) do not exist.

# Part 14.02 discovery: Daily Progress Report (DPR)

Date: 2026-11-23. Module `site` (extended), flag `ff.site.p1402`. Depends on 14.01 (work authorisations, their uses and the daily plan, read in place) and 04.15 (the offline engine, extended with lines in command values). Reads attendance (07.06 register `att_daily`), contractors and workers (08 labour), the item master and the schedule. Provides the event `site.dpr.approved` to progress (14.05), productivity (14.06), material reconciliation (16.10), MB suggestions (22.01) and execution intelligence (27.06).

Searched for `daily`, `progress`, `report`, `site_dprs`, `site_dpr_*`, `dpr`: no report existed. What exists: the offline form code `dpr` listed as planned for this part (04.15); `plan.onDpr`, a consumer of `site.dpr.approved` that reads `lines` with percentages (13.01, "14.05 publishes it"); the work authorisation uses (`site_wa_uses`) that 14.01 made the adapter for material issues, plant logs and DPR quantities; `att_daily` (attendance by person, day and site) with `lab_workers` (trade, contractor) and `hr_employees` (designation). Not built: material issue (16.04), plant logs (17.03), weather (14.03), permits and safety incidents (19.x), the client portal.

Decisions taken here:

- **The event does not carry `lines`.** `plan.onDpr` (13.01) turns `lines` with percentages into schedule progress and moves the data date; 14.05 owns that calculation. This part publishes `work` (quantities against work authorisations), so the plan's consumer does nothing until 14.05 provides percentages.
- **The offline engine takes lines.** A command's values may hold lists of flat records (at most 300 of 20 fields); conflicts compare lists by content. Nothing else of the engine changed; a form may name its own page (`route`) and the Field Forms page opens it.
- **Quantities of the DPR are booked at approval** as `DPR` uses of the work authorisations (what the closure of a WA reads in 14.01); a site's report replaces the bookings of the supervisors' reports it consolidates.

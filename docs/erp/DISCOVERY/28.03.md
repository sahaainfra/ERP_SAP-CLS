## Decisions

| Requirement | Existing | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 Models per use case, ranges and drivers | — | **New** `ai/forecast.js` with three statistical methods: moving average, Holt linear smoothing (level and trend, the ARIMA/Prophet-like baseline) and regression on time and drivers. Each gives an 80 % range from its one-step errors, widening with √horizon, and names its drivers. Use cases: RATE, COLLECTIONS, PRODUCTIVITY and COMPLETION (completion date with its earliest and latest date). |
| FR-02 Material rates by material and region | 15.09 | **Reuse** the rate series as the person; the category price index is a regression driver, extended by its own trend. |
| FR-03 Statistical outliers complementing 27.09 | 27.09 rules | **New** `aif_anomalies`: the robust z-score (median and MAD) of a value against its own series. It covers purchase rates by item and unit, daily issues by project and item, daily attendance by site, and payments by payee, plus paid staff with no attendance in the month (D-780). Each flag names its source record and is shown only to people who may see that source. A person confirms or dismisses it with a note. |
| FR-04 Back-testing, registry, versions, approval | `createFlow` and the workflow engine | **New** `aif_models` versions with method, parameters and horizon. A rolling-origin back-test (MAE, MAPE, RMSE, bias, range coverage, against the last-value forecast) must run before the version is sent. Section 9: model owner → CFO or PCMGR (`AI_MODEL`). One official version per use case; a newer approval supersedes it; retiring needs no approval. |
| BR-01 Never overwrite actuals; labels | — | Forecasts are kept in `aif_forecasts` (one a day per model and subject), never in the owners' tables. Every point is ACTUAL, CALCULATED or FORECAST, and every answer carries "Forecast — statistical, verify". Without an approved model, a forecast is a labelled preview and is not stored. |
| Predictions to dashboards and scenarios | Widget registry, Project 360 | **New** widget `ai.forecast_anomalies` (dashboards and Project 360) and `forecastOf(db).latest` for other modules (scenarios: D-781 → 30.01). |
| Roles | Role templates | `ai.model.*`, `ai.forecast.view/export`, `ai.anomaly.view/review/run`. ITADM and SYSADM configure; CFO, PCMGR and PROCMGR prepare and use; CFO and PCMGR approve; DIR and AUD read. A back-test and a forecast read the history as the person, never with borrowed rights. |

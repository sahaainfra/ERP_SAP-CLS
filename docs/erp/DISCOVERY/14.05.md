# Part 14.05 discovery: Progress Measurement, Earned Value & S-Curves

Date: 2026-11-26. Module `site` (extended), flag `ff.site.p1405`. Depends on 14.02 (the event `site.dpr.approved`, read by a consumer), 13.03 (the current baseline, read in place), 12.01 (the approved budget, read in place) and 13.01 (the schedule, which keeps its percent complete through its own service). Searched `curves`, `earned`, `measurement`, `progress`, `prs_*`: nothing existed except 13.01's schedule progress (`plan_activities.pct_complete`, its `onDpr` consumer reading `lines` that the DPR event does not carry, D-485 of 14.02) and the baseline snapshots of 13.03. 14.07 (actual cost) and 22.01 (measurement book) do not exist.

| Requirement | Decision | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 physical progress from approved DPR quantities, roll-up by weights | New (`prs_activity_progress`, consumer of `site.dpr.approved`; `prs_weights`) | ADR-499, ADR-501. Budget share by quantity among the activities of a WBS node, or a manual weight. |
| FR-02 period close with snapshot, adjustments with reason and approval | New (`prs_periods`, workflow `SITE_PROGRESS_PERIOD`, PM) | ADR-500. |
| FR-03 planned from the baseline, forecast from the schedule | Reuse (`plan_baselines.snapshot_json`, `plan_activities.ef`) | Falls back to the schedule's own dates and says so. |
| FR-04 PV, EV, AC, SV, CV, SPI, CPI, EAC, TCPI | New (`evm.js`, `prs_evm`) | AC typed on the period (D-493). EAC is BAC / CPI. |
| FR-05 S-curves, milestone chart | New (`/progress/scurve`, the dashboard) | Physical and financial; gaps for actual after and forecast before the data date (chart extended to draw gaps). |
| FR-06 dashboards, client report | New (four pages) | Client report has no money and is the snapshot of a closed period. |
| BR-01 only approved DPR quantities; MB overrides where configured | Event consumer; `recordMeasured`, `site.progress.mb_override` | D-494. |
| BR-02 closed periods immutable, adjustments only in an open period | Service | Amendment of the latest closed period only. |
| Event `prog.period.closed` | `site.period.closed` | Naming by module code. |
| Permissions | New `site.period.*` plus `cost` | PLAN and PCMGR manage, PM closes; PD, DIR, QS, COMMGR, AUD read. |

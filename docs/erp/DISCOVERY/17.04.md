# Part 17.04 discovery: Fuel Management & Anti-Leakage Control

Date: 2026-11-13. Depends on 17.03 (log books: working hours, fuel in the log, allocation by date) and 16.04 (stores issues; goods receipts of 16.03 are the source of bulk fuel). 17.01 supplies the fuel type, tank capacity and fuel norm of each machine. Search terms checked (fuel, bowser, dip, tank, diesel, leakage, anti-leakage, plt_fuel): the register already holds `fuel_type`, `tank_capacity`, `fuel_norm` and the log holds `fuel_litres`; no fuel store, issue, dip or detection existed.

## Findings
| Requirement | Decision | Evidence in the repository |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 fuel stores: tanks, bowsers, cards; receipts from PO/GRN or pump purchases | New | `plt_fuel_tanks`, `plt_fuel_receipts`. A receipt against a goods receipt of 16.03 is checked against its litre-unit lines and what was already received; a pump purchase needs the bill and the rate and carries the petty-cash voucher as a reference. The tank has its own book (the stores ledger is not changed). |
| FR-02 mobile fuel issue with meter, litres, issuer, receiver, photo; tank dip | New | `plt_fuel_issues` (approval-controlled, workflow `PLANT_FUEL_ISSUE`, photograph through the file service) and `plt_fuel_dips`. Mobile pages Fuel Issue and Tank Dip Entry. |
| FR-03 daily tank reconciliation | New | `dayOf`: opening + receipts - issues = book, against the dip; tolerance is the larger of a minimum (20 l) and a percent of what the tank held and received (parameters). The dip of a day starts the next. A difference beyond the tolerance raises `TANK_VARIANCE`; a corrected dip withdraws it. |
| FR-04 consumption against the norm from meter deltas | New | Per issue (meter run since the previous issue, only when the previous fill was a full one) and per machine/class/project for a period (first full fill to last, litres of the later fills over the run), against the norm of the register; litres per working hour from verified logs. |
| FR-05 detection rules | New | Above the norm by X percent; fuel to a machine not logged as working; two issues within a short interval; more than a full tank in 24 hours; meter not advancing; plus tank variance and log mismatch. Issue rules run when the issue is sent, the rest in job `plant.fuel_sweep`. |
| FR-06 fuel cost to equipment and project | New | Weighted average rate of the tank up to the day; cost in `plant.fuel.issue_approved` with equipment, class, allocation and project; masked without `plant.fuel.costs`. |
| BR-01 not above the tank of the machine; meter must advance | New | Hard checks in `derive`. An equal meter is allowed only with a reason, and raises `METER_NOT_ADVANCING`. |
| Workflow: fuel anomaly acceptance PMSUP then PM | New | `PLANT_FUEL_ANOMALY`; explained by the plant manager, so the maker never approves. The issue itself is verified by the plant supervisor (`PLANT_FUEL_ISSUE`). |
| Outputs to 17.07, 14.07, 27.09, 19.05 | New | Events `plant.fuel.issue_approved`, `plant.fuel.received`, `plant.fuel.reconciled`, `plant.fuel.anomaly_raised`, `plant.fuel.anomaly_accepted`, `plant.fuel.leakage_confirmed`. |
| Pages | New | Fuel Issue and Tank Dip Entry (mobile), Fuel Stores, Fuel Issues, Fuel Reports (daily tank reconciliation, consumption against norm), Fuel Anomalies. |

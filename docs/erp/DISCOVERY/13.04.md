# Part 13.04 discovery: Look-ahead planning, constraints and weekly work plans

Date: 2026-11-20. Module `plan` (extended), flag `ff.plan.p1304`. Depends on 13.01 (schedule, activities, CPM, calendars); reads the RFIs and drawings of 11.01 and 11.02, the crews and deployments of 07.03 and the resources of 13.02; provides the event `plan.weekly_plan.approved` (the daily plan) and `GET /api/v1/plan/daily-plan` to 14.01, and the events `plan.constraint.raised` and `plan.constraint.cleared` to the dashboards.

Searched for `lookahead`, `look-ahead`, `plan_constraints`, `plan_weekly`, `weekly`, `PPC`, `constraint`, `make-ready`: nothing was built. What exists and is used: the schedule core and the Gantt edit of 13.01 and 13.02 (activities with early dates, float and calendar; the drag edit that recalculates on the server), RFIs with their drawing, WBS node, location, activity reference and required-by date (`engg_rfis`, whose service already listed the activities that open RFIs hold "for the look-ahead (13.04)"), the drawing register with its current IFC revision (`engg_drawings`), crews and approved deployments (`hr_crews`, `hr_deployments`), the sites and locations of the project, and the approval, audit, notification, event, list and export services.

| Requirement | Decision | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 look-ahead generator: activities starting or running in the next N weeks by site or work front | New (`GET /schedules/:id/lookahead`, `plan_lookaheads`, Look-ahead Board) | N is 1 to 12 weeks from the Monday of this week (or a chosen date); filters by site (through the locations of the site), work front (a location and everything under it), blocked only and a search. A look-ahead can be saved: its window and its counts of ready and blocked activities. |
| FR-02 constraint log: type, description, owner, due date, status; found automatically in other modules | New (`plan_constraints`, Constraint Log) | Found by the system: an open RFI on the activity (by activity reference, or by the WBS node and location of the RFI), a drawing of the activity's WBS node and location with no IFC revision yet, a finish-to-start predecessor that should be finished and is not, labour of a trade that no approved crew covers over the dates of the activity. Logged by hand: any of the ten types. Procurement and stores (M15, M16), permits and plant registers are not built: those types are by hand only (D-479). |
| FR-03 make-ready: ready only when all constraints are cleared | New | An activity is ready when it has no open constraint. A weekly plan is not sent with a commitment on an activity that has one (422 naming them); a constraint found by the system that does not apply is waived with a reason and is not raised again for the same source. |
| FR-04 weekly work plan: commitments per crew, actuals, PPC, variance codes | New (`plan_weekly_plans`, `plan_weekly_items`, Weekly Work Plan page and report) | Commitments by activity and crew (quantity above zero, the activity planned inside the week, a crew of the project); approved by the construction manager and then the project manager; the site engineer and the work supervisor then record what was done; a commitment is completed when the completed quantity reaches the committed one; closing the week fixes PPC and needs a variance code for every commitment not completed. An approved plan is changed by a revision, which replaces it when approved. |
| FR-05 daily plan breakdown for work authorisation | New (`GET /weekly-plans/:id/daily`, `GET /daily-plan`, event payload) | Every commitment is spread over the working days of the week between the dates of its activity on the calendar of the schedule; the last day takes the remainder so the total is exact. |
| BR-01 PPC = completed commitments / total commitments × 100 | Service | Worked out on the server on every record (live) and fixed when the week is closed; the trend is of closed weeks. |
| BR-02 auto-detected constraints clear when the source resolves | Service + events | The schedule is looked at again when an RFI is issued, answered or closed, a drawing is issued, progress is updated or a schedule is approved, and on request; a constraint whose source is resolved, or whose activity is finished, is cleared by `system` with the audit row and the event. |
| Permissions | New `plan.lookahead.view`, `plan.constraint.{view,manage}`, `plan.weekly_plan.{view,create,edit,delete,submit,approve,cancel,amend,print,export,record}` | PLAN and CM prepare; SE and WSUP edit commitments in a draft, manage constraints and record what was done; PM and CM approve; the preparers hold no `record`. |

Decisions taken here:

- **Detection is a write, the board is a read.** The board only reads the constraints already stored; detection runs on the events, when a look-ahead is saved, when a plan is submitted and on request, so that a page view never changes data.
- **A weekly plan belongs to one schedule and one week.** One live plan for a project, site and week (and revision); a plan for the whole project has no site. The week starts on a Monday.
- **The chain is CM then PM.** A construction manager who prepared a plan gets no task on it (the engine never gives the originator one), so the other construction manager reviews.
- **No import in this part.** The standard endpoint list names `import` for the weekly plan; the commitments are made from the board and the plan page, and a workbook import is logged as D-479.
- **Drag on the board is the Gantt edit.** Moving a card to another week is the MOVE edit of 13.02 (start no earlier than the Monday chosen), tried first on the server, recalculated and undoable there; it needs the schedule in work and `plan.schedule.edit`.

Prerequisites 13.01 and 13.02 are Ready for review and are used through their services; none of their tables is altered.

## Decisions

| Requirement | Existing | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 Analysis of DPR and inspection photographs | File events, jobs, the integration hub | **Extend**: a consumer of `core.file.available` queues `ai.vision.analyse` for photographs of those records. The company's detector does the work (`ai.cv.detector`): the built-in colour detector, or an AI connector of the new hub provider `vision_http`, an HTTP inference endpoint returning labelled boxes. |
| FR-02 Suggestions with boxes and confidence; confirm to create observations or NCRs | 19.04, 18.04 services | **New** `cv_results` with suggestions (label from an allow-list, confidence, box). **Reuse** the owners' services. Confirming a PPE gap creates an observation of type UNSAFE_CONDITION that names nobody; a defect creates an NCR. Each is created with the reviewer's rights and the (blurred) photograph attached. Progress and material cues are confirmed as notes. |
| FR-03 Face blurring; no biometric identification | `sharp` | **New**: face boxes, and the head third of person boxes, are blurred into a copy (`ai.cv.blur_faces`, on by default). The worklist shows the copy, and the reviewer can blur more by hand. Labels outside the allow-list (identities, names) are dropped. |
| FR-04 Model performance and false positives | — | **New**: "Not right" with a reason. Precision per model and finding = confirmed ÷ (confirmed + wrong). |
| BR-01 No disciplinary use; no identification | — | Observations name no person and the responsible party is the crew. Nothing goes to HR, the penalty flow or attendance. |
| Roles | Templates | `ai.vision.view`, `review` and `run` for HSEE, QCE and PM; AUD reads. HSEE and QCE also gain `site.dpr.view` (read only, within their assignments) so they can see the DPR photographs they review. |

# Part 22.05 discovery: Subcontractor Measurement & Bills

Date: 2026-11-21. Depends on 21.03 (work orders and their adapters), 22.01 (the measurement formulas, the client certified quantity), 21.04 (compliance hold), and the recoveries of 16.04, 16.08, 18.04, 19.04, 19.07. Search terms checked (bill_sub_bill_items, bill_sub_bills, bills, measurement, subcontractor).

| Requirement | Decision | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 subcontractor measurement against WO items, reusing the MB engine, joint signature (portal/OTP) | New table `bill_sub_measurements`, reuse `mbQty` of 22.01 | `sub.js` measurements: the formulas of 22.01, never beyond the ordered quantity of the item; signature recorded by name, reference and method (MANUAL, OTP, PORTAL); verification by someone other than the measurer |
| FR-02 bill: WO items x rates; labour-rate bills; lump-sum milestones | New (`bill_sub_bills`, `bill_sub_bill_items`) | items from the verified, unbilled measurements priced at the rate of the WO item; the milestone items of a lump-sum WO are measured as quantity 1 and billed by a milestone bill; labour-rate WOs bill from measured quantity (attendance-based billing: D-658) |
| FR-03 deductions gathered automatically from the owning modules | New, reads 16.04, 16.08, 18.04, 19.04, 19.07 | retention, security deposit and advance recovery of the work order; free-issue excess (`inv_free_issue_ledger`), material issued on charge, damage and loss of formwork (`rta_damage_recoveries`), NCR back-charges, approved safety penalties, camp and mess; TDS by the section of the WO and the constitution of the party; BOCW cess; typed other recoveries; `bill_sub_claims` stops a recovery being deducted twice; the NCR is marked posted by its owner (`postBackchargeFromBill`) |
| FR-04 compliance check gating approval/payment | Reuse 21.04 | the hold of `ctr_sub_holds` (with the waivers it already honours) stops the last approval when `bill.sub.compliance_gate` is on |
| FR-05 verification chain, payable creation, bill PDF | New, adapters | chain SE verify, QS, PM, COMMGR above 10 lakh, CFO above 50 lakh; `bill.sub.approved` carries the payable data (23.03), the commitment (12.03) and the retention (22.07); `subBills.linkPayable` is the adapter 23.03 calls; PDF with previous, this and cumulative |
| FR-06 subcontract quantity not beyond the client-certified quantity for back-to-back items | New | `bill.sub.back_to_back` WARN, BLOCK or OFF against `mb.certifiedQtyOf` of the client BOQ line of the WO item |
| BR-01 cumulative billed not above cumulative measured; deductions only from the owning modules | New | `build` refuses a cumulative above the verified measurement or the ordered quantity; deductions are read from the owners, never typed (except other recoveries) |

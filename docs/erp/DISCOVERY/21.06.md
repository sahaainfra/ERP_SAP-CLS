# Part 21.06 discovery: Claims, Notices, EOT & Disputes

Date: 2026-11-21. Depends on 21.01 (contract, clauses and dates), 13.05 (delays and time-impact records), 11.05 (letters and time-bars), 21.05 (rejected variations). Search terms checked (claim, notice, time bar, eot, quantum, prolongation, dispute, engg_time_bars, plan_delays, plan_tia, engg_letters).

| Requirement | Decision | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 claim events from delays, hindrances, instructions, rejected variations, suspension; notice within the time-bar with a countdown | New, reusing 11.05 and 13.05 | `ctr_claim_events` captured by the daily job from approved employer delays (`plan_delays`), rejected or disputed change requests and hindrance notes of approved DPRs, or entered by hand; the notice is an outward letter of `engg_letters`; time-bar days from the contract time-bar clause |

| FR-05 submission versions, client responses, negotiation, determination; EOT to the contract date and the baseline | New and reuse | `ctr_claim_log`, `submitToClient`, `logResponse`, `determine`; `contracts.applyEot` moves the revised completion date once per claim (`ctr_eot_awards`); events `ctr.eot.awarded` and `ctr.claim.determined` |

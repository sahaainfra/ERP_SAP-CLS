
# Discovery note 06.04

Inspected (searched: business, clients, master, mdm_partner_*, mdm_partners, partner, subcontractors, vendors, bank, gstin, msme, blacklist, payment hold, encrypt): no partner master with KYC existed. What exists:
- the governed **business partner master type of 06.01** (`mdm_records` type PARTNER, a pilot of the governance framework: name, kind, PAN, one GSTIN, mobile, bank account in the version data);
- the formats, GSTIN check character, name similarity and masking of 06.01;
- the states, banks/IFSC, TDS sections and lookup lists of 06.02 and the item categories of 06.03;
- the vault for encryption at rest (04.07);
- one-time codes (04.07);
- the workflow engine, approval matrix, record registry, files, collaboration tabs, List Report with exports, Excel engine, notifications, parameters and the job scheduler.

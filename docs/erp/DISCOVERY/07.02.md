# Discovery note 07.02

Inspected (searched: hr_candidates, hr_interviews, hr_offers, hr_onboarding_tasks, hr_requisitions, manpower, onboarding, recruitment, requisition, headcount): nothing for recruitment existed. What exists and is reused:
- the **employee master of 07.01** (`employeesOf(db).registration.create`, its workflow, `userLink.createUser`, the senior-level parameter, the file entity pattern);
- the approval matrix and workflow engine (04.01, 04.02) with roles, conditions on the context, distinct approvers, resubmit, withdraw, abandon;
- the **tasks engine** (04.08: `tasksOf(db).create` and `act`, `holders` of a role), notifications (04.06), parameters, files and virus scan (04.10), List Report and export service, collaboration tabs, the job scheduler and the vault (04.07);
- the numbering service (01.03; no series for requisitions or offers, so the same adapter as 07.01);
- not present: the manpower plan (17.08), a PDF template engine for letters (04.11 is available as printable HTML only), an SMS/e-mail connector for the offer link (the link is shown to HR), the skills master (07.10).

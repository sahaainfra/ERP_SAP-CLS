# Discovery 10.06: Project risks, issues and opportunities register

Inspected: the project master and WBS of 10.01 and 10.03 (`proj_projects`, `proj_wbs`), the workflow engine and its resolvers (`proj.pm`, `proj.director` of 10.01, `proj.chair` of 10.05), the task service of 04.08 (auto tasks deduplicated by source), the alert engine of 04.09 (`alertDatasets`, library rules in `alr_rules`, `alertsOf(db).run`), the list, export and idempotency services, the permission templates and route policy, the page tracker and navigation seeds of earlier parts, the cost-code master (`fin_cost_codes`). Nothing for risks existed: no table, no dataset, no rule.

| Requirement | Decision | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 risk register with assessment, response, residual rating, exposure, EMV | New | `proj_risks` (risks and opportunities in one table), score, level and EMV computed on the server. |
| FR-02 opportunity register | New | same table (`rtype`), opportunity strategies exploit, enhance, share, accept. |
| FR-03 issue log | New | `proj_risk_issues`, own lifecycle (open, in progress, resolved, cancelled). |
| FR-04 triggers and alerts | New + Reuse | `proj_risk_triggers`; dataset `proj.risk_trigger` and library rule `risk.trigger_fired` of 04.09; event `proj.risk.trigger_fired`. |
| FR-05 contingency allocation and drawdown | New | `proj_risk_contingency` ledger, pool in `proj_risk_settings`; the drawdown is a request executed by 12.02. |
| FR-06 monthly review with snapshot history | New | `proj_risk_snapshots`, workflow `PROJ_RISK_REVIEW` (project manager, project director). |
| BR-01 EMV and configurable heat-map thresholds | New | thresholds and probability scale per project in `proj_risk_settings.thresholds_json`. |
| BR-02 closing a high risk needs owner justification and PM approval | New | workflow `PROJ_RISK_CLOSE`, resolver `proj.riskpm`. |
| Actions as tasks | Reuse | `tasksOf(db).create` with a unique source per action. |
| Pages | New (5) | Risk Dashboard, Risk and Opportunity Register, Risk, Issue Log, Issue. |
| Outputs to 12.02, 23.11, 21.06 | New (read side) | `riskExposureOf(db, projectId)`, events, `settleDrawdown`. |
| Shared services | Reuse | permissions, workflow, tasks, alerts, export, audit, events, i18n, list engine, feature flags. |

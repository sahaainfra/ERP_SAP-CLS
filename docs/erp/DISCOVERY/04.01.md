# Discovery note 04.01

Inspected (searched: core, definitions, engine, instances, states, wf_definitions, wf_instances, wf_task_attachments, wf_tasks, workflow, approval): there is no workflow engine. The only approval code is the stand-in of part 01.03 (`platform/approvals/index.js`, tables `core_change_requests` and `core_change_request_steps`, defect D-022): a request carries a chain of role codes, each step needs one holder of the role, nobody approves their own request or two steps of one, a rejection or return needs a comment, and the change is applied by the handler of the request type inside the final approval. It is used by 18 request types (period reopening, numbering, schedules, file settings, formula activation, operations configuration, connectors, authentication policy, role and assignment changes, access reviews, navigation, dashboards, wording, page addresses and the sample material request). It has no delegation, parallel steps, clarification, SLA, versioned definitions or amendment. The task inbox and bell of part 03.05 and the "my work" widget of part 03.07 read it through `awaiting()` and the `workSources` registry.

Prerequisites: 02.04 (permission engine: `check`, `scopeFilter`) and 01.04 (outbox, bus, jobs, scheduler) accepted and pushed; the file service (02.x/01.x), the parameter store, the audit trail, the SoD ledger and the change-request engine are used, not copied. The approval matrix (04.02) and the notification service (04.06) do not exist yet: the engine exposes the hooks they will use (resolvers, step conditions, the `workflow.notification.requested` event) and records the gap in DEFECTS.md.

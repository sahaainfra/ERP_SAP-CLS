# Discovery note 03.08

Inspected (searched: command, core_search_index, global, palette, search, fts, objectTypes, recent, favourites): the application had a go-to palette (part 03.05: Ctrl/Cmd+K, pages from the navigation registry, favourites and recent records) and nothing that searches records. Lists search their own columns (`q` of the List Report); there was no cross-record index, no full-text search, no command registry and no search log. The database is SQLite (ADR-1, `node:sqlite`), so the PostgreSQL full-text of the prompt becomes SQLite FTS5 (ADR-141).

Prerequisites: 03.05 (shell, navigation registry, recent records, favourites, palette) and 02.04 (permission engine: `scopeFilter`, `check`, `maskRule`) accepted and pushed (`ea6219d`, `e229420`); 03.07 committed (`c327e77`). No adapter was needed. The records that exist to be searched are the organisation (projects, sites, departments, branches, cost centres), people and roles, the sample documents and bills of the platform, the sample material requests, files attached to records and the discussion on records. Purchase orders, vendors, items, bills, employees, drawings, tasks and messages do not exist yet: their modules register with `searchEntities.register` when they are built (D-157).

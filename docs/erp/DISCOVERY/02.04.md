# Discovery note 02.04

Inspected: after part 02.03 permissions came from one constant map of nine roles (`ROLE_PERMISSIONS` in `platform/auth`), a person's roles were a list on the user record, scope was the company only (D-060, D-068), and the only masked fields were two hand-written ones (the mobile number of a user, the PAN of a company). There was no permission registry, role table, assignment, explainer or comparison (searched: abac, engine, iam_assignments, iam_field_masks, iam_permissions, iam_role_mapping, iam_role_permissions, iam_roles, permission, rbac, role, scopes). The approval engine is the interim one (D-022). The employee master (07.01) and the approval matrix (04.02) do not exist.

# Discovery note 01.06

Inspected: after part 01.05 the repository had exact decimal arithmetic (`src/shared/decimal.js`) and money helpers (`money.js`: `lineAmount`, `percentOf`, `allocate`), the golden-output harness with one fixture (`prog.flag_evaluation_matrix`), and `CALCULATION_REGISTRY.md` from part 00.01, which says "no money, tax, retention, stock or payroll calculations exist yet". Search terms (calculation, central, core_calc_traces, core_formulas, engine, formula, registry) found no registry, no versioned formulas, no trace, no unit conversion and no shadow comparison. The only arithmetic in modules was the numbering formatter and the helpers above.

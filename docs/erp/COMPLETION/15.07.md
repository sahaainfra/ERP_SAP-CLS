## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 218, 219; migrate up/down/up clean): `xpd_expediting_plans`, `xpd_milestones`, `xpd_inspections`, `xpd_shipments`, `xpd_shipment_events`; flag, navigation, notification templates.
- **Expediting plan** (FR-01): per approved order, milestones with planned, forecast and actual dates (a standard template back from the date required on site when none is given); approved by the procurement manager; planned dates are locked after approval, progress is recorded by the buyer or by the vendor on its order link; amend by a copy that replaces the plan.
- **Risk and alerts** (FR-05): the expected arrival (shipments, else dispatch plus transit days) against the date required on site; a late forecast raises `proc.expediting.delay_detected` with the activities of the approved schedule that start before arrival, and tells the expediter, project manager and planner; daily job `proc.xpd_sweep`.
- **Inspections** (FR-02): stage, final, third-party; result and punch points; only a passed inspection goes to the release note, approved by a quality engineer then the quality manager; the note number is issued at approval; re-inspection by amend.
- **Shipments** (FR-03, FR-04, BR-01): transporter, LR or BL, e-way bill, packing list, over-dimensional permit checked against the ETA; dispatch needs an issued release note of a final or third-party inspection where inspection is mandatory, else control CP-PROC-07 stops it; tracking events move the ETA; delivery closes the plan when all shipments are in.
- **API**: 43 routes under `/api/v1/proc` plus 2 public vendor routes (2,633 routes in all). **Browser**: Expediting Dashboard, Expediting Plans, Vendor Inspections, Shipments Tracker (list, object, form each), milestones panel on the vendor order page.

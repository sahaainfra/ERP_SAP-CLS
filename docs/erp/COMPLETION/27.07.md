# Part 27.07 — Monthly Closing Protocol, Project MIS & Budget Master

Execution step 201 · 2026-11-21 · Branch `ccr-6934092b-qrkoec` · **In progress**: built and tested behind `ff.bi.p2707`. Formal acceptance waits for 16.10 and 23.14 and for the company G1/G2 sign-offs.

## 1. Discovery

The finance month close (23.14) is company-wide and has no project MIS. The 16.10 reconciliation service already exposes `missing(companyId, period)` for this close. No module refused back-dated changes in a closed project month, and no `mc_monthly_closes` table existed. The decision for each requirement is in the [discovery note](../DISCOVERY/27.07.md).

## 2. What was built

**A close** covers one project and one month (`mc_monthly_closes`, document `MC/…`).

**Checklist (FR-01)**: eight mandatory steps, read live from their owners. Each has an owner role, dependencies, a detail and a drill link:

| Step | Owner role | Source (part) | Depends on |
|---|---|---|---|
| DPRs complete | PM | 14.02 | — |
| Attendance locked | HRMGR | 07.06 | — |
| Material reconciliation approved | STOREMGR | 16.10 `missing` | — |
| Measurements and bills updated | QS | 22.01 | — |
| CVR approved | QS | 22.09 | Measurements |
| Costs posted | ACCMGR | 23.02 | — |
| Forecast approved | PCMGR | 23.11 | CVR, costs posted |
| Finance period closed | CFO | 23.14 | Costs posted |

**Sending (BR-01)**: a close is sent only when every mandatory step passes. Otherwise it is refused with 422, listing each open step. Sending freezes two things in the record:

- the **Project MIS** (FR-02): progress and SPI/CPI, cost vs budget, CVR, billing and collections, cash, resources, quality and HSE, risks, and the look-ahead;
- the **Budget Master** (FR-03): budget, committed, actual, EAC and variance by cost head, from the approved 23.11 forecast.

**Approval (§9)** goes through the workflow engine as document type `BI_MONTHLY_CLOSE`: QS (when someone else prepared it) → PM → PD (management approval). Approvers must be distinct, the preparer never approves, and return, reject and resubmit are supported, all from the shared approval inbox, including on mobile.

**Approval re-checks every step**, then locks the month (FR-04):

- It publishes `bi.monthly_close.locked`.
- The new shared guard `platform/locks` (`assertProjectPeriodOpen`) refuses a DPR create, update, submit or amend dated in the month, with 422 `PERIOD_LOCKED`.
- An approved close changes only by amendment: a new version is prepared and approved again, and the month stays locked meanwhile.

**Page** `/bi/monthly-close` (**Reports → Monthly Close Status**):

- **Board**: projects in four columns — Not started, Preparing, In approval, Locked. Each card shows the step dots, the blocking steps and its actions.
  - On a desktop, dragging a card to the next column starts or sends the close. The server checks every move, and **Undo** deletes the draft or withdraws the close.
  - On a phone, one column is shown at a time (pill switcher) and cards move through their buttons.
- **Close** (`?id=`): pill tabs Checklist, Project MIS, Budget Master and Approval history. Actions are send, open the approval task, print the PDF, export XLSX, withdraw, cancel and amend.
- Labels are en/hi/te (i18n area `mc`).

**APIs** ([OpenAPI](../bi-openapi.yaml)) under `/bi/monthly-closes`: board, list, export, create, get, update, delete, submit, withdraw, cancel, amend, history, print, export.

**Permissions**: `bi.monthly_close.{view,create,edit,delete,submit,approve,cancel,amend,print,export}`, scoped by project:

| Role | Grant |
|---|---|
| PCMGR | Prepares (no approve) |
| QS, PM | Prepare and approve |
| PD | Approves and amends |
| DIR | Reads, prints and exports |

## 4. Tests

`tests/bi-monthly-close.test.js` has three tests, all passing.

**Test 1, T-01** walks one close through its whole life:

1. Everything is fulfilled except the material reconciliation, and sending is refused with 422 (details field `MATERIAL_RECON`). The board shows 7 of 8 steps and names the blocking one. The dependency logic is checked too: the forecast waits for the CVR.
2. The reconciliation is added; a stale version gets 409.
3. The close is sent. The Budget Master and MIS figures are frozen (a later forecast line does not change them).
4. QS approves, PM returns it, the preparer resubmits, then QS → PM → PD approve. The close is APPROVED and LOCKED, and the outbox event is published.
5. A back-dated DPR update and submit are refused with `PERIOD_LOCKED`; a DPR in the current month is not.
6. Cancelling an approved close is refused (422). An amendment is v2, and the month stays locked.
7. The PDF and CSV are produced, SE is refused, and the history and audit are written.

**Test 2, scope**: project isolation for a PM assigned to one project.

**Test 3, volume**: 100,000 closes. The list, the period filter and the board stay under 500 ms over five reads, and `EXPLAIN` shows the lock check uses an index.

`e2e/bi-monthly-close.spec.js` passes on desktop and mobile. It starts the close on the board, sees the blocked step, sends it, approves QS → PM → PD through the workflow API, then checks the locked close, its MIS and the Budget Master export.

- The visual baseline has new images for `/bi/monthly-close` only.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set `ff.bi.p2701` to `ff.bi.p2703` and `ff.bi.p2707` and record the G1 and G2 gates. Use last month.
2. As a PCMGR, open **Reports → Monthly Close Status**. Choose the month and press **Start the close** on a project card (or drag it to Preparing).
3. Open the close. Each checklist step links to its source record; fix any open step at its source. If the material reconciliation is missing, **Send for approval** stays disabled, and the API refuses it with the step named.
4. When every step is Done, send it. The MIS and Budget Master show "Figures frozen".
5. Approve it in turn as the QS, the PM and the PD, from the approval inbox (or **Open your approval task**). The close shows "Month locked".
6. Try to edit or send a DPR dated in that month: it is refused with "closed and locked … Record the correction in the next open month".
7. Export the Budget Master and print the MIS. As the PD, **Amend** with a reason: version 2 opens as a draft, and the month stays locked.

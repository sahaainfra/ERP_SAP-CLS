# Part 11.03 completion: Submittals: material approvals, method statements and shop drawings

Part: 11.03. Date: 2026-11-10. Feature flag `ff.engg.p1103` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 11.01; used by 18.01 and, through the approved makes, by procurement (15.03, 15.05) and receiving inspection (18.03).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items are listed in section 6.

## 1. Discovery summary
`docs/erp/DISCOVERY/11.03.md`.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 167, 168; nothing existing altered): `engg_submittals`, `engg_submittal_revisions`, `engg_approved_makes`; seed: the flag, two navigation entries, three page-tracker rows, notification template `engg.submittal.responded` (in-app and e-mail, English and Hindi); parameter `engg.po_mar_control`.
- **Server** (`modules/engg/submittals.js`): the submittal service (create, update, send, withdraw, cancel, respond, resubmit, amend, history, print, import, export, schedule, approved makes, purchase-order check), workflow `ENGG_SUBMITTAL`, resolver `engg.submittalReviewer`, evidence, file entity `engg_submittal`, events `engg.submittal.submitted`, `engg.submittal.responded`, `engg.submittal.approved`; `procurementCheck` for 15.x and 18.x.
- **API**: 18 routes under `/api/v1/engg`, in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`.
- **Permissions**: `engg.submittal.*` (11 keys) granted to quality control engineer, quality control manager, construction manager, design coordinator, site engineer, project manager, project director, planner, quantity surveyor, document controller, project controls manager and director.
- **Browser**: Submittal Register with the new-submittal form, Submittal (details, revisions and responses, documents, response and resubmit forms), Submittal Schedule vs Required Dates; English and Hindi.
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 90, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, ADR-428 to 431, D-443 to 446, openapi.

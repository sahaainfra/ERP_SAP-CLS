## 2. What was built

**The report** is built for one person and one date, as that person: their roles and project or site scopes decide every figure. It covers yesterday as at the cut-off (06:00 IST by default) and contains these sections:

- control tower (projects red, open alerts)
- progress (DPRs received and missing per site)
- attendance (present, absent, man-hours)
- stock and shortages (stock-outs, items at or below the reorder level)
- receivables (outstanding, overdue, due today)
- payables (outstanding, due today, MSME due)
- approvals pending
- critical tasks
- risks
- incidents

The report then adds:

- **Exceptions** (FR-04), worst first: DPRs missing, overdue approvals, stock-outs, collections due today, lost-time incidents.
- **Executive summary** (FR-02), written by rules and labelled "Rule-based summary".

The whole report is frozen as a canonical JSON snapshot with its SHA-256 in `mr_reports`. Reopening, **Check reproducibility** and every export read that snapshot (BR-01).

**Delivery (FR-03)** is a job that runs every 15 minutes:

- Each active configuration delivers at its time, once per recipient and date.
- It sends through the notification engine: always in-app, plus e-mail or WhatsApp as configured and as the recipient's preferences allow. The notification links to the interactive report.
- It publishes `bi.morning_report.delivered`.
- PDF, XLSX and CSV are drawn from the snapshot.
- Each person keeps an archive of their reports.

**Configuration (FR-01)** is per role or per user: sections, cut-off, delivery time, channels and on/off. A change is proposed with a reason, and a DIR or the CFO approves it, never the requester. The old and new values go to the audit trail.

**Pages**:

- `/bi/morning-report` (**Reports → Morning Report**) has pill tabs Report, Archive and Settings.
- `/bi/morning-reports` (**Reports → Report Archive**).

Both have en/hi/te labels (i18n area `mr`). On a phone, every table becomes cards.

**APIs**: `/bi/morning-reports/current`, `/bi/morning-reports`, `/{id}`, `/{id}/verify`, `/{id}/export`, `/deliveries`, `/bi/morning-report-settings` and its `requests` routes ([OpenAPI](../bi-openapi.yaml)).

**Permissions**:

| Permission | Roles |
|---|---|
| `bi.morning_report.view` | DIR, PD, PM, CFO, PROCMGR, COMMGR |
| `bi.morning_report.configure` | DIR, CFO |

A report is personal: anyone other than its recipient gets 404.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set the flags `ff.bi.p2701` to `ff.bi.p2705` and record the G1 and G2 gates.
2. Have an approved DPR for one site and none for another, attendance for yesterday, a client invoice due today, and an incident yesterday.
3. As a director, open **Reports → Morning Report**. Check that:
   - the cut-off line reads "Data as at 06:00 IST";
   - the summary reports the missing DPR;
   - the exceptions list links to the site and to the invoice;
   - every card drills to its register.
4. Select **Check reproducibility**: it reports the figures identical. Submit the missing DPR and reopen the report: the figures are unchanged. Export the PDF.
5. Open **Report Archive**: the report is listed, and opening it shows the same figures.
6. Open **Settings → Propose a configuration**. Choose role DIR, sections, cut-off 06:00, delivery 06:30 and e-mail, and give a reason. The same director cannot approve it; a second director or the CFO approves.
7. Select **Deliver due reports now** (or wait for the 15-minute job). After 06:30 each director gets an in-app notification linking to today's report, and nothing more is sent that day.
8. Sign in as a PM assigned to one project: the report shows only that project.

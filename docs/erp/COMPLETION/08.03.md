## 7. How to verify manually
1. As the estimation manager open **Credentials Library**, **Add credential**: a completion certificate with client, sector, value and date; attach its file.
2. Open a tender ready for submission and press **Request EMD**: the amount is the tender's; choose a bank guarantee and send it. A colleague of estimation, the accounts manager and the CFO approve; the accounts manager opens it and records the guarantee (number, bank, validity). Do the same for the fee (online).
3. **Bid Submission**: prepare it, tick the checklist, type the approved estimate and its reference, the final bid (a different bid asks for a reason), link the credential; send it: a colleague and the director approve; record the portal acknowledgement.
4. **Bid Opening Results**: enter every bidder and price: the ranks and the distance from L1 show at once and the server keeps them.
5. **Award / Loss**: add a negotiation round, record the win with the letter of acceptance and value, send it; the director accepts. The tender is awarded and the EMD refund falls due.
6. As the accounts manager open **EMD & Fee Register**: the refund is in the 0-30 days bucket; open it and record the refund. **Competitor Analysis** shows the rivals by sector, region and month. Switch to Hindi.

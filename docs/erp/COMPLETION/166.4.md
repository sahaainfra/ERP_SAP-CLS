## 7. Known limitations
- **D-683** Lighthouse: Performance 73–81 (≥ 80 on Field Home only) and LCP 4.0–4.7 s on Slow 4G; the first paint alone needs the 325 KB first download. Needs a drawn shell in `index.html`, the English text split (D-682) and the menu and the page's data fetched with the session.
- **D-682** English text (about 127 KB gzip) is still in the first download.
- **D-684** Blank page on about one full load in 300–600 with the worker in control (browser-side cause not identified); mitigated by `public/boot-guard.js`, not seen since in two sweeps of every page.
- **D-685** e2e failures found in the full run and not caused by this part (`secure-messaging` on phones fails on `main` too; four specs fail only in a whole run).
- Everything that changes behaviour is behind `ff.perf.p16604`; with it OFF the pages are still lazy (the split itself is not flagged) but the worker strategies, prefetch, low-bandwidth extras, draft-saving update, flag batching and layout-shift holds are off.

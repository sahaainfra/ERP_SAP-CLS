## 7. How to verify manually
1. Sign in as a site engineer, open **Field Forms**, choose **Sample document**. Switch the device to airplane mode (or DevTools, Network, Offline): the status says Offline.
2. Enter a one-letter title: the form says what is wrong with no connection. Enter a title, an amount and a project, add a photo, **Save on this phone**. **Sync Status** shows one item waiting.
3. Go back online: the item is sent, the photo follows; the sample document list shows the record with its photo.
4. As the IT administrator open **Administration > Devices**, revoke the engineer's device with a reason; reopen the application on the phone: it says the device was revoked and its offline data is gone.
5. Change the same field of a record on the server and from a device (see `tests/offline.test.js` T-02): **Conflict Resolution** shows base, mine and server and lets you choose.

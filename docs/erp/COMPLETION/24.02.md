## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 370, 371): `pay_runs` (approval-controlled), `pay_run_lines`, `pay_inputs`, `pay_tax_declarations`; flag, three pages, page-tracker rows, templates.
- **Run** (FR-01, BR-01; ADR-721, ADR-722): the employees in service of the company or branch; only locked attendance; payable days and LOP; overtime; arrears; leave encashment and loss recoveries from their events; advances due; payroll's own inputs; employees left out are listed with the reason.
- **Gross to net and TDS** (FR-02; ADR-723): the 24.01 engine prorated; TDS projected over the year under the declared regime.
- **Checks and holds** (FR-03, FR-05): negative net blocks the submission; no verified bank account and a change beyond 20 % are flagged; a hold with its reason survives recomputation and is out of the net payable.
- **Approval and lock** (FR-04; ADR-724): PAY → HRMGR → CFO (the preparer never approves); the lock applies the inputs, pays or cancels the arrears, records the advance recoveries, locks the calendar period; corrections after the lock in a supplementary run.
- **Cost allocation** (FR-06): by site days of the locked attendance, else the month's deployments.
- **Tax declarations**: the employee's regime and amounts on the phone (ESS), verified or returned by payroll; the TDS uses the verified amounts.
- **API**: 22 operations; **Browser**: Payroll Run (wizard: inputs check, compute, review with holds, approve), Payroll Register with the variance, Tax Declarations; English and Hindi.
- **Defects** D-344 and D-347 closed; D-745 raised (the run is computed in the request).

## 6. Known limitations
- D-745: the computation runs in the request (≈ 1 ms an employee); a background job with progress comes with 24.05.
- A revision effective in the middle of a month is paid from the month it is in force at the month's end; the days before it are settled by its arrears in the next month.
- Proofs are recorded as amounts with optional file ids; the document upload screen of the declaration is the document service's own.
- Payslips are part 24.06 (ESS); the payment file and posting are 24.05.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. Lock October's attendance (07.06). Payroll → Payroll Run: the month → the inputs check → **Compute the payroll**.
2. Review: open an employee for the components and TDS; **Hold salary** with a reason; **Send for approval**; as HR manager and CFO approve.
3. Payroll → Payroll Register & Variance: the register and the variance against last month; export.
4. As an employee: Payroll → Tax Declarations → regime and amounts → **Send to payroll**; as payroll verify.

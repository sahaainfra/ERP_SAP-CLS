## 2. What was built

The page is `/admin/page-audit` (**Administration → Page Completion Audit**), a technical-console report:

- **Go-live verdict** (BR-01) in the header: **Go-live blocked** while any business page of the production navigation fails, has an open defect or was never checked; otherwise **Go-live clear**.
- **Chart**: pages by module (pass, fail, not tested), drilling to the module.
- **Key figures**: business pages blocking go-live, pass, fail, not tested, open defects (and how many were rescheduled), pages audited by hand.
- **Parameters**: module, status, text, navigation only. Inline on a desktop, a bottom sheet on a phone.
- **Pages** (FR-01): each page with its route, module, owner, crawl result by viewport, static scan, manual audit with its role, open defects and status, as cards on a phone with a sticky total. A page opens its panel:
  - **Record a manual audit** (FR-03): the role tested, the 13 points of the Page Completion Checklist (pass, fail with what fails, not applicable), the defects (title, severity), notes and an evidence screenshot. The screenshot is uploaded through the file service, attached to the audit, and only by the tester. A failed point needs a defect.
  - **The audits** of the page: crawl, static and manual, with their defects. Each open defect can be marked **fixed** with the commit and test that fixed it, or **rescheduled** (FR-04) to a later part with a reason. A reschedule is approved by the page's owner through the approval engine, and the requester never decides it. A refusal reopens the defect.
  - **The static scan of its source now**.
- **Static scan** (FR-02): **Run the static scan** reads every page's component source and records a STATIC audit per page. Its rules:
  - buttons of type button without a handler;
  - links to `#`;
  - placeholder text shown to users;
  - business figures typed into the component;
  - data pages without their loading or error state;
  - console output.
- **Route crawl** (FR-02, T-01): `e2e/tst-page-crawl.spec.js` opens every registered route on a 360 px phone, in a company with every flag on, as a user holding every internal role. For each route it records:
  - console errors;
  - API calls that fail on the server;
  - sideways overflow;
  - a blank page.

  It posts the results in batches into one crawl run (`POST /tst/page-audits/crawl`). The last batch reports the coverage of the registered routes, and a route that is not registered is refused.
- **Export**: XLSX, CSV or PDF, audited.

**Placeholder elimination (FR-04)**. The first scan found 14 findings on 10 page files. After narrowing the rules to genuine cases (a brace-aware tag reader; pages whose shared components draw their states), the remaining findings were fixed:

| Fix | Pages |
|---|---|
| Option lists loaded with no loading or error state now show a skeleton while loading and an error with retry | New estimate, Raise RFI, Draft letter, Generate budget, Kiosk |
| The analysis shows a skeleton while it is worked out | Rate change impact |
| The three sample buttons are marked `data-sample` | Design-system style guide |

The scan of all 607 page files is now clean (asserted by FR-02 in `tests/tst-page-audit.test.js`).

**Tooling**: the UI v3 page audit script (`scripts/ui_v3_audit.mjs`, 166.2) now reads pages loaded on demand (`lazyPage`) and treats a page with a chart, parameters and a table as an analytical report. `docs/erp/UI_V3_PAGE_AUDIT.md` was regenerated: 732 pages, 440 migrated, 219 partly, 73 exceptions. The committed copy was out of date, at 633 pages.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. Set `ff.qa.p3003` for a test company.
2. As ITADM, open **Administration → Page Completion Audit**. The header says Go-live blocked: nothing is checked yet.
3. Click **Run the static scan**: "0 with findings".
4. Run `PW_EXE=… npx playwright test -c playwright.local.config.js e2e/tst-page-crawl.spec.js --project=mobile`. The crawl appears under **Crawls and scans** with full coverage.
5. Filter Module tst and open **Page Completion Audit**. **Record a manual audit** as ITADM with all points passed and a screenshot; the audit shows PASS with one evidence file.
6. Record an audit of a site page with a failed point and two defects. Mark one fixed with its commit and test. Reschedule the other to 30.07; the page owner (CM) approves it from the panel.

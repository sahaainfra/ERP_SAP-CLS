# Part 21.05 completion: Change & Variation Management

Part: 21.05. Date: 2026-11-21. Module `ctr`, flag `ff.ctr.p2105` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 21.01 (contract value adapter `applyVariation`), 21.03 (work order amendments), 10.04 (BOQ revision hook `variationProviders`), 12.02 (budget revision from `ctr.variation.approved`).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items D-651 in `DEFECTS.md`.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 304, 305): `ctr_change_requests` and `ctr_variation_orders` (approval-controlled), `ctr_vo_items`; flag `ff.ctr.p2105`, two pages, tracker, templates.
- **Change request**: technical review by the engineer, QS evaluation with the value worked out on the server, internal approval QS, commercial manager, project director above Rs 10 lakh, director above Rs 50 lakh; the pending variation is registered on the contract.
- **Client**: proposal PDF, marked as sent, the answer recorded: approved (makes the variation order), rejected (withdraws the pending value), disputed (kept, for claims 21.06).
- **Variation order**: approved by the commercial manager (and above by value); on approval the contract value moves, the revised BOQ draft is made with the changed quantities and extra items, the budget revision draft is drafted from the event and the back-to-back work order amendments are drafted.
- **API**: routes under `/api/v1/ctr` (change-requests, variation-orders, variation-summary, variation-gate); **browser**: Variation Register, change request, variation order, Variation Summary; English and Hindi.

# Part 14.07 completion: Project Control Engine

Part: 14.07. Date: 2026-11-28. Module `site`, flag `ff.site.p1407` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 14.05 (progress, read through its service) and 12.03 (ledgers, read in place). Provides `site.control.updated` and `site.control.reconciliation_failed` and the facts at `/api/v1/site/control/facts` to Project 360, CVR (22.09), EAC (23.11), P&L (23.12) and the control tower (27.04).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items in section 6.

## 1. Discovery

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 204, 205; schema-diff of existing objects clean): `ctl_facts`, `ctl_unallocated`, `ctl_recon_runs`; seed of the flag, three navigation entries, page-tracker rows, four notification templates (English, Hindi).
- **Fact store** (FR-01, BR-01; ADR-508): rebuilt from the approved budget, the commitments, the actuals and the progress by project, WBS node, cost code, resource type and period; no route writes a fact.
- **Updates and reconciliation** (FR-02, BR-02; ADR-509): events rebuild the facts of their project; the nightly job reconciles by a separate query, records a run, alerts above the tolerance and rebuilds.
- **No double counting** (FR-03; ADR-510): material cost at issue for stock items and at receipt for direct delivery, or every receipt, by company setting; uncounted rows are shown with the reason.
- **Views** (FR-04, FR-05; ADR-512): facts by WBS, cost code, cost head, resource, month; dashboard with exceptions, trend, reconciliation state; quantity and rate variance that add up to the cost variance; planned against earned; committed against budget; XLSX, CSV, PDF; drill-down to budget lines, commitments, postings and activities.
- **Unallocated costs** (FR-06, section 9; ADR-511): queue; QS or project controls propose, the PM approves through `SITE_CTL_ALLOC`; the ledger row is never changed.
- **API**: 15 routes under `/api/v1/site/control` in the route policy and `openapi.yaml` (the part's `/ctl/*`, ADR-513). **Browser**: Project Cost Control, Quantity & Rate Variance Analysis, Unallocated Costs; English and Hindi.

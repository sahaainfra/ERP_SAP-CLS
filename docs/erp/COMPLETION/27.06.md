# Part 27.06 — Daily Execution Intelligence & Work-Done / WIP Valuation

Execution step 200 · 2026-11-21 · Branch `ccr-6934092b-qrkoec` · **In progress**: built and tested behind `ff.bi.p2706`. Formal acceptance waits for 14.07 and 22.04 and for the company G1/G2 sign-offs.

## 1. Discovery

The 22.02 quantity reconciliation already apportions approved DPR work to BOQ items, but only as a cumulative total. The 22.08 QS dashboard values that cumulative chain. Neither gives daily or per-site figures, and no `dei_daily` table existed. The part extends the 22.02 service with a dated, per-site reader instead of copying the apportioning. The decision for each requirement is in the [discovery note](../DISCOVERY/27.06.md).

## 2. What was built

**Valuation per site and day**, from approved DPRs only (BR-01):

| Figure | How it is computed |
|---|---|
| Work done (FR-01) | DPR quantity, apportioned to BOQ items exactly as 22.02 does, × contract rate. Amounts use `amountOf` of 22.04. |
| Planned value | DPR planned quantity × rate. |
| Budget cost | Quantity × budget cost rate of the approved project budget. Hidden without `bi.execution.cost`. |
| Labour | Headcount from the DPRs. |
| Plant | Hours worked, idle and broken down. |
| Hindrances | Hindrances open on the day. |

**The project row of the day** adds:

- cumulative work done;
- WIP (executed, not certified) and executed-not-billed, from the 22.02 chain (FR-02);
- balance work (revised − executed) × rate;
- RFIs raised, answered and open (FR-04).

**Snapshots (FR-03)** are stored in `dei_daily` and recomputed in three ways:

- when a DPR of the day is approved (`site.dpr.approved` consumer, idempotent upsert);
- nightly at 23:45 IST;
- on demand with **Recompute the day** (DIR, PCMGR).

Each capture publishes `bi.execution.snapshot_captured`. `executionOf(db).series()` serves the project series to MIS (27.07) and forecasting.

**Page** `/bi/execution` (**Reports → Daily Execution Intelligence**) has two pill tabs:

- **Overview**:
  - KPI tiles: hero "Work done today" with % of plan and the trend against yesterday, then planned, WIP, executed-not-billed, cumulative, balance, budget cost, margin, labour, plant hours, hindrances and RFIs;
  - a work done vs planned trend chart that drills to the day;
  - exceptions: sites below 80 % of plan, hindrances;
  - a site comparison table that drills to the valuation.
- **Work-done valuation**: BOQ items with quantity × rate, budget cost and margin where allowed, each DPR linked, and XLSX/CSV/PDF export (audited).

Filters are project, site and date. Labels are en/hi/te (i18n area `ex`). Widgets: `bi.work_done_today` on the dashboard and in Project 360 (time tab).

**APIs** ([OpenAPI](../bi-openapi.yaml)): `GET /bi/execution`, `GET /bi/execution/valuation`, `GET /bi/execution/valuation/export`, `POST /bi/execution/captures`.

**Permissions**:

| Permission | Roles |
|---|---|
| `bi.execution.view`, `bi.execution.export` | PM, PD, DIR, QS, PCMGR |
| `bi.execution.cost` | DIR, PD, PCMGR |
| `bi.execution.run` | DIR, PCMGR |

Site rows are scoped by site assignment and project rows by project.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set `ff.bi.p2701` to `ff.bi.p2703` and `ff.bi.p2706` and record the G1 and G2 gates.
2. Have a contract BOQ with rates, activities linked to BOQ lines, and today's DPRs approved for two sites.
3. As a director, open **Reports → Daily Execution Intelligence**. Check that:
   - "Work done today" equals the DPR quantities × BOQ rates;
   - the site comparison shows each site's planned and executed value, labour and plant hours.
4. Select a site. The valuation lists each BOQ item with quantity × rate and opens the DPR. Export it.
5. Approve another DPR of the day. The figures update (the approval event recomputes the day), or use **Recompute the day**.
6. Sign in as a QS: budget cost and margin show "Hidden for your role", and the export has no cost columns.
7. Sign in as a PM assigned to one site: only that site is shown.

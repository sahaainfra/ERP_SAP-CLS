## 1. Discovery summary
Permissions were computed by the role engine and sensitive changes went through the interim approval engine, which only refused the requester as approver. There was no rule for conflicting duties, no way to see who holds them, no time-boxed elevated access (Super Admin was a normal assignment, D-090), no access review and no check for unused accounts. Decision per requirement: `docs/erp/DISCOVERY/02.05.md`. Departures from the pack: exceptions to a rule through the workflow engine are not built (D-093); delegation and reminders wait for 04.01 and 04.06 (D-094); only the approval engine's own actions are guarded today because the documents the other seven action rules protect do not exist yet (D-092).

## 2. What was built
- **Rule engine** (`platform/sod`): a rule is two lists of patterns and a scope (the person, the document, the vendor, the period or the project) with severity block, warn or detect and an optional amount condition. The ten standard rules SOD-01 to SOD-10 are seeded per company and then owned by it; a change is an approved request (`AUD|DIR` decides, never the administrator who asked).
- **Preventive**: giving a role (`iam_assignment`, `user_role_assignment`), asking for an elevation and building a role are checked with "what the person would hold"; a blocking conflict is refused with a message naming the rule, the permissions and the roles on each side, a warning returns as `sodWarnings`, and the check is repeated at approval. The approval engine records who raised each request and refuses an approval by the same person under SOD-01, keeping the blocked attempt on record.
- **Detective**: `iam_sod_violations` (standing conflicts, one open row per rule and person; warned, detected and blocked transactions), a daily job and Scan now; remedies are taking a role away (system administrator) or accepting the risk with a reason (auditor, director, never on one's own access).
- **Elevated access**: request with reason and hours (limit `iam.elevation.max_hours`), approval by the requester's manager, the IT administrator and, for a financial role, the chief financial officer; the permission engine includes an active elevation and never caches past its start or end, so access ends at the end time; a log row for every request made while elevated, with the correlation ID the audit trail now carries; revoke at once.
- **Access reviews**: campaigns for privileged roles, one department or everything; one review per reviewer (department head; the director for a person's own access); certify or revoke one by one or in bulk; reassign; the campaign cannot close while any line is undecided; closing applies the revokes, never removes the last active system administrator, and re-checks the people affected.
- **Dormant accounts**: listed after `iam.dormant.days` (90) days; suspended only where `iam.dormant.action` says so (default report); the last active administrator is never suspended.
- **Pages** (flag `ff.iam.p0205`): SoD Rules (list, rule, propose, switch off, test against a person), SoD Violations (report, remediate, CSV, dormant accounts tab), Request Elevated Access, Elevation Approvals (waiting for me, in force, all; approve, reject, revoke; the log), Access Reviews (campaigns, reviewers, lines, reassign, close, cancel) and My Access Review (worklist with bulk certify or revoke).
- **Roles**: SYSADM proposes rules; AUD and DIR check rule changes, resolve violations and oversee reviews; ITADM approves and revokes elevations; department heads and other internal roles review and may ask for elevation; ordinary employees can do neither.
- **Events**: `iam.sod.violation_detected|rule_changed`, `iam.elevation.requested|started|ended|revoked`, `iam.review.started|closed`, `iam.account.dormant_suspended`. **Jobs**: `iam.elevation_expiry` (every five minutes), `iam.sod_scan` (daily). **Parameters**: `iam.dormant.days`, `iam.dormant.action`, `iam.elevation.max_hours`.
- Also changed: the approval engine now joins an outer transaction for `submit` and `withdraw`; a step written `@username` is decided only by that person; `writeAudit` records the correlation ID by default; 02.03 deactivation does not count `@username` steps as role-held.
- Docs: `ARCHITECTURE.md` section 22, `DECISIONS.md` ADR-88 to ADR-94, `DEFECTS.md` D-092 to D-099 (D-090 narrowed), `DB_CHANGES.md`, `DB_BASELINE.md`, `openapi.yaml`; `schema/baseline.json` re-locked (275 objects; 246 unchanged, 29 new).

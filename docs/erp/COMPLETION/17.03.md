## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 250, 251; up, down and up again clean): `plt_logs`, `plt_breakdowns`; flag, four pages under the Plant group, page tracker, notification templates. No existing table changed.
- **Daily log** (FR-01): machine on site (the allocation of 17.02 found by date), shift, operator on duty (assigned, with a valid certificate), start and end meter (start prefilled from the previous reading or the arrival), working, idle (with reason) and breakdown hours, work authorisation of the site and day, activity, work done, fuel, remarks, photograph of the meter. The server works out everything and refuses an expired mandatory certificate (17.01).
- **Checks** (BR-01, FR-06): end meter not below start, hours not above the shift, one log per machine, day and shift. Flagged and explained before sending: start below the previous reading, a gap, hours not matching the meter run, work without a WA or with a WA that does not name the machine.
- **Verification** (FR-02): another plant supervisor, then the site engineer; hired equipment also the vendor representative's signed log and the project manager. Worklist with bulk approval of unflagged logs; return and reject with a reason.
- **Feeds**: on verification the hours are booked as the plant use of the WA (`recordUse`, read by the DPR), the cost (internal charge x hours) is fixed, and `plant.log.approved` is published.
- **Breakdowns** (FR-03): report marks the machine, asks maintenance for a job card (event), is logged as breakdown hours; resolve puts the machine back at work.
- **Utilisation** (FR-04, FR-05): by equipment, class or site for a period; utilisation = working / (calendar - breakdown), availability = (calendar - breakdown) / calendar; groups add hours; drill to logs; chart; CSV; costs masked.
- **API**: 23 routes (3,094 in all). **Browser**: Daily Equipment Log (mobile, keeps what is typed on the phone), Equipment Log Books (with Breakdowns), Log Verification, Utilisation and Availability; English and Hindi.

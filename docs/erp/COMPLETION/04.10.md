## Click-path for a reviewer
1. As an engineer open **New Document**, give a title and the type Drawing, **Register and go on to the file**, attach a PDF (scanned), **Send for approval**.
2. As the discipline lead and then the project manager approve in **My Approvals**. The **Document Register** shows it Issued; the **Viewer** tab shows the page; draw a markup.
3. **New revision**, attach a second file, approve it: revision A is Superseded, and downloading it carries a watermark.
4. As the document controller **Distribute** to a person and an outside party; the person sees it in My Work and acknowledges; the table shows who has not.
5. Mark a document Confidential: a person without the right does not find it in search or the register.
6. **Document Types**: change a name with a reason; the change waits for approval.

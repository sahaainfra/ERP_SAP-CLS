## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 76 and 77, nothing existing altered): `tpl_templates`, `tpl_records`, `tpl_generated_docs` (with triggers that refuse change and delete), `tpl_deliveries`, `tpl_bulk_jobs`, `tpl_auto_rules`; seed: the flag, three navigation entries, three tracker rows, the notification `tpl.bulk.done` (English and Hindi).
- **Template language and catalogue** (`tpl-engine.js`, `tpl-catalogue.js`): `{{variable | filter}}`, `{{#each}}`, `{{#if}}{{else}}`, `{{qr}}`, `{{logo}}`, `{{page}}`, `{{pages}}`; the controlled variable catalogue of 19 document types; every name checked when a template is saved; scripts, links, outside pictures and variables inside tags refused.
- **Corporate design standard and format library** (`tpl-library.js`): letterhead, IBM Plex Sans (with Devanagari and rupee fallbacks), colours, table style, totals box, signature block, amount in words in English and Hindi (`tpl-words.js`), QR block, footer with page numbers. Standard formats for PO, WO, RA bill, tax invoice, credit note, debit note, payment voucher, GRN, MR, material issue slip, gate pass, DPR, MB abstract, inspection report, NCR, permit, offer letter, payslip and certificate (shared, active rows written when the module starts: ADR-200).
- **PDF renderer** (`tpl-pdf.js`, ADR-199): HTML and CSS subset to A4 PDF on pdf-lib, no browser; header and footer on every page, repeated table header rows, column spans, boxes, images; archival profile option (D-236). 55 ms for a one-page document.
- **Service** (`templates.js`): templates (create, update, check, preview, submit, discard, approval hooks), records (publish with recomputed totals), generation (immutable, versions with a reason, QR code, hash, stored through the file service), bulk generation as a job, milestone rules (FR-08), delivery (e-mail, WhatsApp through the gateway with signed links; message and portal as events), delivery log, verification, file entity `tpl_generated`, QR resolver `DOCVERIFY`.
- **API** `/api/v1/tpl/*` (25 routes, two of them public, all in the route policy; `docs/erp/openapi.yaml`); the spec's three endpoints are `POST /tpl/generate`, `GET /tpl/generated/:id`, `POST /tpl/generated/:id/deliver`. Two public routes: the signed download link and `GET /tpl/verify/:code`.
- **Permissions**: `tpl.template.view|edit`, `tpl.rule.edit`, `tpl.record.publish`, `tpl.document.view|generate|generate_sensitive|deliver`; SYSADM keeps templates (maker), the process owners see them, whoever may print generates and sends, payslips and offer letters need the extra right of the HR manager and the director.
- **Workflow**: document type `TPL_TEMPLATE`, steps by the process owner of the type (PROCMGR purchase order, CFO invoices, notes and vouchers, HRMGR payslip and offer letter, STOREMGR stores documents, QCMGR, HSEO, COMMGR, PM); decision evidence panel with the changed lines and the catalogue check.
- **Events**: `tpl.template.activated`, `tpl.record.published`, `tpl.document.generated`, `tpl.document.delivered`, `tpl.document.delivery_requested`, `tpl.bulk.completed`; consumers `tpl.milestones`, `tpl.delivery_status`; jobs `tpl.bulk`, `tpl.milestone`.
- **Pages**: Template Library `/admin/templates`, Template Editor `/admin/templates/editor` (variable picker, check as you type, live preview drawn by the server from the sample or a real record), Generated Documents Log `/documents/generated` (filters, count by document, drill-down, generate, bulk, deliver, new version, export). Documents: ARCHITECTURE section 45, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, schema lock, DECISIONS (ADR-199 to 203), DEFECTS (D-235 to D-242), openapi, PROGRESS.csv (row 39).

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As the system administrator open **Administration > Templates**: the 19 standard formats are listed, active, version 1. Filter by Tax invoice and open it.
2. **Make a new version**. In the editor, add `{{customerName}}` to the HTML: the check says the variable is not in the catalogue of Tax invoice and Save is off. Remove it, click `bankDetails` in the variable list to insert it, watch the **Preview** draw the invoice from the sample record (watermarked PREVIEW). **Save draft**, then **Send for approval**.
3. As the CFO (the process owner of invoices) open **My Approvals**, see the changed lines and the check, approve. The Library shows version 2 active. The system administrator cannot approve it.
4. As a module or integration (API `PUT /api/v1/tpl/records`, permission `tpl.record.publish`) hand over an approved tax invoice record; a wrong `grandTotal` is refused.
5. As the accounts manager open **Documents > Generated Documents**, choose Tax invoice and the record, **Generate**. Open the PDF: letterhead, QR code, lines, totals, amount in words in English and Hindi, signature block, page numbers. The code printed under the QR opens `GET /api/v1/tpl/verify/<code>` without signing in.
6. **Send it** by e-mail to an address (needs the e-mail channel of 04.07): the log shows the delivery and its status; the link in the message opens the PDF; try a new version without a reason (refused), then with a reason: the earlier version stays, its verification says it was replaced.
7. Choose several records and **Generate as a background job**: progress, then the documents and the notification.
8. As an employee open the same addresses: the no-permission state. As the HR manager generate a payslip; the accounts manager neither sees it nor finds it in the list.

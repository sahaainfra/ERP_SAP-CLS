# Part 15.03 completion: RFQ & Vendor Quotations

Part: 15.03. Date: 2026-12-01. Module `proc`, flag `ff.proc.p1503` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 15.01 and 06.04. Provides quotations to the comparative statement (15.04) and the event `proc.rate.observed` to rate intelligence (15.09).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items in section 6.

## 1. Discovery
`docs/erp/DISCOVERY/15.03.md`.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 210, 211; schema-diff of existing objects clean): `proc_rfqs`, `proc_rfq_lines`, `proc_rfq_vendors`, `proc_quotations`, `proc_quotation_lines`, `proc_rfq_clarifications`; seed of the flag, three navigation entries, page-tracker rows and the notification templates `proc.rfq.quote_received`, `.due_soon`, `.justify_approved`, `.justify_rejected`.
- **RFQ** (FR-01, FR-02, BR-01): made from approved requisition lines of one or many requisitions; vendors from the active vendors of the partner master matched by category, held and blacklisted ones refused; fewer than three vendors on a value from ₹ 1 lakh needs the single-source justification (procurement manager, then project director) or an approved exception of CP-PROC-03.
- **Issue and the vendor's page** (FR-03): each vendor gets a hashed, time-limited, single-use link by e-mail or WhatsApp (through the gateway) or by hand; the vendor opens it with no sign-in, quotes, declines or asks a question; `reissue` and reminders replace the link.
- **Quotations** (FR-04 to FR-06, BR-02): entry grid and import by procurement, or by the vendor; landed rate with GST left out when recoverable (parameter); versions that supersede; withdraw, cancel; clarification log with answers shared with all; regret letters; amend of the due date; reminders by a daily job and on request; the comparison with the lowest marked; rates only with `proc.quotation.rates`.
- **API**: 33 routes under `/api/v1/proc` and 4 public ones for the vendor page, in the route policy and `openapi.yaml` (2,506 routes in all). **Browser**: RFQs (list and object), Create RFQ from PRs, Quotation Entry, and the vendor page; English and Hindi.

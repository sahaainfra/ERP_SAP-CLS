## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 184, 185; nothing existing altered): `plan_activity_resources` (labour by trade, plant and material by item or class, a total or a daily rate, the source MANUAL or BOQ_NORM), `plan_resource_capacity` (availability per project, resource and period; drafted by one person, put in force by another), `plan_gantt_edits` (one row per Gantt edit with the rows before and after, the finish before and after, undone by and when). Seed: the flag, three navigation entries, three page-tracker rows, the permission keys and role grants.
- **Gantt data** (FR-01, `modules/plan/gantt.js`): `GET /schedules/:id/gantt` gives the WBS, the activities with early and late dates, float, critical, progress and resource count, the links, the data date, the last edits and what the person may do, in one call.
- **Editing** (FR-02, BR-01, ADR-471, ADR-472): `POST /schedules/:id/gantt/check` tries an edit (MOVE, RESIZE, EDIT, LINK, UNLINK, ADD, DELETE) through the 13.01 service in a savepoint, recalculates, and gives back the changed dates, conflicts and warnings without keeping anything; `POST /schedules/:id/gantt/edits` does the same and keeps it; `POST /schedules/:id/gantt/undo` restores the row snapshots of the last edit, last-in first-out. Nothing is computed in the browser; an approved schedule refuses every edit.
- **Resources** (FR-03, ADR-473): the resources of an activity by hand or from the BOQ norms of its BOQ lines (shown first, kept only when the planner says so); a save is one Gantt edit and can be undone; a duration change carries the daily figure along.
- **Histograms** (FR-03, ADR-474): demand by day, week or month on early or late dates, by type and window (five years at most), against availability from approved deployments (07.03) and the capacity put in force here; over-allocation flagged with the days over; drill-down from a bar to the activities behind it; export as XLSX, PDF or CSV (formula injection neutralised, audited); the monthly loading at `GET /schedules/:id/loading`; event `plan.resources.updated`.
- **Printable programme** (FR-04, ADR-475, `modules/plan/programme.js`): one layout drawn by the server from its own dates for the PDF (A3 or A1, paged, header on every page) and for the image (SVG; PNG made from it in the browser); parameters for time scale, filter, a WBS branch, a window and the links; every print and export audited.
- **API**: 18 routes under `/api/v1/plan`, in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`.
- **Permissions**: new `plan.resource.{view,edit}` and `plan.capacity.{manage,approve}`; the schedule keys of 13.01 for edit, print and export. The planner edits and assigns resources; the project controls manager puts capacity in force; managers, directors and site roles read (FR-05), with print and export where 13.01 gave them.
- **Browser**: Gantt Planner (WBS-grouped bars, milestones, critical path, progress, links, data-date line, zoom, collapse, filters, a choice of columns, drag to move and resize, link by dragging, inline edit, add, delete, the conflict panel before saving, undo, the resources panel; read-only without the edit permission; at 360 px one column and a scrolling chart), Resource Histogram (parameters, chart and table with totals, drill-down, export), Printable Programme (parameters, the programme by WBS node with totals and links to the activities, the preview being the image that is printed); English and Hindi.
- **Performance**: `npm run erp:plan-gantt-perf` (50 projects, 20 schedules of 5,000 activities with 145,000 links, 100,000 resource rows): Gantt of 5,000 activities p95 48 ms (limit 500 ms); histogram by week 72 ms, by month on late dates 42 ms, by day for a quarter 54 ms; drill-down 36 ms; loading 62 ms; resources of an activity 3.5 ms, saved 8.3 ms; a Gantt edit checked 126 ms, saved 166 ms, undone 118 ms (limit 3 s); programme layout of 5,000 activities on A1 128 ms, the SVG of a 250-activity branch 50 ms, the PDF of 5,000 activities on A3 about 5.0 s (limit 30 s). The query plans are printed and use the indexes.
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 98, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, ADR-471 to ADR-475, D-474 and D-475, openapi.

# Part 17.05 completion: Preventive Maintenance, Job Cards & Spares

Part: 17.05. Date: 2026-11-13. Module `plant`, flag `ff.plant.p1705` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 17.03 and 16.04. Provides `plant.job.closed` (maintenance cost 17.07, downtime 17.03).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Full regression and e2e results below; open items in section 6.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 254, 255): `plt_pm_plans`, `plt_pm_status`, `plt_job_cards`, `plt_job_spares`; flag, three pages, tracker, templates. No existing table changed.
- **Plans and due** (FR-01): by class or machine, hours, km or days; due from the latest meter and the date; critical plans block allocation when overdue (BR-01, parameter); nightly job tells what is due.
- **Job cards** (FR-03): preventive (from due services) and breakdown (linked to the 17.03 breakdown); complaint, diagnosis, mechanic, labour, external vendor and order, downtime; approved by another plant supervisor, and the plant manager above Rs 50,000 external; start puts the machine under maintenance; closing moves the plans on, resolves the breakdown and puts the machine back at work.
- **Spares** (FR-04): requested on the card, reserved and issued by the store keeper through 16.01 (stock falls at the average rate), unused returned; cost per job.
- **Metrics** (FR-05): MTBF, MTTR, history, warranty claims; dashboard; CSV.
- **API**: 32 routes; **browser**: Maintenance Dashboard, PM Schedule, Job Cards; English and Hindi.

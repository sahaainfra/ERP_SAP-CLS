## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 235, 236; up, down and up again clean): `inv_count_plans`, `inv_count_lines`, `inv_scrap_entries`, `inv_scrap_disposals`; flag, four pages under the Stores group, page tracker, notification templates. No existing table changed.
- **Count plans** (FR-01): full counts and cycle counts by ABC class (class from the stock value; cycle A 30, B 90, C 180 days, full 365); the plan is approved, then the store manager starts it, which freezes the counted items (BR-02, ADR-585). A due list and a daily reminder tell the store managers what is overdue.
- **Blind count** (FR-02): the counter sees item and bin only; a difference beyond the 2% tolerance is recounted by another keeper; the count is closed and goes to review. Mobile screen with a scan-or-type item filter.
- **Variance and adjustment** (FR-03, FR-04): book, count, recount, final, difference in quantity, value and per cent; the reviewer gives the reason of every difference; the adjustment is a 16.01 adjustment approved by store manager, procurement manager above ₹ 50,000 and CFO above ₹ 5 lakh. Nobody who counted approves it, at any step (SoD-09, ADR-587).
- **Scrap and disposal** (FR-05): the register (damaged stock is held, cut pieces are not in stock), disposals by sale, auction or write-off, approved by store manager, procurement manager and CFO (a write-off by value band); the stock leaves at the average rate and a gate pass is made.
- **Expiry** (FR-06): shelf-life stock expired or expiring within 30 days from the manufacturing date on the receipt, with the value at risk; a daily job alerts once.
- **API**: 38 routes under `/api/v1/inv` (2,884 routes in all). **Browser**: Count Plans, Blind Count (mobile), Count Variance, Scrap Disposal and Expiry (register, disposals, expiry).

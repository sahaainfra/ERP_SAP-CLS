# Part 21.07 completion: Price Escalation, PVC & Liquidated Damages Computation

Part: 21.07. Date: 2026-11-21. Module `ctr`, flag `ff.ctr.p2107` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 21.01 (contract terms: PVC, LD rate and cap), 21.06 (extension of time awards), 21.03 (work order terms), 15.06 (shared index master `proc_price_indices`).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items D-653 in `DEFECTS.md`.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 308, 309): `ctr_pvc_formulas`, `ctr_pvc_index_refs`, `ctr_escalation_calcs` (approval-controlled), `ctr_ld_assessments`; flag `ff.ctr.p2107`, three pages, tracker, templates.
- **Formula and indices**: versioned formula per contract or work order; index values with source and the document; base values from the master of the base month or typed.
- **Escalation**: R less the exclusions, the indices of the month of execution, the components and the net percent with threshold and cap, negative when the indices fall; workings and trace on the record and on its PDF.
- **Liquidated damages**: delay beyond the completion date plus the days of extension of time, at the rate per day, week or month (a part of a period counts whole unless `ctr.ld.part_period` is PRO_RATA), capped; exposure report; deductions by the client recorded against the assessment.
- **API**: routes under `/api/v1/ctr` (pvc-formulas, pvc-indices, escalations, ld-exposure, ld-assessments); **browser**: PVC Formulas and Price Indices, Escalation Computations with workings, LD Exposure; English and Hindi.

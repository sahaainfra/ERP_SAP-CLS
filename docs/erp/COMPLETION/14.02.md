# Part 14.02 completion: Daily Progress Report (DPR)

Part: 14.02. Date: 2026-11-23. Module `site`, feature flag `ff.site.p1402` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 14.01 (work authorisations and their bookings, read in place) and 04.15 (the offline engine, extended to carry lines); reads attendance (`att_daily`), contractors and workers, items and the schedule. Provides `site.dpr.approved` to progress (14.05), productivity (14.06), material reconciliation (16.10), MB suggestions (22.01) and execution intelligence (27.06).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items are in section 6.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 194, 195; nothing existing altered): `site_dprs`, `site_dpr_work`, `site_dpr_labour`, `site_dpr_materials`, `site_dpr_equipment`, `site_dpr_photos`, `site_dpr_notes`. Seed: the flag, four navigation entries under Site, four page-tracker rows, the widget `site.dpr_compliance`, three notification templates (English and Hindi).
- **Report and prefill** (FR-01 to FR-03, ADR-491): a report of a site or of a supervisor for a day; prefilled from the day's work authorisations, the attendance (by category, contractor and trade), the material issues and plant logs booked against the work authorisations, and the plan of the next day; a site report takes the sums of the supervisors' reports. Weather, hindrances, visitors, instructions, quality and safety notes. The server derives every baseline and every figure; a line that differs from what the day offered, or goes above the work authorisation by more than 5 %, needs a reason at sending (BR-01).
- **Labour against attendance** (BR-02): each line keeps the attendance man-days and the variance; beyond 10 % it is flagged and needs a reason; attendance not in the report is listed.
- **Photos** (FR-04): from the camera on the phone with the position and time, online or through the offline engine in chunks; caption, place and markup labels; the gallery on the report.
- **Cut-off, approval, lock, amendment** (FR-05, BR-03, ADR-492): `site.dpr.cutoff_time` (19:00); late is judged by the time it was sent on the phone; the construction manager or the project manager approves, an amendment goes to the project manager; the sender never decides; approved reports are locked; an amendment replaces its parent, replaces the bookings against the work authorisations and publishes the full new figures with `supersedes`. One approved report a site and day.
- **Offline** (ADR-493): the form `dpr` and the masters `dpr_prefill`, `dpr_contractors` and `dpr_items` of the offline engine; the engine's values may hold lists of flat records; the site picker and the prefill work from what the phone keeps.
- **PDF, print and distribution** (FR-06): the report as PDF and print with the company's name; on approval the roles of `site.dpr.distribution_roles` are notified.
- **Reports and compliance** (FR-07, section 10): the daily site report, the weekly and the monthly progress report from the approved reports (XLSX, CSV, PDF); the missing days; the compliance by site and day; the widget.
- **API**: 21 routes under `/api/v1/site/dprs`, in the route policy (every non-GET audited) and `openapi.yaml`; optimistic locking; `Idempotency-Key` required on create, submit and amend.
- **Permissions**: `site.dpr.{view,create,edit,delete,submit,approve,cancel,amend,print,export}` and `site.dpr.report`. SE and CSUP prepare; CM and PM approve (and print and export); PLAN, QS, PCMGR, PD, DIR and AUD read the reports.
- **Browser**: DPR Entry (a phone, step by step, offline save), DPR Register (missing and late days highlighted) and the report (sections, labour against attendance, photos gallery, approval, PDF), Daily Site and Progress Reports, DPR Compliance; English and Hindi.
- **Performance**: `npm run erp:site-dpr-perf` (50 sites, 100,000 reports with 600,000 section lines): register first page p95 27 ms, search 38 ms; one report 5 ms; prefill 1 ms; a report started from it 8 ms; missing days 2 ms, compliance 3 ms; the daily report of the company 5 ms, the monthly 26 ms (limits 500 ms, 300 ms for a record, 800 ms for a save). The plans use the indexes.
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 103, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, ADR-491 to ADR-493, D-485 to D-487, openapi.

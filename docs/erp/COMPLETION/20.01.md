# Part 20.01 completion: Batching Plant Master & Mix Designs

Part: 20.01. Date: 2026-11-21. Module `rmc`, flag `ff.rmc.p2001` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 17.01 (equipment register), 06.03 (item master).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items D-644 in `DEFECTS.md`.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 290, 291): `bp_plants`, `bp_silos`, `bp_scales`, `bp_mix_designs`; flag, group, two pages, tracker, templates.
- **Plants**: master data, silos and bins each holding one item with level, weighing scales with calibration state (in date, due soon, overdue) and a morning reminder job; the silo level changes by a dip reading with a reason, audited and published.
- **Reassignment**: an approved plant is locked; moving it to another project, site or cost centre is an amendment (a new version, same root) approved by the project manager; the old version stays in force until then and the silos and scales stay with the plant.
- **Mix designs**: kg per m3 of cement, fly ash, GGBS, sand, 10 and 20 mm aggregate, water and admixture; absolute volumes plus entrapped air checked to 1 m3 within a tolerance (T-01), water-binder ratio limit, trials against the IS 456 target mean, approval by the plant quality engineer then the quality control manager (and the consultant when the company asks), versions that take over on their effective date.
- **API**: 27 routes under `/api/v1/rmc` (plants, silos, scales, mix designs incl. import, export, print, history, live check); **browser**: Batching Plants and Mix Designs with a live volume check; English and Hindi.

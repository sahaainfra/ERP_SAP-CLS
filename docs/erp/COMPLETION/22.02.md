## PACK-18 update (2026-11-28)
- With `ff.bill.pack18` on, `GET /api/v1/bill/qty-recon` (by item) adds `money`. Per item it gives the certified value at the contract rate (the bill's rounding), and the billed quantity and value read from the items of the approved client bills. It adds a tie: the bills' gross equals the sum of their items plus milestones, to the paisa. It also names three variances: billed beyond certified, certified not billed, and the measurement book and the bill differing.
- The screen shows these as "Value: certified and billed" in en, hi and te.
- Evidence: `tests/bill-client.test.js` PACK-18, which includes a rate with paise and a bill approved through its chain; `e2e/pack18-mb-phone.spec.js` checks the panel.

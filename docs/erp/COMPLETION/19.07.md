## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 287, 288): `camp_camps`, `camp_rooms`, `camp_allocations`, `camp_meals`, `camp_inspections`, `camp_assets`, `camp_recoveries`; flag, five pages, tracker, templates.
- **Allocation** by badge or person, one bed per person, never beyond the beds of a room, check-in and check-out free the bed.
- **Occupancy** by camp, block and contractor; **muster** list and PDF for roll call.
- **Mess**: meals per contractor per day, corrected not duplicated; **recovery**: meals x rate plus bed-days x rent, worked out on the server and fixed at submit, approved by the camp in-charge then the project manager, locks the meals.
- **Welfare inspections** per area with compliance; a failed area raises a high-severity observation. **Assets** register.
- **Browser**: Camp Occupancy, Camps Rooms and Assets, Room and Bed Allocation, Camp Inspections, Mess and Recovery; English and Hindi.

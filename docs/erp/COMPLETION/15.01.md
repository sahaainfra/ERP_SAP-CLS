# Part 15.01 completion: Material Requests (Site Indents) & Purchase Requisitions

Part: 15.01. Date: 2026-11-29. Module `proc` (new), flag `ff.proc.p1501` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 14.01, 12.03, 06.03, read or called through their services. Provides `proc.pr.approved` (for the enquiry, 15.03), the soft commitment in the ledger of 12.03, and the trace from requisition line to material request line.

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items in section 6.

## 1. Discovery
`docs/erp/DISCOVERY/15.01.md`.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 206, 207; schema-diff of existing objects clean): `proc_material_requests`, `proc_mr_lines`, `proc_requisitions`, `proc_pr_lines`, `proc_pr_sources`, `proc_stock_levels`; seed of the flag, four navigation entries, page-tracker rows, six notification templates (English, Hindi).
- **Material request** (FR-01, FR-02): against a work authorisation, with the free stock of the site, nearby stores and the central warehouse under each line; approved by the construction manager; amended by a revision; lines imported by item code; met by issue, transfer or purchase.
- **Requisition** (FR-03 to FR-06, BR-01 to BR-03): consolidated from requests with the trace kept per line (T-03), or direct with a reason, or an emergency purchase; rate defaults; stock, budget and duplicate checks; controls CP-PROC-01, CP-PROC-02, CP-PROC-06; approval by value (PM, PD above ₹ 5 lakh, PROCMGR above ₹ 25 lakh); the soft commitment in 12.03 at approval; short-close, cancel, amend; balance to order from the orders.
- **API**: 34 routes under `/api/v1/proc` in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`; optimistic locking; `Idempotency-Key` on create, submit, fulfil, consolidate, amend, import.
- **Browser**: New Material Request (phone), Material Requests (list and object with fulfilment), Purchase Requisitions (list, object with budget, stock and duplicate panels, direct form), Consolidate MRs into PR; English and Hindi.

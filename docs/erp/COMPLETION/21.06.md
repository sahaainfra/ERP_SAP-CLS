# Part 21.06 completion: Claims, Notices, EOT & Disputes

Part: 21.06. Date: 2026-11-21. Module `ctr`, flag `ff.ctr.p2106` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 21.01 (contract, clauses and dates), 13.05 (delays and time-impact records), 11.05 (letters and time-bars), 21.05 (rejected variations).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items D-652 in `DEFECTS.md`.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 306, 307): `ctr_claims` and `ctr_disputes` (approval-controlled), `ctr_claim_events`, `ctr_claim_quantum`, `ctr_claim_evidence`, `ctr_claim_log`, `ctr_eot_awards`, `ctr_dispute_log`; flag `ff.ctr.p2106`, three pages, tracker, templates.
- **Claim**: time-bar from the event and the contract clause, notice by letter (late is recorded as lapsed), quantum from templates, evidence snapshots, approval COMMGR, PD, DIR, submission versions, responses, negotiation and determination.
- **EOT**: the days awarded move the contract completion date; the planning engineer is told to revise the baseline.
- **Alerts and watchlist**: claims and free events with the days to the bar, alerts at the configured offsets, escalated near the bar.
- **API**: routes under `/api/v1/ctr` (claims, claim-events, claim-watchlist, disputes); **browser**: Claims Register with the claim object, Time-bar Watchlist, Disputes; English and Hindi.

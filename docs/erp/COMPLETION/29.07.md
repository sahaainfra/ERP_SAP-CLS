## 1. Discovery

Retention existed only as a label on documents, with nothing acting on it. See the [discovery note](../DISCOVERY/29.07.md).

## 2. What was built

The page is `/admin/archive` (**Administration → Retention & Archival**).

**Tiles**: policies set, archived records, legal holds, disposed records.

**Tabs**:

- **Retention policies (FR-01)**: for each record class (daily progress reports, journals, purchase orders, former employees), a policy:
  - archive after N months;
  - keep N years;
  - the legal basis;
  - then dispose, anonymise or review.

  A new policy replaces the old one, which is kept.
- **Archival jobs (FR-02)**:
  - SYSADM previews how many finished records are due (past the archive age, or in a closed project) and how many legal holds will skip, then raises the job. The auditor approves it.
  - Each record is copied with its SHA-256 into the archive, and into a compressed file in archive storage.
  - The row stays in its table, so references, links and search keep working, but a database trigger now refuses any change or deletion of it.
- **Legal holds (FR-04, BR-01)**: on a record, or on a project and all its records. A held record is skipped by every job, disposal and erasure until the hold is released with a reason.
- **Archive (FR-03)**: search by document number, then open a record read-only. It shows the snapshot (personal fields masked for most roles), whether it still matches its hash, the job and who approved it, the policy, and the audit trail. Viewing is audited.
- **Disposal & erasure (FR-05)**:
  - **Disposal**: the auditor proposes disposing of archived records past their retention; a director approves.
    - Records are deleted, or anonymised where other records refer to them.
    - Their archive copies are cleared.
    - A certificate of destruction (PDF) lists each record with its archive hash.
    - Held records are left out, and REVIEW policies are refused.
  - **DPDP erasure**: a former employee's request is checked: no longer employed, no legal hold, eight years since leaving (payroll, PF, ESI, tax). A failed check refuses it at once; otherwise a director approves. The contact and address fields are then redacted, while the employee code and payroll history stay, and the change is audited.

Labels are en/hi/te (area `arc`).

**Permissions**:

| Role | Grant |
|---|---|
| SYSADM | Policies, archival jobs, holds |
| AUD | Approves jobs, places holds, proposes disposal and erasure |
| DIR | Approves disposal and erasure; holds |
| CFO | Views; holds |
| ITADM | Views |

T-01:

- **Held record skipped**: of three old approved reports, one is on hold. The job archives two and skips the held one, which can still be updated.
- **Read-only**: an `UPDATE` or `DELETE` of an archived row fails with "ARCHIVED". This was checked in the API test and again from the browser test's database connection.
- **Retrievable**: the archived report is found by its number and opened, with its snapshot intact, its job and its policy. The archive file holds exactly the two archived rows.
- **Project hold**: a hold on a closed project blocks its job until it is released.

1. **Archival**:
   - the policy and roles;
   - a hold; the preview; nothing archived before approval; the maker cannot approve;
   - the job, the read-only triggers, the held row still writable;
   - search and view; the audited view; the archive file;

## 6. Known limitations

D-795:

- Four record classes.
- Archived rows stay in place, read-only, rather than moving to a separate archive database.
- Linked files are not disposed with their records.
- Erasure covers employees only.
- The eight-year statutory period is a constant.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set `ff.admin.p2907`.
2. As SYSADM, open **Administration → Retention & Archival** and set the daily progress report policy.
3. As AUD, place a hold on one old report.
4. As SYSADM, under **Archival jobs**, preview and raise the job; as AUD, approve it.
5. Under **Archive**, open an archived report: it is read-only, with its snapshot and trail. The held report was skipped.

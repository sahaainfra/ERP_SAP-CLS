## What was built
- **Database** (migration 274): the flag, three pages, tracker, two dashboard widgets and a template; no table is added.
- **Dashboard** (FR-01): eight KPI tiles with value, trend, target and colour, the ageing of open requests and NCRs, the exceptions with their drill-downs, pending approvals, project and period filters, CSV and PDF export.
- **Control charts** (FR-02, T-01): X-bar and R charts of the 28-day cubes by grade and supplier with centre line and limits, points beyond them, long runs and the standard deviation trend; the limits use the factors A2, D3, D4 and d2.
- **Ratings** (FR-03): subcontractors, suppliers and sites scored out of 100 with the points shown; worst first; supplier scores published nightly for the vendor scorecards.
- **Project 360** slot `quality` and two widgets of the dashboard framework.
- **API**: 5 routes plus a project lookup; **browser**: Quality Dashboard, Concrete Control Charts, Quality Ratings; English and Hindi.

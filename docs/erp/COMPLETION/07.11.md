## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 128, 129; nothing existing altered): `perf_cycles`, `perf_kpis`, `perf_kpi_values`, `perf_kpi_disputes`, `perf_goals`, `perf_feedback`, `perf_reviews`; seed: flag, six navigation entries and seven tracker rows, notification templates (English, Hindi), a widget.
- **Service** (`performance.js`): the metric registry and scoring; KPI library (a change is a draft until a second person activates it); cycles (open, close); monthly computation with evidence, leave and transfer days left out, a nightly job; reproduction of a value from its evidence; disputes (frozen until HR resolves; upheld = adjusted value that is never overwritten); goals with manager agreement and weights within 100; feedback, recognition and one-to-one notes with a private flag; dashboards of the person and the team; the review (self, manager, calibration with a reason, submit blocked by open disputes, approval, appraisal letter from the template library, print, history).
- **Workflow**: `HR_PERF_REVIEW`: manager, skip-level reviewer, HR manager, the director for senior grades (resolvers `perf.manager`, `perf.skip` from the reporting line).
- **API**: 45 routes under `/api/v1/hr/reviews` and `/api/v1/hr/performance/*` in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`; review export.
- **Permissions**: `hr.review.{view,create,edit,delete,submit,approve,cancel,amend,print,export,view_all,calibrate,letter}`, `hr.perf.{own,team,feedback,configure,config_approve,compute,dispute_resolve}`.
- **Events**: `perf.cycle.{opened,closed}`, `perf.review.{launched,self_submitted,manager_reviewed,calibrated,approved}`, `perf.kpi.{disputed,dispute_resolved}`, `perf.goal.agreed`, `perf.feedback.given`.
- **Browser**: My Performance (tiles, KPI cards with trend and evidence drill-down, dispute), Team Performance, Goal Setting, Appraisal Review (form for self, manager, calibration), Appraisal Reviews (list), Feedback & One-to-One, KPI Library & Cycles; English and Hindi (`prf` namespace, 198 keys).
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 71, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, ADR-325 to 328, D-360 to 364, openapi.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As HR open **KPI Library & Cycles**: make a KPI (metric: tasks finished by their due date); a second HR person activates it. Make a cycle and open it; in **Appraisal Reviews** press Make the reviews.
2. As an employee open **My Performance**: the KPI card shows the value, the target, the score and six months; Show evidence lists the records behind the figure, the days left out for leave or transfer, and that the same figure comes out again. Dispute it: it is frozen until HR resolves it.
3. **Goal Setting**: write a goal and send it; the manager sees it on **Team Performance** and agrees it.
4. Open the review: the employee writes the self-review; the manager writes theirs (the employee cannot see it yet); HR calibrates (a different rating needs a reason) and writes the development plan, then sends it: the manager, the skip-level reviewer and the HR manager approve (the director too for a senior grade). HR issues the appraisal letter; the employee downloads it.
5. Switch to Hindi: headings and labels change.

## 7. Known limitations
- **Period filter.** The overview pages use the framework's filter bar (project, site, financial-year period, default this financial year). The HSE and quality pages used the last twelve months by default; the cards follow the period chosen. The QS dashboard, control tower, stores and plant services take no period, so on those pages only the project filter changes the cards (their subtitles say "as of" the day).
- **Own filters of a page** (HR branch / department, plant group-by, budget "by vendor / cost code") are on the detailed page only.
- **T-02 for every card.** The drill-down total is checked in a test for the to-do card; the other cards open the same list with the project and site of the dashboard, but a list's total is not compared with each card in a test (D-687).
- **Finance (FR-03).** No card until the services of 23.x exist.
- **Hindi titles** of the new cards are not written (`title_hi` empty: the English title is shown on a Hindi screen) (D-688).
- `materials-dashboard` (16.11), utilisation (17.03), and the other module dashboards outside the list of the prompt (engineering, site, risk, BD) are not rebuilt in this part.

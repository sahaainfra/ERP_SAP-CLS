# Part 04.08 completion: Task Management and Senior-Initiated Task Routing

Status: **Ready for review, with the open items below** (producers from alerts, meetings, NCR and HSE, the Project 360 tab, richer recurrence, a one-click reassign on escalation and the translation of the pages are later: D-218 to D-223). Feature flag `ff.eng.p0408` (OFF by default; ON in development and test).

## What was found
No task tables existed. The workflow engine had approval tasks only; the RACI (04.05), the escalation path and delegations (04.02), the notification engine (04.06), the file service and the "My Work" sources were reused.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 70 and 71; nothing existing altered): `tsk_tasks`, `tsk_assignees`, `tsk_checklist_items`, `tsk_dependencies`, `tsk_comments`, `tsk_escalations`, `tsk_routing_matrix`; reference seed: the flag, five notification templates in English and Hindi, five navigation entries and tracker rows.
- **Engine** (`platform/tasks`, ADR-189 to 192): `create` (any module; the same source gives one task), `route` (topic to department to role to employee), `act` (accept, start, block, unblock, complete, verify, close, reopen, cancel with the rules BR-01, BR-02 and FR-04), dependencies without loops, recurrence (daily, weekly, monthly).
- **Module side** (`modules/eng/tasks.js`): who may see and do what (own tasks; team tasks within the projects of `tsk.team.view`; the assigner and a delegate verify, never the person who did it), lists by view with filters, the team dashboard, the responsibility matrix (a change is approved by the system administrator or a director and audited), the hourly sweep (next occurrences, reminders, escalation along the path of 04.02), the sample document's rejection leaving a task for its author, the work source for My Work.
- **API** (`/api/v1/tsk`): tasks (list, counts, export, one, change, nine actions, comments, checklist, dependencies, people), instructions with a routing preview, the team dashboard, the matrix. 24 routes in the route policy.
- **Pages**: My Tasks `/tasks` (list, board, calendar), Task `/tasks/task`, New Task / Senior Instruction `/tasks/new` (with the routing preview), Team Tasks `/tasks/team`, Department Responsibility Matrix `/admin/task-routing`.
- **Permissions**: `tsk.team.view`, `tsk.task.assign`, `tsk.instruction.create` (DIR, PD, PM, CFO, PROCMGR, COMMGR, HRMGR), `tsk.routing.view`, `tsk.routing.edit` (HRMGR, SYSADM; SYSADM or DIR approves a change). Parameters: `tsk.dependency_block`, `tsk.escalate_level1_days`, `tsk.escalate_level2_days`. Job `tsk.sweep` (hourly at :05).
- Documents: ARCHITECTURE section 42, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, the schema lock, DECISIONS (ADR-189 to 192), DEFECTS (D-218 to D-223), PROGRESS.csv (row 36).
- Also changed: the engines of 04.07 and 04.08 read parameters through a store of their own, so a value changed through the API takes effect at once (ADR-192).

## Click-path for a reviewer
1. As a project manager open **New Task / Senior Instruction**, make a task for yourself with a checklist, a due date and "Needs evidence". On the task: **Start**; **Mark done** is refused ("needs evidence"); in **Evidence** attach a photo; **Mark done** closes it. The history lists each step.
2. **My Tasks**: switch between **List**, **Board** (columns by status) and **Calendar** (by due date); the figures at the top count open and overdue tasks.
3. As the HR manager open **Department Responsibility Matrix**, add a topic (department, role, days, evidence), **Send for approval**; as the system administrator approve it in **My Approvals**; the topic is now in force.
4. As a senior choose **Senior instruction by topic**, type the topic: the page shows the department, the role and the person it would go to, and why; send it. The person sees it in My Tasks, accepts it, does it; with verification the senior verifies it.
5. **Team Tasks**: workload by person, overdue first, the weekly trend, the most overdue list. After a task is overdue, the sweep escalates it to the next role of the escalation path and shows the level on the task.
6. A rejected sample document leaves a task for its author.

## Tests and acceptance
- **T-01** an instruction goes to the holder of the role with the fewest open tasks; with nobody in the role to the fallback person, then to the head of the department; a topic with no row goes to the head of the department the sender named, and with none is refused. **T-02** an overdue task escalates once for each level along the path, is reminded once a day before, and a finished task is left alone. **T-03** a task that needs evidence cannot be completed without a clean file (an infected file does not count); a task that needs verification is not closed before the assigner or a delegate verifies, and the person who did it cannot.
- Also in `tests/tasks.test.js` (8 tests): dependencies block or warn by setting, loops refused; recurrence; views, checklist, comments, change with version check, reassign, cancel; a module makes a task once for the same source; the sample document leaves a task; the matrix with approval; the team dashboard and who may see it. Browser: `e2e/tasks.spec.js` (6 runs, desktop and phone).
- **AC-01** tasks as list, board and calendar; an automatic task from the sample module; instruction routing. **AC-02** page checks and the visual baseline (the five new pages in three themes at three widths). **AC-03** permission tests (200, 403, 404 for a stranger, cross-project through the team scope). **AC-04** the matrix is a workflow document and an audit entry. **AC-05** see Results. **AC-06** this report.

Inside the 500 ms, 300 ms and 800 ms standards.

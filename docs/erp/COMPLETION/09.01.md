## 7. How to verify manually
1. As the estimation manager open **Estimates**, **New estimate**: choose a project (or a registered tender), the basis date, the region (MH), the HO overhead, contingency, escalation and GST percentages.
2. **Import client BOQ**: paste the rows from Excel (section, section name, item no., description, unit, quantity, client rate), **Check**, then **Add to the estimate**.
3. On **Items**, price each item: a typed cost rate, a released rate analysis (its direct cost per unit), or a build-up of components (an item is priced from the library on the basis date); give the markup; **Save and price**.
4. **Indirect costs**: site establishment (lump sum), staff (months x rate), BG charges (% of direct). **Risk and tax**: the risks with impact and probability; the percentages. The header shows selling, cost, margin, GST and bid value; **Cost and margin** shows each section.
5. **Resource summary**: cement, steel, labour man-days and plant of the estimate. **New version**: change a quantity; **Compare versions** shows the changes.
6. **Send for approval**: a colleague of estimation and the director approve. Sign in as a quantity surveyor: the estimate shows quantities and selling rates only. Switch to Hindi.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 118, 119; nothing existing altered): `att_daily`, `att_regularisations`, `att_overtime_requests`, `att_manual_entries`, `att_period_locks`, `att_arrears`; seed: flag, six navigation entries and tracker rows, notification template (English, Hindi).
- **Services**: status engine and recompute, register, daily list, muster roll, exports and print, lock, reopen workflow, arrears (`att-register.js`); the three requests and the approvals worklist (`att-requests.js`); the device import type (`reg-integrations.js`); `recordDeviceSync` and the IMPORTED flag in the punch service.
- **Workflows** (in the matrix): `HR_REGULARISATION` (supervisor or PM; HR manager from the fourth in a month), `HR_OVERTIME_REQUEST` (supervisor; PM beyond 4 h a day or 50 h a month; HR manager outside the policy), `HR_ATT_MANUAL` (HR manager, maker cannot check), `HR_ATT_REOPEN` (HR manager, finance head).
- **API**: `/api/v1/hr/regularisations`, `/hr/overtime-requests`, `/hr/manual-entries` (list, get, create, update, delete, submit, withdraw, cancel, amend, history, print, export); `/api/v1/att/register|muster|daily|day|recompute|approvals|periods/:id/lock|reopen|period-locks|arrears`. 55 new routes in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`. A separate import endpoint for regularisations was not built (D-342).
- **Permissions**: `hr.regularisation.*`, `hr.overtime_request.*`, `att.manual.*` (view, create, edit, delete, submit, approve, cancel, amend, print, export), `att.register.{view,export}`, `att.daily.recompute`, `att.period.{lock,reopen}`, `att.arrears.view`, `att.device.import`. EMP has own requests; WSUP, PM, CM first-level; HRMGR all; PAY and AUD read; CFO approves reopening.
- **Events**: `att.period.locked`, `att.period.reopened`, `att.arrears.raised`, `att.regularisation.approved`, `att.overtime.approved`, `att.manual.approved`.
- **Browser**: Monthly Attendance Register (colour and code, day panel, exports, recompute), Daily Attendance (exceptions filter), Regularisation Request, Overtime Request, Attendance Approvals (bulk, manual entry), Period Lock.
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 66, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, ADR-303 to 307, D-340 to 344, openapi.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As HR open **Monthly Attendance Register** for a past month: codes by day, totals at the right; click a day for the punches and flags.
2. As an employee open **Regularisation Request**, give the missing out time, send; as the supervisor open **Attendance Approvals** and approve: the day becomes present.
3. As HR open **Period Lock** after the month: the checklist names what is open; lock; a later correction appears as arrears and the register does not change; ask to reopen and have another HR manager and the finance head approve.
4. Load a device file through the Excel import type "Device punches": loading it again stores nothing new.

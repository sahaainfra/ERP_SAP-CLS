## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 310, 311): `bill_mb_entries` (approval-controlled); flag `ff.bill.p2201`; three pages (Record Measurement, Measurement Book, MB Abstract), tracker rows, notification templates.
- **Server**: `src/server/modules/bill` (validators, `mb.js`, routes, module); 19 route-policy lines, every POST audited; permissions `bill.mb_entry.*` (view, create, edit, delete, submit, approve, cancel, amend, print, export, check).
- **Workflow and events**: `BILL_MB_ENTRY` (QS check, PM certify); events `bill.mb.certified` (carries the certified quantity, the BOQ code and the page) and `bill.mb.checked`.
- **Browser**: Record Measurement (phone form with the formula templates, live quantity and checks, DPR suggestions, photos on the entry), Measurement Book list and entry with the check form and approvals, MB Abstract with the book in print; English and Hindi.
- **Also fixed**: update parsers of the contract module (21.xx) applied schema defaults to omitted fields, so a partial update could reset them; they now strip the defaults (helper `nd`).

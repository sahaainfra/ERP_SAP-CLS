## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 114, 115; nothing existing altered): `att_shifts`, `att_policies`, `att_policy_assignments`, `att_rosters`, `att_roster_changes`, `att_period_defs`, `att_periods`; seed: flag, six navigation entries and tracker rows, a notification template (English and Hindi).
- **Calculations** (`att-calc.js`, no database): the shift window across midnight, one day of attendance under a policy (grace, late and early marks, "3 lates = half a day", half and full day, missing punch modes, overtime with grade and category eligibility, multiplier and day and week caps, comp-off, rounding), weekly off patterns (fixed, nth Saturday, rotational), rotating patterns, attendance periods from a start day.
- **Service** (`attendance.js`): shifts (a shift in use keeps its times), policy versions with effective dates, assignments with the hierarchy and resolution, rosters, changes and swaps, periods and their closing.
- **Workflows** with matrix rows: `HR_ATT_POLICY` (HR manager; the director too when pay is touched), `HR_ROSTER_CHANGE` (supervisor, project manager; the project manager alone when the supervisor raised it). The workflow engine now also finds approvers through the holder sources of 07.03.
- **API** `/api/v1/hr/att/*` (39 routes in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`); events `att.policy.approved`, `att.assignment.changed`, `att.roster.changed`, `att.period.closed`; widget `att.roster_waiting`.
- **Permissions**: `att.shift.{view,edit}`, `att.policy.{view,create,edit,delete,submit,approve,cancel,amend,print,export}`, `att.assignment.{view,edit}`, `att.roster.{view,edit,change,approve}`, `att.period.{view,manage}`. HRMGR all; DIR approves policies; PM and PD plan rosters and approve changes within their projects; CM plans; WSUP asks for changes and approves as supervisor; SE views shifts; PAY and AUD read; everyone with an employee record sees their own shift (`my-shift`).
- **Browser**: Shifts, Shift Editor, Roster Planner (grid by person and day, a list in each cell and drag from the palette, swap by dragging, undo, legend, zoom, print, rotating patterns), Attendance Policies, Policy Rules Editor (rules, and examples worked out by the server as the rules are typed), Policy Assignment (hierarchy, who gets what with the source of each part, attendance period and closing).
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 64, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, DECISIONS ADR-293 to 297, DEFECTS D-331 to D-334, openapi.

## 6. Known limitations
D-331 to D-334. A policy takes effect on its date: an assignment that names a policy not yet in force resolves to no policy until then.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As HR open **Shifts > New shift**: make a day shift and a night shift (22:00 to 06:00 says "the next day").
2. Open **Attendance Policies > New policy**: change the rules and watch the examples change; send it. Another HR manager and, because overtime touches pay, the director approve.
3. Open **Policy Assignment**: assign the policy, the shift and the Sunday off to the company; "Who gets what" shows the policy, shift and the level each came from; assign a crew or an employee and see it win.
4. As the project manager open **Roster Planner**, choose a site, make a rotating pattern, ask for a change of a day (it waits, Undo withdraws it); the supervisor and the project manager approve another and the grid shows it.
5. In **Policy Assignment** set the attendance period start day (for example 26) and close a finished period.

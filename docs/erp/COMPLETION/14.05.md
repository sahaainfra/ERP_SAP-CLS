## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 200, 201; schema-diff of existing objects clean): `prs_activity_progress`, `prs_periods`, `prs_evm`, `prs_weights`; seed of the flag, four navigation entries, four page-tracker rows, three notification templates (English, Hindi).
- **Quantities** (FR-01, BR-01): a consumer of `site.dpr.approved` writes the executed quantity of each activity and day (an amendment replaces its parent, a site report replaces the supervisors' reports of its day, a replay changes nothing) and the schedule gets its percent complete and actual dates through 13.01 (ADR-501).
- **One calculation** (`progress.js`, `evm.js`; ADR-499): activity progress; weights (the approved budget of the WBS node shared by quantity, or a manual weight); planned from the current baseline; forecast from the schedule; PV, EV, SV, CV, SPI, CPI, EAC (BAC / CPI), TCPI; WBS nodes and the project roll up by weight. A measured quantity replaces the DPR quantity in the financial progress only where `site.progress.mb_override` is on.
- **Periods** (FR-02, BR-02; ADR-500): weekly or monthly, PLAN prepares (adjustments with a reason, actual cost) and the PM closes; the snapshot is taken at sending and again at close, the adjustments are written, `prs_evm` rows (project and each WBS node) are kept and `site.period.closed` is published; closed periods are immutable and the latest is amended by a copy that replaces it with its figures.
- **Reports** (FR-05, FR-06): S-curves (physical and financial, planned, actual, forecast), dashboard (tiles with target and colour, behind-plan list, my pending actions, variance by WBS, milestones, each drilling to the report), progress by WBS and activity (XLSX, CSV, PDF), client report (percentages and dates, no money; a closed period's is its snapshot; PDF, XLSX, print). Money (budget, PV, EV, AC, CPI, EAC) needs `site.period.cost`.
- **API**: 22 routes in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`; optimistic locking; `Idempotency-Key` on create, submit, amend.
- **Browser**: Progress Dashboard, Progress by WBS and Activity, Progress Period Close (form with adjustment grid, object with snapshot and decision panel), Client Progress Report; English and Hindi. The chart library now draws a missing value as a gap.

## PACK-20 update (2026-11-28)
- With `ff.plan.pack20`, the S-curve (`GET /api/v1/site/progress/scurve` and the dashboard) carries three lines:
  - planned value (PV);
  - earned value (EV), the same as the actual financial line;
  - the **actual cost (AC)** of the approved periods, as a step at each period end, nothing after the as-of date.

  It is shown only to people who see cost. The dashboard's financial chart then draws PV, EV, AC and the forecast, in en, hi and te.
- Evidence: `tests/site-progress.test.js` PACK-20. It checks that EV on the curve equals the EV of the closed period, that AC equals the period's actual cost, and that the construction manager gets no cost. `e2e/site-progress.spec.js` was re-run.

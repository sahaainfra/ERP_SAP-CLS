## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 360, 361): `pl_structure` (template seeded: revenue, material, labour, plant, subcontract, site overhead, gross profit, corporate overhead, foreseeable losses, other, operating profit), `pl_snapshots` (approval-controlled); flag, two pages, page-tracker rows, two templates.
- **Structure** (FR-01; ADR-691): lines and the accounts or groups each takes; a company copy on first change; an account named by one line only; each line shows the accounts it takes.
- **Project P&L** (FR-02, FR-03; ADR-692): month, year to date and to date from the ledger, budget and forecast side by side, % of revenue, six-month trend, reconciliation to the ledger, drill-down to journal lines with their source documents.
- **Snapshots** (FR-04, BR-01; ADR-693): made by the accounts manager for an ended month, frozen by the CFO only equal to the ledger and with the ledger unchanged since; frozen snapshots never change (a correction supersedes); `fin.pl_snapshot.frozen`.
- **Portfolio** (FR-05; ADR-695): KPIs, ranking by gross margin, sector, region, client, loss-making projects, snapshots waiting.
- **Masking** (section 8; ADR-694): the PM sees his projects with the corporate overhead and operating profit masked.
- **API**: 18 routes under `/api/v1/fin/project-pl`, `/pl-structure`, `/pl-snapshots`, `/profitability`. **Browser**: Project P&L (with the snapshot page and its decision panel), Profitability Overview; English and Hindi. **Project 360**: P&L of the year. **Job** `fin.pl_monthly`.

## 6. Known limitations
- The structure is edited per line through the API (accounts, groups); there is no drag-and-drop editor of lines and subtotals.
- Segments come from the project type, branch and contract client; there is no separate segment master.
- No scheduled e-mail of the reports.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. Finance → Project P&L: a project and month; the month, year and to-date columns, budget and forecast; **Equal to the ledger: Yes**; tap a line to see its journal lines.
2. **Make the month's snapshot** (an ended month) → **Send to be frozen**; as CFO **Freeze**.
3. Finance → Profitability Overview: KPIs, ranking, sector / region / client, loss-making projects.

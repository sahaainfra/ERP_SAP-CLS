## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 362, 363): `cf_forecasts` (approval-controlled), `cf_lines`, `cf_bank_limits`, `cf_priority_partners`; flag, two pages, page-tracker rows, templates `fin.cf.decided`, `fin.cf.alert`.
- **Forecast** (FR-01; ADR-696): 13 weeks (4–26) from this Monday, built from ten sources, each line with its source document, due date, counterparty and confidence; refresh keeps the adjustments.
- **Adjustments and scenarios** (FR-02, BR-01; ADR-697): manual lines with their reason, audited; collections late / payments early / both, shift 1–8 weeks.
- **Publication** (ADR-701): workflow `FIN_CASH_FORECAST` ACCMGR → CFO; earlier published forecasts superseded; `fin.cash_forecast.published`; alert when cash goes below zero; Monday reminder job `fin.cf_weekly`.
- **Reports** (FR-03; ADR-698): actual bank movement against the published forecast by week; the project cash curve with its peak funding need (PM and PD through `fin.cash_curve.view`).
- **Treasury** (FR-04; ADR-699): bank-wise balance, available, blocked (deposits, escrow, margin money), OD/CC use, guarantee limits, facilities (term loans with instalments, CC/OD, BG/LC).
- **Payment priority** (FR-05; ADR-700): statutory → MSME → critical → other with the running total against what is available; critical suppliers also rank in the 23.04 payment proposals.
- **API**: 22 operations under `/api/v1/fin/cash-forecasts` and `/api/v1/fin/treasury`. **Browser**: Treasury & Cash Position (with the forecast object page, its adjustments, scenarios and decision panel), Cash Reports (project curve, forecast vs actual); English and Hindi.

## 6. Known limitations
- Client delay is the average over the client's settled invoices; no seasonality or per-project model.
- No bank-statement feed: actuals come from the bank ledger, so they are as current as the reconciliation (23.05).
- Facilities are entered by hand; no interest calculation on CC/OD use.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. Finance → Treasury & Cash Position: balances, available, blocked; **Prepare this week's forecast**.
2. In the forecast: weeks, lines by source, a scenario; add an adjustment with its reason; **Send for publication**; as CFO **Publish**.
3. Treasury shows the published forecast; tabs Facilities and Payment priority.
4. Finance → Cash Reports: a project's cash curve; forecast vs actual.

# Part 05.04 completion: Communication Center (auto groups, instructions, acknowledgements, message-to-action)

Part: 05.04. Date: 2026-11-04 (late, IST 5 November). Feature flag `ff.msg.p0504` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 05.01 and 04.08.

Status: **Ready for review, with the open items in section 6** (D-280 to D-284). The main one: only the **task** can be made from a message today; RFI (11.02), NCR (18.04), site instruction (14.04), material request (15.01) and HSE observation (19.04) are not live and are listed as such (D-280).

## 1. Discovery summary
See `docs/erp/DISCOVERY/05.04.md`. 05.01 made project, site and department channels by hand with lazy automatic members; there were no instruction messages, announcements or conversation escalation. Reused: the unified Messages service, the platform task core (04.08), escalation paths (04.02), trace links (04.14), notifications (04.06), the file service, the workflow engine, the job scheduler.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 92 and 93, nothing existing altered): `msg_auto_group_rules`, `msg_auto_group_links`, `msg_instructions`, `msg_acknowledgements`, `msg_announcements`, `msg_announcement_reads`, `msg_escalations`, `msg_actions`; seed: flag, six navigation entries, six tracker rows, six notification templates (en, hi).
- **Auto groups** (`autogroups.js`, ADR-235): project, site and department groups for every active project, site and department (adopting the ones made by hand), team and discipline groups from rules; members follow assignments (an assignment event runs the sync at once, the minute job catches the rest); a system message says who joined or left and why; hand-added members stay. Rules change only through workflow `MSG_AUTOGROUPS` (maker-checker).
- **Instructions** (`comcenter.js`, ADR-236): a sender (right `msg.instruction.send` in the project or site of the conversation) marks an own message; the other members must acknowledge; high and urgent ones cannot be acknowledged in bulk; at the due time the people who did not are escalated to their managers on the escalation path, the sender and the group are told; register with percentages and CSV export; cancel.
- **Announcements** (ADR-237): audience by roles, projects, sites, departments or everyone (scope-checked), live count, publish now or later, read tracking, confirmation with optional quiz (answers never sent to recipients), expiry, cancel, sender statistics.
- **Escalated conversations** (ADR-239): a member flags a conversation; unresolved after the chosen hours the managers are told and, in a group, join it; a direct conversation stays private.
- **Message to action** (ADR-238): registry; the task is made from the message (title, text, attachments copied by the new `filesOf(db).copyTo`, project, assignee, due date), linked back by a trace link event and shown on the message.
- **Communication Center**: summary of tiles and pending items; Messages list views Teams, Projects, Sites, Departments, ERP-linked, Important, Escalated.
- **Browser**: Communication Center (dashboard), Pending Acknowledgements (worklist), Instruction Register (report), Announcements, New Announcement (form), Auto Groups (config), and in Messages the instruction badge and form, the "Make a task or record" panel and the "Flag" panel.
- **Permissions**: `msg.instruction.send`, `msg.announcement.send` (PM, PD, DIR, HRMGR, CFO, PROCMGR, COMMGR, ACCMGR, PCMGR), `msg.comm.view_all` (SYSADM, AUD, DIR, PD), `msg.autogroup.view` (SYSADM, ITADM, AUD, DIR), `msg.autogroup.edit` (SYSADM). Acknowledging needs no permission.
- Documents: ARCHITECTURE section 53, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, DECISIONS ADR-235 to 239, DEFECTS D-280 to D-284, openapi, PROGRESS.csv (row 47), screenshots in `docs/erp/screenshots/comcenter/`.

## 6. Known limitations
D-280 only the task is live (RFI, NCR, site instruction, material request, HSE observation wait for their parts; a task has no draft state, the filled form is the draft); D-281 one escalation level, managers by role path, acknowledgement feed to 07.11 only as an event; D-282 register has CSV only and no chart, tiles have no trend or target, announcements are plain text; D-283 file copy takes the bytes at that moment and skips files not yet scanned, secure conversations cannot be turned into records; D-284 English only, pages and a11y stages not run, no 100k-row timing.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As a project manager open **Messages**, a group, open the menu of one of your messages and choose **Make this an instruction**: priority High, a due date, **Send as an instruction**. The message shows the instruction badge (0 of N acknowledged).
2. As a person in the group open **Communication Center**: the tile **Pending acknowledgements** shows it, and **My pending actions** lists it. Open **Pending Acknowledgements** and **Acknowledge**.
3. As the manager open **Instruction Register**: the row shows 1 of N, the details list who has not; **Export CSV**.
4. In the group choose a message, **Make a task or record…**: the other kinds show "not live yet"; choose Task, give it to someone, **Make it**: the link opens the task and the message shows a Task chip.
5. **New announcement**: write it, choose a role, add a quiz question, **Publish**; the recipient opens **Announcements**, answers wrong then right and confirms; the manager sees the confirmed percentage.
6. As the system administrator open **Auto Groups**, add a discipline rule, send it for approval; the IT administrator approves it; the group appears with the people assigned to the project, and in Messages under **Teams** the system message says who joined.
7. In a group choose **Flag**, give a reason and one hour: after the hour the managers are told and join the group.

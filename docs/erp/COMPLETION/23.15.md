# Part 23.15 completion: multi-company, inter-company and consolidation

Part: 23.15 (step 181). Date: 2026-11-19. Module `fin`, flag `ff.fin.p2315` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 23.14 (statements by Schedule III line); uses the posting engine and its reversal (23.01). Provides `fin.ic_transaction.posted` and `fin.consolidation.finalised` (consolidated reports, MIS 27.07).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items in section 6.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 366, 367): `ic_relationships`, `ic_transactions` (approval-controlled), `ic_consolidations`, `jv_shares`; accounts 160090 / 230090 (engine-only) in the template and every company; flag, four pages, page-tracker rows, templates `fin.ic.decided`, `fin.ic.alert`.
- **Relationships** (FR-01; ADR-709): kept by the parent's CFO with the holding and both sides' inter-company account codes, checked to exist in both companies.
- **Transactions** (FR-02, BR-01; ADR-710): charge, transfer, loan, settlement; the originating accounts manager approves (not the maker); the counterpart's accounts manager accepts with the account and dimensions of its side (or disputes); both mirror journals posted through the engine in one database transaction; dispute → drop; reversal in both companies.
- **Reconciliation** (FR-03; ADR-711): pair balances from both sides, one-sided lines on inter-company accounts (cleared by an opposite entry), items waiting or disputed.
- **Consolidation** (FR-04; ADR-712): parent + subsidiaries from the 23.14 statements; inter-company balances and charges eliminated; investment against share capital; non-controlling interest in equity and profit; eliminations and balance sheet balanced; runs saved and finalised by the CFO only when nothing is unmatched or open.
- **JV** (FR-05; ADR-713): partner shares per project (own share the rest) and the partner statement.
- **API**: 29 operations (`/fin/ic-transactions`, `/ic-relationships`, `/ic-reconciliation`, `/consolidations`, `/jv-shares`, `/jv-statement`). **Browser**: Inter-company Transactions (list report with sent / received, new, the transaction with its mirror, approval, acceptance or dispute, reversal; relationships), Inter-company Reconciliation, Consolidated Statements (BS, P&L, eliminations, runs, finalise), JV Partner Statement; English and Hindi.

## 6. Known limitations
- One level of subsidiaries under a parent; no step acquisitions, no goodwill above par (the investment is eliminated against the held share capital, any excess to capital reserve).
- Consolidation is in INR only (no foreign subsidiaries' translation).
- A relationship is kept by the parent's CFO; the counterpart sees it but does not approve it separately.
- The counterpart company is chosen by its number when a relationship is added (the group's company list is not offered to a single-company user).

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As the parent's CFO: Finance → Inter-company Transactions → Relationships → add the subsidiary.
2. As an accounts manager: **New inter-company transaction** → save → **Send for approval**; a second accounts manager **Approves**.
3. In the subsidiary, as its accounts manager: Received → the transaction → choose the account (and cost centre) → **Accept and post**; both companies' journals appear.
4. Inter-company Reconciliation: the pair **Matched**. Consolidated Statements: eliminations, NCI; **Save the run** → **Finalise** (CFO).
5. JV Partner Statement: a project, its partners and their shares.

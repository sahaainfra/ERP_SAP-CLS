# Part 21.04 completion: Subcontractor Workspace, Compliance, Labour Strength & Performance

Part: 21.04. Date: 2026-11-21. Module `ctr`, flag `ff.ctr.p2104` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 21.03 (work orders), 07.08 (contract labour and the labour compliance register), 07.06 (attendance), 14.05 (daily progress reports), 18.04 (NCRs), 19.04 (contractor safety scores).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items D-650 in `DEFECTS.md`.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 302, 303): `ctr_sub_compliance`, `ctr_sub_waivers` (approval-controlled), `ctr_sub_manpower`, `ctr_sub_holds`, `ctr_sub_ratings`; flag `ff.ctr.p2104`, three pages, tracker, templates.
- **Compliance**: opened per month for every live subcontractor, compiled by the coordinator (a reference is needed for an item in order), verified by a second person or returned with a note.
- **Hold and waiver**: past the due date a missing mandatory item (or a returned record) holds payment until it is fixed or an approved waiver covers it until its end date; the change is published once.
- **Strength, rating and workspace**: daily strength against the committed manpower, ratings by weights, and the workspace with compliance, strength, performance and financial summary.
- **API**: routes under `/api/v1/ctr` (subcontractors, sub-compliance, sub-waivers, sub-manpower, sub-strength, sub-ratings, sub-payment-check); **browser**: Subcontractor Workspace, Monthly Compliance Verification (checklist, waivers), Labour Strength vs Committed; English and Hindi.

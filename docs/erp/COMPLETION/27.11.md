Execution step 205 · 2026-11-22 · Branch `ccr-6934092b-qrkoec` · **In progress**: built and tested behind `ff.bi.p2711`. Formal acceptance waits for 27.04 and for the company G1/G2 sign-offs.

## 1. Discovery

The cost forecast (23.11), the schedule CPM (13.01) and the 13-week cash roll-up (23.13) are all available as pure functions over their records, so a scenario can be computed on copies without touching them. The decisions are in the [discovery note](../DISCOVERY/27.11.md).

## 2. What was built

**Drivers (FR-01)**, each validated against its range on the server and in the form:

| Driver | Effect |
|---|---|
| Material price change | Matching lines (cost code, head or name): remaining quantity × rate × % |
| Wage increase | Labour lines: labour to complete × % |
| Productivity loss | Matching (or labour) lines: ETC ÷ (1 − %) − ETC |
| Equipment breakdown | Days × cost per day; optionally lengthens an activity |
| Procurement delay | Lengthens the activity named |
| Schedule delay | Lengthens the activity named (else the last open critical activity) |
| Client payment delay | The project's collections shifted by the weeks given |
| Quantity variation | Matching lines: remaining quantity × rate × % |
| Scope change | Extra cost, extra revenue and days |
| Cash constraint | The lowest cash allowed; the weeks below it are listed |

**Impact on copies (FR-02, BR-01)**:

| Figure | How it is computed |
|---|---|
| EAC and margin | From the latest approved cost forecast, with each line's estimate to complete worked out the way 23.11 does (`etcOf`). Margin = contract revenue (plus any scope revenue) − EAC. |
| Completion date | The current schedule's CPM recalculated in memory with the delays added. The working days lost × the site overhead per day go into the cost. |
| Lowest cash and closing | The approved 13-week cash forecast through its own weekly roll-up, with collections shifted and the extra cost spread over the weeks. |

Nothing live is written.

**Page** `/bi/scenarios` (**Reports → Scenario Builder**):

- **Builder**: a wizard with numbered step pills (Scope → Drivers → Results).
  - Inputs follow the phone rules (16 px, `inputmode`). Validation is inline and mirrors the server, and the impact updates live as you type.
  - A sticky footer keeps the live totals (EAC, margin, completion) with Save draft / Save scenario / Cancel (with confirmation). On a phone, the main action is full width above the bottom bar.
  - Results show the cost by forecast line (base, change, scenario), what each driver did and why, and the base against scenario cash chart.
- **Saved scenarios**: the scenario record with its impact tiles, edit, share, and turning it into a task or a forecast assumption. Ticking two or more scenarios and pressing Compare opens the comparison.
- **Compare** (Scenario Comparison report): the EAC change chart and a side-by-side table (base and each scenario: EAC, margin, completion, lowest cash, drivers), exported to XLSX, CSV and PDF.

Labels are en/hi/te (i18n area `scn`).

**APIs** ([OpenAPI](../bi-openapi.yaml)): drivers, preview, list, create, get, update (rowVersion, 409), delete, share, actions, compare, compare export.

**Permissions**: `bi.scenario.{view,create,edit,delete,share,convert,export}` for DIR, CFO, PD and PCMGR, scoped by project. Only the author edits, shares or deletes a scenario. A draft is private until shared or saved.

## 4. Tests

`tests/bi-scenarios.test.js` has three tests, all passing:

1. **T-01 and BR-01**:
   - Steel +10 % changes the EAC by exactly the remaining quantity × 10 % × rate.
   - A full scenario adds +8 % wages on labour, a 5-day delay on A20 that moves completion (the overhead of the working days lost goes into the cost), collections 2 weeks late (the lowest cash falls) and a floor (the weeks below it are listed).
   - Validation gives 400 and a stale version gives 409; an update recomputes.
   - The live-data fingerprint is unchanged throughout.
2. **Compare, share, convert**:
   - Compare, with the base and the delayed scenario only, and its export.
   - A private draft is hidden until shared (an unknown user gets 400), and only the author edits it.
   - A task and a forecast assumption are created (task and event; the forecast is untouched).
   - Roles, project scope and the flag off.
3. **Volume**: 100,000 scenarios; the list stays fast and the filter uses an index.

`e2e/bi-scenarios.spec.js` passes on desktop and mobile:

- builds a scenario through the wizard and checks the live EAC and the completion of a six-day-week schedule (10 working days = 12 days);
- saves it, compares two scenarios, and checks the live forecast is unchanged.

Regression:

- `authz-api` and `i18n-api` (namespace `scn`);
- lint, typecheck (0 errors), route, nav, schema lint, migration up/down, schema-diff and the permission audit.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set `ff.bi.p2701` to `ff.bi.p2703` and `ff.bi.p2711` and record G1 and G2. You need a project with an approved cost forecast, a schedule and an approved cash forecast.
2. As a CFO, open **Reports → Scenario Builder**.
   - **Scope**: name the scenario and choose the project.
   - **Drivers**: Material price change, lines matching "steel", 10 %. The sticky EAC shows +(remaining steel × rate × 10 %).
3. Add **Schedule delay** with an activity code and days. The completion moves by the working days on the schedule's calendar.
4. On **Results**, check the line table and the cash chart, then **Save scenario**.
5. On the saved scenario:
   - **Share** it with a director, who is notified;
   - turn it into a **forecast assumption** for the project controls manager (the task appears in their inbox).
6. Make a second scenario. In **Saved scenarios**, tick both and press **Compare**, then export XLSX.
7. Open the cost forecast, the schedule and the cash forecast: nothing changed.

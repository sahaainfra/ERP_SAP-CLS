## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 222, 223; up, down and up again clean): `rti_observations` (append-only: triggers refuse update and delete), `rti_indices`; flag and navigation.
- **Rate history** (FR-01, FR-02, BR-01): approved quotations, awarded comparative statement lines, approved purchase orders and approved rate contracts enter by themselves (on their events and every night), once each, with the source and its id; market surveys are entered by the procurement manager. Each rate is stored as recorded and as landed rate per base unit (unit conversion, freight included, GST left out). A rate far from the median of earlier observations (company parameter, once enough exist) is marked an outlier, kept, and left out of figures. A wrong observation is corrected by a new one that replaces it and names the reason.
- **Indices** (FR-01, FR-03): cement, steel, bitumen, fuel or any group by region and month, entered or uploaded as lines; a new value for the same month replaces the old one and the audit keeps the old one.
- **Benchmark** (FR-04): the median landed rate of the window (180 days), outliers and replaced observations left out; falls back to all regions when a region has too few. The comparative statement shows it as the market rate and the purchase order uses it for the variance check once five observations exist, else keeps the last purchase rate.
- **Trend and volatility** (FR-03): by month the count, lowest, average, highest, median and volatility, with the index of the item's category; dashboard of the items with most observations and their change against last month.
- **Project exposure** (FR-05): quantity still to buy (net requirement of the latest completed MRP run) times the benchmark less the budgeted rate, per item, with totals; CSV export.
- **API**: 13 routes under `/api/v1/proc` (2,679 routes in all). **Browser**: Rate Intelligence (dashboard with trend, observation object with correction), Project Rate Exposure (report), Market Survey and Index Upload (form).

# Part 17.07 completion: Fleet Control Tower, Owning & Operating Cost and Telematics

Part: 17.07. Date: 2026-11-14. Module `plant`, flag `ff.plant.p1707` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 17.04, 17.05, 17.06. Provides cost rates (`plant.cost.rate_published`) for internal hire (17.06) and project cost (14.07), and `plant.equipment.disposal_recommended` for fixed assets (23.09).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Full regression is run with 17.08 (see its report).

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 258, 259): `plt_cost_items`, `plt_cost_snapshots`, `plt_telematics`, `plt_disposal_recs`; flag, three pages, tracker, templates. No existing table changed.
- **Cost** (FR-01): owning (cost items, or straight-line depreciation) and operating (fuel, maintenance, hire, operator) per machine and class, cost an hour and a kilometre, monthly snapshots that publish the rates; CSV.
- **KPIs and tower** (FR-02): availability, utilisation, MTBF, MTTR, fuel an hour, cost an hour, idle cost, ranking charts, position plot, alerts, candidates; costs masked without `plant.fleet.costs`.
- **Telematics** (FR-03, BR-01): ingest with duplicate protection, positions, meter suggestion, alerts for engine hours that differ from the log and for ignition on a day with no log; logs are never changed.
- **Replacement** (FR-04): candidates by age, repairs against purchase value and low use; disposal recommendation raised by the project manager and approved by the plant manager, the CFO and the director.
- **API**: 25 routes; **browser**: Fleet Control Tower, Owning and Operating Cost, Replacement Candidates; English and Hindi.

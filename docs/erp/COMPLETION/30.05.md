## 2. What was built

**Security Findings & Remediation** (`/admin/security-findings`) is restricted and never cached.

- **Header:** the go-live verdict (BR-01): *blocked by n critical and n high* or *clear*.
- **Chart first:** findings by severity, open against closed.
- **Key figures:**
  - critical open and high open;
  - endpoints with authorisation tests: **100 %** (5,136 routes, 231,120 cases);
  - controls reviewed, with their gaps.
- **Cards:**
  - **What blocks go-live**;
  - **Latest scans**;
  - the **findings** list (severity, status, source and text; cards on a phone; bottom sheet; sticky total);
  - the **DPDP checklist and sensitive paths**, with a review form.
- **Finding panel:**
  - fix with the commit;
  - the independent re-test (pass closes, fail reopens);
  - risk acceptance or false positive sent to the director, who approves or refuses there;
  - the finding's history.
- **"…" menu:** run the AUTHZ, SAST or SECRETS scan; export (marked restricted, audited).

**FR-01 Authorisation tests.**

- `authzMatrix()` gives, for every route, the expected answer of each of the 44 roles and of nobody.
- `tests/sct-authz-matrix.test.js`:
  - checks that every **live** endpoint (from the route registry) is in the matrix: **100 %**;
  - asks the Authorise stage in the process for every route × role: **225,984 cases**;
  - over HTTP, checks 401 for nobody on every non-public route, 403 for a role without the key on every permission route, and lets a holder through on every permission read.
- The HTTP holder cases found **three 500s**, fixed here (D-808):
  - `/proc/analytics/spend/detail` without a key;
  - `/inv/grns/prefill` without a source;
  - `/bill/retention/entries` without a contract.
- The AUTHZ scan raises policy findings:
  - a public route that writes (HIGH);
  - one that takes a link token (INFO, listed for the pen test: 17);
  - a key no role holds;
  - an unknown key;
  - a write route not marked audited;
  - a failing route self-test.
- No High finding comes from the application's own policy.

**FR-02 DAST, dependencies, pen test, mobile.**

- `scripts/dast_scan.mjs` runs against a running installation, or starts the application itself. It checks:
  - headers, CORS from a foreign origin, error leakage and the body limit;
  - five injection payloads on **every guarded list (1,087)**;
  - path traversal;
  - a sample of signed-out routes.
  - Result: **5,648 requests, 0 findings**. It is a CI job (`dast`) that fails on a Critical or High finding.
- `scripts/dependency_audit.mjs` (npm audit): **387 production dependencies, 0 advisories**.
- SAST rules: eval, shell commands from templates, SQL from request input, TLS off, weak random for tokens, HTML sinks, stack traces in answers, CORS to every origin, weak password hashes.
  - On the application: **9 findings, all MEDIUM HTML sinks**. They are QR-code SVGs built from geometry only, so they are triaged as false positives through the director.
- The SECRETS scan reuses the 02.07 scanner: **0 secrets**.
- The tester records PENTEST and MOBILE findings.
- Scans deduplicate by fingerprint. A closed finding seen again is reopened as a regression.

**FR-03 Sensitive paths and FR-04 DPDP.**

- Sixteen controls, each reviewed PASS, GAP or NA with evidence:
  - DPDP: notice and consent, purpose, minimisation, accuracy, retention and erasure, safeguards, breach notification, rights, processors, children and transfer;
  - paths: upload, export, AI tools, webhooks, sockets, PWA.
- A gap raises a REVIEW finding with its severity.

**FR-05 Remediation.**

- The flow is fixed → independent re-test → closed. The person who fixed a finding cannot re-test it (403).
- A risk acceptance (with an end date; never for a Critical) or a false positive goes to a director through the approval engine (`sct_risk_acceptance`). The requester never decides, and a refusal reopens the finding.
- A Critical or High raised by a scan or a tester notifies the security leads (in-app and e-mail).

## 7. How to verify manually

1. Set `ff.qa.p3005`. As ITADM, open **Security Findings**.
2. From "…", run the **AUTHZ** scan (100 %), the **SAST** scan and the **SECRETS** scan.
3. Run `node scripts/dast_scan.mjs --base http://localhost:3000 --post`.
4. As AUD, record a HIGH penetration-test finding: the verdict reads *blocked*.
5. As ITADM, mark it fixed with a commit. Then as AUD, re-test it as passed: the verdict reads *clear*.

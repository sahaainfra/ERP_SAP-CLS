## 7. How to verify manually
1. As a BD executive open **Tender Register**, **New tender**: choose the authority from the partner master, give the reference, dates (pre-bid, submission, opening), cost, EMD and fee; send it. A colleague of the estimation team approves the registration.
2. As the estimation manager open the tender: enter A (highest yearly value of works) and B (works in hand): the bid capacity is A × N × 2 − B. On **Eligibility** the figures decide pass or fail; the others are marked, or waived with a reason.
3. **Bid / no-bid evaluation**: score the five criteria; the total and the recommendation show at once; send it: a colleague of the estimation team and then the director approve.
4. **Addenda**: record a corrigendum that moves the submission (the log keeps the change; the addendum must be acknowledged). **Queries**: raise, send, record the clarification. **Team tasks**: give a task (it is a task of the person). **Documents**: register the notice inviting tender.
5. **Mark ready for submission**: refused until the decision is approved, eligibility is complete and the addenda are read; then `bd.tender.ready` goes to estimation and submission.
6. **Tender Calendar** and **Tender Dashboard** show the dates and the bid ratio; the daily job reminds the owner and the team before each date. Switch to Hindi.

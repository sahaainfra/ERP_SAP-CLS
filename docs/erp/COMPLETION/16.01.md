## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 225, 226; up, down and up again clean): `inv_stores`, `inv_store_changes`, `inv_bins`, `inv_stock_ledger` (append-only), `inv_balances`, `inv_valuation`, `inv_fifo_layers`, `inv_reservations`, `inv_adjustments`; flag, seven pages under the Stores group, notification templates.
- **Stock engine** (`stock.js`): one posting path for every movement. Quantities in thousandths, rates and values in paise. Moving weighted average (the whole value goes with the last unit) or FIFO layers per store; batch and serial tracking; owner project or company pool; unit conversion from the item's own units; a balance never goes below zero unless the store allows it (BR-01), reserved and under-inspection stock is not issued; an issue without a bin takes from the oldest receipts across bins and batches.
- **Ledger and corrections** (BR-02): entries are immutable (triggers); a correction is a reversing entry that names the entry; a receipt that was partly used under FIFO cannot be reversed.
- **Stores and bins** (FR-01, FR-02): a store is a draft, sent for approval and approved by a store manager other than the maker; name, address and keepers change directly (audited), but the type, valuation method, negative stock, project and site change only by a change request approved by the store manager and the accounts manager. A store holding stock cannot be closed.
- **Movements** (FR-03, FR-07): receipt, issue, return, scrap and transfer posted by hand with a reason until the receiving and issuing documents of 16.02 to 16.06 post them; a transfer keeps the value it left with, can change the owner project (then a financial transfer event is published); stock can be held for inspection and released.
- **Valuation and adjustments** (FR-05): the valuation report by store, project and item category; quantity adjustments and revaluations (a corrected rate after a GRN price correction) are approved before they post, the accounts manager too for a revaluation or ₹ 1 lakh and above.
- **Reservations** (FR-06) against work authorisations, material requests and transfers; opening stock import (FR-08).
- **Dashboard and checks** (BR-03): stock value by store, items below reorder level, age of stock, approvals waiting, latest movements and the state of the books; nightly job `inv.integrity_check` tells the store and accounts managers of a difference.
- **API**: 37 routes under `/api/v1/inv` (2,723 routes in all). **Browser**: Stores Dashboard, Stores and Bins (list, object, form), Stock Balances with reservations, Stock Ledger (item card), Stock Valuation, Stock Adjustments (list, object, form), Stock Posting and Opening Stock.

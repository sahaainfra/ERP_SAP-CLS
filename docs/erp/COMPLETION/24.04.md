## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 374, 375): `stat_filings` (a statutory payment of a period, approval-controlled, workflow `PAY_STAT_FILING`), `stat_registers` (a generated file or register with its content and checksum), `stat_rules` (due dates and numbers as effective-dated master data with the legal reference and the status APPROVED or TO_CONFIRM), `stat_bocw_months` (BOCW cess per project and month); flag, two pages, page-tracker rows, templates, the seeded rules. The run keeps the PF and ESI wage bases on each line (`wages_json`: `pfWage`, `esiWage`, `pfBase`), in the existing column.
- **Files and reports** (FR-01, FR-03, FR-04; ADR-733, ADR-736, ADR-739): the ECR (EPFO, eleven `#` fields, whole rupees) and the ESI contribution file, each validated before it is kept (a bad record stops the file and names the employee); the PT and LWF state reports; the quarterly TDS statement (per month, with the regime); the Form 16 / Form 130 batch (per employee: salary of the year, standard deduction of the regime, tax deducted; PDF and CSV); the labour registers per site from the approved wage runs (workmen, overtime, deductions, advances).
- **Payments** (FR-02; ADR-734): a statutory payment prepared by payroll for a locked period (ECR, ESI, PT, LWF, TDS deposit, BOCW cess) with its amount tied to the file or the payroll; sent for approval; approved ACCMGR → CFO (the preparer never approves); the approval releases `pay.statutory.filing_approved`; the challan is recorded (number, challan date, date of payment, bank reference) and a payment after the due date is flagged late; a rule that is TO_CONFIRM blocks the payment (422).
- **Calendar** (FR-02; ADR-735): the year's items from the rules — each month's ECR, ESI, PT and LWF per state, TDS deposit (March on 30 April), the four quarterly statements, the Form 16 / 130 batch, the BOCW cess — with due date, overdue and the days left, and the column of the board: to file, in approval, approved, paid.
- **Bonus and gratuity** (FR-05; ADR-737): the bonus of the year (eligibility on the monthly wages, base capped at ₹7,000 a month, the rate between the statutory minimum and maximum); gratuity on leaving (15 / 26 of the last basic and DA for each completed year from five years, capped at the rule's ₹20 lakh); the gratuity provision of the year from the GRATUITY component of the locked runs.
- **BOCW cess** (FR-06; ADR-738): the construction cost of a project for a month, with its source, at the rate of the rule (1 %); the cess of the company's projects for the month filed as one payment; each project's cess marked due and then paid.
- **Rules** (ADR-735): the statutory rules listed with their legal reference and status; a new rule or a confirmation is saved by the CFO (a legal reference of at least ten characters).
- **API**: 20 operations (OpenAPI `docs/erp/openapi.yaml`, part 24.04). **Browser**: Statutory Calendar & Filings (board with the columns, files and reports, BOCW cess, rules) and Labour Registers, Form 16 / 130, Bonus and Gratuity; English and Hindi.
- **Defects**: D-349 partly closed (workmen, overtime, deductions and advances registers from the wage runs; Form XII and the site approver remain, target 24.05); D-747 to D-752 raised (see section 6).

## 6. Known limitations
- D-747: the bank payment of a statutory payment is not made by 24.04; the approved payment request is released as an event and paid through 24.05 (23.04 pays bills only).
- D-748: professional tax, labour welfare fund, BOCW cess and the Form 130 layout are TO_CONFIRM; their payments are refused until each rule is checked against the notification, state by state.
- D-749: the 24Q statement and the Form 16 / 130 batch carry the last four digits of the PAN; the full PAN is kept encrypted and is not yet read into the files.
- D-750: the ECR, ESI and Form 138 layouts are checked against the record layouts in the decisions; the portal template versions are to be confirmed before the first upload.
- D-751: files are kept in the register row (with checksum), not in the document service.
- D-752: each state's rules must be added before the company employs staff there (the default is TO_CONFIRM).
- D-349 (remaining): Form XII (contractor register), the site-level approver of a worker, the licence count by site and the age check on import: 24.05.
- The gratuity is computed on the last basic and DA as the Act defines wages for a single leaver; the provision uses the GRATUITY component of the structure.
- The bonus eligibility uses each month's wages against the ceiling; where a month is not in the payroll the employee is not counted as ineligible for that month.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. Payroll → Statutory Calendar & Filings: the financial year; the **Calendar** tab shows the month's ECR with its due date.
2. Under the item, **Prepare the payment** (payroll). Open **Files & reports** → ECR for the month → **Generate**; the file is kept with its rows and checksum; download it.
3. Back on the calendar, the item is in **To file**: **Send for approval**; as the accounts manager, then the CFO, approve it from the approvals inbox.
4. In **Approved**, **Record the challan** with its number and date; the item moves to **Paid**, flagged late if paid after the due date.
5. Calendar → **BOCW cess**: record a project's construction cost for the month with its source; the cess is worked out at 1 %.
6. **Rules**: the statutory rules with their legal references; those to be confirmed are marked.
7. Payroll → Labour Registers, Form 16 & Bonus / Gratuity: the labour registers per site (after 24.03 wage runs), the Form 16 / 130 batch, the bonus and gratuity for the year.

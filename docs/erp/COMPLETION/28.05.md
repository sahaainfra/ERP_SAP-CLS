# Part 28.05 — Computer Vision Decision Support

Execution step 210 · 2026-11-22 · Branch `ccr-6934092b-qrkoec` · **In progress**: built and tested behind `ff.ai.p2805`. Formal acceptance waits for 28.01, 14.02 and the company G1/G2 sign-offs. **No trained vision model is bundled** (D-788). Out of the box only the colour detector runs (high-visibility vests). Helmets, people and faces, and defects need a connected model endpoint; the tests use a local one.

## 1. Discovery

Photographs, the hub and the target services were in place. There was no vision model or analysis. See the [discovery note](../DISCOVERY/28.05.md).

## 2. What was built

**Analysis (FR-01)** — `src/server/modules/ai/vision.js`:

- **Trigger**: when the virus scan clears a photograph (JPEG or PNG) attached to a DPR, a quality inspection or request, or a safety observation, a job analyses it. **Run** analyses an older photograph on request.
- **Detectors** (`ai.cv.detector`):
  - **Built-in colour detector**: fluorescent yellow-green and orange regions as high-visibility vests, with boxes. It is a colour cue, never above 75 % sure.
  - **Model endpoint**: an AI connector of the new hub provider `vision_http`. It is sent the image and gets back `{ detections: [{ label, confidence, box }] }`. The hub handles its key, rate limit, breaker and log.
- **Labels** come from an allow-list: person, face, helmet, vest, no helmet, no vest, crack, honeycombing, exposed reinforcement, slab cast, formwork, reinforcement placed, material. Anything else, including identities, is dropped (BR-01). Boxes and confidences are clamped.

**Privacy (FR-03, BR-01)**:

- Faces, and the head third of each person, are blurred into a copy of the photograph on the same record (on by default).
- The worklist always shows the copy, which is also the evidence attached to an observation or NCR. The reviewer can blur more boxes by hand.
- Nobody is identified: observations name no person (crew responsibility), and nothing goes to HR, penalties or attendance.

**Review (FR-02, FR-04)** — page `/ai/vision` (**My Work → Vision Suggestions**):

- **Worklist**: oldest first with an SLA chip (24 h), tiles by status.
- **Photograph**: the image with each suggestion's box (green PPE, red defect, amber progress or material) beside the suggestions. Each shows its confidence and an action:

| Suggestion | Confirm creates (with the reviewer's rights) |
|---|---|
| No helmet, no vest | A safety observation, type UNSAFE_CONDITION, severity MEDIUM, naming nobody (19.04) |
| Crack, honeycombing, exposed reinforcement | An NCR, type WORKMANSHIP, severity MINOR. Its origin is the inspection for an inspection photograph, otherwise "OTHER" with the vision reference (18.04). |
| Slab cast, formwork, reinforcement placed, material | A confirmed note |

- **Wrong suggestions**: "Not right" takes a reason.
- **Model performance**: for each model and finding, the suggestions, confirmed, wrong, precision and average confidence.
- On the phone, the actions are 48 px and full width.

Labels are en/hi/te (area `cv`).

**Permissions**: `ai.vision.view`, `review` and `run` for HSEE, QCE and PM; AUD reads. HSEE and QCE also read DPRs, within their assignments, to see the photographs. The person must be able to open the photograph's record. The observation or NCR is created only with the reviewer's own right in that module (a PM cannot raise an NCR).

## 4. Tests

`tests/ai-vision.test.js` has three tests, all passing:

1. **Detector and filtering**:
   - the colour detector finds the vest within 3 % of its place;
   - blurring flattens the chosen region only;
   - the allow-list drops an identity, and boxes and confidences are clamped.
2. **A DPR photograph with the connected model** (a local endpoint):
   - analysed on upload, with the identity label dropped;
   - the face is blurred into a copy that the worklist shows;
   - the roles;
   - the HSE engineer's observation (UNSAFE_CONDITION, nobody named, the blurred copy attached);
   - the quality engineer's NCR, a false positive, precision per finding, and blurring more.
3. **Built-in detector and volume**:
   - a vest worn gives "nothing to act on" and no copy;
   - 100,000 results list in under 500 ms using an index.

`e2e/ai-vision.spec.js` passes on desktop and mobile:

1. A site engineer uploads a DPR photograph; a local model endpoint answers, and the running server analyses it.
2. The HSE engineer sees the blurred photograph with its boxes.
3. They confirm the missing helmet into an observation and mark the slab cast wrong.

Regression: `i18n-api` (namespace `cv`), lint, typecheck (0 errors), route, nav, schema lint, migration up/down, schema-diff and the permission audit.

## 6. Known limitations

D-788:

- No trained model is bundled; the colour detector sees vests only.
- Faces are blurred only where a model finds them.
- Images are not sent to the Anthropic provider yet.
- Video frames are not sampled.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set `ff.ai.p2805` and record G1 and G2.
2. In **Administration → Connectors**, add an AI connector of provider **Vision model endpoint** with the model server's URL. Set the company parameter `ai.cv.detector` to its code; without one, the colour detector runs.
3. As a site engineer, attach a photograph to a daily progress report.
4. As the HSE engineer, open **My Work → Vision Suggestions**. The photograph shows blurred with its boxes; confirm a missing helmet into an observation, or mark a suggestion **Not right**.
5. The **Model performance** tab shows the precision.

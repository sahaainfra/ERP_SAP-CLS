# Part 04.06 completion: Notification Engine, Notification Centre and Real-Time Socket Layer

Status: **Ready for review, with the open items below** (trying the Redis adapter, conversation rooms, notifications of the modules from M07 onward, settings export of templates, and the translation of the pages are later parts: D-207 to D-211). Feature flag `ff.eng.p0406` (OFF by default; ON in development and test).

## What was found
No notification tables existed. The workflow engine already said who should be told what (`workflow.notification.requested`) with nobody to deliver it; the room guard of part 02.07 waited for a real-time server that was never written (the prompt says "reuse", but there was no Socket.IO server), so the server is new. The bell counted the requests waiting. The integration hub (e-mail, text, WhatsApp), the push sender, the screen language and the audit trail were reused.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 66 and 67; nothing existing altered): `ntf_templates`, `ntf_notifications`, `ntf_preferences`, `ntf_deliveries`, `ntf_realtime_events`; reference seed: the flag, 15 shared templates in English and Hindi for five channels, four navigation entries and tracker rows.
- **`notify()`** (`platform/notify`, ADR-181): people by `user:`, `role:` (holders for the project) or `permission:`; each person's language; facts marked sensitive are shown only to people with the permission, and a fact named like a rate, cost, salary or bank number is never shown (BR-01); one notification per person and source; the centre's notification plus one delivery per channel the person chose. Security and statutory notices always reach the centre and the e-mail.
- **Quiet hours and digest** (ADR-182): non-urgent messages on e-mail, text, WhatsApp and push are held by quiet hours (India time) or by an hourly or daily choice, and leave as one digest message per person and channel; the centre is told at once. A failed send is tried again after 1, 5, 30 and 120 minutes, then marked failed; a channel nobody set up is skipped with the reason; an administrator sends it again from the log.
- **Sockets** (`platform/realtime`, ADR-183): Socket.IO on the application's HTTP server; the same sign-in as a request; `user:` rooms for each person; `project:`, `site:`, `dept:` (through the guard of 02.07) and `record:<entity>:<id>` rooms after a permission check, each refusal a security event; every event checked again for each member; events carry ids only (BR-02); a join with the last event number replays what was missed; events are kept 24 hours and a screen that was away longer is told to load afresh. The Redis adapter loads when `REDIS_URL` is set.
- **The engine's events become notifications**: tasks assigned, reminders, escalations, returns, questions and answers, approvals and rejections, and the escalation of an emergency exception, each with a link to the task or the document.
- **API** (`/api/v1/ntf`): notifications (list with filters, counts, read, unread, archive, read-all, a test to oneself), preferences (with a version), templates (list, one, preview, a change), deliveries (list, summary, retry, export).
- **Pages**: the bell with its drawer, Notification Centre (`/notifications`), My Notification Preferences (`/notifications/preferences`), Notification Templates (`/admin/notification-templates`, with a preview in either language and a list of the facts a template may carry), Delivery Log (`/admin/notification-deliveries`, with a chart, filters, export and retry). The sample documents, the approval inbox, the workflow instance and the centre follow their records and refresh the affected data when something in their rooms changes (`useLiveRefresh`).
- **Permissions**: `ntf.template.view` and `ntf.template.edit` (SYSADM; ITADM reads), `ntf.delivery.view` (ITADM, AUD), `ntf.delivery.retry` (ITADM); own notifications and preferences need none. Route policy, OpenAPI, ARCHITECTURE section 40, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, the schema lock, DECISIONS (ADR-181 to 183), DEFECTS (D-207 to D-211), PROGRESS.csv (row 34).

## Click-path for a reviewer
1. As a project director send a **Sample Document** into approval; as the project manager reject it. With the director's **Notifications** page open (no reload): the bell shows 1 within seconds, the list says "Rejected: Sample document WSD-…" with the reason. Click the bell: the drawer lists it; click it: the document's approval opens and the bell clears.
2. In the centre: **Mark unread**, the **Unread** tab, **Mark all as read**, **Archive**, the **Archived** tab.
3. **My Notification Preferences**: Security and Statutory are fixed on; turn off Approvals by e-mail, set quiet hours and an hourly digest, **Save**, reload (kept), **Send me a test** (it arrives in the centre).
4. As the system administrator open **Notification Templates**, open `workflow.task.assigned`: the preview in English and in Hindi; change the text (a fact the template does not carry is named in red); **Send for approval**. As the IT administrator approve it in **My Approvals**: the new version is in force.
5. As the IT administrator open **Delivery Log**: what left on which channel, what was held for a digest, what failed and why; **Send again** on a failed one; **Export XLSX**.
6. As a person without the permission: the templates and the delivery log say so.

## Tests and acceptance
- **T-01** the handshake is authenticated; a project room is refused to a person without scope (a security event is written); a personal room is for one person; an event carries ids only; the record room is refused to a stranger. **T-02** after a disconnect a join with the last number replays exactly what was missed in that room (and nothing from other rooms); a person who may not be in the room gets nothing; after the log was purged the join says `gap`. **T-03** quiet hours hold ordinary messages (the centre still gets them), an urgent one and a mandatory one are not held, the digest comes at 07:30 India time as one message of the held ones, an hourly choice holds to the next hour, a failing channel is retried with back-off then marked failed, shown in the log and sent again by an administrator (`tests/notifications.test.js`).
- Also: the workflow events become notifications with links, in Hindi for a person who chose it, once each; read, unread, archive, read-all, someone else's notification 404; preferences (mandatory kinds refused, stale version 409, bad quiet hours 400); masked facts per recipient; templates (preview in both languages, an undeclared fact refused, a change approved by the IT administrator, in force for the company only, audited). Browser (`e2e/notifications.spec.js`, 4 runs): the live delivery to an open screen and the centre on desktop and phone; preferences, the templates and their approval, and the delivery log on desktop.
- **AC-01** workflow events produce notifications with deep links in real time, preferences respected. **AC-02** the template editor with English and Hindi preview. **AC-03** page checks. **AC-04** permission tests (the permissions above, 404 for another person's notification, 403 for the log and the templates). **AC-05** a template change is a maker-checker document and an audit entry; preferences are audited. **AC-06** see Results. **AC-07** this report.

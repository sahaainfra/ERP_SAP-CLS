## 2. What was built

**Why? (FR-01, FR-03, BR-01)**: `GET /bi/explain/why?projectId&kpi&period`. Every answer is deterministic and labelled with its method. Its components plus an explicit residual equal the total.

| KPI | Total | Components |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Earned − actual (14.07 facts) | Material, subcontract, transport, other: quantity and rate. Labour and plant: productivity and rate. Scope / variations: cost lines with no budget. Delay-related: idle and breakdown plant hours of approved DPRs × the budget plant rate. |
| Schedule | Earned − planned | Contribution of each activity: the ten largest, then the rest summed. The residual is value not tied to a scheduled activity. |
| Margin | Change between the previous and current approved CVR | Each value line (adds) and cost line (reduces). The residual shows a margin that does not match its lines. |

There is no AI narrative (`narrative: null`, D-779). Exports in XLSX, CSV and PDF carry the residual and total rows and are audited.

**Drill-down (FR-02)**: `GET /bi/explain/drill?level&id`. The levels are Company → Project → Site / WBS / Resource → Activity → BOQ → Transaction → Document → Audit.

- Each level reads its owner: the 14.07 facts and ledger, plan activities and their BOQ links, approved DPRs, measurement-book entries, and the 04.14 trace graph and timeline.
- The server builds the breadcrumb, so a record outside the person's scope is never named; it shows as "Restricted record".

**Page** `/bi/explain` (**Reports → Variance Explainer**):

- **Why?** tab: a project picker (the projects in scope), an optional month and Cost / Schedule / Margin pills. Tiles show the total, the explained part and the residual. A horizontal waterfall (a phone shows the label over the bar) is followed by the contributors table, with residual and total rows and a "Records" link on every component.
- **Drill-down** tab: the breadcrumb and the level's children, with their figures, the "Why?" links and links to each record.
- Labels are en/hi/te (i18n area `why`).

**Reusable components**:

- `DrillBreadcrumb` and `drillHref`;
- `WhyLinks`, shown in the control tower's project drill and the Project 360 header (AC-01);
- the `bi.why_cost` widget, on the dashboard and the Project 360 cost tab.

**Permissions**: `bi.explain.view` and `bi.explain.export`, scoped by project, for PM, PD, DIR, CFO, PCMGR, QS, CM, ACCMGR, COMMGR and PLAN. The owning module still decides each figure: a planner without `site.control.view` gets 403, "costs are hidden for your role".

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set `ff.bi.p2701` to `ff.bi.p2703` and `ff.bi.p2708` and record the G1 and G2 gates.
2. As a PM or DIR, open **Reports → Variance Explainer**, then choose a project with control facts.
3. Check that the contributors plus the residual equal the total (the total row says so), and open a component's **Records**.
4. Switch to **Margin** to see the CVR bridge.
5. Open **Drill-down**, go Project → WBS → a transaction → its document → its audit trail, and climb back with the breadcrumb.
6. On the control tower's project drill or in Project 360, use the **Why?** links.

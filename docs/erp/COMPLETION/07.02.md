- **Workflows** with matrix defaults: `HR_REQUISITION` (project or department head, HR manager, director beyond the plan), `HR_OFFER` (HR manager, director above the requisition range or for a senior grade).

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As a hiring manager open **New Requisition**; choose department and designation and see the plan banner; send for approval. The project head, then an HR manager approve.
2. As HR open **Candidate Pipeline**, add a candidate, move it to Screened; schedule an interview on the candidate; the panel members give feedback; move to Selected.
3. **Make an offer**: the breakup and "who approves" show as the CTC is typed; send for approval; approve; **Send the offer** shows the link once.
4. Open the link without signing in: accept, then give the joining details. As HR set verification Clear and medical Fit and press **Convert to employee**.
5. Approve the employee as another HR manager; **Onboarding Checklist** shows each department its own tasks; IT creates the user account from the employee.

Part: 21.02. Date: 2026-11-21. Module `ctr`, flag `ff.ctr.p2102` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 21.01 (contract terms, `contracts.termsOf`), 04.10 (documents), 22.07 (advance recovery, future).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items D-648 in `DEFECTS.md`.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 298, 299): `ctr_bank_guarantees`, `ctr_bg_events`, `ctr_advances`, `ctr_insurances`, `ctr_insurance_claims`, `ctr_bank_limits`; flag `ff.ctr.p2102`, three pages, tracker, templates.
- **Guarantees** checked before sending (dates, claim period after expiry, bank limit); approved by accounts and the CFO; later changes only by requests that another person confirms; commission recalculated on extension; events for billing and treasury.
- **Insurance** with claims (period and sum insured checked, settlement above the claim refused) and renewals that take over on approval.
- **Alerts** once per offset per record; lapsed guarantees (past the claim period) and policies are closed by the daily job.
- **API**: routes under `/api/v1/ctr` (bank-guarantees, bg-requests, insurances, insurance-claims, bank-limits, securities); **browser**: Bank Guarantee Register with object view, Insurance Policies, Securities Exposure with bank limits; English and Hindi.

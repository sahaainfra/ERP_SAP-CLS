## 5. Visual baseline
Every page changed (the shared PageHeader, the KPI tiles and `ui-v3.css` reach all of them), so the whole baseline of 03.10 was regenerated
with `npx playwright test e2e/a0-visual.spec.js --project=desktop --update-snapshots`: **5,688 images, 632 pages × 3 themes (light, dark, high
contrast) × 3 widths (360, 768, 1280)**, none missing and none left over from a page that no longer exists. The run is about 3 hours on this
machine: the first run stopped at the background time limit after light, dark and high contrast to page 270; the rest of high contrast was run on
its own (pages 270–632, 26 min, passed). The spec's time limit was raised to 2 hours a theme (`8e909db`).

Reviewed by eye, at least one page per module group and theme: the Page Patterns gallery (high contrast, 360), Users (dark, 1280), client
bills (light, 360), the HSE dashboard (light, 1280), stock ageing (dark, 1280), access review and drafts (all three themes). The header card, the
tiles, the cards and the phone layout are as intended in the three themes; nothing is cut off or overlaps.

What the baseline does **not** show (D-672): it opens every page as the super administrator, who holds only the platform permissions, so 504 of
the 632 pages are photographed in their no-access state; their own layout is checked by the crawler instead (`e2e/ui-v3.spec.js`, which from
this part opens every page with all internal roles, section 4).

# Part 166.7 completion: List Report and Object Page consistency, saved views and document flow

Part: 166.7. Date: 2026-11-17. Module M03 (all modules). Feature flag `ff.ui.p16607` (OFF by default; ON in development and test). Runs after 166.6, before 167.

Status: STATUS_PLACEHOLDER

## 1. Discovery
`docs/erp/DISCOVERY/166.7.md`: what the List Report (03.03) already has — filter bar with quick chips and an advanced builder, saved views
personal / shared by role / default, title and count, sort, column chooser, totals of money and quantity columns, server export honouring field
masks, cards on a phone (154 pages) — and what it lacked (group-by, a filter sheet on a phone); no document flow anywhere; the link columns of the
procurement, billing and subcontract chains in the existing tables. Screenshots on `main`: `docs/erp/screenshots/166.7/before-*`.

## 2. What was built
| Requirement | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FR-03 document flow | `src/server/modules/ui/docflow.js`, `GET /api/v1/ui/doc-flow?type=&id=`, `src/client/platform/object/DocumentFlow.jsx` | from any document, the chain read from the link columns: **MR → PR → RFQ → CS → PO → gate entry → GRN → material test, MR → issue, PO → vendor invoice → payment**; **client bill → certification → tax invoice → receipt**; **subcontract measurement → sub bill**. Each node: kind, number (a link to it), status, amount (₹, from paise), date; the current document marked; a document the person may not open shown as "a document you may not open" without its number; payment and receipt (23.x) listed as "not yet available", never invented. Drawn as a timeline under the header of 13 record pages: MR, PR, RFQ, CS, PO, GRN, issue, vendor invoice, client bill, certification, tax invoice, subcontract measurement, sub bill |
| FR-02 key facts in one place, "Related apps" | `TYPES` of `docflow.js` | the key facts (number, status, amount, date, party) and the page of each kind are defined once and used by the flow; "Related lists" under the flow opens the list of each kind in it. The record headers keep their own facts and get no anchor tabs (D-696) |
| FR-01 List Report | `src/client/platform/list/ListReport.jsx`, `state.js`, server `ui/lists.js` | added: the **title with the count** at the top of every list; **group-by** for the columns that make natural groups (text and status; the rows are sorted by the group on the server, each group starts with a header with its rows on the page and the totals of its money and quantity columns), kept in a saved view. Already there and kept: variants personal / shared / default, column chooser, sort, totals row, export with masks |
| FR-04 smart table on a phone | `ListReport.jsx`, `ui-v3.css` | on a phone the quick filters, the more-filters builder and the active filters are in a **sheet from the bottom** ("Filters (n)"), the search stays on the bar; cards and views as before |
| T-01 crawler | `e2e/pages.spec.js` | every page with a List Report must have its title with the count, its filter bar and its export |

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 220, 221; up, down and up again clean): `vnd_prequalifications`, `vnd_avl`, `vnd_scorecards`, `vnd_score_inputs`, `vnd_suspensions`, `vnd_actions`; flag, navigation, notification templates.
- **Prequalification** (FR-01): five weighted sections scored 0 to 100 (financials 25, experience 25, capacity 20, certifications 15, references 15), site visit record, validity; the server works out the score and the result (qualified, conditional, not qualified) from the company thresholds; approval by the procurement manager, the quality manager (technical) and, for a strategic category, the direction; amend for re-evaluation.
- **Approved vendor list** (FR-02): approval puts the vendor on the list for each category and region, conditional when the result says so, with validity; a new approval reinstates a suspended entry.
- **RFQ gate** (BR-01): an RFQ whose estimate reaches the threshold goes only to vendors with a valid entry for one of its categories; a vendor suspended for all its categories is never sent one.
- **Scorecard** (FR-03): on-time delivery and acceptance from receipts, price competitiveness from CS ranks, responsiveness from RFQ answers and speed, safety, invoice accuracy and quality inputs from quality and safety people; weights are company parameters; a measure without evidence leaves its weight out; the evidence is stored with the scorecard and the figures are worked out again from it.
- **Reviews and actions** (FR-04): review, corrective action request (due date, answer, then close) and development note.
- **Re-evaluation** (FR-05): daily job reminds procurement once per end date, works out last quarter's scorecards, and a score under the threshold suggests a suspension (draft, system-raised) that procurement sends and the procurement manager and direction approve.
- **API**: 33 routes under `/api/v1/proc` (2,666 routes in all). **Browser**: Vendor Prequalifications (list, object, form), Approved Vendor List with suspensions (list, object), Vendor Scorecards (list, vendor card with trend, evidence, inputs, actions), Vendor Ranking report.

## 2. What was built

**Reading on upload (FR-01)** — `src/server/modules/ai/docintel.js`:

1. When the virus scan clears a file (`core.file.available`), a job reads it. This applies to PDF, text and image files in companies with the flag on.
2. **Text**:
   - a PDF's text layer (pdfjs, up to 50 pages);
   - text files;
   - scanned images, through the local OCR engine when it is installed. It is loaded automatically: `tesseract.js` 6.0.1 with English data was verified offline here, reading an invoice image correctly at 89 % confidence in under a second, but was not added (D-786). Without it, the document is queued as "No text found" with the reason.
3. The text is kept with the extraction and searchable from the worklist.
4. A file uploaded before the flag was switched on is read on request (**Extract**, `ai.docintel.run`).

**Classification and duplicates (FR-03)**:

- **Type**: weighted cues give invoice, certificate, letter, contract or other, with a confidence.
- **Duplicates**: the same bytes as an earlier file are a duplicate. A SimHash within 8 of 64 bits is a near-duplicate; one changed word moves about 5 bits, while another invoice from the same vendor is about 30 away.

**Extractors (FR-02)** — each field has a confidence and its position in the text:

| Type | Fields | Accepting creates (with the reviewer's rights) |
|---|---|---|
| Vendor invoice | GSTIN (check digit verified), invoice number and date, taxable value, GST, total (cross-checked: taxable + GST = total), vendor | A vendor invoice DRAFT with source OCR (22.06). It is against the chosen purchase order, with the goods accepted and not yet invoiced at the order rate and tax, and the extracted total declared. The vendor is found by the GSTIN. 22.06's own matching then checks it. |
| Certificate | Number, issued by, issue date, valid until | The validity date of its controlled document (04.10), whose expiry reminders follow |
| Letter | Their reference, date, subject, sender, category | An inward letter of the chosen project (11.05), with the text as its body |
| Contract | Clauses (number, title, category, summary, time-bar) and a summary (counts, liquidated damages, retention) | The chosen clauses in the contract's clause library (21.01) |

**Review (FR-04, BR-01)** — page `/ai/documents` (**My Work → Extraction Review**):

- **Worklist**:
  - the oldest open work first, with an SLA chip (48 h);
  - tiles by type, filters and text search;
  - export (XLSX, CSV, PDF) without the document text.
- **Review**: the document's text with the source of each field marked, beside the fields. Each field shows its confidence and "From the document: …". The page has the target pickers, **Accept and create the draft** and **Reject** with a reason.
- **Confirmation**: a field below 85 % used as suggested must be ticked "I checked this", or acceptance is refused (422); editing a field counts as checking it. The same applies to clause categories.
- **Locking**: a stale version gives 409, and an extraction is accepted only once.
- **Accuracy**: the report from reviews shows the fields accepted unchanged, by type and field.

Labels are en/hi/te (area `di`).

**Permissions**: `ai.docintel.view`, `review` and `run` for FINEX, DOC and COMMGR; AUD reads. An extraction is visible only within the person's scope and only if they may open the file. What accepting creates is decided again by the owner module.

## 6. Known limitations

- D-786: scanned images and PDFs without text need the OCR engine. The code loads `tesseract.js` and `@tesseract.js-data/eng` as soon as they are installed.
- D-787:
  - the contract summary is computed, not written by the AI;
  - invoices are pre-filled against purchase orders only;
  - letters are inward only;
  - the worklist has no assignment and no bottom-bar badge;
  - Hindi and Telugu documents are not read.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set `ff.ai.p2804` and record G1 and G2.
2. As the document controller, upload a PDF invoice (one with text) to a document in the **document register**.
3. As the finance executive, open **My Work → Extraction Review**. The invoice is in the queue; open it.
4. Check the fields against the document. Tick "I checked this" on the vendor, choose the purchase order and **Accept and create the draft**.
5. The vendor invoice DRAFT (source OCR) opens from the link.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 372, 373): `pay_wage_runs` (approval-controlled, workflow `PAY_WAGE_RUN`), `pay_wage_lines` (days, overtime and piece quantity in thousandths; money in paise), `pay_contractor_wage_checks`; flag, three pages, page-tracker rows, templates; the working-day rules added to the dated `OT` parameter and the Code-on-Wages payment rules as a dated `MIN_WAGE` parameter (ADR-726, ADR-728).
- **Wage run** (FR-01, FR-02; ADR-725–728): a site, the departmental gang and a weekly (7 days), fortnightly (1–15, 16–end) or monthly cycle; the workers who punched at the site in the period; a day from the first IN to the last OUT less the rest (full, half, overtime in 30-minute blocks; no OUT = a flagged full day); the contract rate, never below the state minimum (07.08); overtime at twice the ordinary hourly rate; piece work from approved measurements of the project with the minimum wage of the days guaranteed; allowances, advance, canteen, other; PF with a UAN, ESI with an ESIC number; deductions above 50 % block; muster corrections with a reason. Lines keep their ids and inputs across recomputation; the run is computed again at submission.
- **BR-01**: a rate below the minimum is refused on the line (400) and blocks the submission.
- **Approval and payment** (FR-04; ADR-731): SE / SACC prepares; SACC → PM → HRMGR (the SACC step skipped when the SACC prepared it; the preparer never approves); approval locks the run; payment per worker by bank (account and reference required) or cash, late when after the due date of the cycle; the worker's acknowledgement on the phone by the worker card (QR or code — it must be the paid worker's card) or a photograph; a dispute notifies the HR manager; amendment of an approved run not yet paid.
- **Outputs** (FR-04, FR-05): wage slips as a printable page in English or Hindi; muster roll (Form XVI layout, CSV/PDF); register of wages (Form XVII layout, CSV/PDF) of approved runs by period, project, site, contractor — read by 24.04; events `pay.wage_run.approved`, `pay.wage_run.paid` for 24.04 and 24.05.
- **Contractor wage verification** (FR-03, BR-01; ADR-729, ADR-730): a contractor's month by project (and site), compiled by SUBCO, SACC or payroll: the wage sheet (rows or CSV) compared at once with the attendance (missing, short days, below minimum, unpaid or late block; extra days, no attendance, unknown worker, deductions above the cap warn), the signed sheet and the proof of payment attached through the document service; the HR manager (not the compiler) verifies, returns or **holds** — the hold is a reason of the 21.04 payment hold of the contractor on the project (`ctr.sub.hold_changed`), lifted when the corrected sheet is verified; events `pay.contractor_wage.verified` / `.variance`.
- **Permissions**: `pay.wage_run.{view,create,edit,delete,submit,approve,cancel,amend,print,export,disburse,acknowledge}`, `pay.wage_rate.view` (rates masked otherwise), `pay.wage_check.{view,manage,verify}`; grants PAY, SACC, SE, PM, HRMGR, SUBCO, DIR, AUD; `pay.wage_check.manage` in the permission baseline. Scope by project and site in every list and record.
- **API**: 28 operations (OpenAPI `docs/erp/openapi.yaml`, part 24.03); **Browser**: Wage Run (wizard: period, muster and wages, review, approval, pay and acknowledge), Wage Register & Muster Roll, Contractor Wage Verification (worklist cards by due date with the SLA chip, Verify / Return on each card, detail with variances, upload and decision); English and Hindi, Telugu for the field screens.
- **Defects**: D-349 re-assigned to 24.04 with the reason (Forms XVI and XVII now produced); D-746 raised.

## 6. Known limitations
- D-746: the worklist counts are on the page, not in the bottom-bar badge; the acknowledgement needs the network (not in the offline queue).
- D-349 remainder (Forms XII and XIII, site-level worker approver, licence count by site, import age check) with 24.04.
- Payment posting and the bank file are 24.05 (they read `pay.wage_run.approved` / `.paid`); the statutory returns (ECR, ESI) of labour wages are 24.04.
- The state of the minimum wage is the project's branch state, else the worker's home state; zone-wise minimum wages are not distinguished (the 07.08 master has no zone).

## 7. How to verify manually
1. Register a departmental gang and its workers with skills (07.08), approve the state minimum wages and the gang's contract rate; let the workers punch at a site for a week.
2. Payroll → Wage Run: site, gang, weekly, the Monday → **Compute the wages**; open a worker: days, overtime, the rate against the minimum; enter an allowance or piece work; try a rate below the minimum (refused).
3. **Send for approval**; approve as site accountant, project manager and HR manager.
4. As the site accountant: **Pay & acknowledge** → choose all → cash → pay. On the phone as the site engineer: **Take acknowledgement** → scan the worker card.
5. **Wage slips** (English or Hindi, by the language of the page); Payroll → Wage Register & Muster Roll → export.
6. Payroll → Contractor Wage Verification → new check for a contractor's month → upload the CSV, attach the signed sheet and the proof → send. As the HR manager: the card with its variances → **Hold payments** → the contractor's payments on the project are held in the subcontractor workspace (21.04); correct the sheet and verify to lift it.

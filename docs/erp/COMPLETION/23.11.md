## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 358, 359): `fc_forecasts` (approval-controlled, versions), `fc_forecast_lines`; flag, two pages, page-tracker rows, two templates.
- **Forecast** (FR-01–FR-03; ADR-685–687): a version of a month from the control facts by WBS and cost code; the ETC by the method of each line (remaining budget, CPI, bottom-up, committed plus, manual with justification); EAC with the risk allowance (EMV of open risks) and pending variations; VAC; loss-making against the contract revenue.
- **EAC bridge** (FR-04; ADR-688) against the last approved forecast; confidence and commentary (low confidence needs its commentary).
- **Approval and publication** (FR-05, BR-01; ADR-689): `FIN_COST_FORECAST` PCMGR → PM → PD, the CFO for a loss-making project; the maker never approves; a new version supersedes on approval; approved forecasts are not changed. `fin.eac_version.approved`; revenue recognition (23.10) now takes the approved EAC (EAC source FORECAST).
- **Reports**: EAC trend of a project (with budget and revenue), forecast against budget for all projects (worst first). CSV export, PDF print, line import.
- **API**: 18 routes under `/api/v1/fin/cost-forecasts` and `/api/v1/fin/forecast-reports` (ADR-690). **Browser**: Forecast Workbench (projects of a month; the forecast with header facts, bridge chart, lines with a method editor, decision panel), EAC Trend & Forecast Variance; English and Hindi.
- **Job** `fin.fc_monthly` (daily 09:05 IST): from the 10th, projects without an approved forecast of last month.

## 6. Known limitations
- Bottom-up does not look up the current rates of 15.09; it uses the rate paid to date, the budget rate or one entered (named on the line).
- Accrued cost is entered per line (no accrual feed by cost code).
- Pending variations are one header amount with a note, not linked to variation records of 21.x.
- The facts' `forecast_cost` column of 14.07 is not written (another module's table); 14.07 can read `fin.eac_version.approved`.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As QS: Finance → Forecast Workbench → month → **Prepare the forecast** for a project with control facts.
2. **Change the method** of a line (CPI, bottom-up with a rate, manual with a justification); set pending variations and confidence; the EAC bridge shows what changed.
3. **Send for approval**; as PCMGR, PM, PD (and CFO when loss-making) **Approve**.
4. EAC Trend & Forecast Variance: the project's EAC by month; all projects against budget.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 216, 217; schema-diff of existing objects clean; migrations re-apply cleanly): `proc_price_indices`, `proc_rate_contracts`, `proc_rc_lines`, `proc_rc_revisions`, `proc_service_orders`, `proc_so_lines`, `proc_service_entries`, `proc_se_lines`, and the link tables `proc_po_rc`, `proc_po_line_rc`; seed of the flag, three navigation entries, page-tracker rows and five notification templates.
- **Rate contract** (FR-01): vendor, lines with rates and ceiling quantities, ceiling value, validity, projects, price-variation formula, terms; approved by the procurement and commercial managers (the direction above ₹ 1 crore); amend by a copy; close; cancel.
- **Call-off** (FR-02, BR-01): a purchase order with source rate contract at the contract rate, within validity, projects and ceilings (checked at creation, submit and approval), approved by the procurement manager alone below ₹ 25 lakh.
- **Consumption and alerts** (FR-03): per line and in value, told once at 80 percent, at 100 percent and on expiry (job `proc.rc_alerts`).
- **Price variation** (FR-04): index values by code and month; the revision worked out by one formula and approved by the procurement and commercial managers; rates change only then.
- **Service orders and service entry sheets** (FR-05): lump sum, per unit, per month; approval by value; commitment at approval, released at close; sheets certified by a site engineer and the project manager; amend and reversal.
- **API**: 46 routes under `/api/v1/proc` (2,590 routes in all). **Browser**: Rate Contracts (list, object, form), Service Orders (list, object, form), Service Entry Sheets (list, form, object), and the rate contract source on Create Purchase Order; English and Hindi.

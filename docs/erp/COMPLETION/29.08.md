## 2. What was built

**Help on every page (FR-03, FR-04)**: where the help desk is on, a **Help** button on every page opens:

- the page's articles;
- the tour for the person's roles (shown step by step and remembered once seen);
- the latest release;
- **Raise a ticket**.

A current announcement shows as a notice above the button. The help call answers "not enabled" where the help desk is off, so other pages are unchanged.

**Help & Support** (`/help`, every internal role; phone first):

- **Raise a ticket (FR-01)**, in three numbered steps:
  1. what happened: kind and one line, with the page you came from and, after a failed call, a box to attach its status and correlation ID;
  2. details, priority and a screenshot or photo;
  3. updates in the app, plus e-mail or WhatsApp if wanted.

  The ticket is routed by its kind to a team (application, IT and access, data), and its priority sets the SLA: urgent 4 h, high 8 h, medium 24 h, low 72 h (parameters).
- **My tickets**: the timeline, a reply box, cancel, reopen, and a rating of 1 to 5 stars once resolved (FR-05).
- **Knowledge base**: search, and read articles (Markdown).
- **Release notes**: as written by the release tool (00.03).

**Support Desk** (`/admin/support`, ITADM and SYSADM):

- **Queue**: open tickets by priority and due time, past-SLA ones marked.
  - A ticket shows the page, the full correlation ID, the failed call and the browser.
  - Support takes it, asks the person, adds internal notes, and resolves it with the resolution.
  - A job escalates a ticket past its SLA: level 1 to the team lead with the priority raised, level 2 to ITADM. Each level notifies and sets a new due time.
- **Data corrections (FR-02, BR-01)**:
  - Support requests a correction on a ticket: module, record, field, current and correct value, reason.
  - The module's owner approves it (for example ACCMGR for finance), then the auditor for financial modules.
  - Nobody edits the data directly: the owner makes the change through the module's own transaction and records its reference (for example a reversal journal), which resolves the ticket.
  - The ticket cannot be resolved while the correction is open.
- **Knowledge base (FR-04)**: articles, tours (steps) and announcements (dates), with pages, roles and status. Each change raises the version.
- **Dashboard (FR-05)**: open, past SLA, average resolution time and satisfaction over 30 days, and open tickets by priority, status, kind and team.

Labels are en/hi/te (area `hd`).

**Permissions**:

| Role | Grant |
|---|---|
| Every internal role | Raise tickets, read help |
| ITADM, SYSADM | Work tickets, manage articles |
| AUD, DIR | See all tickets |
| Module owners | Make approved corrections |

## 6. Known limitations

D-796:

- The screenshot is attached by the person, not captured automatically.
- E-mail and WhatsApp depend on the gateways.
- Teams and correction owners are code tables.
- External partners do not raise tickets here.
- Tours do not highlight page elements.
- No release notes have been written yet.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set `ff.admin.p2908`.
2. As ITADM, open **Administration → Support Desk → Knowledge base** and publish an article for `/home`.
3. As a site engineer, open the home page, then **Help**: the article is listed. **Raise a ticket**, give a line and the details, choose WhatsApp, and send.
4. As ITADM, under **Queue**, open the ticket: the page is shown. **Take it**, write the resolution and **Resolve**.
5. As the engineer, under **My tickets**, rate it.

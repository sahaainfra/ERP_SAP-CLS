## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 146, 147; nothing existing altered): `proj_projects` (1:1 with `org_projects`), `proj_milestones`, `proj_lifecycle_log`; seed: flag, the navigation group Project Master with two entries, three tracker rows.
- **Shared pieces extended**: in the organisation part `applyProjectStatus` (the status of the organisation project follows the lifecycle, and the draft sites activate with it) and `activationGuards` (a project with a master is activated only by its own approval); the messaging auto-groups sync on `proj.project.activated`.
- **Service** (new module `proj`, `modules/proj/project.js`): the wizard (organisation project, sites with geofences, project master, numbering series and document folders from templates, milestones, in one transaction; optionally sent for activation at once; prefilled from an awarded tender); activation by approval; the eight lifecycle states with allowed moves, a checklist at each and readiness checks; suspension and closure by chain; amendments of the contract terms of a live project by approval; milestones and the key dates register; settings; portfolio with health; print; export; Excel import of milestones.
- **Workflows**: `PROJ_ACTIVATION` (project director other than the maker, then the director), `PROJ_TRANSITION` (project manager other than the requester, project director, director), `PROJ_CHANGE` (project director, then the director).
- **API**: 20 routes under `/api/v1/proj` and the lookups, in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`.
- **Permissions**: `proj.project.{view,create,edit,delete,submit,approve,cancel,amend,print,export,view_all,transition,import}`, `proj.milestone.manage`; PCMGR and PD create and configure, PM edits and moves within the project, DIR approves, transitions, prints and exports.
- **Parameters**: `proj.numbering_templates`, `proj.folder_template`, `proj.settings_defaults`, `proj.lifecycle_checklists`.
- **Browser**: Project Portfolio, Project (details, sites, milestones, settings, lifecycle history, approvals, papers; activation, move and amendment panels), Project Setup Wizard in seven steps with validation at each; English and Hindi.
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 80, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, ADR-368 to 373, D-399 to 402, openapi.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As the project controls manager open **Project Setup Wizard**: basics (code, name, type, sector, place, client, consultant, funding agency), contract summary, sites with circle geofences, team (project manager and project director), numbering series and folder template, settings and milestones; on the review tick the checklist and **Make and send for activation** (or save a draft). An awarded tender can fill the wizard (`?tenderId=`).
2. The project page shows the sites, the milestones, the numbering series and the folders that were made. The project director and then the director approve the activation in the inbox: the project is in Mobilisation, its organisation project and sites are active.
3. As the project manager open the project: **Move to Execution** shows the checks (the budget and the baseline are warnings), the checklist and a reason; tick all and move. Add a milestone and mark it achieved.
4. **Move to Suspended** goes to another project manager, the project director and the director; **Amend the contract terms** (value, dates, retention) goes to the project director and the director; the lifecycle history keeps every move with its checklist.
5. Continue to Substantially complete, Defect liability period, Closed (by the chain) and Archived. **Project Portfolio** lists every project with its state and health. Switch to Hindi.

# Part 23.14 completion: period close, financial statements and finance dashboard

Part: 23.14 (step 180). Date: 2026-11-19. Module `fin`, flag `ff.fin.p2314` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 23.06 (bank reconciliation) and 23.10 (revenue recognition); uses the core period statuses (01.03), the GL and its carry-forward (23.01), depreciation (23.09), GST (23.08), AR (23.03), AP (23.02). Provides `fin.period.closed` (MIS 27.07, consolidation 23.15), `fin.period.reopened`, `fin.year.closed`.

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items in section 6.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 364, 365): `close_periods` (approval-controlled), `close_tasks`, `close_reopens` (approval-controlled); flag, four pages, page-tracker rows, templates `fin.close.decided`, `fin.close.alert`.
- **Close and checklist** (FR-01; ADR-702): ten default tasks with owners, due dates, dependencies and automatic checks (inventory locked, payroll posted, depreciation run, revenue recognised for every project with activity, bank accounts reconciled to the month end, ITC review approved, AR / AP sub-ledgers against their control accounts, other modules locked, trial balance and balance sheet balanced); evidence notes and files; extra tasks; optional tasks by the CFO with a reason. A mandatory task open blocks the submission.
- **Locks** (FR-02; ADR-703): the other modules soft-closed from the close; GL soft-closed when sent, every module closed when the CFO approves (workflow `FIN_PERIOD_CLOSE`, ACCMGR → CFO); a posting into the closed month is refused by the GL. Reopen: workflow `FIN_PERIOD_REOPEN`, ACCMGR → CFO → DIR, applying the core reopen to each module named.
- **Statements** (FR-03, BR-01; ADR-704, ADR-705): trial balance, Schedule III balance sheet and P&L, cash flow (indirect) from posted journals only; month, quarter, year to date, year; previous period and last year; the accounts of each line with a link to the ledger; tie-out to the trial balance; CSV and PDF.
- **Year end** (FR-04; ADR-706): wizard — every month closed, next year set up, closing entries (the 23.01 carry-forward journal) approved, year closed by the CFO.
- **Dashboard** (FR-05; ADR-707): revenue, gross margin and %, EBITDA, cash, receivables and payables with ageing, DSO, DPO, working capital, debt and debt/equity; 12-month trend; exceptions (months not closed, overdue tasks, sub-ledger differences, TB); my pending tasks; reconciliation statement.
- **API**: 30 operations under `/api/v1/fin/periods`, `/period-reopens`, `/statements`, `/year-end`, `/finance-dashboard`. **Browser**: Period Close (list, board with column switcher on the phone, locks, decision panel, reopen), Financial Statements, Finance Dashboard, Year-End Closing; English and Hindi. **Job** `fin.close_monthly` (reminders).

## 6. Known limitations
- The board moves a task by its buttons (start, done, reopen); there is no drag and drop.
- The default checklist is in the code; a company changes it on each close (extra and optional tasks), not as a stored template.
- Accruals and provisions are posted as journals (23.01); there is no accrual-reversal schedule of its own.
- Taxes paid in the cash flow are taken as the tax expense of the period (the change in tax provisions is in working capital).

## 7. How to verify manually
1. Finance → Period Close: choose an ended month → **Start**; **Lock the other modules**; mark each task done (with evidence where its check fails).
2. **Send to the CFO** (GL soft-closed); as CFO **Approve** — every module closed. **Ask to reopen** → CFO → director.
3. Finance → Financial Statements: the month, quarter, YTD; tabs BS / P&L / cash flow / TB; tap a line for its accounts.
4. Finance → Finance Dashboard; Finance → Year-End Closing.

# Part 04.04 completion: Protocol & Control Engine: control points, exceptions and reason codes

Status: **Ready for review, with the open items below** (the control points of the modules from M07 onward, effective dating and export of settings, retention of the check log, maker-checker of reason codes and the translation of the pages are later parts: D-195 to D-200). Feature flag `ff.eng.p0404` (OFF by default; ON in development and test).

## What was found
No protocol or control engine existed. The workflow engine (04.01 to 04.03), the file service, the reason picker and table of 03.04 (cancel, reject, return), the audit trail, outbox, parameters, list framework and widget framework were reused. The `controls.js` of part 04.02 is the limits and delegation service and is unrelated.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 62 and 63; nothing existing altered): `pcs_control_points`, `pcs_check_results` (append-only), `pcs_exceptions`, `pcs_reason_codes`, `pcs_control_changes`, `pcs_cycle_stages`; the flag, the reason codes, four navigation entries, four tracker rows and the `controls.exceptions` widget as reference seed.
- **The engine** (`platform/protocol`): `controlRegistry.define` and `protocolOf(db).check/enforce(actor, code, context)` (ADR-172, 173). OFF does nothing; OBSERVE writes `WOULD_*` and affects nobody; WARN warns; ENFORCE blocks (`CONTROL_BLOCKED`), asks for an exception (`EXCEPTION_REQUIRED`) or warns, by enforcement. The check runs in the transaction of the action, so it applies to every path that calls the service (screen, API, import; the same call is for jobs, offline sync and AI drafts). The result of a blocked action is written after the rollback.
- **Controls** (`GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /api/v1/pcs/control-points`): observation figures (days observed, what would have been stopped, value at risk), a control written as data, changes. A control in ENFORCE, or going to it, is changed only through the approval of its process owner and then the auditor or director, and goes to ENFORCE only after the 14 days (parameter) of OBSERVE (BR-01, ADR-176).
- **Exceptions** (`/api/v1/pcs/exceptions`): started by the person the control stopped, for that record, only after a failed check (the value at risk, project and level come from the server's check, ADR-175); evidence through the file service; submitted into approval (level 1 PM, level 2 PD, level 3 DIR, and the director from ₹10 lakh); approved, it lets that one action through once within 30 days. A blocking control cannot be excepted. An emergency exception lets the action through at once and must be approved within 72 hours; a job climbs the approval, tells the owner and the dashboard flags it (BR-02, ADR-177).
- **Reason codes** (`/api/v1/pcs/reason-codes`): the master per action (cancel, reject, return, reverse, deviate, override); a company adds its own or switches a shared one off for itself; the picker of every dialog reads it (ADR-174).
- **Control cycle** (`GET /api/v1/pcs/cycle/:entity/:id`) and the **dashboard** (`GET /api/v1/pcs/dashboard`: tiles, trend, top failing controls, ageing, by control and person, rollout of modes) and the **register export** (xlsx, csv, pdf).
- **Sample controls** on the sample document (`CP-ENG-01` exception above ₹5 lakh with evidence and emergency way, `CP-ENG-02` warning, `CP-ENG-03` block), called by `submit` and by the new import path `POST /api/v1/eng/sample-docs/import`.
- **Pages**: Control Points (`/admin/controls`), Reason Codes (`/admin/reason-codes`), Exceptions Register (`/controls/exceptions`, with saved variants and export), Controls Dashboard (`/controls/dashboard`), and the Raise Exception dialog that opens from the blocked action; the Control Cycle panel is on the sample document.
- **Permissions**: `eng.pcs.view`, `eng.pcs.manage`, `eng.pcs.raise`, `eng.pcs.emergency`; the route policy, OpenAPI, ARCHITECTURE section 38, DB_CHANGES and DB_BASELINE are updated; the schema lock holds 491 objects.

## Click-path for a reviewer
1. As the system administrator open **Administration > Control Points**: `CP-ENG-01` is in Observe, `CP-ENG-03` is Off. Open `CP-ENG-01`: observed 0 of 14 days; set the mode to Enforce with a reason and **Save**: "at least 14 days of OBSERVE data" (BR-01). (For a trial company set the parameter `eng.pcs.observe_days` to 0; the change then goes to the process owner and the auditor.)
2. As a project director raise a **Sample Document** of ₹7,50,000 and submit it while the control is in Observe: it goes into approval and the registry shows "would have stopped 1". With the control in Enforce the submit stops: the **Raise an exception** dialog opens. Choose a reason, write the justification, **Save and add evidence**; **Send for approval** says a file is needed; attach a photo, send.
3. As the project manager approve the exception in **My Approvals**. As the project director submit the document again: it goes into approval; the **Control cycle** panel shows Plan and Verify done.
4. Open **Exceptions Register**: the exception is Used; open it: reason, justification, evidence, the checks, the approval; **Export XLSX**. As the auditor open **Controls Dashboard**: tiles, top failing controls, ageing, the rollout of modes.
5. **Reason Codes**: the reverse codes are there; add `WRONG_GST`; open a cancel dialog anywhere (a sample request): the picker lists the master.
6. Raise an **emergency** exception (project director; `CP-ENG-01` allows it): the document goes ahead at once; after 72 hours without approval the job climbs it and the dashboard shows it in red.

## Tests and acceptance
- **T-01** OBSERVE logs and never blocks, ENFORCE blocks, an approved exception lets the action through once, the control cycle and the warning control (`tests/protocol-control.test.js`, `e2e/controls.spec.js`). **T-02** the same controls stop the API path and the import path, a blocking control cannot be excepted, OFF writes nothing. **T-03** an emergency exception goes ahead, is not escalated before its window, is escalated once after it (the approval task climbs, the event is published, the dashboard counts it) and is regularised late.
- Also: BR-01 (days of observation, the approval chain, maker cannot approve their own change, rejection leaves the control as it was), the registry and reason codes (a control as data, company reason codes, a shared code switched off for one company only), and who sees what (a store keeper sees none of it, 404 across companies, stale version 409, idempotent replay, export).
- **AC-01** registry, check, exceptions workflow and dashboard with the sample controls on the sample document. **AC-02** the reason picker of the cancel and reject dialogs reads the master, which also holds reverse, deviate and override. **AC-03** the new pages pass the page checks. **AC-04** permission and isolation tests. **AC-05** the exception chain cannot be skipped: its only status writers are the engine's hooks; control changes in ENFORCE the same. **AC-06** see Results. **AC-07** this report.

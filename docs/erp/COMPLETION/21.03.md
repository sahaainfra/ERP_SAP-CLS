# Part 21.03 completion: Subcontract & Work Order Management (Item-Rate and Labour-Rate)

Part: 21.03. Date: 2026-11-21. Module `ctr`, flag `ff.ctr.p2103` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 21.01 (client contract and terms), 06.04 (business partners), 10.04 (BOQ and work packages), 12.03 (budget commitments).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items D-649 in `DEFECTS.md`.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 300, 301): `ctr_work_orders` and `ctr_wo_amendments` (approval-controlled), `ctr_wo_items`, `ctr_wo_free_issue`; flag `ff.ctr.p2103`, two pages, tracker, templates.
- **Work orders** worked out on the server (amounts, value, margin, flags); a client-supplied value that differs is refused; checked before sending (items, selection, free issue for labour-rate, milestones for lump sum, advance recovery).
- **Approval** by band and by margin; on approval the work order is issued and its commitment posted to the budget ledger; **acceptance**, **amendment** (one open at a time, never below what is executed) and **closure**.
- **Adapter** `workOrders.progress` for executed and billed quantities of the subcontractor measurements and bills (22.05): only up, never beyond the ordered quantity.
- **API**: routes under `/api/v1/ctr` (work-orders, wo-amendments, wo-margin); **browser**: Work Orders list, work order object (scope, terms with back-to-back client terms, free issue, margin, amendments, progress, documents), amendment comparison, Back-to-Back Margin report; English and Hindi. The margin and client rates are masked without `ctr.work_order.margin`.

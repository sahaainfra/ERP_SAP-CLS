# Part 16.10 completion: Monthly Material Reconciliation, Theoretical vs Actual

Part: 16.10. Date: 2026-11-12. Module `inv`, flag `ff.inv.p1610` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 16.04, 16.06, 16.01 and 14.02; the norms are the BOQ resource norms (10.04). Provides `inv.reconciliation.approved` (monthly close 27.07, detection tower 27.09) and `inv.reconciliation.recovery_approved` (subcontractor bills 22.05).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items in section 6.

## 1. Discovery
`docs/erp/DISCOVERY/16.10.md`.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 243, 244; up, down and up again clean): `mrc_key_materials`, `mrc_reconciliations`, `mrc_lines`, `mrc_contributions`; flag, two pages under the Stores group, page tracker, notification templates. No existing table changed.
- **Reconciliation** (FR-01, FR-02): for each active site and month, for the key materials: opening, receipts, transfers in and out, returns to vendor, issues, site returns, adjustments and the book closing from the stock ledger; the physical closing from the month's count (16.06); the theoretical consumption from the approved progress reports x the BOQ norm x (1 + allowed wastage); the actual consumption (issues less site returns); the variance, per cent and wastage against the allowed. Always worked out by the server, and again when it is sent.
- **Explanation and recovery** (FR-03, BR-02): a variance beyond the allowance needs a reason code and an explanation before it goes up; excess wastage by a subcontractor carries the party and the amount to recover. Stores, procurement and accounts add notes.
- **Approval** (FR-04): the QS prepares and sends; the project manager reviews and the project director or director approves; approved is locked, an amendment is a new draft that replaces it when approved.
- **Status and close** (BR-01): the status page lists every active site for the month with the missing ones highlighted and says whether the month can be closed; `missing()` is the list for the monthly close. A job makes the reconciliations on the 2nd of each month.
- **Trend** (FR-05): wastage against the allowed by material, site and month, and the top variances.
- **API**: 22 routes under `/api/v1/inv`. **Browser**: Monthly Material Reconciliation (status by site, list, key materials, workbench with drill-down), Wastage Trend.

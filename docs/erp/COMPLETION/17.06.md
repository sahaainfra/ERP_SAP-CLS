## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 256, 257): `plt_hire_agreements`, `plt_hire_bills`; flag, three pages, tracker, templates. No existing table changed.
- **Agreements** (FR-01, FR-03, FR-04): hire-in, hire-out, internal; rate per hour, day or month; minimum guarantee; idle and breakdown rules; mobilisation; GST; no overlap; approval by plant supervisor, project manager and (above Rs 5 lakh a month) the plant manager.

- **Comparison** (FR-05): hire per hour against owned cost per hour by class.
- **API**: 33 routes; **browser**: Hire Agreements, Compute Hire Bill, Hire Cost vs Owned Cost; English and Hindi.

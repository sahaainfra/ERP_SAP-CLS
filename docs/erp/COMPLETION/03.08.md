# Part 03.08 completion: global search and command palette

Status: **Ready for review, with the open items below.** Feature flag `ff.ui.p0308` (OFF by default, ON in development and test).

## What was built
- **Search index service** (`platform/search`). Modules register what can be searched with `searchEntities.register` (a `load` that returns the record as a document, its permission, its events, its table, where it opens). 12 kinds are registered: projects, sites, departments, branches, cost centres, people, roles (`modules/iam/search.js`), sample documents, bills, files attached to records (`modules/core/search.js`), sample material requests and discussion comments (`modules/ui/search-entities.js`). The index is four tables: `core_search_index` (a row per record), `core_search_fts` (SQLite FTS5, trigram: substring and partial-code matching), `core_search_secret` (sensitive values) and `core_search_queries` (the log of searches). A consumer `ui.search_index` re-indexes the record each domain event names; `ui.search_reconcile` (every five minutes) indexes what changed without an event and drops what is gone; `ui.search_reindex` rebuilds a kind; `ui.search_purge` removes old search text.
- **Search API** (`/api/v1/ui/search`, `/types`, `/commands`, `/click`, `/status`, `/analytics`, `POST /reindex/:entity`). Results are grouped by kind or paged for chosen kinds, filtered by project, site, status, dates and sort. The person's rights become SQL (one clause per permission key held, with the scope columns of the index row) and every hit is read again from its record before it is returned. Counts per kind come from the filtered set, so they never reveal another project.
- **Matching** in six ways, strictest first: the whole document number (`MR/2026-27/000123`, in any case, with spaces or no separators), the start of a number, the start of a title, the words anywhere (also pieces of words and partial codes), the sound of a name (Devanagari and Latin: शर्मा, Sharma and Sarma are one) and a typing mistake (edit distance). Ranking: kind of match, then favourites and recent records, then bm25.
- **Field masks (BR-01).** A value with a mask rule (mobile number, request total) is kept in `core_search_secret`, never in title, subtitle or body, and matched only for a person whose mask rule for that field is `show`.
- **Command palette** (Ctrl/Cmd+K everywhere, also from a text field): grouped results (actions, pages, records of each kind with their count), highlighted words, "show all results", `>` for actions only, arrows, Page Up/Down, Tab, Enter, Esc; with nothing typed, the actions of the current page, favourites and recent records. 16 actions in `commandRegistry` (open My Work, create a request, review approvals, invite a person, choose project and site, light/dark/high-contrast theme, English/Hindi, keyboard shortcuts, advanced search, rebuild the index), filtered by permission and feature flag, with the ones of the page first; the "create" shortcuts of the navigation registry appear as actions too. Opening a record adds it to the recent records (and any searchable kind can be a favourite).
- **Advanced Search** (`/search`): search box, kind chips with counts, project, site, status (offered by the kinds chosen), date range, sort, page size 25/50/100, grouped results with "Show all N", paging, saved filters (the saved-view service, list `ui.search`), CSV export of what is listed, filters in the address, loading, empty, no-result and error (with correlation ID) states, English/Hindi kind names.
- **Search Administration** (`/admin/search`, `ui.search.analytics`): the index per kind (records, indexed, waiting, last indexed), the rebuild of one kind or all with a reason (`ui.search.reindex`, the IT administrator; a background job with progress; audited; events `ui.search.reindex_requested`, `ui.search.reindexed`), and analytics (searches, share with no result, median and p95 time, most searched, searches that found nothing, results opened).
- **Parameters:** `ui.search.min_query_length`, `ui.search.results_per_group`, `ui.search.retention_days` (changes go through the parameter service, audited).
- **Permissions:** `ui.search.reindex` (ITADM, SUPADM), `ui.search.analytics` (ITADM, SYSADM, SUPADM). Search itself needs none: each result is filtered by the right of its own kind.

## Click-path for a reviewer
1. Sign in (or use the development identity) as a project manager. Press **Ctrl+K** on any page: your actions for the page, favourites and recent records show.
2. Type `shuttering` (a word of a request), then a full document number such as `MR/2026-27/0001`: that record is first. Try `shutring` (a mistake) and `hutter` (a piece of a word). Press Enter on a record: it opens and is now under recent records. Type `>dark` and Enter: the theme changes.
3. Type `shuttering` and choose **Show all results** (or open **Advanced Search** from My Work): pick the kind, a status and a project; save the filters under a name, clear them, apply the saved set; **Export CSV**.
4. As a person assigned to one project only: the other project's records, sites and requests are not found, not even by exact document number, and the counts do not include them.
5. As an auditor or HR manager, search a person by mobile number: the HR manager finds them, the auditor (who sees the number masked) does not.
6. As the IT administrator open **Search Administration**: the index per kind; **Rebuild** a kind with a reason and watch the job; the analytics list the searches that found nothing. A system administrator sees the page but has no Rebuild button; a project manager gets the no-permission state.

## Defects found on the way (fixed)
- The palette's Escape did not close it when focus had not yet reached the field (found by the phone end-to-end test): Escape is now handled on the document.
- The "saved filters" menu opened under the phone's bottom bar and could not be pressed: it opens in the page now.
- The checksum used by the backup and migration checks read every table `ORDER BY rowid`, which a full-text settings table (`WITHOUT ROWID`) does not have: it orders by the first column for such tables (`scripts/lib/backup.mjs`).

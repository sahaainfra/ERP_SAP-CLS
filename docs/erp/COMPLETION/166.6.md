## 2. What was built
| Requirement | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| §2.1 HR overview page (cards) | dashboard `ov-workforce` (`modules/ui/overviews.js`), `/hr/workforce` (`modules/hr/GatePages.jsx`) | on the overview framework of 166.5. **Attendance today** (present, absent, late from today's attendance report; on leave from the leave service), **present today** (of expected and deployed), **contract labour on site** by contractor (present, deployed, registered), **headcount by site**, **overtime this month** (paid hours of the register; no budget exists: D-691), **leave liability** (days of paid leave to be taken, by type: D-691), **attrition** (leavers ÷ headcount + leavers in the period), **HR approvals waiting** (Approve / Return), **documents expired or expiring** (employee documents, contract labour licences, training certificates). The site cards (attendance, present, labour) come first, so a phone shows them first. Each card names its source. Two small additions to services: `leave.balances.summary` (on leave today, liability by type) and `register.overtimeMonth`, both within the person's data scope |
| §2.2 gate checklists as object pages | `modules/hr/gates.js`, `gate-routes.js`, `gate-catalogue.json` (`scripts/gate_catalogue.mjs`), tables `hr_gates`, `hr_gate_criteria`; `/admin/gates`, `/admin/gate?code=G2` | G1 Platform (55 criteria), G2 Workforce-First (15), G3 Project Baseline (29), G4 Execution (55): one criterion per part of the gate in `PROGRESS.csv` with its completion report and discovery note as evidence, one for the open Critical/High defects of its parts (`DEFECTS_BY_PART.md`, `DEFECTS.md`). Each criterion: evidence links (opened through the API from `docs/erp` only, ADR-623), status open / met / not met (met only with evidence that resolves; not met only with a reason), reviewer, date. The gate's header: status, criteria met, not met, open, passed on; filter by status |
| Sign-off | `gates.js` (`HR_GATE` workflow type) | "Send for sign-off" only when every criterion is met (T-G2); the workflow engine routes it to the technical lead (ITADM, never the person who sent it) and then the director (`07_APPROVAL_WORKFLOW_REFERENCE.md`, ADR-622); the director's approval passes the gate; a return reopens it. The criteria are locked while it is with the reviewers |
| §2.3 gate report PDF | `gates.report` (server PDF of 04.11) | the gate, its status, the sign-off (step, decision, by, date, comment) and every criterion with status, reviewer, date and evidence; audited as an export |
| Gate evidence documents | `docs/erp/GATES/G1.md` … `G4.md` | written by the same script: the criteria, each part's status in `PROGRESS.csv`, the evidence links, the open Critical/High defects of the gate's parts (G1: D-010, D-146, D-169; G2–G4: none) |
| Permissions | `templates.js` (`ADDED_07_14`), `route-policy.js` | `hr.workforce.view`: HRMGR, PAY, WSUP, PM, CM, PD, DIR, AUD; `hr.gate.view`: ITADM, SYSADM, DIR, HRMGR, AUD; `hr.gate.review`: ITADM; `hr.gate.export`: ITADM, DIR, AUD |
| Fixes of 166.5 found here | `modules/ui/overviews.js` | the HR "people by site / department" cards counted 0 (the overview's groups are `{ label, key, n }`); several "View all" addresses were not pages (`/workflow/inbox` → `/approvals`, procurement analytics, quality tests); the quality exceptions had no titles.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 208, 209; schema-diff of existing objects clean): `proc_mrp_runs`, `proc_mrp_lines`, `proc_mrp_proposals`, `proc_item_params`, `proc_open_supply`; seed of the flag, four navigation entries, page-tracker rows, five notification templates (English, Hindi).
- **The run** (FR-01 to FR-03): requirements from the approved schedule through the BOQ norms (wastage, percent complete, base unit), phased by week; released take-off quantities; hand-entered demand; netting by `netWeeks` against stock, open orders, goods in transit and open requisitions with safety stock, minimum order and whole packs; required-by and order-by dates; weekly job and on demand; a later run supersedes the earlier.
- **Proposals** (FR-04, FR-06, BR-01): accept into a DRAFT requisition of 15.01 (source MRP), change quantity or date, take an approved alternate with free stock, reject with a reason; the run closes when none is open.
- **Reports** (FR-05): shortage heat-map by item and week and the long-lead list with order-by dates, days to order and supplier capacity; XLSX, CSV, PDF; result grid with drill to the activities.
- **Parameters** (section 9): item planning parameters proposed by planning or procurement and approved by the procurement manager.
- **API**: 33 routes under `/api/v1/proc` in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`. **Browser**: Run MRP (parameters and result grid), PR Proposals, Shortage Heat-map and Long-Lead Items, Item Planning Parameters; English and Hindi.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 233, 234; up, down and up again clean): `inv_transfers`, `inv_transfer_lines`, `inv_transfer_discrepancies`; flag, four pages under the Stores group, page tracker, notification templates. No existing table changed.
- **Request and approval** (FR-01): from store, to store, items, quantities and the date wanted (an approved material request pre-fills them); the chain is the construction manager or store manager of the source, and between projects two distinct project managers as well.
- **Dispatch** (FR-02, BR-01): the keeper of the source dispatches; the stock leaves the source at its average (the book rate) into transit (`qty_in_transit` at the destination), an approved gate pass is made, and an e-way bill number is required between states or at or above the threshold; the document is a delivery challan or, between states, a tax invoice.
- **Receipt** (FR-03): the keeper of the destination receives in parts; received goes into stock with its part of the value; damaged and short quantities are held as discrepancies once the receiver says that is all; photos of the damage are filed with the transfer.
- **Discrepancies**: a found shortage or a damaged item is accepted into the destination, damaged goods go back to the source at their value, a loss is written off after the store manager and then the procurement manager (never the person who raised it).
- **Between projects** (FR-05): the destination project owns the received entry, so the cost moves at the book rate; `inv.transfer.received` carries the cost transfer.
- **In-transit report** (FR-04): on the road and held quantities by transfer and item, with age, value and ageing buckets; a daily job (08:15 India time) tells the store managers of stock past the alert days.
- **API**: 20 routes under `/api/v1/inv` (2,846 routes in all). **Browser**: Stock Transfers (list with the in-transit filter; transfer with dispatch, receipt, discrepancies and postings), Transfer Request, Receive Transfer (mobile), In-Transit Stock Ageing.

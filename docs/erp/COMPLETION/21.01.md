# Part 21.01 completion: Client Contract Register, Clauses, Obligations & Milestones

Part: 21.01. Date: 2026-11-21. Module `ctr`, flag `ff.ctr.p2101` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 10.02 (commercial baseline and project set-up), 04.10 (documents), 11.05 (letters, read only).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items D-647 in `DEFECTS.md`.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 296, 297): `ctr_contracts`, `ctr_clauses`, `ctr_obligations`, `ctr_milestones`, `ctr_amendments`, `ctr_value_log`; flag, three pages under Contracts & Billing, tracker, templates, Project 360 slot `contract`.
- **Contract** made from the signed-off baseline (LoA, value, dates, retention, advance, clauses proposed, nothing re-typed); checked before it is sent (client, dates, value, payment periods, retention cap, LD cap, PVC reference, LoA or agreement, payment milestones of a lump-sum style contract); approved by the commercial manager, the project director and the director; then in force and locked.
- **Clauses** by category with summaries and time-bars; **obligations** with owners, recurrence (the next occurrence is made when one is done), reminders and overdue escalation; **milestones** with payment amounts, achieved with evidence.
- **Value**: the value history is the single source; revised = original + approved amendments + approved variations (T-01); pending variations shown apart; values hidden without `ctr.contract.value`.
- **Amendments** through the same chain; on approval the value, completion date and terms change and the version goes up; events for billing, securities, PVC/LD and claims.
- **API**: 47 routes under `/api/v1/ctr`; **browser**: Contract Register with Contract 360 (terms, clauses, obligations, milestones, value history, amendments, documents, correspondence), Obligations Calendar, Contract Amendments; English and Hindi.

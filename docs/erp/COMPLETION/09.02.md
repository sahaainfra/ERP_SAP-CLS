## 7. How to verify manually
1. As a quantity surveyor open **Take-off Sheets**, **New take-off sheet**: the project, the drawing number and revision. Add lines: the BOQ item, the description, the template (concrete volume), the work item (RCC M25), number, length, breadth, depth; the quantity shows at once. Attach the drawing: it shows beside the lines. **Save and work out**.
2. **Send to the checker**: another QS or the design lead releases it. **Material explosion** shows cement and sand with wastage.
3. **New revision** (drawing revision B), change a line, save, **Delta**: the BOQ item shows the change against the released revision. Once released, **Feed an estimate** puts the quantities into a draft estimate with the same item numbers.
4. **Bar Bending Schedules**, **New**: the member and drawing; add bar marks (diameter, shape code from the library, lengths a to d, members and bars): cut length and weight show at once; the summary by diameter is below.
5. Send it to the checker; **BBS Summary by Diameter** adds up the released schedules of the project. An engineering MTO (kind Engineering MTO) takes its lines pasted from the design tool or from the Import Center (type EST_MTO). Switch to Hindi.

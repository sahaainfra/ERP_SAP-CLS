# Part 06.04 completion: Business Partner Master (Vendors, Subcontractors, Clients and Consultants)

Part: 06.04. Date: 2026-11-05. Feature flag `ff.mdm.p0604` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 06.02; the `mdm` module also needs `ff.mdm.p0601` and `ff.mdm.p0602` on.

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** The open items are in section 6 (D-303 to D-306). The ones that matter most:
- There is no GST or banking connector (D-303). GSTINs are checked by format and check character, then marked verified when the accounts manager confirms a portal check. Bank accounts are confirmed by the partner (code or call-back), not by a penny drop.
- Partner 360 shows only this master's data until procurement, billing and finance report into it (D-304).
- The 06.01 pilot partner records are not moved into this master (D-306).

## 1. Discovery summary
See `docs/erp/DISCOVERY/06.04.md`. The only partner data was the 06.01 governance pilot (master type PARTNER), so the master was built in its own tables (ADR-260). Reused:
- formats, GSTIN check character, similarity and masking (06.01);
- states, banks/IFSC, TDS sections and categories (06.02, 06.03);
- the vault for encryption, one-time codes, parameters and the job scheduler;
- the workflow engine and approval matrix, record registry, files, collaboration tabs, the List Report with exports, the Excel engine and notifications.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 102 and 103, nothing existing altered): nine tables with the standard columns. The seed adds the flag, four navigation entries and tracker rows, and three notification templates (en, hi).
- **Service** (`modules/mdm/partners.js`, ADR-260 to 264):
  - **Registration:** a partner has roles (a partner may be a vendor and a client), legal and trade name, constitution, PAN, Udyam and category, CIN, GSTINs per state, addresses, contacts, an optional bank account and TDS sections. The checks:
    - PAN format, and its 4th character against the constitution;
    - GSTIN format and check character, the state code of the GSTIN and the PAN inside it;
    - IFSC (the bank is filled in from the 06.02 list), Udyam and CIN formats;
    - one GSTIN per state, a registered address and at least one contact.
  - **Duplicates** (FR-07): the same PAN, GSTIN or bank account blocks; the same mobile or a name 85% alike warns and needs confirmation.
  - **Saving and activation:** a registration is saved as a Prospect and sent for activation. Activation goes to the steward of its roles (procurement or commercial manager), then the accounts manager; the requester never approves. Return and correction work, and the requester is notified.
  - **Banks** (FR-02, BR-01, BR-03):
    - the account number is encrypted with the vault, with a hash for duplicates and the last four digits for display;
    - the full number is shown only to ACCMGR and CFO, and each view is in the audit trail;
    - a bank change holds payments at once and sends a code to the registered primary mobile, or a call-back to a number registered before the change is noted;
    - the change is approved by the accounts manager and then the CFO; the requester cannot approve (SoD-02);
    - approval starts a cooling-off period (default 3 days) during which payments stay held;
    - an account of another partner is refused.
  - **Documents** (FR-04, T-03): compliance documents with validity, verified by an accounts manager other than the person who added them. A daily job tells the stewards and the accounts manager, once per stage, when a document is due or has expired.
  - **Blocking on expiry:** if the company lists blocking document types (`mdm.partner.blocking_documents`), an expired one stops new POs, work orders and RFQs until a valid one is added.
  - **TDS** (FR-05): sections from 06.02, and lower-deduction certificates with rate (below the section rate), limit and validity.
  - **Status** (FR-06, BR-02):
    - On hold / release (stewards, accounts manager) and inactive / active (steward) are direct, with a reason;
    - blacklisting with reason and period, and lifting it, go to the director for approval;
    - a blacklisting with an end date lapses;
    - `selectable()` and the lookups refuse partners that are not active.
  - **Partner 360** (FR-08): tiles, whether the partner can be chosen, history, and a registry for later modules.
  - **Worklist:** registrations, bank changes, blacklisting requests and expiring documents. What waits for the person comes first, then the oldest, coloured against a 48-hour SLA.
- **Integration:**
  - the List Report `mdm.partners` with saved views, column chooser and export, PAN hidden outside finance;
  - lookups `partners`, `vendors`, `subcontractors`, `labour-contractors`, `clients`, `consultants`, `transporters` and `service-providers` (active partners only);
  - Excel import `MDM_BUSINESS_PARTNER` (each row a registration sent for approval);
  - object type and file entity `mdm_partner`;
  - seven events;
  - approval-matrix defaults for the three workflows;
  - three parameters.
- **Browser:**
  - Business Partners (quick filters for role, category and documents expiring);
  - Partner 360 (header with status, payments and documents; the actions allowed; tabs Details, GST registrations, Bank accounts, Documents, Contacts and addresses, TDS, Activity, Approvals, Timeline, Discussion);
  - Partner Registration wizard (5 steps, duplicates shown while typing, Save as prospect or Send, edit of a prospect or a returned request, draft kept in the browser);
  - Partner Approvals & Bank Changes (worklist).
- **Permissions:**

- **T-01:** a PAN in the wrong form or wrong for the constitution, a GSTIN with a wrong check character, of another state or with another PAN, and a short account or bad IFSC are refused; a second partner with the same PAN is blocked.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. **Register a vendor:** as a procurement manager open **Partner Registration**. Type the legal name, choose Private limited company and type a PAN: a partner with the same PAN shows as "Already registered". Use a new PAN and tick Vendor. Add the GSTIN of Maharashtra, the registered address, a contact with a mobile, the bank account and IFSC, then choose **Send for approval**.
2. **Approve it:** another procurement manager, then the accounts manager, approve it in **My Approvals**. Partner 360 shows Active.
3. **Change the bank:** on Partner 360 tab **Bank accounts**, choose **Ask for a bank change**. Payments show On hold. Enter the code the partner received, or note a call-back to its registered number. The accounts manager and the CFO approve it in **My Approvals**. The new account is in use and payments stay held until the cooling-off date.
4. **Check the masking:** as a site engineer, the account shows only its last four digits and the PAN is masked. As the accounts manager, **Show (logged)** gives the full number.
5. **Blacklist:** choose **Ask to blacklist**; the director approves; the partner shows Blacklisted and cannot be chosen in a vendor lookup.
6. **Use the worklist:** open **Partner Approvals & Bank Changes**: registrations, bank changes, blacklisting requests and expiring documents, what waits for you first.

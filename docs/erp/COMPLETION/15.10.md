# Part 15.10 completion: Procurement Dashboard & Analytics

Part: 15.10. Date: 2026-11-12. Module `proc`, flag `ff.proc.p1510` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 15.05. Provides the procurement widget of Project 360 (10.08).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items in section 6.

## 1. Discovery
`docs/erp/DISCOVERY/15.10.md`.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migration 224, seed only): flag, navigation, page tracker. No table: every figure is worked out from live procurement data on request, inside the data scope of the person (`scopeFilter` on the project of each record).
- **KPIs** (FR-01): open requisitions (count, value, ageing, oldest), requisition-to-order cycle time (average, median, against the previous period), orders awaiting approval, overdue deliveries, spend this month (against last month) and this financial year, savings against the budget rate and against the next quote (L2), share of orders without competition (direct, or a statement with one quoting vendor), share of emergency requisitions and open control exceptions; each with target and status colour from company parameters.
- **Charts** (FR-02): spend by category, vendor, project and month, cycle-time distribution, vendor concentration.
- **Work queues** (FR-03): requisitions awaiting an RFQ, RFQs awaiting quotes, statements awaiting approval, orders awaiting release.
- **Drill-down and export** (FR-04): every tile opens the records it counts; the spend report opens the orders behind each row; reports download as CSV.
- **Reports**: Spend Analysis, Procurement Cycle Time (with stages), Procurement Compliance (single-source, emergency, non-L1, exceptions).
- **Project 360**: the procurement slot shows open requisitions, orders awaiting approval, overdue deliveries and spend of the project.
- **API**: 6 routes under `/api/v1/proc/analytics` (2,685 routes in all). **Browser**: Procurement Dashboard, Spend Analysis, Procurement Cycle Time, Procurement Compliance.

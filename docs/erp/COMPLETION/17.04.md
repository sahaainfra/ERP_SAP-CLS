## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 252, 253; up, down and up again clean): `plt_fuel_tanks`, `plt_fuel_receipts`, `plt_fuel_issues`, `plt_fuel_dips`, `plt_fuel_anomalies`; flag, six pages under the Plant group, page tracker, notification templates. No existing table changed.
- **Fuel stores** (FR-01): tank, bowser or card with capacity, opening stock and rate; receipts against an approved goods receipt (checked against its litre lines and what is already received) or a pump purchase (bill, rate, petty-cash voucher); a wrong receipt is cancelled with a reason if the book stays above zero.
- **Fuel issue** (FR-02, BR-01): on the phone; not above the tank of the machine, the meter must advance (an equal meter needs a reason), the tank must hold the fuel and be of the machine's fuel; meter and pump photograph; receiver named; verified by the plant supervisor (the maker never verifies). Typed values stay on the phone until saved.
- **Dip and tank reconciliation** (FR-02, FR-03): opening + receipts - issues = book, against the dip; tolerance from parameters; a dip is the next day's start; a difference beyond the tolerance raises an anomaly, a corrected dip withdraws it.
- **Consumption against the norm** (FR-04, FR-06): per issue and per machine, class or project from the meter run (fill to fill); litres per working hour; cost at the weighted average rate of the tank, masked without `plant.fuel.costs`; drill to the issues; CSV.
- **Detection rules** (FR-05): above the norm, close issues, more than a tank in 24 hours, meter not advancing (at submission); fuel to a machine not logged as working, log against fuel issued, tank without a dip (nightly job `plant.fuel_sweep`); tank variance (at a dip). One anomaly per cause.
- **Anomaly handling**: explained by the plant manager, accepted by the plant supervisor then the project manager; or confirmed as loss by the project manager.
- **API**: 36 routes under `/api/v1/plant/issues` and `/api/v1/plant/fuel` (3,130 in all). **Browser**: Fuel Issue and Tank Dip Entry (mobile), Fuel Stores, Fuel Issues, Fuel Reports, Fuel Anomalies; English and Hindi.

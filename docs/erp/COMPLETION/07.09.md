## 7. How to verify manually
1. As an employee open **My HR**: today, the month, balances, what is pending; **My Attendance** for any month; **Profile Change Request** for a new mobile number.
2. Open **Letter Request**, ask for an employment letter and send it; the HR manager approves in the approval inbox; HR issues it on the same page; the employee downloads the PDF.
3. As the manager open **My Team**: reportees, who is in, on leave or absent, what waits for you; no bank, ID or salary is shown.
4. Switch the language to Hindi: the headings and labels change.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 241, 242; up, down and up again clean): `ast_small_assets` (register, approval-controlled), `ast_custody_log`, `ast_loss_reports` (approval-controlled); flag, three pages under the Stores group, page tracker, notification templates. No existing table changed.
- **Register** (FR-01): tool, equipment or spare items with an automatic QR tag (`ERP:A:<tag>`), serial, make, model, purchase reference, value, store, condition, calibration due and inspection interval; registered by the keeper and approved by the store manager; CSV-style import makes drafts; an approved entry is corrected by sending it back.
- **Custody** (FR-02, BR-01): issue and transfer wait for a six-digit code told to the receiver's own phone (or a keeper-recorded signature for a subcontractor); return with condition; an overdue calibration, or a broken tool, is not issued.
- **Inspection, calibration, repair** (FR-03): recorded with certificate number, laboratory and next due date; a calibration-due report and a daily reminder.
- **Loss and damage** (FR-04): report with the investigation, recovery from salary or from a subcontractor or write-off; construction manager, project manager, and for salary the HR manager; the approved recovery is an event for payroll or the subcontractor bill.
- **Custody statement** (FR-05): what a person holds, what waits for their code, recoveries from salary, with a PDF; read by HR, the store manager and the person.
- **Disposal** (FR-06): scrap, sale or write-off after the store manager and project manager; events carry the value and whether it passes the capitalisation threshold.
- **API**: 40 routes under `/api/v1/inv`. **Browser**: Small Asset Register (with the asset and the loss reports), Issue or Return by QR (mobile, with the employee's own confirmation), Custody Statement and Calibration Due.

# Part 166.8 completion: statutory updates retrofit — GST 2.0 rates, Labour Codes, Income-tax Act 2025 references

Part: 166.8. Date: 2026-11-17. Modules M06 (tax masters), M07 (HR), M21/M22 (commercial). Feature flag `ff.mdm.p16608` (OFF by default; ON in
development and test). Runs after 166.7, before 167.

Status: **In progress** — built and tested behind `ff.mdm.p16608`; the values were confirmed against published copies of the notifications, not the
official PDFs (outside the build environment's network), so the flag stays OFF in production until the CFO and HR check them (D-697); entries that
could not be confirmed are not seeded (D-698, D-699).

## 1. Discovery
`docs/erp/DISCOVERY/166.8.md`: on `main`, GST12 and GST28 open-ended and no GST40, cement (2523) at 28 % with no end; the sub bill falls back to
the section number `194C`; only 1961-Act sections and no form master; no pay components or pay structure; contract-labour screens named after the
repealed Act and no state-rule master.

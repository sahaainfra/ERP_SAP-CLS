# Part 14.01 completion: Work fronts, daily work plans and work authorisation

Part: 14.01. Date: 2026-11-22. New module `site`, feature flag `ff.site.p1401` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 13.04 (approved weekly plans and their daily breakdown), 12.03 (the budget check) and 07.03 (crews, members, deployments); reads activities, quantities, locations and norms (13.01, 13.02), drawings and IFC revisions (11.01) and the constraint log (13.04). Provides `site.wa.approved`, `site.wa.closed`, `site.daily_plan.issued` and `GET /api/v1/site/work-authorisations/active` to material issue (16.04), plant (17.03), attendance (07.05), the DPR (14.02) and progress (14.05).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items are in section 6.

`docs/erp/DISCOVERY/14.01.md`.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 192, 193; nothing existing altered): `site_work_fronts`, `site_daily_plans`, `site_daily_plan_lines`, `site_work_authorisations` (approval-controlled, with revisions, emergency and closure), `site_wa_resources`, `site_wa_uses`. Seed: the flag, four navigation entries under the existing Site group, four page-tracker rows, three notification templates (`site.wa.approved`, `site.wa.rejected`, `site.wa.regularise`) in English and Hindi.
- **Work fronts** (FR-01): a location of a site; its open activities from the current schedule, its status (open, blocked, in progress, completed) and the summary of its open constraints, worked out from the schedule and the constraint log.
- **Daily work plans** (FR-02): per site, day and supervisor, from the approved weekly plans of the day (13.04), copied from the supervisor's previous plan or empty; a line grid of activity, front, crew and quantity; issued, every line becomes a WA in draft (all or none, BR-02 checked line by line).
- **Work authorisations** (FR-03, ADR-488): activity, WBS, location and front (defaults from the activity), quantity and unit, crews and headcount by trade, materials and plant from the norms of 13.02 (or by hand), budget amount checked by 12.03, drawing revision checked against the current IFC revision, permit, hold points, time window, supervisor; the checks are kept on the WA and shown in a checks panel. BR-02: the quantity cannot pass what remains of the activity (with 5 % tolerance).
- **Approval** (FR-04, section 9): workflow type `SITE_WA`, the construction manager; the project manager too when the work is outside the weekly plan or an emergency. The raiser never decides. CP-SITE-01 (BR-01) is checked at submit and again in the approval hook: WARN by default, a block in ENFORCE. Approved WAs are locked; a change is a revision that supersedes it when approved; uses follow the revision.
- **Emergency WA** (FR-05, ADR-489): for today, with a reason, usable at once (announced on creation), regularised through CM and PM within 24 hours; overdue regularisation is flagged on the board and the WA.
- **Closure** (FR-04, BR-03): the quantity done (the DPR quantity booked against the WA by default) and each resource's use; a material off by more than 10 % needs the reason; `site.wa.closed` carries the plan against the actual.
- **Uses** (FR-07, ADR-490): `POST /site/wa-uses` books attendance, material issue, plant log, DPR or measurement against a WA, once per source; without a live WA, on another day, or beyond what it authorises (5 %), CP-SITE-02 asks for an approved exception (ENFORCE, the default) or warns.
- **Board and phone** (FR-06, FR-04): lanes by front with the WA cards of the day, drag of a draft to another front checked by the server, undo, status filter, compact view, legend, print; the phone screen lists my approval tasks with their checks (approve, return, reject through the workflow engine's own task) and the approved WAs I can close.
- **API**: 30 routes under `/api/v1/site`, in the route policy (every non-GET audited) and `openapi.yaml`; optimistic locking on updates; `Idempotency-Key` required on create, submit, amend, close, daily plan create and issue.
- **Permissions**: `site.work_authorisation.{view,create,edit,delete,submit,approve,cancel,amend,print,export,close}`, `site.work_front.{view,manage}`, `site.daily_plan.{view,manage}`, `site.wa.use`. SE raises, sends and closes; CM also approves; PM approves, cancels, prints and exports; WSUP and CSUP read crews and plans; QCE, HSEE and QCMGR read the checks; SK and PMSUP book uses; PLAN keeps fronts; PD, PCMGR, DIR, AUD and QS read (with print or export as listed).
- **Browser**: Site Work Board, Work Authorisations (list and the WA with its checks, resources, uses, closure, files, discussion and history), Daily Work Plan builder, Approve and Close WA (phone); English and Hindi.
- **Performance**: `npm run erp:site-wa-perf` (50 projects, 100,000 WAs with 200,000 resources and 100,000 uses): list first page p95 61 ms, approved of a site 2 ms, a day of a project 2 ms, search 133 ms; one WA 4 ms; board of a site 7 ms; form options 9 ms; a WA raised with its checks 7 ms; a use booked 3 ms; a closure 3 ms (limits 500 ms for a list, 300 ms for a record, 800 ms for a save). The query plans are printed and use the indexes.
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 102, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, ADR-488 to ADR-490, D-482 to D-484, openapi.

## 6. Known limitations
D-482: attendance, stores, plant, DPR and measurement do not call the WA check yet (those modules are not built); they book through `POST /site/wa-uses`. D-483: the approval hook re-checks the drawing and the permit, the budget as checked at submit. D-484: permits and hold points are references until 19.02 and 18.02; the 24-hour regularisation is flagged, not escalated by a job; no workbook import of WAs. `docs/erp/openapi.yaml` does not parse as strict YAML at earlier lines (unquoted colons in older summaries); the new paths follow the file's style. The delegation and escalation of the approval are those of the workflow engine (04.01) and were not tested again here.

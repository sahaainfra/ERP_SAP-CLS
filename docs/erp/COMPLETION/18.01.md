## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 262, 263): `qa_quality_plans`, `qa_itps`, `qa_checklist_templates`, `qa_inspections`, `qa_inspection_answers`, `qa_planned_points`; flag, four pages, tracker, templates. No existing table changed.
- **Rules**: numeric tolerance (target +/- or limits) fails a question; an auto-fail or critical failure fails the inspection; below the pass mark fails; any other failure is Conditional; not-applicable answers do not count (T-01). A photo-required question cannot be answered without a photograph (BR-02, enforced when sent). An approved template or ITP never changes: it is amended into a new version, the old one is superseded on approval (BR-01).
- **Approvals**: quality plan and ITP by the quality control manager; template by the owner of its module (HSE officer, plant manager, store manager) then the quality control manager; inspection by the quality control manager (quality, handover, snag), HSE officer, plant manager or store manager. The maker never approves.
- **Offline**: one command per inspection; photographs named `<question>__name` join the answers.
- **API**: 56 routes under `/api/v1/qa`; **browser**: ITP Library and Editor, Checklist Template Designer with preview, Perform Inspection, Inspections; English and Hindi.

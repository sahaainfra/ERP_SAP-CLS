# Part 05.01 completion: Unified Messages App

Part: 05.01. Date: 2026-11-04. Feature flag `ff.msg.p0501` (default off; on in development and test).

Status: **Ready for review, with the open items in section 6** (D-265 to D-269). Every statement below names the test or command that shows it.

## 1. Discovery summary
There was no Team Chat or secure messaging store in this repository (only the gateway of 04.07); so unification here means one thread index over the new conversations and the gateway's external threads, read in place. Reused: the real-time rooms (the reserved `conv:<id>` kind is now live), notifications, the file service, the object type registry (record access, summaries, a new `search` hook), assignments, the parameter engine and the audit trail. Full table: `docs/erp/DISCOVERY/05.01.md`.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 86 and 87, nothing existing altered): `msg_conversations`, `msg_members`, `msg_messages`, `msg_reactions`; seed: the flag, one Messages navigation entry, two tracker rows, the notifications `msg.message` and `msg.mention` (English and Hindi).
- **Module `msg`** (`modules/msg`: service, routes, validators, index; registered in `modules/registry.js`): conversations of every type, membership and automatic membership from assignments, messages (replies, mentions of members only, files, voice notes, record links, idempotent by client id, forward), edit and delete with the window, reactions, per-person settings (pin, mute, mention-only, archive, read mark), the indexed external threads, record picker, migration check, compliance export, purge job.
- **API** `/api/v1/msg/*` (22 routes, all in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`); real-time events `msg.message.created|updated|deleted` and `msg.conversation.updated` (ids only) in rooms `conv:<id>` and `user:<name>`.
- **Browser**: `platform/messages` (hooks and the shared components) and `modules/msg`: Messages (three panes on a computer, two on a tablet, one on a phone), Conversation details (third pane or drawer), New conversation, the mini chat dock and small windows over any page.
- **Permissions**: none for direct and group messaging; `msg.channel.manage`, `msg.admin.view`, `msg.compliance.export`. Parameters `msg.edit_window_minutes` (15), `msg.deleted_content_retention_days` (30), `msg.group_max_members` (250).
- Documents: ARCHITECTURE section 50, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, schema lock, DECISIONS ADR-221 to 225, DEFECTS D-265 to D-269, openapi, PROGRESS.csv (row 44).

## 7. How to verify manually
1. Open **Messages**. Choose **New**, A person, search a name, **Start**; write a message and **Send**.
2. Attach a photo, link a record with the record button (search the number), send: the record appears as a card with its status.
3. Sign in as the other person: the conversation is first with an unread count; open it, reply; the first person sees the reply with no reload.
4. As a person without the right to the record, open the same conversation: the card says "Restricted record". Delete your own message within 15 minutes: both sides see "message deleted".
5. As a project manager choose **New**, A project channel: the people assigned to the project are its members; end an assignment and the person loses the channel at their next visit.
6. On a computer press the small-window button on a conversation and go to another page: the chat stays over the page and the dock shows the unread count. At 360 px the list and the conversation are separate screens with a back button.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 270, 271): `qa_pour_cards`, `qa_pour_tickets`, `qa_cube_samples`, `qa_cube_results`; flag, four pages, tracker, templates. No existing table changed.
- **Pour card** (BR-01): linked to the approved inspection requests for reinforcement, shuttering and embedments and the pre-pour checks; chain quality engineer then construction manager. After approval: tickets (batch, truck, quantity, slump) add up to the volume poured; the pour is completed.
- **Cubes**: sample numbers, QR label, 7 and 28-day due dates, results by age; a daily job tells tests due. A testing machine out of calibration is refused (18.03).
- **IS 456 acceptance** (T-01): individual result at 28 days against fck - 3; groups of four 28-day results against the greater of fck + 0.825 s (s rounded to 0.5, assumed by grade) and fck + 3. A failure is announced (`qa.cube.failed`) and raised as an NCR with core tests.
- **Traceability** from batch, ticket, truck, sample or pour card to the cubes and results.
- **API**: 27 routes; **browser**: Pour Cards (register, card, new), Cube Register (with QR), Concrete Strength Acceptance, Concrete Traceability; English and Hindi.

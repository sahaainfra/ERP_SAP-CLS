## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 227, 228; up, down and up again clean): `inv_gate_appointments`, `inv_gate_inward`, `inv_gate_weighments`, `inv_gate_passes`; flag, five pages under the Stores group, page tracker, notification templates. No existing table changed.
- **Gate entry** (FR-01, FR-02): vehicle, driver, transporter, vendor, PO, challan, e-way bill, material, photos of the vehicle and the challan, time in. The server checks the PO (open, vendor, project and site, items with a balance, order not fully received, repeated challan). A clean entry is recorded at once; a flagged one is held and goes to the store manager through the workflow engine (never the guard who entered it), who accepts, returns or refuses it.
- **Weighbridge** (FR-03, BR-02): gross at the gate, tare after unloading, net and variance against the challan worked out on the server; tolerance by parameter `inv.gate.weight_tolerance_pct` (2%); a difference beyond it flags the entry and tells stores and procurement (event and notification). Slip photo filed with the entry; device source with a reference.
- **Unloading and handover** (FR-04): the store is assigned (16.01 store rules), the handover is refused while the weighing is half done, then the event `inv.gate.handed_over` carries the entry, order, store and net quantity.
- **Outbound gate pass** (FR-05, BR-01): returnable, non-returnable, scrap and vendor return; approval CM or STOREMGR, then PM for non-returnable and scrap; the QR code appears at approval, is verified once by the guard (right vehicle, within validity), and an amendment issues a new code.
- **Returnables** (FR-06): tracked until marked back; the overdue filter and a daily job (08:00 India time) tell the requester, the store manager and the security supervisor once a day.
- **Register and turnaround** (FR-07): gate register of vehicles in and passes out with the figures of the day, turnaround average and slowest vehicles; appointments (also created from a dispatched shipment; missed ones marked by the daily job).
- **API**: 39 routes (inward, passes, appointments, gate options, dashboard, lookups); 2,762 routes in all, each with a policy entry, writes audited. **Browser**: Gate Entry (mobile), Gate Exit Verification (mobile, QR by camera or typed), Gate Register, Returnable Gate Passes, Gate Pass Request (form and object).

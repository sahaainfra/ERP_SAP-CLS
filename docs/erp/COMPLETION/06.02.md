## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 98 and 99, nothing existing altered): twelve tables with the standard columns, `effective_from` and `effective_to`, a row status and a link to a change request. A row with no company is a system row (ADR-250).
- **Seed data:**
  - 37 states and union territories with their GST codes;
  - 8 currencies, 7 GST rates (from 2017-07-01), 15 construction HSN/SAC codes with default rates and reverse charge, 7 TDS/TCS sections (194C, 194J, 194Q, 194I(a), 194I(b), 194H, 206C(1H));
  - 29 units in six categories with factors to their base unit;
  - 13 payment terms, delivery terms and Incoterms (one with milestones);
  - 9 lookup lists (priorities, document categories, disciplines, trades, skills, defect categories, delay causes, weather types, incident types) with Hindi labels.
- **Engine** (`modules/mdm/common.js`): one definition per master with its fields, key, steward permission, and whether it is dated and needs approval. It provides:
  - **Reading:** the company's view of every master (its own rows over system rows, by date for dated masters), with filters, search and "as of" a date.
  - **Changes:** a non-statutory change is saved at once and audited; changing a system row gives the company its own row.
  - **Statutory changes** (BR-01, ADR-251): GST rates, HSN/SAC and TDS sections change only by a proposal from a date. It is approved by another accounts manager and then the CFO (workflow `MDM_TAX_CHANGE`, also in the approval-matrix defaults), with evidence for the approver. Approval ends the company's previous row the day before; return, resubmit, reject and withdraw all work.
  - **Values on a date:** the GST rate of a code or HSN/SAC code, the TDS section, and the exchange rate (latest on or before the date).
  - **Unit conversion** (BR-02, ADR-252): exact, within a category, through the base unit.
  - **Field rules:**
    - IGST equals CGST plus SGST;
    - a unit's base must be the base unit of its category;
    - the advance plus milestones of a payment term add up to 100%;
    - the rupee has 2 decimals and no exchange rate to itself;
    - IFSC must have the right form (T-02).
  - **Lookup lists** in the person's language (T-03, ADR-253), and saving a whole list that never deletes a value.
- **Integration:**
  - **Form lookups:** `/api/v1/mdm/lookups/<name>` for every master and every list.
  - **Excel:** an import type and an export source per master (FR-09); a statutory import row is a proposal.
  - **Events and checks:** event `mdm.common.changed`; the integrity checks know the system rows.
  - **Partner form (AC-01):** the 06.01 partner form takes its State from the states master and checks the GSTIN state code.
- **API** `/api/v1/mdm/...`:
  - `common`, `common/:master`, `common/:master/:id`, `…/retire`;
  - `tax/gst`, `tax/tds`, `exchange-rate`, `uoms/convert`;
  - `lookup-lists`, `lookup-lists/:list`;
  - `lookups/:name`.

  All of these are in the route policy (writes audited) and in `openapi.yaml`.
- **Browser:**
  - a register page per master: Units of Measure, GST Rates, HSN/SAC Codes, TDS/TCS Sections, States & Places of Supply (with districts), Currencies & Exchange Rates, Banks & IFSC, Payment & Delivery Terms, Lookup Lists;
  - each register has filters, search, "as of", in force / waiting / all, paging 25/50/100, CSV export, a link to the Import Center, a row panel with history and change requests, and a form;
  - the Lookup List Manager;
  - the partner form's State select.
- **Permissions:**

  | Permission | Roles |
  |---|---|
  | read (`mdm.record.view`, from 06.01) | every internal role |
  | `mdm.tax.edit`, `mdm.finance.edit` | ACCMGR |
  | `mdm.uom.edit` | PROCMGR |
  | `mdm.lookup.edit` | SYSADM |

  Tax approvals go to the ACCMGR and CFO roles through the workflow.
- **Documents:** ARCHITECTURE section 56, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, DECISIONS ADR-250 to 253, DEFECTS D-294 to D-298, openapi, PROGRESS.csv (row 50), screenshots in `docs/erp/screenshots/common-masters/`.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. **Propose a rate change:** as an accounts manager open **HSN/SAC Codes** and search 2523. Choose **Open**, then **Propose a change from a date**: default GST rate GST18, a date a few days ahead and the notification as justification. Send it.
2. **Approve it:** another accounts manager, then the CFO, approve it in their approval inbox.
3. **Check the dates:** on **HSN/SAC Codes** set **As of** to the day before the date (GST28), then to the date (GST18). The row panel shows both versions in its history.
4. **Check IFSC:** on **Banks & IFSC** add a bank with IFSC `SBIN001234`; the field error appears. With `SBIN0001234` it saves.
5. **Edit a lookup list:** as the system administrator open **Lookup List Manager** and choose Weather types. Add `DUST` with a Hindi label, give a reason and save.
6. **See Hindi labels:** a person whose language is Hindi gets the Hindi labels in forms.
7. **Use the states master on a form:** on **Master Records**, New business partner: State is a list from the states master. A GSTIN starting with 27 and the state Gujarat is refused.

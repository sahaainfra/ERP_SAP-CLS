# Part 27.10 — Role Dashboards Consolidation

Execution step 204 · 2026-11-21 · Branch `ccr-6934092b-qrkoec` · **In progress**: built and tested behind `ff.bi.p2710`. Formal acceptance waits for 27.04 and for the company G1/G2 sign-offs.

## 1. Discovery

All 129 widgets were already on the 03.07 framework, each with a provider. Only 12 roles had a default dashboard (Home insights). Seven widgets duplicated an overview card, and the brief's example widgets for site, stores and accounts did not exist. The decisions are in the [discovery note](../DISCOVERY/27.10.md).

## 2. What was built

**Role dashboards (FR-02)**: dashboard `role-dashboard` on the 03.07 framework, with a layout for each of the 44 roles of the catalogue.

- It resolves the usual way: the person's own copy first, then the role default approved in the Dashboard Designer (maker-checker), then the role's code layout.
- Every card in a role's layout is one the role may use; the inventory checks this for every role.
- The page is `/bi/role-dashboard` (**Reports → My Role Dashboard**). Customise, drill on every card and the refresh are those of the framework.

Examples:

| Role | Cards |
|---|---|
| Site engineer | Waiting for my decision, WAs today, DPR on time, MRs open, RFIs open, notifications, quick actions |
| Storekeeper | GRNs awaiting approval, issued today, below reorder, pending, … |
| Accounts manager | Payments due in 7 days, bank lines not reconciled, month-close tasks open, … |
| Director | Executive alerts, the certified EAC, revenue, margin, SPI, CPI, DSO and LTIFR, outstanding, … |

**New widgets**: eight widgets, each reading its owner's records in the person's project and site scope, with a drill to the list behind it:

- `site.wa_today`, `proc.mr_open`, `engg.rfi_open`;
- `inv.grn_pending`, `inv.issues_today`;
- `fin.payments_due`, `fin.bank_unmatched`, `fin.close_tasks`.

**Duplicates removed (FR-03)**: seven older widgets are retired in favour of the overview cards that read the same services:

| Retired | Replaced by |
|---|---|
| `bill.certified_not_billed` | `ov.qs.certified_not_billed` |
| `bill.unbilled_wip` | `ov.qs.unbilled_wip` |
| `hse.ltifr` | `ov.hse.ltifr` |
| `hse.safe_manhours` | `ov.hse.safe_hours` |
| `qa.first_time_ir` | `ov.qa.first_time_ir` |
| `qa.open_ncr` | `ov.qa.open_ncr` |
| `hr.headcount` | `ov.hr.headcount` |

A new alias registry (`widgetAliases`) shows the replacement in any saved layout that still names a retired widget.

**Dashboard Inventory (FR-01)** at `/bi/dashboard-inventory`, a technical console report for SYSADM, ITADM, SUPADM, AUD and DIR:

- parameters: module, status and source;
- a chart of widgets by module (drillable), and tiles for total, OK, to review, retired, certified-metric widgets, roles covered and scan findings;
- three views:
  - **widgets**: source, permission, the dashboards and role dashboards using it, status and drill;
  - **role coverage**: per role, its cards, the usable ones and any it cannot use;
  - **dashboards**: layouts, role defaults and personal copies.
- export to XLSX, CSV and PDF (audited).

**No hard-coded figures (BR-01)**: `scanSources` scans the client pages and the server widget providers for:

- KPI tiles with a literal value;
- sample, mock or demo data;
- placeholder text;
- providers returning a literal figure.

It shows on the inventory page, runs again with **Scan again**, and is part of T-01. Result: **0 findings in 1,394 files**.

## 4. Tests

`tests/bi-role-dashboards.test.js` has three tests, all passing:

1. **Scan**: clean on the codebase, and it detects a planted literal tile, mock data and a literal provider.
2. **Every role**: each of the 44 roles resolves its role dashboard and every card loads. Duplicates are retired, and a saved layout naming `hse.ltifr` shows `ov.hse.ltifr`.
3. **New widgets and inventory**:
   - Today's WAs count only submitted and approved WAs, not drafts.
   - Payments due counts only bills due within 7 days, net of what was paid.
   - The accounts widget is refused to an SE.
   - The inventory: no widget without a provider, 7 retired, certified sources, every role fully usable, a clean scan, the status filter and the export.
   - SE gets 403 on the inventory, and the flag off gives 404.

`e2e/bi-role-dashboards.spec.js` passes on desktop and mobile (4 tests).

Regression: `widget-api`, `hse-kpi`, `qa-dash`, `bill-qsdash` (the retired widgets' providers still answer their services), `p360`, `authz-api`, `i18n-api` (namespace `rd`), lint, typecheck (0 errors), route, nav, schema lint, migration up/down and the permission audit.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set `ff.bi.p2701` to `ff.bi.p2703` and `ff.bi.p2710` and record G1 and G2.
2. Sign in as a site engineer and open **Reports → My Role Dashboard**: WAs today, DPR on time, MRs open and RFIs open, each opening its list. Repeat as a storekeeper and an accounts manager.
3. Press **Customise**, move a card and save. The page shows "your own layout"; reset brings the role default back.
4. As SYSADM, open **Reports → Dashboard Inventory**:
   - filter Status = Retired to see the seven duplicates and their replacements;
   - open **Role coverage**;
   - press **Scan again**: "No hard-coded figures".

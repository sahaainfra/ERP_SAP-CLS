## 2. What was built

The internal `subcontractorScope` guard reuses the live 25.01 session, its active partner contact and the session’s company gate checks. It requires an `X-SUB` partner account and company enablement of `ff.portal.p2503`, then returns a frozen grant with the account, company, partner, project and site IDs. The feature flag defaults to off. Business routes, navigation and writes are deferred under the prerequisite rule.

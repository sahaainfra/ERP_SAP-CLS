## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 94 and 95, nothing existing altered): `pol_policies`, `pol_versions`, `pol_acknowledgements`, `pol_requirements`; seed: flag, four navigation entries, four tracker rows, five notification templates (en, hi).
- **Module `pol`** (`modules/pol`, ADR-240 to 244): the register and the lifecycle (Draft, In approval, Published, Active, Under revision, Superseded, Archived) with numbers `POL-nnnn`; versions with rich text cleaned on the server, attachments, change summary, quiz and pass mark, applicability, effective and review dates; a published version is never edited (a revision is a new version through the approval again); comparison of two versions by paragraph and word; approval workflow `POL_VERSION` (department head, then director; the owner cannot approve), also seeded in the approval matrix; publication resolves the people it applies to (everyone, departments, roles, sites, projects: any of them), notifies them and tracks acknowledgement; acknowledgement with a knowledge check (pass mark, repeat tries); the hourly job `pol.cycle` (effective dates, people who come into scope later, reminders every three days, escalation once at the due date to the owner and the managers on the escalation path, review reminders); archive.
- **Policy-to-protocol**: the owner tags clauses as requirements (validated by the engine that will own them); after approval **Make a draft** creates a control point that is **OFF**, an approval-matrix **DRAFT** version with the new rule first, or an alert rule that is **switched off**; each is written by its own engine through a registry (`platform/protocol/drafts.js`, `modules/eng/policy-drafts.js`), linked back by a trace link, and listed on the administrators' worklist with the state it has in its engine; only that engine's own screen and approval activate it (BR-02).
- **API** `/api/v1/pol/*` (21 routes, all in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`); events `pol.policy.published|superseded|archived`, `pol.acknowledgement.recorded|overdue`, `pol.requirement.converted`.
- **Browser** (`modules/pol`): Policy Register with the policy page (versions, acknowledgements with CSV, compare), Policy Editor (rich text, attachments, dates, applicability with a live count, quiz, requirements, compare, autosave every 30 seconds), My Policies (read, acknowledge, quiz, earlier versions), Policy-to-Protocol Drafts (worklist).
- **Permissions**: `pol.policy.create` and `pol.requirement.manage` (HRMGR, CFO, QCMGR, PROCMGR, HSEE, ACCMGR, COMMGR, PCMGR); `pol.policy.view_all` (those and DIR, AUD, SYSADM); `pol.draft.view` (SYSADM, ITADM, DIR). Reading and acknowledging a policy that applies to a person needs no permission.
- Documents: ARCHITECTURE section 54, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, DECISIONS ADR-240 to 244, DEFECTS D-285 to D-289, openapi, PROGRESS.csv (row 48), screenshots in `docs/erp/screenshots/policies/`.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As an HR manager open **Policy Register, New policy**: title, category, write the text, set the effective and review dates, tick the role **EMP** (the count shows how many people), add a knowledge check question, **Save as a draft**, **Send for approval**.
2. The department head (for example the CFO) and then the director approve it in their approvals inbox; the policy becomes **Active** and the employees are notified.
3. As an employee open **My Policies**: the policy waits; **Read and acknowledge**, answer the question wrongly (it asks you to try again), then rightly.
4. As the HR manager open the policy: the version shows 1 of N acknowledged, the table lists who has not; **Export CSV**. **Start a new version**, change the text, say what changed, send it for approval; after approval open **Compare versions**: the changed words are marked.
5. As the owner of a policy open its **Requirements for controls** tab: tag clause 4.2 as a control point; **Make a draft** is available after approval. As the system administrator open **Policy-to-Protocol Drafts**: the draft control point is listed as inactive; **Review in control point screen** opens it in the control engine, where you put it in observe mode.

# Part 14.04 completion: Site Instructions & Site Memos

Part: 14.04. Date: 2026-11-25. Module `site`, flag `ff.site.p1404` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 14.01 (sites, crews, activities) and 11.05 (letters, read and linkable). Provides variation requests (21.05), back-charges (22.05) and letter links (11.05) as events and links.

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items in section 6.

## 1. Discovery
`docs/erp/DISCOVERY/14.04.md`.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 198, 199; schema-diff of existing objects clean): `site_instructions`, `site_instruction_acks`; seed of the flag, two navigation entries, two page-tracker rows, four notification templates (English, Hindi).
- **Types and fields** (FR-01, FR-02): EI, CI (received), ISI (issued), MEMO; number from the numbering series, date, from/to, subject, description, location, activity, compliance due date, cost and time flags, letter, attachments.
- **Received** (FR-03): registered by CM or PM; a cost or time implication sets the notice due date (7 days, parameter) and publishes `site.instruction.variation_requested` (variation draft for 21.05) with a notification to PM and COMMGR; the letter can be linked.
- **Issued** (FR-04): recipients are subcontractors, crews or a person; each acknowledges and then complies with a note and at least one evidence file; recipients can be added later. A back-charge (subcontractor and amount, in paise, masked without `site.instruction.cost`) sends the instruction CM, then PM before it is issued, and publishes `site.instruction.backcharge`.
- **BR-01**: an instruction with a cost implication cannot close before the variation decision (accepted, rejected or withdrawn, by the commercial manager); an issued one cannot close before every recipient has complied. Open instructions are locked; amendment replaces.
- **Register and tracker** (FR-05): received/issued tabs, filters (overdue, cost, back-charge), tracker tiles (open, awaiting acknowledgement, awaiting compliance, variation pending, notice due, overdue), daily reminders job; export XLSX, CSV, PDF; print and PDF of one instruction.
- **API**: 21 routes in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`; optimistic locking; `Idempotency-Key` on create, submit, amend, close, variation decision, acknowledge and comply.
- **Browser**: Site Instructions (register, tracker, instruction with notice prompt, variation decision, recipients, close, approval) and Issue Instruction (phone form, 44 px targets); English and Hindi.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 277, 278): `hse_permit_types`, `hse_permits`; flag, five pages, tracker, templates `hse.permit.decided`, `hse.permit.alert`.
- **Permits**: request, issue by HSE engineer, authorisation by the construction manager for high risk, activation, extension, suspension (also automatic on a failed gas reading), resume, closure with handback, expiry sweep, amendment, PDF, CSV.
- **Checks**: certifications, induction (when blocking), gas tests, isolation, checklist, job safety analysis, conflicts.
- **Work authorisation**: `PERMIT_TO_WORK` check.
- **Pages**: Request Permit (phone), Permit Approvals, Active Permits Board, Permit Register and object, Permit Types; English and Hindi.
- **API**: 40 routes under `/api/v1/hse/permits` and `/permit-types`.

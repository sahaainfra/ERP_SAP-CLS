## 7. How to verify manually
1. As the subcontractor coordinator open **Register Worker**: a date of birth under 18 is refused on the screen; give the details and send; the supervisor level and the HR manager approve in the approval inbox.
2. As HR open the worker: record induction and medical clearance and PPE; open **Labour Contractors**, set the licence and the licensed number; open **Print Labour ID Cards**, pick the worker and make the card (QR).
3. Deploy the worker (Deployments, 07.03): refused without clearance or a licence, or above the licensed number; at the site kiosk scan or type the card: the punch is recorded as the worker's.
4. Open **Wage Rates & Minimum Wages**: set the minimum wage of a state; a contract rate below it is refused.

# Part 04.05 completion: Accountability Matrix (RACI) and Action Ledger

Status: **Ready for review, with the open items below** (the WBS scope, the ledger feeds of the modules from M07 onward, the e-mail schedule of the report, a check of names against the user list and the translation of the pages are later parts: D-202 to D-206). Feature flag `ff.eng.p0405` (OFF by default; ON in development and test).

## What was found
No RACI and no ledger existed. The workflow engine and its matrix, the event outbox, the audit trail, the protocol engine's exceptions and the permission engine were reused; `platform/accountability` is the new shared piece.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 64 and 65; nothing existing altered): `acc_raci_templates`, `acc_raci_assignments`, `acc_action_ledger` (append-only by trigger), `acc_ledger_cursors`; reference seed: the flag, 15 standard processes with their usual R, A, C and I, three navigation entries, three tracker rows and the `accountability.mine` widget.
- **RACI** (`GET /api/v1/acc/raci`, `POST /acc/raci/changes`, `POST /acc/raci/apply-template`, `/acc/raci/gaps`, `/acc/raci/resolve`, `/acc/raci/history`, `/acc/raci/scopes`, `/acc/raci/templates`): a change names the whole answer for each process it touches, with exactly one Accountable who is a person (BR-01) and any number of Responsible; the old rows end the day before the new date. A scope that is a live project changes only through the approval `ACC_RACI`, project manager and then project director (default matrix row and an equivalent default definition); every other scope changes at once; one change waits per scope. A template needs a person for each accountable role.
- **Gap check**: for every project not closed, the processes nobody is accountable for (an answer at company level counts) and those with more than one by a fault of the data.
- **Resolution** for 04.08, 04.02 and 07.11: `accountabilityOf(db).resolve` and `GET /acc/raci/resolve`; the site, then the project, then the department, then the company.
- **Ledger** (`GET /api/v1/acc/ledger`): read, from where each feed stopped, from the workflow events (submitted, approved, verified, rejected, returned, cancelled, with the role, the due time and whether it was on time), from the exceptions raised, and from the audit trail of the sample document and of the use of an exception; a module writes with `ledgerSources.onEvent`, `onAudit` or `record()`. Each source row and action is written once. Updates and deletes are refused (BR-02). A person sees their own actions; others' need `acc.ledger.view`, within the project scope.
- **Scores** (`GET /api/v1/acc/scores`, `/acc/scores/export`): by person, role or project: actions, on time, late, exceptions raised, returns and rejections received, overdue open items, and a figure for a review. Every answer and export says that it is an input to the review of 07.11 and never a penalty (ADR-180).
- **Pages**: RACI Matrix (`/admin/raci`: a scope picker, the grid of processes against people and roles, apply the standard processes, change with a reason and a date, the pending approval, the gap check), My Accountability (`/accountability`: tiles, what I answer for, mine past their time, what I did lately), Accountability Report (`/reports/accountability`: parameters, chart, table with totals, drill-down to the actions, export XLSX, CSV and PDF).
- **Permissions**: `acc.raci.view`, `acc.raci.maintain` (SYSADM, PM, PD, HRMGR), `acc.ledger.view`, `acc.report.view`; My Accountability needs none.
- Route policy, OpenAPI, ARCHITECTURE section 39, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, the schema lock, DECISIONS (ADR-178 to 180), DEFECTS (D-202 to D-206), PROGRESS.csv (row 33).

## Click-path for a reviewer
1. As the HR manager open **Administration > RACI Matrix**, choose a project that is not yet live: the gap check says 15 processes have nobody accountable. **Apply the standard processes**, name who holds PM, PD, STOREMGR, CFO, COMMGR and HRMGR: "Applied: 15", the gap check says 0.
2. Add a column for a person; set them Responsible for **Purchase order**; **Save**. Make a second person Accountable for the same process and save: the server says exactly one. **Discard the changes**.
3. For a live project the same change says "Send for approval"; it waits for the project manager and then the project director (**My Approvals**), and the grid says nothing else can be changed meanwhile.
4. Raise and approve a **Sample Document** as a project director and project manager. Open **My Accountability** as the project manager: the processes you answer for, your approval in "What I did lately", on time 100 %. Make a task late (a document waiting long enough): it shows under "Mine, past their time".
5. As the auditor open **Accountability Report**: scores by person, role and project; open a row to see the actions behind it; **Export XLSX**.
6. As a store keeper: your own page is empty with a message, the other pages say you have no permission.

- **AC-01** templates applied to a project and My Accountability populated from real actions. **AC-02** page checks. **AC-03** permission and isolation tests. **AC-04** a live project's RACI can be changed only through its approval; the hooks are the only writers of its status. **AC-05** see Results. **AC-06** this report.

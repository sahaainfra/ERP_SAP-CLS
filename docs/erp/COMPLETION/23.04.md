## 4. Click-path
1. As CFO: Finance → Company Bank Accounts → **New bank account**: HDFC current account, IFSC, account number, ledger 150002, bulk CSV, cheques 100001–100050.
2. As FINEX: Finance → Payment Proposals → **New payment proposal**: the bank, NEFT, today → **Next**: tick the bill of Deccan Cement Traders → **Next**: one line, ₹1,16,000, "Approved by the accounts manager" → **Save and send for approval**.
3. The accounts manager approves (from the inbox or the run page). On the run page the accounts manager sees "You approved this proposal: another person releases it."
4. As CFO: open the run → **Release** → **Download the bank file**.
5. As FINEX: on the run, enter the UTR → **Paid** (or upload the bank's response file): the run is Confirmed, the advice goes to the vendor's e-mail.
6. Payments & Cheque Register: the payment with its UTR; the cheque register of the bank account.

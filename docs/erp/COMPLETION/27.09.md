# Part 27.09 — Wastage, Leakage & Duplication Detection Tower

Execution step 203 · 2026-11-21 · Branch `ccr-6934092b-qrkoec` · **In progress**: built and tested behind `ff.bi.p2709`. Formal acceptance waits for 27.03 and for the company G1/G2 sign-offs.

## 1. Discovery

The modules already flag some of their own records (fuel anomalies, attendance anomalies, material reconciliation over the norm, PO rates against a benchmark), and the control engine (04.04) records exceptions. Nothing valued these signals together, routed them to an owner or recorded an outcome. The decisions are in the [discovery note](../DISCOVERY/27.09.md).

## 2. What was built

**Rule library (FR-01)**: 13 rules, each reading its owner's records read-only, with default thresholds that change only by maker-checker.

| Rule | Source | Value at risk | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duplicate vendor invoice | AP bills (23.03): same vendor and invoice number, or same amount within 3 days | The smaller bill | RACI VENDOR_PAY → PM → ACCMGR |
| Duplicate payment | Payments: same payee and amount within 7 days | The amount | RACI VENDOR_PAY → ACCMGR |
| Split purchase orders | POs: same vendor and project, each below ₹5 lakh, together above it within 7 days | Their total | RACI PO_ISSUE → PM → PROCMGR |
| PO above benchmark | PO lines more than 10 % over the benchmark rate | (rate − benchmark) × qty | as above |
| Consumption above norm | Monthly material reconciliation (16.10) over the allowed wastage | Excess × average rate | RACI MAT_RECON → PM → STOREMGR |
| Fuel anomaly | Fuel anomalies (17.04) | Litres at risk × issue rate | PM → PLANTMGR |
| Attendance anomaly | Shared device, selfie mismatch, mock location or impossible travel, 3+ times for one person in 30 days | Days × daily wage | RACI ATTENDANCE → PM → HRMGR |
| Idle hired equipment | Plant logs of hired-in equipment, 16+ idle hours in 30 days | Idle hours × hourly hire rate | PM → PLANTMGR |
| Stock ageing | Stock ledger: no movement for 180 days | Stock value | RACI STOCK_ADJ → PM → STOREMGR |
| Unbilled work ageing | Approved DPR work older than 30 days with no measurement-book entry | Qty × BOQ rate | RACI RA_BILL → PM → QS |
| Overdue approval | Workflow tasks past their due time by 3 days | — | The approver |
| Emergency not regularised | Control exceptions (04.04) past their regularise-by date | Its value at risk | The person who raised it |
| Repeated exceptions | One person raising 5+ exceptions in 30 days | Their total | AUD |

The rules run as a background job daily at 05:30 IST (before the control tower and the morning report), or on demand from **Run the rules now**. Each rule runs on its own, so a failing rule is reported and the rest still run.

**Findings (FR-02)**: `det_findings`, one per piece of evidence. Each finding carries:

- the evidence, with links to the source records;
- the value at risk;
- severity and a due date (3, 7 or 14 days by severity);
- the owner and the basis on which they were chosen (record, RACI, project manager or role);
- a task (04.08), a notification and the event `bi.finding.raised`.

A rerun refreshes open findings. A closed finding is never raised again for the same evidence. Overdue findings are escalated once to PD and AUD (`bi.finding.escalated`).

**Outcome (BR-01)**: findings are advisory; nothing is blocked or penalised. A finding closes only with an outcome, and a database check enforces it:

- **confirmed loss**, with the amount;
- **prevented**;
- **false positive**, which goes through the workflow engine to the internal auditor (`BI_FINDING_FP`, owner → AUD). Return or reject sends it back to review, and the owner never approves.

**Rules configuration**: thresholds, severity and on/off change only by maker-checker. CFO or PCMGR proposes; AUD or CFO approves, never the requester. Each approved change is a new `det_rules` version and is audited. The false-positive rate per rule is shown beside it, so outcomes inform the thresholds.

**Tower (FR-03)** at `/bi/leakage` (**Reports → Leakage & Controls Tower**):

- **Tower tab**:
  - tiles for value at risk, overdue, confirmed loss, prevented, false-positive rate and findings awaiting the auditor;
  - cards for my pending actions, value at risk by type (drillable bars), findings against control exceptions per week, controls by mode (ENFORCE / WARN / OBSERVE / OFF), by project, and the largest open findings.
- **Findings tab**: the worklist with filters, the record with its evidence, and the review and close actions. It exports to XLSX, CSV and PDF.
- **Detection rules tab**: the rules, the change form, the pending changes and the run button.

Owners without a BI role see the Findings tab with their own findings. A widget and a Project 360 slot show the open findings.

## 4. Tests

`tests/bi-leakage.test.js` has three tests, all passing.

1. **T-01**: every rule on its seeded scenario, with exact values and owners (record, RACI, project manager, role holder), task and event. A rerun raises nothing new, and the RACI assignment takes precedence once it exists.
2. **BR-01 and §9**:
   - The loss amount is required, and a stale version gets 409.
   - Review, then close as confirmed loss.
   - A false positive is returned by AUD, resubmitted, then approved.
   - Closed findings are suppressed on the next run.
   - The tower figures, the export, the history, and the role checks.
3. **Maker-checker and scale**:
   - A threshold outside its bounds gets 400, a PD gets 403, the requester cannot approve, and AUD approves. The next run applies the new threshold.
   - The run is queued as a job, scope is enforced, and the flag off gives 404.
   - Volume: 100,000 findings; the list, the filtered list and the tower each stay well under the limit, and `EXPLAIN` uses an index.

`e2e/bi-leakage.spec.js` passes on desktop and mobile.

Regression, all passing:

- `authz-api` and `i18n-api` (namespace `lk`);
- lint, typecheck (0 errors), route, nav, schema lint, migration up/down, schema-diff, permission audit (`bi.det_rule.configure` added to the baseline for CFO and PCMGR) and the defect register.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set `ff.bi.p2701` to `ff.bi.p2703` and `ff.bi.p2709` and record G1 and G2.
2. As a CFO, open **Reports → Leakage & Controls Tower → Detection rules** and press **Run the rules now**.
3. On **Tower**, check the value at risk by type and the controls by mode, then click a bar to open its findings.
4. As the owner (for example the PM), open the finding from the task, press **Take into review**, and close it as **Prevented** with a resolution.
5. Close another as **False positive**. As AUD, approve it from the approval inbox; the finding shows Closed · False positive.
6. As a CFO, propose a threshold change on a rule. As AUD, approve it. The next run uses it.

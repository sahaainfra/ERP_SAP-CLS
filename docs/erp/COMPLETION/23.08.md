## 7. How to verify manually
1. As ACCMGR: Finance → GSTR-2B Reconciliation & ITC Review, month 2026-10 → **Choose the 2B file** (the GSTN JSON) → **Import and match**: the tiles show matched, differences and missing in 2B.
2. **Differences**: a card shows the books' and 2B's amounts → **Follow up with the vendor**; another → **Accept** with a note.
3. **ITC review** tab → **Review the ITC of 2026-10**: eligible, held, blocked, reversed; on a held invoice **Claim with a reason**; **Send to the CFO**. As CFO (phone): the review → **Approve**: the reversal is posted.
4. GST & TDS Returns → GSTR-1 / 3B: **Prepare** GSTR-3B, **Mark as filed** with the ARN. TDS statements: quarter 2026-27-Q3 shows Form 140; add the challan → **Save the challan and prepare** → **Mark as filed**; the Form 131 certificate of a deductee. Compliance calendar: GSTR-3B of October done.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 175, 176; nothing existing altered): `bud_changes`, `bud_change_lines`; seed: the flag, two navigation entries, three page-tracker rows, templates `bud.change.approved` and `bud.change.variation_draft`.
- **Server** (`modules/bud/changes.js`): options, impact preview, create, update, delete, submit, withdraw, cancel, raise again, history, print, list, export, import, the comparison of versions and its CSV export; applying an approved change as a new approved version with its phasing; workflows `BUD_CHANGE` and `BUD_DRAWDOWN` with evidence; consumer `bud.variation` of `ctr.variation.approved`; events `bud.change.approved` and `bud.budget.revised`.
- **API**: 17 routes under `/api/v1/bud` (changes, change-options, compare), in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`.
- **Permissions**: 10 keys `bud.change.*`; project control manager and project manager request; project director and director approve; quantity surveyor and commercial manager read.
- **Browser**: Budget Change Requests (list), Budget Change Request (form with the live impact preview, then the record with its lines before and after, approval and the version it made), Budget Version Comparison and History (report); English and Hindi.
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 94, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, ADR-448 to 453, D-459 to 461, openapi.

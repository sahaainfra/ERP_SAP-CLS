# Part 29.02 — Business Connectors: Banking, Accounting Export, Biometrics & Maps

Execution step 212 · 2026-11-22 · Branch `ccr-6934092b-qrkoec` · **In progress**: built and tested behind `ff.admin.p2902`. Formal acceptance waits for 01.08, 23.04 and the company G1/G2 sign-offs.

## 1. Discovery

The integration hub (01.08) is the connector engine, and the owners' services (23.06, 07.05, 17.07) receive the data. See the [discovery note](../DISCOVERY/29.02.md).

## 2. What was built

The page is `/admin/connectors` (**Administration → Business Connectors**), with pill tabs:

- **Connectors (FR-07)**: a card per business connector with its health, worked out from the hub:
  - Healthy, Failures in 24 h, Down (breaker open), Not checked, Not enabled;
  - the last call and its error, and the last sync;
  - **Test connection**.

  Each card offers the syncs of its type:
  - **Bank (FR-01)**:
    - **Fetch statement** for a bank account and period. The lines go into the 23.06 import, which skips lines seen before and auto-matches the rest to payments and receipts. The bank is asked by account code, never the stored account number.
    - **Validate account** (penny-drop). The name at the bank is compared with ours; a score of 60 % or more passes.
  - **Biometric (FR-03)**: **Pull punches**. Each punch is stored through the 07.05 device punch, under the approved device-user mapping, with a stable id. A punch pulled twice is stored once, and the punch rules (duplicate window, site, shift) apply.
  - **Telematics (FR-05)**: **Pull readings** into the 17.07 ingest, under the approved tracker mapping.
- **Export to Tally (FR-02)**: the approved journals of a period as Tally XML, with the ledgers they use, through the approved account mappings.
  - Journals with an unmapped account are skipped and listed.
  - The reconciliation (journals, exported, debit, credit, balanced) is shown, and the XML can be downloaded.
- **Field mappings**: IT proposes how our value matches the connected system's value. The mapping is used only after the data owner approves it on the shared approval engine:
  - accounts, GST rates and TDS sections: ACCMGR;
  - device users: HRMGR;
  - trackers: PLANTMGR.

  The maker cannot approve; an approval retires the mapping it replaces.
- **Sync runs**: every run with its counts, errors and outcome, filterable by status, exportable as Excel or CSV.

An hourly job pulls the biometric and telematics connectors set to `autoSync`, as the data owner's role.

**Inbound data (BR-01)**: every sync calls the owner's service as the person running it. So the statement import needs `fin.bank_statement.import`, and IT alone cannot import a statement (the run fails with the reason).

**Activation (section 9)**: bank activation keeps the hub's chain, ITADM then the CFO. A connector that is not enabled cannot be synced (422).

Labels are en/hi/te (area `con`).

**Permissions**:

| Role | Grant |
|---|---|
| ITADM | View, run, configure (propose mappings) |
| ACCMGR, FINEX | View, run, export |
| HRMGR, PLANTMGR | View, run (and approve their mappings) |
| CFO | View, export |
| AUD | View |

## 4. Tests

`tests/admin-connectors.test.js` has three tests, all passing. A local HTTP server plays the bank, the device bridge and the tracker provider, so the real hub path is used.

1. **Bank**: the statement sync, its matching and duplicate skip; the hub log; penny-drop pass and name mismatch; roles; a connector awaiting activation is refused.
2. **Biometric**: the mapping approved by HR (IT cannot approve its own); punches before and after approval; the duplicate and the replay.
3. **Export, telematics, health and runs**: the Tally export with one unmapped journal skipped and a balanced reconciliation; the XML content; telematics through the tracker mapping; health per connector; the runs report and its export; roles; a tracker cannot be mapped twice (409); 100,000 runs list in under 500 ms.

`e2e/admin-connectors.spec.js` passes on desktop and mobile:

1. The connectors with their health.
2. The statement fetch, which matches the payment.
3. A device-user mapping proposed by IT and approved by HR in the page.
4. The biometric pull, which stores the duplicate once.
5. The sync runs.

## 6. Known limitations

D-790:

- No payment file upload (H2H/SFTP), payment status callbacks or SFTP statement drops.
- No weighbridge connector: the hub has no weighbridge type (D-546).
- Weather and maps stay on the hub's existing connectors.
- GST rate and TDS section mappings are proposed by the company, not seeded.
- The runs report filters by status only.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set `ff.admin.p2902`.
2. In the integration hub, add a `bank_http` connector with its base URL and have it approved (ITADM, then the CFO).
3. As ACCMGR, open **Administration → Business Connectors**, choose the bank account and period, and click **Fetch statement**. The result shows the matched lines, and 23.06 shows the import.
4. As ITADM, under **Field mappings**, propose a device user → employee code mapping. As HRMGR, approve it.
5. **Pull punches** on the biometric connector twice; the second pull stores nothing new.

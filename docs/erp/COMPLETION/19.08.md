## What was built
- **Database** (migration 289): flag, the dashboard page, tracker, two widgets, template `hse.kpi.alert`. No new table: every figure is read live from the HSE tables and attendance, inside the data scope.
- **Lagging**: LTIFR, TRIR, severity rate, near misses per recordable incident, man-hours, safe man-hours since the last lost-time injury (a fatality also restarts it).
- **Leading**: toolbox talks held against expected, observations closed, inspections on time, PPE in date, induction cover, contractor scores, welfare.
- **Trend** by month, **site comparison**, **exceptions** with drill-down, pending HSE approvals, CSV export and the **monthly HSE report** PDF; the nightly check tells the HSE head once a month when the year LTIFR is over target and publishes `hse.kpi.breached`.
- **Browser**: HSE Dashboard; English and Hindi.

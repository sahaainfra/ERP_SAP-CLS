## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 239, 240; up, down and up again clean): `rta_assets`, `rta_positions`, `rta_movements`, `rta_hire_periods`, `rta_damage_recoveries`; flag, four pages under the Stores group, page tracker, notification templates. No existing table changed.
- **Catalogue** (FR-01): items of type Returnable with group, unit weight, internal hire rate and basis, replacement value and life.
- **Movements** (FR-02, FR-03, BR-01): receipt, issue to a work front or subcontractor with the expected return date, return by condition (good, repairable, scrap, lost), transfer, hire-in, hire-out, repair out and in. Each is approved by the construction manager or store manager before it posts; the available quantity is checked at save, submit and approval.
- **Hire charges** (FR-04, FR-05, T-01): hire-in periods per vendor and internal periods for company assets issued to a project, in quantity-days by the day or by the month; a part return cuts a closed slice; the vendor's bill is checked against the charge.
- **Recoveries** (FR-03): damage (30% of the replacement value), scrap and loss (the whole value) are made at the approval of a return; recovery goes quantity surveyor then project manager, a write-off construction manager then project manager.
- **Dashboard and reconciliation** (FR-02, FR-06): quantity by location, idle, overdue, utilisation, hired-in cost, and issued against on site; a daily job tells the store managers of overdue returns.
- **API**: 29 routes under `/api/v1/inv/rta`. **Browser**: Formwork and Scaffolding (dashboard and catalogue), Asset Movements, Issue or Return Assets (mobile), Hire Charges and Recoveries.

# Part 15.05 completion: Purchase Orders, Amendments & Short-Close

Part: 15.05. Date: 2026-12-03. Module `proc`, flag `ff.proc.p1505` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 15.04 and 04.11. Provides `proc.po.approved` and the other order events to the commitment ledger (12.03), the goods receipt (16.02/16.03), expediting (15.07), invoice matching (22.06) and the supplier portal (25.02).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items in section 6.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 214, 215; schema-diff of existing objects clean): `proc_purchase_orders`, `proc_po_lines`, `proc_po_schedules`, `proc_po_amendments`, `proc_dispatches`, `proc_po_receipts`, `proc_po_advances`; seed of the flag, four navigation entries, page-tracker rows and five notification templates.
- **Order** (FR-01 to FR-03, BR-01, BR-03): from an approved comparative statement (quoted rate, discount, GST and charges; quantity within what is left of the selection and the requisition balance plus tolerance) or directly with a reason; terms, delivery schedule, GST by the place of supply (CGST and SGST or IGST).
- **Approval and release** (FR-04): by value; the commitment is posted to the ledger, the requisition balance falls, the order is sent to the vendor with a hashed link; a rate above the statement needs an exception (CP-PROC-05), above the benchmark the project manager approves.
- **Amendment** (FR-07): a draft with the before and after and the comparison, approved by the size of the change, applied when approved, the commitment and balance follow. **Short-close and cancel** (FR-08, BR-02): approved by the procurement manager and project manager; cancel refused after receipt; the commitment is released.
- **Advance** (FR-05), **dispatch/ASN** by the vendor on its link or the buyer, **receipts** with the delivery status and schedule, **overdue alerts** (daily job), **tracker** and **vendor performance** (FR-06, FR-09).
- **API**: 26 routes under `/api/v1/proc/purchase-orders` and 3 public ones (2,547 routes in all). **Browser**: Purchase Orders (list and object), Create Purchase Order, Amendment with comparison, Open PO and Delivery Tracker, the vendor page; English and Hindi.

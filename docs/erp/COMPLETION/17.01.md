Part: 17.01. Date: 2026-11-12. Module `plant` (new), flag `ff.plant.p1701` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 06.03 and 06.04. Provides equipment to allocation (17.02), fixed assets (23.09) and the batching plant (20.01), and the plant slot of Project 360.

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items in section 6. Full regression: 1,269 tests, 0 failures; e2e passes on desktop and mobile.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 246, 247; up, down and up again clean): `plt_equipment`, `plt_documents`, `plt_timeline` (append-only); flag, three pages under the Plant group, page tracker, notification templates. No existing table changed.
- **Register** (FR-01, FR-03): class from the item master, make, model, year, serial, chassis, engine, registration, capacity, ownership with owner partner, purchase value (masked without `plant.equipment.values`), meter and fuel with the norm in the unit that fits the meter, GPS id, operator requirement, current project and site, status.
- **Approval** (section 9): the supervisor or plant manager prepares and sends; the plant manager approves; an owned asset also goes to accounts. The maker never approves; approved is locked; an amendment goes back for correction while the machine stays in service. Import creates drafts only.
- **Certificates** (FR-02, BR-01): RC, insurance, fitness, PUC (road vehicles), load test or third-party inspection (lifting), road permit and others, each with number, validity and a scan. A renewal replaces the current certificate and keeps the old one. A missing or expired mandatory certificate blocks allocation and deployment (`allocatable`, the one check for 17.02 and 17.03) unless the parameter is off, when it only warns. A morning job tells the plant managers and raises events.
- **Status and history** (FR-03, FR-04): transition matrix, deployment needs a project and passes BR-01, a breakdown needs a note; every change is in the history timeline. QR tag, scan, photographs, print sheet.
- **Dashboard**: fleet by status, utilisation against target, breakdowns, expired and expiring certificates, machines that cannot be put to work, registrations awaiting approval, alerts, charts, my pending actions, drill-down and CSV export; Project 360 plant slot.
- **API**: 25 routes under `/api/v1/plant` (3,030 routes in all). **Browser**: Plant Dashboard, Equipment Register with Equipment 360 (details, documents and certificates, history), New Equipment; English and Hindi.

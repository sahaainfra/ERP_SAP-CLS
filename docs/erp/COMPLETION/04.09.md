## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 72 and 73; nothing existing altered): `alr_rules`, `alr_alerts`, `alr_rule_runs`; reference seed: the flag, the words of an alert in English and Hindi, 18 library rules, three navigation entries and tracker rows.
- **Engine** (`platform/alerts`, ADR-193 to 195): rules over events or over a schedule (hourly, daily at an hour, weekly); conditions (`all` and `any` of field, comparison, value); actions (tell people, make a task, escalate to a role, call a webhook, set a flag); the throttle window; the dry-run; the run log. A module offers what rules may look at with `alertDatasets.register`.
- **Datasets live today**: open tasks, open approval tasks, emergency exceptions to regularise. The other 15 library rules can be taken but not switched on until their module is live ("activated as modules go live").
- **Module side** (`modules/eng/alerts.js`): who may see and change rules and alerts, versions of a rule, the approval of one that sends something outside (process owner, then system administrator; the creator cannot approve it), the Alert Centre lists, acknowledge, resolve and bulk acknowledge, the events consumer, the job `alr.run` every 15 minutes, the webhook job, My Work.
- **API** (`/api/v1/alr`): the builder's lists, rules (list, one, save, library adopt, switch on or off, run, dry-run of a saved rule and of a draft), the run log, alerts (list, one, summary, export, acknowledge, resolve, bulk). 17 routes in the route policy.
- **Pages**: Alert Rules `/admin/alert-rules` (rules, dry-run, run now, run log, the library), Rule Builder `/admin/alert-rules/editor` (trigger, conditions, actions, message, throttle, a try-out on today's data), Alert Centre `/alerts` (my and project alerts, filters, acknowledge, resolve, bulk, export).
- **Permissions**: `alr.rule.view`, `alr.rule.edit` (the system administrator and the process owners DIR, PD, PM, CFO, PROCMGR, COMMGR, HRMGR; the IT administrator and auditor view), `alr.alert.view`, `alr.alert.manage` (the alerts of one's projects); a person's own alerts need none. Events `alr.alert.raised`, `.acknowledged`, `.resolved`, `alr.rule.changed`, `alr.flag.set`.
- Documents: ARCHITECTURE section 43, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, the schema lock, DECISIONS (ADR-193 to 195), DEFECTS (D-224 to D-230), PROGRESS.csv (row 37).

## Click-path for a reviewer
1. As a project manager open **Alert Rules**: the library lists the standard rules; the ones for modules not yet live say "waits for ...". **Take task.overdue**, give a reason: the rule is on.
2. **Dry-run task.overdue**: it lists the late tasks it would raise alerts for and what it would do, and says nothing was written. **Run now**: "1 matched, 1 new alert". Run again: "1 held back" (the same alert is not repeated within its window). The **Run log** shows both runs.
3. **Alert Centre**: the alert is there, with its severity and the record linked; **Acknowledge**, then **Resolve** with a note; filter on Resolved to see it. The owner was also told in the notification centre.
4. **Rule Builder**: choose "Tasks (open)", the condition "Days late is at least 3", a message and a severity; **Try it on today's data** shows which tasks match; **Save the rule**. Tick "Also by e-mail" on another rule: the page says it goes for approval; as the director and then the system administrator approve it in **My Approvals**; the rule is in force.
5. Switch a rule off: its alerts stay (BR-02). A person without the right sees the pages say so and name the permission.

## Tests and acceptance

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 356, 357; schema-diff of existing objects clean): `pa_revenue_recognition` (approval-controlled), `pa_overhead_allocations`; accounts 170010, 230020, 240010, 410090, 560020, 560030, 560031; flag, two pages, page-tracker rows, two notification templates.
- **Revenue recognition** (FR-02, BR-01; ADR-679–683): a project's month by cost to cost (cost incurred to date ÷ EAC) or output (value certified by the client, or a surveyed % with its note); cost to date and billed come from the ledger's project dimension; EAC from the approved forecast of 23.11 when there is one, else the approved budget; contract revenue from the approved contracts. Recognised to date = POC × contract revenue; this month = less the last approved month. Expected loss provided in full at once. Each record keeps its inputs, results and the trace of each figure (FR-05).
- **Journals** (FR-03; ADR-680): on the CFO's approval the movement from the last approved position is posted through the posting engine: unbilled revenue 170010, billing in excess 230020, the difference to 410090; the provision 560020 / 240010. The project's revenue in the ledger then equals revenue recognised.
- **Approval** `FIN_REVENUE_RECOGNITION`: the accounts manager when someone else (the project controls manager) prepared it, then the CFO; the maker never approves. Amendment of the latest approved month (posts only the difference; the old record superseded); withdraw, cancel, print (PDF), CSV export and row import.
- **Overhead allocation** (FR-04; ADR-684): a posted 12.04 allocation run's event posts one journal Dr 560030 by project / Cr 560031, once per run; the reversal event reverses it; a catch-up posts runs made before the flag was on.
- **Reports** (FR-01): project trial balance (opening, debit, credit, closing by account with head; revenue, direct cost, overhead, margin), WIP & unbilled revenue of all projects to a month, the overhead posted by project; CSV.
- **API**: 20 routes under `/api/v1/fin/revenue-recognition` and `/api/v1/fin/project-accounts` in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`. **Browser**: Revenue Recognition (run of a month: choose → preview with the journal → save and send; the record with its decision panel), Project Trial Balance & WIP (three tabs); English and Hindi.
- **Job** `fin.pa_monthly` (daily 08:55 IST): from the 5th, tells who recognises revenue when last month is not recognised for a project.

## 6. Known limitations
- The EAC comes from the approved budget until 23.11 publishes approved forecasts (the adapter is in place and named on each record).
- Recognition is by project, not by contract within a project; revenue of several client contracts of one project is recognised together.
- No scheduled e-mail of the reports; no Project 360 widget (23.12 adds the P&L widget).

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As PCMGR: Finance → Revenue Recognition → month, choose a project with an approved contract → **Preview the figures** (POC, this month, unbilled or in excess, the journal) → **Save and send for approval**.
2. As ACCMGR, then CFO: open the record → **Approve**; the posted journal appears.
3. Finance → Project Trial Balance & WIP: the project's revenue equals revenue recognised; **WIP & unbilled revenue** for the month; **Overhead allocation** after a 12.04 run is posted (or **Post them now**).

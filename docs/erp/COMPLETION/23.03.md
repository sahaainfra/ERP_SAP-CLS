## 4. Click-path
1. As FINEX: Finance → AP Bills → **New direct expense bill**: vendor, project, bill 5-7-2026, 560004, ₹50,000 + GST ₹9,000, nature *Contract work*: the bar shows TDS ₹1,000 under s.393(1) (2025 Act). **Save and send**.
2. The head of the maker's department, then the accounts manager approve (the CFO above ₹1 lakh): the bill is posted.
3. As ACCMGR: open the bill → **Hold** (a dispute): it shows "On hold" and leaves the list of bills to pay.
4. **Vendor Ledger & Payables Reports**: the vendor's ledger (bill, TDS), the statement and the confirmation letter; **Ageing**, **MSME due**, **TDS deducted**.

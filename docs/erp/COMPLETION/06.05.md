## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 104 and 105, nothing existing altered): seven tables with the standard columns: groups, accounts, dimension rules, cost heads, cost codes, item category mappings (dated) and changes.
- **Seed:** a construction company's template (ADR-265):
  - 30 groups on the lines of Schedule III Division I;
  - 43 ledgers, with the control accounts AP, AR, GST input and output, TDS payable and receivable, retention payable and receivable, inventory and WIP;
  - the dimension rules (project mandatory on WIP, direct costs, revenue and subcontractor payables; not allowed on interest and head office overheads);
  - 22 cost heads, 1 Material (1.1 Cement …) to 6 Head office overheads, each top head mapped to an account;
  - 18 cost codes.

  A company gets its own copy, with the default mapping of every item category of the template, the first time it opens the chart.
- **Service** (`modules/fin/service.js`):
  - **Chart and account** (FR-01, FR-02): type and nature must agree; the normal side follows the nature unless a contra reason is given; a control account kind fits its type; a missing control account is reported.
  - **Changes** (ADR-266): every new or changed group, account, cost head, cost code or mapping, every block, unblock and import is a change in `fin_master_changes`. It goes through workflow `FIN_MASTER_CHANGE`: the accounts manager first when project controls proposed it, then the CFO; the maker never approves. A change is checked when proposed and again when applied. It is refused at approval if the record changed in between. There is one change per record at a time; return, resubmit, withdraw and reject work, and the maker is notified.
  - **Posting check** (FR-04, BR-01, T-01, ADR-268): a posting is refused when:
    - the account is blocked;
    - it is a manual journal to a control account, unless manual postings to it were explicitly allowed by an approved change;
    - a mandatory dimension is missing, or a not-allowed dimension is given;
    - a value is not in the company;
    - the site does not belong to the project.
  - **BR-02** (ADR-267): an account with postings (counted through `accountPostings`) keeps its nature, type, normal side and control setting and cannot be deleted. It can be blocked, and a blocked account takes no postings. An unused account is deleted, with the audit trail.
  - **Schedule III** (T-02): every line with its groups and accounts. Groups without a line, accounts without a line, accounts on a line of another nature and lines with no group make it incomplete.
  - **Item category mapping** (FR-05, AC-01): stock account, consumption account and cost code per category. A category inherits from its parent. Categories without a mapping are listed. A mapping applies from a date and retires the earlier one the day before. `resolveCategory()` serves the posting engine.
  - **Import** (FR-06): a CSV of ledgers (name, group, code) is mapped through the Tally group map or the chart's own groups. The mapping report shows each ledger's group, type and control kind, or why it does not map. The mapped ledgers become one change for the CFO.
  - **Export:** CSV of the chart, written to the audit trail.
- **Integration:**
  - lookups `/fin/lookups/accounts`, `/manual-accounts` and `/cost-codes`;
  - object type `fin_account`;
  - four events;
  - approval-matrix default and two workflow-matrix bands;
  - the approver sees what changes and an evidence panel (accounts of the same name, a control account opened to manual journals).
- **Browser** (module `fin`, navigation group Finance):
  - **Chart of Accounts:** tree with search and filters (type, control, status); new account and new group; tabs Schedule III, Import (paste or upload, mapping report, send) and Changes (withdraw, send again, decide); export CSV.
  - **Account:** properties, dimension rules, mapping, changes, and "Check a posting".
  - **Cost Heads & Cost Codes:** tree; new head, sub-head and code.
  - **Item Category → GL / Cost Code Mapping:** completeness banner, edit rows, send from a date.
- **Permissions:**

## 7. How to verify manually
1. **Open the chart:** as the accounts manager open **Finance › Chart of Accounts**. The five primary groups show with their groups, Schedule III lines and ledgers. Filter Control accounts to see AP, AR, GST, TDS, retention, inventory and WIP.
2. **Propose and approve an account:** choose **New account**, put it under 5600 Other expenses, and send it. It is not in the chart yet. The CFO approves it in **My Approvals** and it appears.
3. **Check a posting** (T-01): open 510001 Materials consumed and choose **Check a posting** without a project. It is rejected because the account needs a project. Do the same on 220001 Trade payables as a manual journal: it is rejected as a control account. As the posting engine, it is accepted.
4. **Schedule III** (T-02): tab **Schedule III** says every group and account lands on a line.
5. **Cost codes:** as the project controls manager open **Cost Heads & Cost Codes**, choose 1.1 Cement and add a cost code. The accounts manager and then the CFO approve it.
6. **Mapping** (AC-01): **Item Category → GL / Cost Code Mapping** says it is complete. A new item category shows as missing until a mapping is sent and approved.
7. **Import:** on the **Import** tab, paste a Tally ledger list (name, Under). The mapping report shows where each ledger lands; send the mapped ones to the CFO.

## 4. Click-path
1. As FINEX: Finance → Receivables & Receipts: the ageing tiles and the receivables. **New receipt**: the client, the bank account, UTR, ₹1,50,000 received, TDS ₹2,000, GST-TDS ₹2,000, retention ₹5,000; "Oldest first" sets ₹1,18,000 against the first invoice and ₹41,000 against the second, ₹0 on account → **Post**.
2. The second invoice now shows a balance of ₹18,000.
3. As PM: Finance → Collections Follow-up: the card of the second invoice (65 days overdue, interest on delay) → **Log a follow-up**: a call, the promise of 15-12-2026 → saved; the escalation follows if the date passes.
4. **Ageing & DSO**, **Client statement** (PDF), **TDS by clients**.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 229, 230; up, down and up again clean): `inv_grns`, `inv_grn_lines`, `inv_rtvs`; flag, three pages under the Stores group, page tracker, notification templates. No existing table changed.
- **GRN** (FR-01, BR-01): made from the gate entry (store, order, vendor and challan are filled in) or from an order; each line shows ordered, balance, challan, received (counted), accepted and rejected (worked out), batch, heat, manufacturing date, certificate and photos. The server checks the order balance and the tolerance (`inv.grn.excess_tolerance_pct`, 5%): more than that is refused with the instruction to send it back at the gate; any excess within it needs the procurement manager as a second approver (FR-03).
- **Approval and posting** (BR-02, BR-03, FR-06): store manager or construction manager approves (never the maker); on approval the accepted quantity is posted to the stock ledger at the order rate; the rejected quantity never enters the stock; one event `inv.grn.posted` carries the lines; the order's received quantity is applied by the procurement subscriber (idempotent).
- **Inspection hold** (FR-02): lines needing inspection (a mandatory vendor inspection of the order sets the default) are held in the inspection stock; quality control passes (released) or fails (stays held) in parts; the failed quantity becomes returnable.
- **Direct receipts** (FR-04): a non-stock item moves no stock and is charged to the WBS or cost code of the order line. **Emergency receipts**: a receipt without an order needs a reason, the emergency right and the procurement manager's approval.
- **Return to vendor** (FR-05): rejected at the store, then failed by QC, goes back; the procurement manager approves; the approval takes the held stock out, prepares the vendor-return gate pass (a draft for the storekeeper) and triggers the debit note.
- **API**: 30 routes under `/api/v1/inv` (2,792 routes in all). **Browser**: Create GRN (mobile), Goods Receipts (list with inspection status and the receipt with its lines, QC panel, documents, postings and returns), Returns to Vendor (list, form, object).

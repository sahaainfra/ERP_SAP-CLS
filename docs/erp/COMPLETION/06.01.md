## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 96 and 97, nothing existing altered): `mdm_records`, `mdm_record_versions`, `mdm_change_requests`, `mdm_duplicates`, `mdm_merge_map`, `mdm_quality_rules`, `mdm_stewards`, all with the standard columns. Seed: the flag, five navigation entries, five tracker rows and two notification templates (en, hi).
- **Platform registry** `platform/mdm`:
  - `masterTypes` and `masterReferences`;
  - formats PAN, GSTIN (with the check character), IFSC, PIN, mobile, e-mail and bank account;
  - normalisation (capitals; a mobile number stored without +91 and spaces);
  - name similarity, which ignores company suffixes and word order;
  - masking (last four characters shown).
- **Module `mdm`** (ADR-245 to 249):
  - **First master:** the business partner (AC-01). It is a financial master, and PAN, GSTIN, bank account and IFSC are sensitive.
  - **Change requests:** a new record, a change from a date, inactivation, reactivation and merge each go through workflow `MDM_CHANGE` or `MDM_MERGE`. The approvers are the stewards of the master, then the accounts manager for a financial master. Each request has a justification. The requester can withdraw a request, and correct and resend one that was returned. A non-sensitive change by an editor applies at once and is audited.
  - **Duplicates:**
    - an identical PAN or GSTIN blocks the save (409 `DUPLICATE`);
    - an identical mobile or a similar name (85% or more) warns, and the person must confirm (409 `DUPLICATE_WARNING`);
    - the pairs appear in a report, which an hourly scan also fills;
    - a pair can be dismissed with a reason.
  - **Merge:** an approved merge writes the merge map and keeps the merged record (BR-01). `resolve`, `assertSelectable`, the `mdm_record` object type and the references view all follow the map.
  - **Effective dating:** every version keeps the date it applies from. A future version is SCHEDULED and the hourly job puts it in force. `asOf` reads any date. Past dates are refused.
  - **Data quality:** rules (required, format, stale) carry a severity; ERROR format rules block the save. The score is weighted, a person can confirm a record is still correct, and a quality report lists the failing rules.
  - **Configuration:** the rules and stewards change only by a versioned proposal `MDM_CONFIG`, made by SYSADM and approved by ITADM or DIR. A proposal must name at least one steward per master.
  - **Selectable records:** only ACTIVE records can be chosen in a new transaction (BR-02); inactive records stay listed.
  - **Dashboard:** one card per master with records, waiting changes and their age, duplicates, score against the target of 90, staleness and changes in 30 days. It also shows "waiting for me" and exceptions (old requests, duplicates, low scores, a master without a steward).
  - **Excel:** import type `MDM_PARTNER`, where each row becomes a change request with the same checks (an import never approves), and export source `mdm.partners` (sensitive fields masked) (FR-06).
- **API** `/api/v1/mdm/*`: 25 operations on 22 paths, all in the route policy (every write audited) and in `openapi.yaml`. Events: `mdm.change.requested`, `mdm.record.changed`, `mdm.record.merged`.
- **Browser** (`modules/mdm`):
  - Master Data Governance (dashboard);
  - Master Change Requests (worklist: mine, to review, all; field-by-field detail; withdraw and resend);
  - Duplicate Candidates (side-by-side compare with differences marked, choose the survivor, ask to merge, not a duplicate, scan now);
  - Data Quality Rules & Stewards (scores, failing rules, proposal editor, history);
  - Master Records (list with as-of and score filters; the record page with data, quality, history, change requests and references; the new and change form with a live duplicate check).
- **Permissions:**

  | Permission | Roles |
  |---|---|
  | `mdm.record.view` | every internal role (anyone may request) |
  | `mdm.record.edit`, `mdm.record.import` | PROCMGR, COMMGR, ACCMGR, CFO |
  | `mdm.sensitive.view` | ACCMGR, CFO, AUD, DIR |
  | `mdm.governance.view` | the editors and DIR, AUD, SYSADM, ITADM |
  | `mdm.config.edit` | SYSADM |

  A steward is a named person per master, not a role; stewards also see sensitive fields of their master and may edit it.
- **Documents:** ARCHITECTURE section 55, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, DECISIONS ADR-245 to 249, DEFECTS D-290 to D-293, openapi, PROGRESS.csv (row 49), screenshots in `docs/erp/screenshots/mdm/`.

## 6. Known limitations
- **D-290:** one governed master only; the partner's contacts, addresses and bank verification come in 06.04. There is no dedicated import end-to-end test.
- **D-291:** fuzzy matching is in-process and was not measured at large volumes; there is no phonetic or transliteration matching and no address matching.
- **D-292:** stored references are resolved, never rewritten; later modules must call `mdmOf(db)`.
- **D-293:** the decision on a request is taken in the approval inbox; English only.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. **Name a steward:** as the system administrator open **Data Quality Rules & Stewards**, choose **Propose a change**, add a steward for Business partner, give a reason and send it. The IT administrator approves it in their inbox.
2. **Request a partner:** as a purchase manager open **Master Records**, then **New business partner**. Enter a name, kind, PAN and GSTIN, give a justification and send. The steward, then the accounts manager, approve it, and it becomes Active.
3. **See a blocked duplicate:** request another partner with the same PAN. It is refused, with the code of the existing one.
4. **See a warned duplicate:** request a partner whose name differs by one letter. The form shows "This looks like…" and asks you to confirm.
5. **Merge:** open **Duplicate Candidates**, then **Compare**. Keep one, give a justification and choose **Ask to merge**. After approval, the merged record shows "Merged into …".
6. **Schedule a change:** on a record choose **Change**, change the state with a date 10 days ahead and save. The record still shows the old state; **Show as of** that date shows the new one.
7. **Open the dashboard:** open **Master Data Governance**.

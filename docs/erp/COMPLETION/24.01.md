# Part 24.01 completion: salary structures, pay components and payroll set-up

Part: 24.01 (step 182). Date: 2026-11-19. Module `pay` (new), flag `ff.pay.p2401` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 07.01 (employees) and 23.02 (posting rules; the components carry a GL account code for 24.05); uses 166.8 (pay lines, Code-on-Wages classes and rule) and the calculation engine (01.06). Provides the structures, assignments, arrears and parameters to 24.02 and 24.03; event `pay.assignment.approved`, `pay.statutory.approved`.

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items in section 6.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 368, 369): `pay_components`, `pay_structures`, `pay_assignments` (approval-controlled), `pay_arrears`, `pay_statutory_params` (approval-controlled), `pay_calendar`; flag, menu group Payroll, four pages, page-tracker rows, templates; 27 template components; the statutory parameters in force as approved system rows.
- **Components and formulas** (FR-01, FR-02; ADR-715, ADR-716): the company copy of the template; methods fixed / percent / formula / balance / statutory / input; formulas of the calculation engine checked on save and testable.
- **Structures** (FR-03; ADR-717): versioned; the CTC breakup preview (earnings, employer contributions, deductions, net, wages under the Code, PF wages).
- **Assignments** (FR-04, BR-01; ADR-718): new, revision, promotion, correction; computed on the server; HRMGR → CFO, the director for a senior grade; on approval the pay lines of the employee and, for a retro date, the arrears by month and component.
- **Statutory parameters** (FR-06; ADR-719): by code, state and date; a company change proposed by payroll and approved by the CFO; resolution by date.
- **Calendar** (FR-05): the twelve periods of a year with cut-offs and pay date; open periods editable.
- **API**: 30 operations under `/api/v1/pay`. **Browser**: Pay Components & Salary Structures (formula form, structure form, CTC preview), Employee Pay Assignments & Revisions (list, new, object with breakup, arrears and decision), Statutory Parameters, Payroll Calendar; English and Hindi.
- **Defects** D-352, D-361, D-366 closed; D-047, D-055 re-assigned to 04.07 (sending by email / WhatsApp, not payroll).

## 6. Known limitations
- The statutory values are seeded from the published rates and marked to be confirmed against each notification before go-live (DECISIONS ADR-719).
- No import of assignments from Excel yet; increments are entered one by one (the rating is taken from the appraisal).
- The structure editor lists components with checkboxes; there is no drag-and-drop ordering (the component order is its sequence).

## 7. How to verify manually
1. Payroll → Pay Components & Salary Structures: components by type; **New component**, method Formula, **Test the formula**; tab Structures → a structure → CTC → **Preview**.
2. Payroll → Statutory Parameters: **Propose a change**, send to the CFO; as CFO approve.
3. Payroll → Payroll Calendar: **Set up the year**.
4. Payroll → Employee Pay Assignments: **New assignment** → preview → save → send; approve as HR manager and CFO. A revision dated back shows its arrears.

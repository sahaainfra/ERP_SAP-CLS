## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 132, 133; nothing existing altered): `hr_separations`, `hr_clearances`, `hr_exit_interviews`, `hr_grievances`, `hr_disciplinary_cases`; seed: flag, navigation and tracker rows, notification templates `sep.decided`, `sep.clearance`, `grv.update`, widget `grv.overdue`.
- **Service** (`exit.js`): notice and last working day from the policy (probation shorter), waiver or buyout, pay in lieu for a short termination; acceptance schedules NOTICE and SEPARATED through the 07.01 change documents (`employees.exits`), so access goes on the last working day; clearances per department with system checks and waivers; exit interview and analysis; F&F inputs (leave to encash, notice served, recovery or pay days, gratuity eligibility, pending claims, recoveries) blocked until all clearances are done and frozen at release; relieving and experience letters; grievance register with anonymity, tracking code, officer, SLA, shared and internal notes, resolution, accept or reopen; disciplinary cases with show-cause, response, inquiry, action, closing; daily job for overdue grievances.
- **Workflow**: `HR_SEPARATION` (resignation: reporting manager, HR manager; termination: HR manager, director).
- **API**: 45 routes under `/api/v1/hr/{separations,clearances,grievances,disciplinary}` in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`; exports of separations and grievances.
- **Permissions**: `hr.separation.*` and `hr.grievance.*` (the ten of each), `hr.separation.{view_all,terminate,interview,fnf,letter}`, `hr.clearance.{view,clear,waive}`, `hr.grievance.manage`, `hr.disciplinary.{view,manage}`.
- **Parameters**: `hr.exit.notice_days`, `hr.exit.notice_days_probation`, `hr.exit.clearance_departments`, `hr.fnf.gratuity_min_years`, `hr.grievance.sla_days`.
- **Browser**: Resignation (self-service), Separation (clearance tracker, interview, F&F, letters), Separations, My Clearance Items, Grievances (with tracking by code), Raise Grievance (mobile, anonymous), Grievance, Disciplinary Cases; English and Hindi.
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 73, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, ADR-333 to 336, D-370 to 374, openapi.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As an employee open **Resignation**: the notice of the policy and the last working day show; ask for an earlier day with a waiver or buyout; send it. The manager and the HR manager accept it.
2. The employee fills the exit interview; each department sees its items in **My Clearance Items**, clears them (an item the system knows is not done is refused) or HR waives one.
3. On the **Separation** page the F&F stays blocked until every clearance is done and the last day has passed; then release it to payroll and issue the relieving letter.
4. On the last working day the daily cycle marks the employee separated and closes the user account.
5. **Raise Grievance** anonymously on a phone: keep the tracking code; HR notes and resolves it; follow it on **Grievances** by the code and accept.
6. **Disciplinary Cases** (HR): open a case, issue a show-cause; the employee answers. Switch to Hindi: headings and labels change.

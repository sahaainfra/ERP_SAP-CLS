## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 268, 269): `qa_ncrs`, `qa_capa_actions`; flag, three pages, tracker, templates. No existing table changed.
- **Lifecycle**: draft, open, rectified, concession review, closure review, closed, cancelled. Raised from an inspection, a test, an audit, the client or otherwise; root cause; actions as tasks; rectification by the responsible party; re-inspection by an approved passed inspection or request.
- **Rules** (BR-01, T-01): closure is refused with an open action, without a verified re-inspection (rework, repair), without an approved concession (use as is), or without a root cause (major and critical). Closure chain by severity: quality engineer; a major also the quality control manager; a critical also the project director. Concession: quality control manager, then the consultant; a refusal sends the NCR back to rework.
- **Back-charge**: held as pending on closure, event `qa.ncr.backcharge_raised`, listed for the bill module, marked posted by it.
- **Repeats and analysis**: repeat flag, ageing, closure time, by type, party and severity, cost where permitted. Daily job `qa.ncr_sweep` tells overdue actions.
- **API**: 23 routes; **browser**: NCR Register and object, Raise NCR (phone layout), NCR Analysis; English and Hindi.

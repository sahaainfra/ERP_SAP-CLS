# Part 14.03 completion: Site Diary, Weather, Hindrances & Site Conditions

Part: 14.03. Date: 2026-11-24. Module `site`, flag `ff.site.p1403` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 14.02 (prefill and notes, extended) and the platform gates. Provides weather and hindrances to the DPR (14.02), delay drafts to 13.05 and the events the claims (21.06) read.

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items in section 6.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 196, 197; nothing existing altered, schema-diff clean): `site_weather`, `site_hindrances`, `site_diary`, `site_visitors`; seed of the flag, four navigation entries, four page-tracker rows, four notification templates (English, Hindi; in-app, e-mail).
- **Weather** (FR-01): one record a site and day, typed or fetched through the integration hub (`weather_sandbox`, `weather_http` providers on a `maps` connector, ADR-494); condition, concrete-pour restriction (rain, temperature thresholds as parameters) and shutdown validated and derived on the server; rainfall by month with chart and table.
- **Hindrances** (FR-02, BR-01): SE raises, CM then PM approve (raiser never decides), locked on approval, amendment replaces; closing needs an end date and evidence (file or linked letter); daily job escalates those open beyond `site.hindrance.escalate_days`.
- **Delay drafts** (FR-05): 13.05 consumes `site.weather.shutdown` and `site.hindrance.approved|closed` and drafts or updates its delay (cause, responsibility, activities, letter and files as evidence).
- **Diary** (FR-03): notes by category with attachments; signed off by someone other than the writer, then locked; sign-off of a whole day. **Visitors** (FR-04): visits with purpose, instructions, times and a linked letter.
- **DPR link**: prefill carries `conditions`; a report started from it takes the weather and adds linked hindrance and visitor notes.
- **API**: 42 routes, all in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`; optimistic locking, `Idempotency-Key` on create/submit/close/amend/fetch/sign-day.
- **Browser**: Weather Log (form, fetch, chart, table, list), Hindrance Register and Hindrance (evidence, decision panel, close, amend, PDF, history), Site Diary, Visitor Log; English and Hindi. Phone cards of the 14.01/14.02 lists repaired (D-490).

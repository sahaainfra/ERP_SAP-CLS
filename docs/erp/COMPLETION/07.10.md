## 7. How to verify manually
1. As HR open **Training Set-up**: make a certificate type, a mandatory course that gives it, a skill. Open **Training Calendar**, Plan session for today and Save and send: it is open at once (an external course with a fee goes to the manager and the HR manager).
2. On the session page nominate people (a manager nominates their team), Show sign-in code; an employee opens **My Training** with the code (or scans the QR) and signs in; HR enters the scores and Complete session: those who passed get the certificate.
3. **Mandatory Training Compliance** shows who is compliant, due, overdue or never trained; the Cost tab shows the fees by project and department.
4. **Certifications & Licences**: record a licence (it is not verified until another HR person verifies it); an expired licence shows Expired; try to deploy a person whose post needs it: the deployment is refused.
5. **Skill Matrix**: the manager picks a level in a cell. Switch to Hindi: headings and labels change.

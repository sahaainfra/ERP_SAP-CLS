## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 237, 238; up, down and up again clean): `inv_reorder_params`, `inv_reorder_changes`, `inv_reorder_suggestions`, `inv_labels`; flag, four pages under the Stores group, page tracker, notification templates. No existing table changed.
- **Reorder parameters** (FR-01, AC-04): minimum, maximum, reorder level, quantity, lead days and an automatic-calculation option per store and item. Every change (edit, automatic calculation, CSV import) is a change set approved by a second store manager; nothing in force changes before the approval.
- **Suggestions** (FR-02, BR-01): a daily job (08:00 India time) and a "run now" action suggest items whose projected stock (free plus in transit) is below the reorder level; urgent at or below the minimum. A person turns suggestions into DRAFT material requests, one per store, or dismisses them with a reason.
- **Ageing** (FR-03): buckets 0-30, 31-90, 91-180, 181-365, > 365 days by item and store with a chart; non-moving and slow-moving lists; redeployment opportunities where idle stock matches a store that is short of the same item.
- **Labels and scanning** (FR-04, FR-05): QR labels for items, bins and batches on a 50 x 25 mm thermal roll or an A4 sheet, logged; the GRN, issue, transfer and count screens take a scanned or typed code.
- **API**: 22 routes under `/api/v1/inv`. **Browser**: Reorder Parameters, Reorder Suggestions, Stock Ageing and Non-moving (with Redeployment), Print Labels.

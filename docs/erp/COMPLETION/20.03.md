# Part 20.03 completion: Batching Plant Costing & Cost per m3

Part: 20.03. Date: 2026-11-21. Module `rmc`, flag `ff.rmc.p2003` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 20.02.

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items D-646 in `DEFECTS.md`.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 294, 295): `bp_cost_periods`, `bp_cost_lines`, `bp_cost_grades`, `bp_transfer_rates`, `bp_charges`, `bp_budgets`; flag, three pages, tracker, widget `rmc.cost_per_m3`, templates.
- **Cost period**: collect (replaces the lines the system made, keeps the lines entered by hand), enter labour, depreciation, power and overheads with a basis, close, reopen by the CFO with a reversal of the charges.
- **Close**: blockers listed in the message; total, volume put in place from approved tickets, cost per m3, cost and margin per grade, the charge to each project (volume x approved rate), cost allocated and variance, profit of the plant; the charges are published.
- **Transfer rates**: proposed by accounts, approved by the plant in-charge and then the CFO, in force from their effective date, never in a closed month.
- **Dashboard** (cost per m3 by month with trend, volume, profit, wastage, volume and charge by project, exceptions: month to close, over budget, grade below cost, grade without a rate) and the **cost per m3 by grade** report with CSV.
- **API**: 31 routes; **browser**: Batching Plant Costing, Cost per m3 by Grade, Internal Transfer Rates; English and Hindi.

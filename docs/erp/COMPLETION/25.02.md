## 2. Implemented workflows

- The buyer can publish selected scanned RFQ attachments to invited suppliers. A supplier can open them, submit or revise a quotation with attachments before the due date, and ask for clarification. Procurement can create a comparative statement from the received version.
- A supplier can acknowledge its PO and delivery schedule, submit an ASN with transport details and a scanned invoice copy, and view GRN quantities and inspection outcomes.
- A supplier can submit a GST invoice PDF against its PO, using accepted GRNs for the standard three-way check. A phone photo can be converted to PDF. Billing checks the registered GSTIN, fiscal-year duplicates, quantities, rates, tax and declared total before submission. Matched invoices enter the existing Accounts approval chain.
- Suppliers can see invoice match status, outstanding balances and settled payments with UTRs and downloadable Finance advices. They can also update milestones on approved expediting plans.
- Engineering can share a VDR requirement after linking it to an approved or closed PO in the same company and project. The supplier can upload a scanned document against an open requirement and read the published engineering review code.
- Company-wide supplier accounts can send compliance renewals to the existing verification worklist and see their own scorecard. An unverified renewal cannot clear an expired-document procurement block.
- Supplier access comes from the current external session and the company, partner, project and site grants. Staged uploads are bound to that grant and become a business attachment only after scanning succeeds. Version checks and idempotency protect the submit actions.

## 4. Verification

The supplier API test uses fresh migrated SQLite, the portal session mechanism and the shared scanner. It covers explicit RFQ publication, real comparative-statement creation, quote deadlines and replay, attachment consumption and rollback, ASN linkage, GSTIN checks, invoice duplicate and GRN validations, invoice approval handoff, payment advice, VDR and compliance verification. The existing engineering and workflow suites cover review decisions and shared approval behavior. The final focused API and unit run passed 31 tests. After the shared translation-loader fix, all 12 desktop and mobile compatibility tests passed across the portal, billing, engineering, RFQs and purchase orders.

The full unit and API run covered 1,592 tests. The initial run had three failures caused by a non-idempotent migration and one Gantt print timing failure. The migration was converted to additive link tables. The three affected migration tests passed on focused reruns. The Gantt timing test passed without coverage and exceeded its threshold when rerun with coverage. The full browser run stopped at the global Feature Flags visual baseline with a navigation-shell difference. The other 803 tests did not run in that attempt.

Lint, type checking, route registration, permission audit, schema lint, locked schema comparison, data-integrity checks, navigation, design tokens, golden outputs and secret scan passed after the migration correction. The production build, PWA check and bundle budget also passed.

The Supplier Portal end-to-end test passed on desktop and on a 360 px phone with a simulated camera image converted to an invoice PDF. It compares the RFQ and order screenshots and verifies the invoice, VDR, compliance and scorecard flows. It also checks RFQ layout at 360, 768, 1280 and 1920 px and exercises Hindi, Telugu, dark mode, high contrast and large text.

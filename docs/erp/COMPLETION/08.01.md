## 7. How to verify manually
1. As a BD executive open **Leads**, **New lead**: rate budget, authority, need and timeline, choose the client from the partner master, send. A colleague approves it; open the lead and **Convert to opportunity**.
2. On the opportunity ask for the go-ahead (above ₹10 crore a colleague and then the director approve); until then **Pipeline** refuses stages from Pre-bid. Move the stage and give a reason for a probability that is not the default.
3. **Opportunity Pipeline** shows the board; move a card with the list or by dragging. Log an interaction with a next action: a task and a reminder follow.
4. **Client 360** shows the contacts with the influence map and its warnings. The **Dashboard** shows the weighted forecast, win rate and the follow-ups. Switch to Hindi.
5. Mark an opportunity lost with a reason, or ask for a direct award (the director decides); the win and loss analysis follows.

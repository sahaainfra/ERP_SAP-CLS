## 2. What was built

- **Tables** (migration `20261228000433_cls_closeout`): `cls_closures`, `cls_checklist_items`, `kb_lessons`, `kb_benchmarks`.
- **Routes** under `/api/v1/close` (13 in the route policy): closeouts, closeout items, lessons, benchmarks.
  - FR-01 checklist: automatic checks read live from their owners (an issued handover, every defects liability period completed, no work
    order open, no snag open, the final account agreed, an approved and frozen P&L snapshot linked, a lesson written) and manual items done
    with evidence (retention and securities, guarantees, subcontract settlements, demobilisation; records archive optional).
  - FR-02 final account: the value agreed with the client, the day and the signed copy.
  - FR-03 final P&L from the linked snapshot (cumulative revenue and operating profit) and the performance of the project (duration and delay
    against the scheduled completion, NCRs, snags, incidents, defects liability complaints).
  - FR-04 lessons: written by any project team role, submitted by the author, reviewed by the project controls manager (`KB_LESSON`), then
    published and searchable; benchmarks proposed with the closeout (suggested from its figures) and approved with it; the benchmark report
    gives count, lowest, median and highest per metric.
  - FR-05 approval PM -> PD -> CFO -> DIR (`CLS_CLOSURE`); `cls.project.closed` follows. Project Setup's move to CLOSED gains a blocking
    readiness check "the project closeout (26.04) is approved" where the part is on.
  - BR-01 the closeout is refused while a mandatory item is not met; the record is frozen once requested.
- **Permissions**: `cls.view`, `cls.manage`, `cls.request`, `cls.export`, `kb.lesson.view`, `kb.lesson.create`, `kb.benchmark.view`.
- **Pages**: Project Closeout (list, start, export), the closeout record, Lessons Learned, Benchmarks. Text in the `close` area; Hindi and
  Telugu are drafts that need review.

## 4. Known limitations

- Archival to the records archive (29.07) is a manual item; the archive does not yet consume `cls.project.closed`.
- Benchmarks are the figures the closeout can compute (margin, duration, delay, NCR rate); quantities such as cost per square metre need a
  built-up area the project master does not hold.

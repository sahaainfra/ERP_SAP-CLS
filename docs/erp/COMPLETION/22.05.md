## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 318, 319): `bill_sub_measurements`, `bill_sub_bills` (approval-controlled), `bill_sub_bill_items`, `bill_sub_claims`; flag `ff.bill.p2205`; pages Subcontractor Measurement, Subcontractor Bills, Prepare Subcontractor Bill; tracker, templates.
- **Server**: `sub.js`; 24 route-policy lines (every write audited); permissions `bill.sub_bill.*`, `bill.sub_measure.{view,create,sign,verify,cancel,export}`; workflow `BILL_SUB_BILL`; adapters added to quality (`postBackchargeFromBill`) and stores (`freeIssue.certifyFromBill`, which feeds the free-issue entitlement with the certified quantity); the work order `progress` adapter of 21.03 is called on approval.
- **Events**: `bill.sub.measured`, `bill.sub.approved`.
- **Browser**: record, sign and verify a joint measurement; prepare the bill with a live preview of items, recoveries and net; the bill with its deductions breakdown and compliance status; English and Hindi.
- **Also fixed**: a bill number is not reused after a bill is deleted or cancelled (the sequence now follows the highest number ever given); this also corrected the client bills of 22.03, where preparing a bill after deleting a draft failed.

# Part 26.03 — Defect Liability Period & Warranty Management

Built 2026-11-25 on the owner's instruction to build module M26 now (audit AUD-DEF-004), which overrides the prerequisite rule for this
module. The new business routes are gated by the part flag `ff.close.p2603` only (default off); the earlier Billing release evidence adapter
keeps its G1/G2 check.

## 1. Discovery summary

[Requirement and service inventory](../DISCOVERY/26.03.md). Handover (26.02) starts the period; Billing (22.07) owns retention release;
Subcontractor Bills (22.05) own deductions; the partner master owns subcontractors and suppliers.

## 2. What was built

- **Tables** (migration `20261228000432_dlp`): `dlp_periods`, `dlp_complaints`, `dlp_warranty_claims`, and two in-app notification templates.
- **Routes** under `/api/v1/close/dlp` (14 in the route policy).
  - FR-01 a period per issued handover, from its effective date for the agreed months: opened by the `ho.handover.issued` event (consumer
    `close.dlp.open`, when the part is on) or by hand for a handover issued before. Periods ending within 30 days are told to the project and
    commercial managers once (hourly job `close.dlp_sweep`).
  - FR-02 complaints within the period, by the client or the team, with location, the asset of the client register and a severity whose
    response time sets the due time (critical 24 h, major 72 h, minor 7 days).
  - FR-03 assignment to own crew, a subcontractor or a supplier; a recovery on closure against a subcontractor or supplier publishes
    `dlp.defect.backcharge_raised` for their bill.
  - FR-04 rectification with evidence; verification by someone other than the rectifier, closing it or sending it back; overdue complaints
    escalate once a day (level 1 the project manager, level 2 and more the QA/QC manager too).
  - FR-05 completion: requested by the project manager when the period has ended and no complaint is open (BR-01), approved by the
    commercial manager and the CFO through the workflow (`DLP_COMPLETION`); `dlp.period.completed` follows. The retention itself is released
    through Billing's release requests on the DLP end basis (22.07), which keep their own controls.
  - FR-06 warranty claims to the supplier of a handed-over asset (within its warranty), accepted or rejected, then resolved.
- **Permissions**: `dlp.view`, `dlp.complaint.create`, `dlp.manage`, `dlp.rectify`, `dlp.verify`, `dlp.complete.request`, `dlp.export`.
- **Pages**: Defects Liability (periods and figures), the period (completion, complaints with assign/rectify/verify, warranty claims),
  DLP Complaints (filters, overdue, export). Text in the `close` area; Hindi and Telugu are drafts that need review.

## 1. Discovery

The 28.01 gateway, the governed datasets and metrics, the explainer and the engineering services were in place; there was no assistant. See the [discovery note](../DISCOVERY/28.02.md).

## 2. What was built

**The assistant (FR-01, FR-02)** — `src/server/modules/ai/assistant.js`, every call through `aiOf(db).run`:

- **Dock in the shell**: an "Ask AI" button on every page when the flag is on, opening a side panel (a bottom sheet on phones) with three pill tabs: **Ask**, **Copilots** and **Draft**. It sends the project and route of the page. The same panel is the page **My Work → AI Assistant** (`/ai/assistant`).
- **Questions**: the parser matches plain words to a governed query:
  - a certified metric (27.03);
  - "why" a figure moved (27.08);
  - stock of a material (inventory balances);
  - the status of projects (the 27.02 project-status dataset).
- Each query runs through the owner's service **as the person**. The answer shows the query that ran (for example `dataset(project-status).fields(code,name,state,status,health)`) and links the records it cites.
- **Unsure (BR-01)**: an unmatched question, or one with no data, is answered "I could not find…" with no model call, and is logged as BLOCKED / NO_DATA.
- **Scope**: a project named outside the person's scope is refused (404). An owner that refuses the person gives an "unsure" answer naming the query.

**Role copilots (FR-04)**, read-only:

| Copilot | Reads, as the person |
|---|---|
| PM | The control-tower project and the schedule "Why?" |
| QS | The leakage findings and the margin "Why?" |
| Procurement | A vendor's purchase orders |
| Finance | The cost and margin "Why?" |

**Drafts (FR-03)**: RFI, letter, RFQ, minutes of meeting, DPR narrative and MIS commentary.

1. The draft is kept in `ai_drafts` as PROPOSED, labelled.
2. The person reviews and completes it (subject, dates, discipline…).
3. **Save as draft** calls the module's own service with the module's permissions: an RFI becomes a DRAFT engineering RFI and a letter a DRAFT outgoing letter.
4. Other kinds give 422 and stay as text (D-782). Discard keeps the history.

**Feedback (FR-05)**: thumbs up or down on one's own answers (one vote per answer; a second replaces it), with a summary by feature for `ai.usage.view`. Each feature carries evaluation cases run by the 28.01 harness before it is enabled.

**Variance narrative (closes D-779)**: the "Why?" page shows **Explain in words (AI)** when the assistant is on. It runs the approved `ai.variance_narrative` feature as the person and shows the labelled answer under the computed figures.

**Gateway changes**: a feature may return "no answer" from its context (logged, no model call), and the query metadata travels back with the answer.

Labels are en/hi/te (area `aa`, plus two keys in `why`).

**Permissions**: no new keys. Who may use a feature is its approved enablement (roles and projects, 28.01). Each owner decides the data and the draft targets. The routes are self-scoped; the feedback summary needs `ai.usage.view`.

## 4. Tests

`tests/ai-assistant.test.js` has three tests, all passing:

1. **Parser**: plain words to METRIC, VARIANCE, STOCK, PROJECT_STATUS or UNKNOWN.
2. **Governed answers**:
   - the query and the citations are shown;
   - a project outside the scope is refused;
   - no data gives an "unsure" answer with no model call;
   - feedback is recorded, and is refused on another person's answer;
   - the kill switch stops the assistant.
3. **Drafts and copilots**:
   - an SE drafts an RFI and saves it as a DRAFT engineering RFI;
   - a PM without the RFI right is refused by the module (403);
   - an RFQ gives 422;
   - the finance copilot answers from the person's figures.

Browser tests, on desktop and mobile:

- `e2e/ai-assistant.spec.js`: a director asks from the dock, sees the query and the cited project, and gives a thumbs-up; a site engineer drafts an RFI and saves it as a DRAFT.
- `e2e/bi-explain.spec.js` (extended): the narrative on the Why? page.

Regression: `i18n-api` (namespace `aa`), lint, typecheck (0 errors), route, nav, schema lint, migration up/down, schema-diff and the permission audit.

## 6. Known limitations

- D-782: only RFI and letter drafts become module records. There is no voice input or document Q&A yet.
- Questions are matched by a deterministic parser to four kinds of governed query; other questions get an "unsure" answer instead of free text.
- Load: the `ai_drafts` list is per person and indexed; a 100,000-row volume run was done for `ai_logs` in 28.01, not repeated here.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set `ff.ai.p2801` and `ff.ai.p2802` (and the 27.0x flags of the data you will ask about), record G1 and G2, and set up a provider as in 28.01.
2. As SYSADM, in **AI Features & Enablement**, prepare `ai.assistant`, `ai.draft_document` and the copilots for the roles, run the evaluation and send them; approve as a director.
3. Open any page of a project and click **Ask AI**:
   - ask "What is the status of the projects?";
   - the answer shows the query and links the project;
   - give a thumbs-up.
4. Ask about a project outside your scope: it is refused.
5. As a site engineer, in **Draft**:
   - choose RFI, write a brief and draft it;
   - complete the subject and the date needed, and **Save as draft**;
   - the RFI is in Engineering → RFIs as DRAFT.
6. On **Variance Explainer** for a project, click **Explain in words (AI)**.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As a quantity surveyor open **Import BOQ**: choose the project, paste the client sheet with its header row, check the column mapping, **Check the rows**: wrong rows are listed with their numbers; fix the sheet, check again, **Import as a draft version**.
2. On the BOQ: bills, sections and items with amounts; add a non-schedule item. **Map to WBS and locations**: tick several items and give them to one node; open **Splits** (the sum must equal the quantity) and **Norms** (typed or from the rate analysis).
3. **BOQ Versions**, **New**: a contract version from the tender. The estimator **Sends for the freeze**; QS, the commercial manager and the project manager approve in the inbox. The BOQ is frozen. A change of mapping is raised from the mapping page with a reason; a second QS and the project manager approve it.
4. **BOQ Reports**: abstract, comparison of two versions, unmapped items. Sign in as a site engineer: quantities, no rates.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As an HR manager open **HR & Payroll > New Employee**. Fill the steps; type the PAN of an existing employee and see "same PAN" appear; change it. Save and add documents (with an expiry), then **Send for approval**.
2. As another HR manager open **My Approvals** and approve. Open the employee in **Employee Directory**: Approved, Active, history shows Joined.
3. As the project manager the employee reports to open the same record: statutory and bank are masked, no documents or contacts. As an unrelated site engineer the record is not found.
4. As HR choose **Amend > Employment details**, pick a new designation and "Promotion", a future date; approve as the second HR manager (the director too for a senior grade). On the date (or via the daily job) History shows the promotion.
5. **Amend > Employment status**: Separated on a date; approve. On that date the user account shows Deactivated in User Management.
6. **HR Overview**: tiles and bars open the filtered directory. **Create or link a user account** on an approved employee.

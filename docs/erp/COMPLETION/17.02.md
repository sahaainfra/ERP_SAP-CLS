# Part 17.02 completion: Mobilisation, Allocation, Transfer & Demobilisation

Part: 17.02. Date: 2026-11-13. Module `plant`, flag `ff.plant.p1702` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 17.01 and 10.01; receives certifications from 07.10. Provides allocations to the log books (17.03), the hire decision to 17.06 and the availability service to resource planning (17.08).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Full regression: 1,275 tests, 0 failures; e2e passes on desktop and mobile. Open items in section 6.

## 1. Discovery
`docs/erp/DISCOVERY/17.02.md`.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 248, 249; up, down and up again clean): `plt_requisitions`, `plt_allocations`, `plt_operator_assignments`; flag, five pages under the Plant group, page tracker, notification templates. No existing table changed.
- **Requisition** (FR-01): site, class, capacity, quantity, period, purpose, activity, priority; approved by the construction manager then the project manager; availability across the fleet for the period (free of other allocations, certificates in order on the first day, expiry during the period flagged); the plant manager decides hire (event for 17.06).
- **Allocation** (FR-02, BR-01): one machine, project, site, period, internal rate and basis with a server-worked estimate; one live allocation per machine (checked at create, update, submit and again at approval); the certificate check of 17.01 on the first day. Approved by the project manager of the project.
- **Mobilisation** (FR-03): transport, trailer, driver, gate pass (16.02, by number) and departure meter; arrival with the meter and an eight-point checklist (a failed critical item stops the acceptance); the machine is then deployed at the site through the status service of 17.01; demobilisation with the departure meter, usage, and the machine becomes demobilised.
- **Transfer** (FR-04): a new allocation that takes over when the machine arrives (the first is released with its usage); approved by the project manager, and between projects also the source project manager and the plant manager.
- **Operators** (FR-05): assigned only with a valid, verified certificate of the licence class set on the machine, checked on the first and last day (07.10); one operator per machine and shift, one machine per operator at a time; `operators.validOn` for the log books.
- **Gantt** (FR-06): machines against the calendar with allocation bars and certificate expiry markers; a draft is moved by drag or arrow keys with server validation and undo; zoom, filters, legend, print, CSV.
- **API**: 41 routes (3,071 in all). **Browser**: Equipment Requisitions with the allocation desk, New Requisition, Equipment Allocations (object with mobilisation, operators and approvals), New Allocation, Equipment Availability Gantt; English and Hindi.

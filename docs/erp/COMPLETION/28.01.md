## 1. Discovery

The integration hub already had a connector type `ai` with no operation, plus a vault, rate limits, a breaker and a log. Every module checks the person in its own service. There was no AI code. The decisions are in the [discovery note](../DISCOVERY/28.01.md).

## 2. What was built

**The gateway** — `aiOf(db).run(actor, feature, input)` is the only way to call AI (new module `ai`). Each call:

1. Checks the kill switch, then that the feature version is approved and live, then the person's role and the project against its scope.
2. Applies the quotas: calls a day per person and the monthly budget. An alert goes to SYSADM and ITADM at the threshold.
3. Builds the context through permission-checked services **as the person**. A refusal blocks the call and is logged. Masked values and sensitive keys (salary, bank, PAN, Aadhaar, …) are dropped.
4. Sends the prompt of the registered version, with the data fenced in `<data>…</data>` and a system instruction to treat it as data and never to approve, post, pay or delete. The call goes through the integration hub connector (key in the vault, rate limit, breaker, timeout, hub log).
5. Writes `ai_logs`: references to the context, the redacted prompt with a hash, the redacted answer, tokens in and out, cost, user, feature and outcome (OK / BLOCKED / ERROR, with its reason).
6. Answers with the text, the label **"AI-generated — verify"**, the sources, the model, tokens, cost and the log id.

**Providers (FR-01)**: Anthropic Claude (Messages API), OpenAI-compatible (Azure OpenAI deployments or self-hosted), and a sandbox model (no network) for development and evaluation. Each `ai_providers` row names a hub connector, a model and the prices per million tokens. Each feature version names its provider, which is its routing.

**Feature registry (FR-03)**: `aiFeatures.register` declares the versioned prompt, the tools (READ or DRAFT only) and the evaluation cases. A tool named for approve, post, pay, delete, cancel, reverse or release is refused at start-up (BR-01). The first feature is **ai.variance_narrative**: the plain-words narrative of the 27.08 "Why?" decomposition, whose context comes from the explainer service as the person.

**Enablement (FR-05, §9)**:

- A version holds roles, projects (all in the person's scope, or a list), quotas and the provider. Every change is audited.
- **Run the evaluation (FR-06)** executes every case through the gateway with the rights of the first enabled role. A version is sent for approval only when every case passes.
- The approval goes SYSADM → DIR in the approval inbox (document type `AI_FEATURE`). The preparer has no task.
- A live version can be **switched off** at once without approval. Switching it on again is a new version, evaluated and approved again.

**Kill switch**: one control per company, for DIR, SYSADM and ITADM, with a reason. It stops every call before anything reaches a provider (423), and publishes an event, sends a notification and writes an audit entry.

**Log retention**: per company (default 180 days), applied by a daily job.

**Page** `/ai/features` (**Administration → AI Features & Enablement**), with four pill tabs:

- **Features**: the kill switch card, the feature cards (live version, versions, tools, evaluation cases) and the version detail (settings, change, evaluation results, send, approval task, switch off, new version).
- **Providers**: the list and the form, which links to the hub's connectors.
- **Usage & cost**: tiles, cost by day and by feature (drillable to the log), and the monthly budgets.
- **AI log**: filters, the list, the entry with its context links and the redacted prompt and answer, and the export (AUD).

Labels are en/hi/te (area `ai`).

**Permissions**:

| Role | Grant |
|---|---|
| SYSADM, ITADM | Configure features and providers, the kill switch, read the usage and log |
| DIR | Approve, kill switch, usage |
| AUD | Read and export the log, usage |

`ai.provider.manage` and `ai.kill_switch.manage` are in the permission baseline. Using a feature needs no key: it is decided by the approved enablement (roles and projects) and by the owner's own permissions on the data.

## 4. Tests

`tests/ai-gateway.test.js` has three tests, all passing:

1. **T-01 and the gateway path**:
   - provider, draft, refused send, evaluation (3 of 3 pass), send, DIR approval;
   - a labelled answer whose log has references, tokens and cost, and the hub log shows the call;
   - a role that is not enabled gets 403, and the unscoped PM is blocked and logged;
   - the daily quota gives 429;
   - the kill switch: refused to the SE, on for DIR gives 423 for all with no OK call logged, then off again.
2. **Registry, redaction and the auditor's view**:
   - the registry refuses a tool that approves and a write mode;
   - redaction covers six kinds of personal data;
   - masked and sensitive keys are stripped;
   - a provider must match its connector;
   - a prompt-injection attempt is fenced and the PAN redacted in the log;
   - switching off is immediate and a new version is a draft;
   - the auditor's usage, log, audited detail and export (no prompts); SE and SYSADM limits;
   - retention purge, and the flag off.
3. **Volume**: 100,000 log entries; the filtered lists and the usage stay well under the limit, and the queries use an index.

`e2e/ai-features.spec.js` passes on desktop and mobile:

1. SYSADM prepares a version, sets the roles and runs the evaluation (3 passes), then sends it; DIR approves.
2. A PM call is answered and labelled.
3. DIR pulls the kill switch, and the next call gets 423.
4. Usage, and the auditor's log with the fenced prompt.

Regression: `architecture` (the guide now also describes `platform/locks` from 27.07), `authz-api` and `i18n-api` (namespace `ai`), lint, typecheck (0 errors), route, nav, schema lint, migration up/down, schema-diff and the permission audit.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set `ff.ai.p2801` (and `ff.bi.p2701` to `ff.bi.p2703` and `ff.bi.p2708` for the narrative), record G1 and G2, and add an AI connector in **Administration → Connectors** (sandbox, or Anthropic with its key).
2. As SYSADM, open **AI Features & Enablement**:
   - **Providers**: add one that names the connector.
   - **Features → Variance narrative → Prepare a version**: set the roles (PM, DIR), then **Run the evaluation** and **Send for approval**.
3. As a director, approve it in the approval inbox.
4. Call `POST /api/v1/ai/run/ai.variance_narrative` as a PM on one of their projects. The answer is labelled; a project outside their scope is refused.
5. As a director, **Stop all AI** with a reason; every call is refused at once.
6. As the auditor, open **AI log** and an entry: the context links and the redacted prompt and answer.

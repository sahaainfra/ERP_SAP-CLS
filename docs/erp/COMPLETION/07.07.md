## 7. How to verify manually
1. As HR run the accrual (`POST /hr/leave/accrual/run`), then as an employee open **Apply Leave**: choose type and dates; the days, balance after and clashes appear.
2. Send it; as the reporting manager open **Leave Approvals** and approve; **My Leave** shows it and the balance; **Team Leave Calendar** shows it; the attendance register shows the days as leave.
3. Cancel it before it starts: the balance is back. As HR open **Leave Balance Report**, **Leave Types & Rules** and **Year-End Processing** (the preview of a past year).

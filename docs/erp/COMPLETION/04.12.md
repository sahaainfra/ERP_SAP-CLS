## 7. How to verify manually
1. As the procurement manager generate a purchase order (04.11) from an approved record. Open **Documents > Generated Documents**, open it: the **Signatures** panel shows no signature.
2. As the project manager **Sign** by electronic approval; as the accounts manager add a picture under **Signature Register > My signature picture** and sign by picture. **Open the signed copy**: both stamps are on the last page.
3. Scan the QR code of the PDF or open `/verify/<code>` (no sign-in): genuine and current, the two signers. No amount or party is shown. Choose the original file, then the signed copy: both match. Change one byte of a copy and choose it: "NOT the document".
4. Generate a new version with a reason: the old code now says "Superseded on <date>". Cancel the current one as the project manager: "Cancelled on <date>".
5. As the system administrator open **Signature Register**, revoke a signature with a reason: the public check shows it revoked. Under **Settings** add "amount" to the public fields: it waits for approval; a director approves it under My Approvals; only then does the public page show the amount (never for a payslip).
6. Approve a document in the workflow that has an automatic generation rule: its approvers appear as signatures with the time of their approval.

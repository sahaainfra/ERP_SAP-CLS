# Part 04.03 completion: approval inbox, approval actions UI and decision evidence panel

Status: **Ready for review, with the open items below** (the evidence of procurement, stores and finance documents, delivery of links by e-mail and WhatsApp, and the translation of the pages are later parts: D-188 to D-194). Feature flag `ff.eng.p0403` (OFF by default, ON in development and test).

## What was found
The engine had the tasks and a decision form on the Workflow Instance page, My Work as a list of titles and the bell as a count. There was no place that joined a person's tasks with the time left, the amount and the project; no evidence; no bulk or link approval. The modules that will supply evidence (budget, stores, procurement, finance) do not exist yet. See `docs/erp/DISCOVERY/04.03.md`.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 60 and 61; nothing existing altered): `wf_approval_policies` (per document type: bulk approval and its limit, approval from a link, its life, the amount above which the approver signs in again) and `wf_link_tokens` (hash of the token only); the flag, four navigation entries, four tracker rows and the `workflow.inbox` widget.
- **Inbox** (`GET /workflow/inbox`, `/inbox/counts`, `/inbox/submissions`): the tasks that are mine by name, by a role held for the project or site, or by a delegation, most urgent first, each with the document, project, amount, who raised it, age, time status (on track, due soon, overdue, escalated), risk flags and whether it can be approved with others; filters, three orders, pages, counts; saved variants as saved views of the list `workflow.inbox`.
- **Task page and evidence** (`GET /workflow/tasks/:id/evidence`): the document at a glance, sections and risk flags computed when opened, what this person may do and why not. Modules register providers with `evidenceProviders.register(docType, fn)`; the framework adds, for every type, the approver's authority against the amount, the time, the decisions so far and earlier decisions on documents with the same title in the same project. A provider that fails is reported without stopping the others; masked values are hidden by the field masks; a type without a provider says so in words.
- **Bulk approval** (`POST /workflow/tasks/bulk-act`): each task is checked against the policy of its type (eligible type, amount under the bulk limit, no risk flag, no step that needs a file or checklist, no question waiting, the person may decide it) and approved through the ordinary call; refused ones come back with the reason. Audited as one action.
- **Secure links**: made by the approver for their own task (`POST /workflow/tasks/:id/links`), opened before sign-in at `/approve-link`; hashed token, minutes of life by policy, used once, replaced by a newer one, the same answer for every bad token, rate-limited; above the policy amount the person must be signed in as themselves; audited and evented; the token is in no event.
- **Pages**: My Approvals (worklist), Approval Task (object page), Approvals on a phone (cards, one-tap approve, a reason required to reject or return, offline notice), My Submissions & Watch List; the approval settings are a section of the Escalation Paths & SLA page and change through the approval of the engine. The bell counts the engine's tasks and the widget `workflow.inbox` shows them.

## Click-path for a reviewer
1. Raise four sample documents as a project director (₹12,000, ₹18,000, ₹90,000 and ₹7,000 with the emergency fact). As the project manager open **My Approvals**: four waiting, the emergency one flagged. Filter "crane", save the variant, clear, pick the variant again.
2. Tick "only those that can be approved together": two remain. **Select every one…**, **Approve 2 selected**: "2 approved". Select the emergency one and approve: "1 not approved… risk flags".
3. Open a task: the Evidence tab (the request against that person's earlier requests, the time, decisions so far, earlier decisions on similar items), Document, History, Attachments, and the decision form. A document type without a module's evidence says so.
4. **Make a secure link for my phone**; open it in a window where nobody is signed in: the document and Approve; used once; the second time it says it has expired or was used.
5. On a 360 px screen open **Approvals**: cards by urgency, Approve is one tap, Reject asks for a reason.
7. **My Submissions & Watch List**: what you raised and who it waits for; follow and stop following.

## Tests and acceptance

- **AC-01** inbox and task page on desktop and phone with the sample document type. **AC-02** the evidence panel shows live data for the sample document and an empty-state message for types whose module has not registered evidence; live budget and commitments appear when the procurement module registers its provider (D-188). **AC-03** the new pages pass the page checks. **AC-04** permission and isolation tests (own tasks, stranger 404, auditor reads and cannot decide, other company, link for another person). **AC-05** policy changes go through the approval of the engine and are audited. **AC-06** see Results. **AC-07** this report.

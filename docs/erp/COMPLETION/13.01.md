## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 182, 183; nothing existing altered): `plan_calendars`, `plan_schedules`, `plan_activities`, `plan_dependencies`, `plan_activity_boq`, `plan_templates`, with the rules held by the schema too (no negative duration, no link to itself, one link per pair, a constraint needs its date, percentages 0 to 100 %, one schedule in force and one open version per project). Seed: the flag, a navigation group with five entries, the page tracker, notification templates `plan.schedule.approved` and `plan.schedule.rejected` (English, Hindi), and two system templates: `STD-RCC-FLOOR` (shuttering 3 → reinforcement 3 → concreting 1 → curing 7 → deshuttering 2, repeated per floor and chained at concreting) and `STD-FOUNDATION` (per WBS node).
- **CPM engine** (`modules/plan/cpm.js`, pure functions): forward and backward pass over the precedence diagram with FS, SS, FF and SF links and lags in working days; total float (LF − EF), free float (the least slack to any successor), critical at total float ≤ 0; calendars (working week, holiday calendar of 01.03, site exceptions) with an activity's own calendar converted to the schedule's; SNET and FNLT constraints; progress at a data date (done work is fixed, work in progress is scheduled from the data date with its remaining duration); level-of-effort and summary activities spanning what they cover; loop detection that returns the loop's path.
- **Server** (`modules/plan/service.js`): calendars and templates (drafted by one person, put in force by another); schedules (create, update, delete a draft, calculate, progress, submit, withdraw, cancel, amend into a new version, history, print, import, current schedule of a project); activities (grid with filters, critical, delayed, a WBS branch, search, paging; create, update, bulk update, delete); links (create, update, delete; a loop refused with its path); generation from a template per WBS node or location, or one activity per WBS node with its BOQ lines, previewed before it is made, skipping what exists; the critical path and float report (bands, paths, near-critical list, CSV); workflow `PLAN_SCHEDULE` approved by the project controls manager, after which logic and durations are locked and only progress is taken; the event consumer `site.dpr.approved` for progress from the DPR; events `plan.schedule.calculated`, `plan.schedule.approved`, `plan.progress.updated`; object type and file entity `plan_schedule` (discussion, timeline, attachments, related).
- **API**: 43 routes under `/api/v1/plan`, in the route policy (every change audited) and `openapi.yaml`.
- **Permissions**: `plan.schedule.{view,create,edit,delete,calculate,progress,submit,approve,cancel,amend,print,export,import}`, `plan.calendar.{view,manage,approve}`. The planner prepares; the project controls manager approves and puts calendars and templates in force; project director and director read and approve; project and construction managers read, print and export; the auditor reads; site roles read the schedule and calendars of their project.
- **Browser**: Schedules, Activities and Links (grid with the CPM columns, critical and delayed filters, WBS tree, bulk edit, links), Generate Activities from Template, Critical Path and Float Report, Work Calendars; English and Hindi.
- **Performance**: `npm run erp:plan-perf` (50 projects, 1 000 schedule versions, 20 live schedules of 5 000 activities: 100 000 activities and about 150 000 links): CPM pass of 5 000 activities p95 171 ms (limit 3 s); schedule list p95 8 ms; one schedule 2 ms; activities grid (first page, critical, WBS branch, search, page 50) p95 ≤ 14 ms; float report p95 109 ms; save 7 ms; the query plans are printed and use the indexes.
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 97, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, ADR-466 to ADR-470, D-472 and D-473, openapi.

## 6. Known limitations
D-472: the DPR (14.05), look-ahead (13.04), work authorisation (14.01), MRP (15.02) and resource planning (17.08) are not built; progress from the DPR arrives as the event `site.dpr.approved` and the approved schedule is read at `GET /api/v1/plan/current`. Baselines and variance are 13.03; the Gantt is 13.02. D-473: the schedule import takes JSON rows (codes, durations, predecessors such as `A10;A20SS+2`), not a workbook of the 04.13 engine; the float report exports CSV and is not e-mailed on a schedule; calculation runs in the request (about 0.15 s for 5 000 activities), not as a background job. Durations are whole working days (ADR-467).

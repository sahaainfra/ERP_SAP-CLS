## 2. What was built

**Forecasts (FR-01, FR-02, BR-01)** come from `src/server/modules/ai/forecast.js`. Each history is read from its owner as the person:

| Use case | History | Period | Extra |
|---|---|---|---|
| Material rate | 15.09 monthly average landed rate of an item and region; the category price index as a driver | month | — |
| Collections | approved client receipts of a project or of every project the person may see | month | — |
| Productivity | quantity of an activity a week | week | finish of the remaining quantity |
| Completion date | percent complete of a project, activities weighted by duration | week | earliest, forecast and latest completion against the scheduled finish |

- **Methods**:
  - moving average;
  - Holt linear smoothing (level and trend);
  - regression on time and, for rates, on the price index.

  Each forecast gives an 80 % range from the method's one-step errors, widening with the horizon, and lists its drivers.
- **Labels**: every point is labelled Actual, Calculated or Forecast. Every answer carries "Forecast — statistical, verify".
- **Official forecasts** come from an approved model and are kept in `aif_forecasts`, one a day per model and subject, never in the owners' tables. Without an approved model the page shows a labelled preview with the default method, and nothing is stored.

**Model registry and back-testing (FR-04, section 9)**:

1. A version holds the method, parameters (checked against bounds) and horizon.
2. The preparer runs a rolling-origin back-test on a subject they may see. It reports MAE, MAPE, RMSE, bias and the share inside the 80 % range, compared with the last-value forecast (skill). The result is kept on the version, audited and exportable (XLSX, CSV, PDF).
3. The version can be sent only after its back-test. The CFO or the project controls manager approves it in the inbox (`AI_MODEL`); the preparer can never approve their own.
4. One official version per use case: a newer approval supersedes the earlier one. Retiring is immediate. A new version is prepared from an approved one.

**Anomaly detection (FR-03)** complements the 27.09 rules with robust z-scores (median and MAD) of each value against its own series:

| Kind | Series | Visible to holders of |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase rate | order lines of an item and unit | `proc.purchase_order.view` |
| Material issue | daily issues of a project and item | `inv.issue.view` |
| Site attendance | daily attendance of a site | `att.register.view` |
| Payment | payments to a payee | `fin.payment.view` |
| Paid without attendance (D-780) | staff paid in an approved run for a month in which they have no attendance record while others do | `pay.run.view` |

- **Settings**: the threshold (z ≥ 3.5), minimum history and look-back are company parameters.
- **Runs**: detection runs nightly at 03:40 and on request. Each run adds one flag per source record and publishes `ai.anomaly.flagged`.
- **Review**: a person confirms or dismisses a flag with a note. The review is audited and publishes `ai.anomaly.reviewed`.

**Predictions to dashboards and scenarios**: the widget `ai.forecast_anomalies` serves dashboards and Project 360, and `forecastOf(db).latest` serves other modules.

**Page** `/ai/forecasts` (**Reports → Forecasts & Anomalies**), with three pill tabs:

- **Forecasts**:
  - use case and subject;
  - the label;
  - tiles: next period with its range, last recorded, back-test MAPE, completion;
  - the chart with history, forecast and range;
  - the drivers with their source link;
  - the labelled points.
- **Anomalies**: open flags by kind, filters, run, and review with a note.
- **Models & back-testing**:
  - the registry and a new model;
  - the version with its back-test tiles and rows, export, send and the approval task;
  - retire, and a new version.

Labels are en/hi/te (area `af`).

**Permissions**:

| Role | Grant |
|---|---|
| ITADM, SYSADM | Configure models, run detection |
| CFO, PCMGR | Prepare models, use forecasts, review anomalies, approve models, run detection |
| PROCMGR | Prepare models, use forecasts, review anomalies |
| DIR, AUD | Read |

## 4. Tests

`tests/ai-forecast.test.js` has four tests, all passing:

1. **Statistics**:
   - the robust z-score;
   - the three methods on exact series;
   - the regression uses a non-collinear driver;
   - ranges contain the point and widen;
   - the back-test metrics, and too short a history gives no back-test.
2. **Models and forecasts**:
   - a preview is not stored;
   - the back-test is required before sending, and is refused to IT;
   - approval by PCMGR, history and export;
   - the official forecast with six forecast points, ranges, the price-index driver and labels;
   - observations untouched, and one stored forecast a day;
   - completion and productivity forecasts, and collections;
   - roles;
   - a version 2 approved by the CFO supersedes version 1;
   - retiring returns the use case to previews.

## 6. Known limitations

D-784:

- The models are statistical baselines with no seasonal model.
- Official forecasts are stored when opened, not refreshed nightly.
- Confirmed flags do not become 27.09 findings automatically.
- IT configures models but cannot back-test them on data it may not see; a business owner runs the back-test.

## 7. How to verify manually

1. In a test company, set `ff.ai.p2803` (and `ff.proc.p1509` for rate intelligence), and record G1 and G2.
2. As the procurement manager, open **Reports → Forecasts & Anomalies → Models & back-testing → New model**:
   - use case Material rate, method Regression;
   - open it, choose an item with rate history, **Run the back-test**, then **Send for approval**.
3. As the project controls manager, approve it in the approval inbox.
4. **Forecasts**: choose the item. The forecast is official, labelled, with its range, drivers and the kind of each point.
5. **Anomalies**:
   - as PCMGR, **Look for anomalies now**;
   - as the procurement manager, open a flagged purchase rate and confirm it with a note.

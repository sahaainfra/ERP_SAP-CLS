## 7. How to verify manually
1. Open a conversation with a second person on another browser: send a message; the sender's tick turns to read when the other opens it.
2. Search a word in the list; open the hit: the thread jumps to that message and highlights it. Star and pin it from the message menu.
3. Details: Media shows photos, documents and links; Calls shows the history.
4. Press the phone button: the other person sees a ringing banner; join; mute, camera, hang up. (With a TURN server set in Messaging Settings, repeat across two networks: this is T-03.)
5. Administration, Messaging Settings: change the edit window with a reason; a second administrator approves it; it applies from its date. Place and release a legal hold there.

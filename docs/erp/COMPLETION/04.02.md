# Part 04.02 completion: approval matrix, authority limits, rules, delegation, escalation and SLA

Status: **Ready for review, with the open items below** (leave-based delegation, notification delivery, use of the matrix by the other modules: D-182 to D-187). Feature flag `ff.eng.p0402` (OFF by default, ON in development and test).

## What was found
The engine of 04.01 had fixed steps per definition, plain-hours due times, one escalation to the administrators and delegation of single tasks. Calendars (01.03) existed. There is no leave module. See `docs/erp/DISCOVERY/04.02.md`.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 58 and 59; nothing existing altered, schema-diff of the 437 locked objects empty): `wf_matrix`, `wf_matrix_rules`, `wf_authority_limits`, `wf_delegations`, `wf_escalation_paths`, `wf_sla_settings`; the flag, six navigation entries, six tracker rows.
- **Approval matrix**: versioned decision tables per document type for the company, a department or a project; rules with a priority, conditions on any fact the document is submitted with, and steps (role, `A|B`, the reporting manager, hours allowed, parallel group, a condition of their own, final authority); first full match wins; effective from a date; gaps block activation; shadowed rules and duplicate priorities are reported; history and comparison. The reference bands of section B (32 document types, 63 limits) are written for a company when first needed.
- **Routing**: a definition with the setting "route by the approval matrix" takes its steps from the matrix when the instance is made; the instance keeps a copy and the matrix version and rule in its history. The sample document type is routed by it.
- **Authority limits**: the final step of a chain must belong to a role that may approve the amount, else the step passes up the hierarchy to the first role with authority (ADR-164), and the trace says so.
- **Delegation**: all or selected document types, a period, an optional cap within the delegator's own limit; the delegate sees and decides the delegator's tasks, recorded "on behalf of"; new tasks of a person who has delegated are made for the delegate; refused to oneself, to a lower clearance, to someone who raised a waiting document; more than 15 days waits for the delegator's manager.
- **SLA and escalation**: time in working hours (day hours and company calendar), reminder at 50 %, escalation to the next level of the hierarchy at 100 %, to the director at 200 %, per document type; defaults 24 h, payments 8 h, permits to work 2 h; the points are stored on each task when it is made; a companion task is made for the next level and whichever decides settles the slot.
- **Simulator**: the chain a described document would get, on the version in force or on a draft; it is the function that routes real documents.
- **Every change is an approval** (SYSADM, then the CFO or the process owner, then the director above ₹1 Cr) on documents of the engine: `WF_MATRIX`, `WF_AUTHORITY`, `WF_CONFIG`, `WF_DELEGATION`.
- **API** (21 routes under `/api/v1/workflow`: matrix, authority-limits, delegations, escalation-paths, sla-settings; route policy; OpenAPI) and **six pages**: Approval Matrix, Matrix Rule Editor, Authority Limits, Approval Simulator, Delegations, Escalation Paths & SLA.
- **Permissions** `eng.matrix.view|manage`, `eng.delegation.use|admin`; parameters for the working day and the 15 days; events for matrix, limits, settings and delegations.

## Click-path for a reviewer
1. As a system administrator open **Administration > Approval Matrix**: 33 document types with version 1 in force. Make a new version of **Payment release**; remove its last rule and press **Check for gaps**: it names a document no rule fits, and **Request activation** is refused. Add a rule for everything else (the director), check again (clean) and request activation from tomorrow.
2. A second system administrator, then the chief financial officer, approve on the Workflow Instance page. In **Approval Simulator** the same ₹2 Cr payment is routed to the director from tomorrow and to the accounts manager, CFO and director today.
3. **Authority Limits**: change the project manager's limit for purchase orders, ask, approve; the grid shows it. A limit above ₹1 Cr also needs the director.
4. In the simulator choose a draft whose last step is a project manager and a ₹80 lakh purchase order: the step passes to the project director and says why.
5. As a project manager open **Delegations**, delegate to a site engineer for two days: the engineer approves the manager's task, the record says "on behalf of"; end the delegation. A delegation of 25 days waits for the manager.
6. **Escalation Paths & SLA**: the five levels and the hours per document type; change payments to 6 hours through approval.

## Tests and acceptance
- **T-01** each default band resolves to its chain, written out again in the test rather than read from the seed, for all 32 document types (`tests/workflow-matrix.test.js`). **T-02** an approver over the limit is passed over; a delegation applies within its dates only (time moved with the engine's clock). **T-03** reminder, first and second escalation come at the configured working times (a Friday submission skips Sunday). **T-04** the simulator's steps and approvers equal those of the real submission and the rule is recorded in its history.
- Also: gaps, shadowed rules, duplicate priorities, effective dates and history, maker-checker of every change (the maker cannot approve), limit changes with the director above ₹1 Cr, delegation rules and the 15-day approval, a project's own matrix over the company's, isolation between companies, the SLA and hierarchy changes governing new tasks, six browser tests (`e2e/matrix.spec.js`).
- **AC-01** `tests/workflow-matrix.test.js` T-01 and the Approval Matrix page. **AC-02** delegation, escalation and the simulator from the browser. **AC-03** the six pages pass the page checks. **AC-04** permission tests (view, manage, own delegations, administrators, other company). **AC-05** a matrix, limit, setting or delegation takes effect only through its own approval; no route sets a status. **AC-06** see Results. **AC-07** this report.

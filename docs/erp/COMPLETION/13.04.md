# Part 13.04 completion: Look-ahead planning, constraints and weekly work plans

Part: 13.04. Date: 2026-11-20. Feature flag `ff.plan.p1304` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 13.01 (schedule, activities, CPM, calendars) and reads the RFIs and drawings of 11.01 and 11.02, the crews and deployments of 07.03 and the resources of 13.02; provides `plan.weekly_plan.approved` (the daily plan) and `GET /api/v1/plan/daily-plan` to 14.01, and `plan.constraint.raised` and `plan.constraint.cleared` to the dashboards.

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items are in section 6.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 188, 189; nothing existing altered): `plan_lookaheads`, `plan_constraints` (a partial unique index keeps one open constraint for an activity, type and source), `plan_weekly_plans` (one live plan for a project, site, week and revision) and `plan_weekly_items` (commitments by activity and crew with the completion and the variance code). Seed: the flag, three navigation entries, three page-tracker rows, two notification templates in English and Hindi.
- **Look-ahead** (FR-01, FR-03, `modules/plan/lookahead.js`): the activities that run in the next 1 to 12 weeks by week, site and work front, with their open constraints and whether each is ready; a look-ahead can be saved with its counts.
- **Constraints** (FR-02, BR-02, ADR-481, ADR-482): found by the system from the open RFIs and the drawings without an IFC revision of engineering, the late predecessors of the schedule and labour no approved crew covers; they clear by themselves (as `system`, with the audit row and the event) when the RFI is closed, the drawing is issued or cancelled, the predecessor is finished or a crew is deployed; logged by hand with owner and due date; a constraint found by the system that does not apply is waived with a reason and is not raised again for that source.
- **Make-ready** (FR-03): an activity is ready when none of its constraints is open; a weekly plan is not sent with a commitment on an activity that is not (422 naming the activities).
- **Weekly work plan** (FR-04, FR-05, BR-01, ADR-483, ADR-484): commitments by activity and crew (quantity above zero, the activity planned inside the week, a crew of the project); the construction manager and then the project manager approve (whoever prepared it gets no task); the site engineer and the work supervisor then record what was done; a commitment is completed when the completed quantity reaches the committed one; the week is closed with PPC fixed and a variance code for every commitment not completed; an approved plan is changed by a revision that replaces it when approved; the daily breakdown spreads each commitment over the working days between the dates of its activity for work authorisation; the PPC trend and the variance reasons as a report with XLSX, PDF and CSV.
- **API**: 31 routes under `/api/v1/plan`, in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`; optimistic locking on update; `Idempotency-Key` required on create, submit, amend and close.
- **Permissions**: `plan.lookahead.view`, `plan.constraint.{view,manage}`, `plan.weekly_plan.{view,create,edit,delete,submit,approve,cancel,amend,print,export,record}`. PLAN prepares; CM prepares and approves; SE and WSUP commit in a draft, manage constraints and record the week; PM manages constraints and approves; PD, PCMGR and AUD read, print and export; the rest of the project reads.
- **Events and notifications**: `plan.constraint.raised`, `plan.constraint.cleared`, `plan.weekly_plan.approved` (with the daily lines), `plan.weekly_plan.closed` (with PPC and the variance counts); the originator is told of an approval and of a rejection.
- **Browser**: Look-ahead Board (a column for each week, constraint indicators, the ready mark, drag or "Move to week" with the server check and undo, detect now, save, take ready cards into a plan), Constraint Log (list with filters, export, new, detail with clear, waive and edit), Weekly Work Plan (list; one plan with commitments, approval, record, close, daily breakdown and history; the PPC trend and variance reasons report); English and Hindi.
- **Performance**: `npm run erp:plan-lookahead-perf` (50 projects, 20 schedules of 5,000 activities, 100,000 constraints, 100,000 closed weekly plans with 300,000 commitments): look-ahead board of six weeks of 5,000 activities p95 74 ms; constraint log first page 194 ms, open ones of a project 10 ms, search 238 ms; weekly plans first page 48 ms, closed ones of a project 3 ms; one plan 6 ms; daily breakdown 4 ms; PPC report over 2,000 weeks 53 ms; a plan saved 12 ms (limits 500 ms, 300 ms for one record, 800 ms for a save); detection on a 5,000-activity schedule 291 ms (limit 3 s). The query plans are printed and use the indexes.
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 100, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, ADR-481 to ADR-484, D-479, openapi.

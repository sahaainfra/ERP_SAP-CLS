## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 231, 232; up, down and up again clean): `inv_issues`, `inv_issue_lines`, `inv_site_returns`, `inv_sub_certified`, `inv_free_issue_ledger`; flag, four pages under the Stores group, page tracker, notification templates. No existing table changed.
- **Issue slip** (FR-01, FR-02, FR-03): made from a work authorisation (the authorised quantity, what was issued before and the balance are filled in) or an approved material request, to the own crew, a subcontractor (free or chargeable, with a rate) or equipment; WBS and cost code come from the authorisation. More than the authorised quantity (plus the tolerance), or no authorisation, stops the submit until the control exception CP-INV-01 is approved (BR-02). Free stock is checked at submit.
- **Hand over and post** (BR-01): the slip is ready to hand over (a chargeable one after the subcontractor and the quantity surveyor approve); the receiver acknowledges with a code made on request and sent to their own device (hashed, expiring, five tries) or with a signature photo; only then does the stock go out at the store's average rate, charged to the WBS and cost code, and `inv.issue.posted` is published once.
- **Site return** (FR-04): unused, damaged or scrap material comes back against the slip with its condition and reason; the store manager approves; good goes back into stock at the original issue rate (not the current average), damaged goes in held for inspection, scrap stays out; the event credits the cost.
- **Free-issue ledger** (FR-06): entitled = norm x certified quantity with wastage; excess = issued less returned less entitled; recovery at the weighted average rate; kept by month; told once per change; every row opens the slips behind it; certified quantities are loaded until the subcontract measurements exist.
- **API**: 35 routes under `/api/v1/inv` (2,827 routes in all). **Browser**: Issue Material (mobile; item scan or typed), Material Issues (list and the slip with the hand-over panel, postings, returns), Site Returns (list, form, object), Subcontractor Free-Issue Reconciliation (report with drill-down and CSV).

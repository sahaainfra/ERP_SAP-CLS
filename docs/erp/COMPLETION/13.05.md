## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 190, 191; nothing existing altered): `plan_delays` (the delay, its classification, its revision and its source), `plan_tia` (each analysis with its fragnet, the schedule it was made on and the completion before and after), `plan_delay_evidence`. Seed: the flag, three navigation entries, three page-tracker rows, two notification templates in English and Hindi.
- **Delays** (FR-01, ADR-485, `modules/plan/delays.js`): event, dates (delay days worked out by the server), eight causes, three responsibilities, the affected activities kept with their code and name, mitigation and concurrency notes; not sent without activities and evidence; the classification approved by the project manager and then the commercial manager (the planner holds no approval); locked once approved; a revision copies it with its evidence and replaces it when approved.
- **Drafts** (FR-02): an RFI answered with a time impact drafts a delay (late drawings, employer, from the required-by date of the RFI, the activity the RFI names, the RFI and its drawing as evidence); the same event again drafts nothing more; a new answer updates the draft while it is one.
- **Time-impact analysis** (FR-03, BR-01, ADR-486): the fragnet's activities and links are added to an in-memory copy of the schedule as at the event date; the CPM pass of 13.01 runs with and without them; the completion before and after, the shift in calendar and working days, the activities that moved and the fragnet's own dates are shown first and kept on request; the schedule and the baselines are never written.
- **Evidence** (FR-04): RFIs, letters, drawings, files attached to the delay (through the file service), constraints and weekly plans of the same project; candidates searched by number or title.
- **Summary and claims** (FR-05, ADR-487): by cause and responsibility, in delay days and impact days, with excusable, compensable and not-excusable totals, drill-down to the delays and XLSX, PDF and CSV; `GET /delays/claims` gives the approved delays of a project with their analysis and totals.
- **API**: 21 routes under `/api/v1/plan`, in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`; optimistic locking on update; `Idempotency-Key` required on create, submit, amend and the saved analysis.
- **Permissions**: `plan.delay.{view,create,edit,delete,submit,approve,cancel,amend,print,export}` and `plan.delay.analyse`. PLAN records, analyses and sends; PM and COMMGR approve; PCMGR analyses; PD cancels; CM, QS read, print and export; DIR reads and prints; AUD reads and exports; SE, DOC and DESIGN read.
- **Events and notifications**: `plan.delay.drafted`, `plan.delay.approved`; the originator is told of an approval and of a rejection.
- **Browser**: Delay Register (list; one delay with its figures, activities, evidence and files, analyses, approval, revision and history), Time Impact Analysis (three steps: the delay and the date, the fragnet, the result with its chart), Delay Summary (parameters, chart, matrix with totals, drill-down, export); English and Hindi.
- **Performance**: `npm run erp:plan-delay-perf` (50 projects, 100,000 delays with an analysis and evidence each, 20 schedules of 5,000 activities): register first page p95 106 ms, approved of a project 2 ms, by cause and responsibility 10 ms, search 120 ms; one delay 1 ms; analysis of a 5,000-activity schedule 248 ms previewed, 194 ms saved (limit 3 s); summary of a project 15 ms, of the company 390 ms; what the claims read 10 ms; a save 2 ms (limits 500 ms, 300 ms for one record, 800 ms for a save). The query plans are printed and use the indexes.
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 101, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, ADR-485 to ADR-487, D-480, openapi.

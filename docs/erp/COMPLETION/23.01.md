## 4. Click-path for the reviewer
1. As FINEX: Finance → **Manual Journal**. Type JV, date 15-06-2026, narration; line 1 account 560004 Site overheads — the Project field appears (the account needs it), choose the project, debit 25,000.75; line 2 account 150002 Bank, credit 25,000. The bar says "Not balanced · Difference ₹0.75" and **Save and send** is disabled; make the credit 25,000.75: "Balanced". Send.
2. As ACCMGR: open the journal (Approvals inbox or Journals) → **Approve and post**: voucher `JV/2026-27/000001`, period, posted by.
3. **Account Ledger** of 560004: one row, closing ₹25,000.75 Dr, the voucher a link. **Trial Balance** on 30-06-2026: balanced; open the account from its row.
4. Journal → **Reverse** (reason, date) → ACCMGR approves → both journals show the link; the trial balance nets the account to zero.

## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 376, 377): `pay_fnf` (approval-controlled, workflow `PAY_FNF`), `pay_payslips`, `pay_transfers`, `pay_transfer_lines`, `pay_fnf_rules` (dated settlement rules with legal references); eleven payroll accounts in the chart template and every company's chart; five standard posting rules; the flag, three pages, page-tracker rows, two notification templates. No existing object changed (schema-diff clean).
- **FR-01 posting** (ADR-741 to 743): the 23.02 engine now takes events registered by other modules (`definePostingEvent`) and rule lines that repeat over a list (`each`). The approved payroll run posts salary cost by project, site, department and cost centre (24.02 allocation, split to the paisa), employer contributions, the gratuity provision, PF / ESI / PT / LWF / TDS / other payables, advances by employee, recoveries and net pay payable; the approved wage run posts site wages on the project and site; each wage payment posts from salaries payable to cash or bank; the approved F&F posts its own journal. Once a run's journal is posted, `hr.payroll.posted` carries the labour cost of each project to the 12.03 cost ledger and so to project control (14.07). A run approved before the engine took the event, or a failed posting, is posted from the page (once per event).
- **FR-02 bank transfer** (ADR-744, 745): the net pay of a locked run (or a settlement) in the bank file of the company's account, built and checked by the 23.04 builder; payroll prepares, the accounts manager or CFO releases (never the preparer); release posts Dr salaries payable Cr bank; the bank's answer per line (UTR, or returned → reversed and payable again). Held salaries, cash or cheque payees, employees without a verified account or whose account name does not match are left out and listed. A salary can be held or released after the lock (HR or payroll, with the reason).
- **FR-03 payslips** (ADR-746): generated from the 04.11 `PAYSLIP` type (official, QR-verifiable) — standard or the new shared Hindi format `PAYSLIP_HI` by the employee's language — encrypted with AES-128 (PDF standard security handler R4; opened with the employee code in capitals followed by the date of birth DDMMYYYY), e-mailed as a signed link by the 04.11 delivery, and in self-service at **My Payslips** (views recorded). Large runs are generated as a background job with progress.
- **FR-04 full-and-final** (ADR-747 to 749): drafted automatically when HR releases the inputs (BR-01: only after the clearance; refused otherwise); last salary through the 24.02 engine for months no approved run paid; leave encashment with its s.10(10AA) exemption; notice pay or recovery; gratuity (part year beyond six months, five years or one year fixed-term under the Code on Social Security from 21-11-2025 on the Code-on-Wages base, capped and exempted); bonus of the year; recoveries; adjustments (on the page or from a CSV); TDS as the tax of the year less tax deducted; due date two working days after the last working day (Code on Wages s.17(2)) with the late flag; approval PAY → HRMGR → CFO; amendment with comparison; payment by bank transfer; F&F statement PDF; the relieving letter issued from the page (07.13). The next regular run leaves out a leaver whose last month the settlement pays.
- **API**: 35 operations under `/api/v1/pay` (posting, transfers, payslips, fnf; every route in the route policy). **Browser**: Post Payroll & Create Payment (wizard: Post · Pay · Payslips; wage runs tab), Full & Final Settlement (object page: Components · Adjustments · Approvals · Payment · Related; sticky action bar), My Payslips; English and Hindi (My Payslips also Telugu).
- **Repairs and extensions of other parts** (no copied engine): 23.02 engine (module events, `each` lines, rule validator), 04.11 (password option of generation, internal delivery for a module that checked its own right, Hindi payslip in the library), 24.02 (shared regime deductions, the settled leaver left out of the run), 24.04 (`bonusOf`, `regularRun` exposed). The payroll performance script was repaired (it had stopped at the D-745 background job).

## 7. How to verify manually
1. Payroll → Post Payroll & Create Payment: open an approved run. **1. Post**: the journal the run makes, balanced, by project; **Post to the ledger** if it is not posted yet.
2. **2. Pay**: the employees not paid and why; hold or release one with a reason; choose the bank account and the value date; **Prepare the transfer**.
3. As the accounts manager (or CFO): open the run, **Release to the bank**, download the **Bank file**; open the lines, enter each UTR (or mark returned with the reason), **Confirm**.
4. **3. Payslips**: **Generate the payslips**, then **Send by e-mail**; the counts of generated, e-mailed, without address and seen.
5. As the employee: My Payslips → Download; the PDF asks for the password (employee code + DDMMYYYY).
6. Payroll → Full & Final Settlement: a leaver whose clearance HR completed is listed; **Prepare**; review the components with their working, add an adjustment, **Send for approval**; HR manager and CFO approve from the approvals inbox; on **Payment**, prepare the transfer; after it is confirmed paid, **Issue the relieving letter** on **Related**; **F&F statement** prints the PDF.

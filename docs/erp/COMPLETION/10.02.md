## 7. How to verify manually
1. As the project controller open **Project from Award**, choose an awarded tender: read what will be copied, add the code, type, start, team, a site and a clause, **Create project and baseline pack**.
2. On the pack: **Priced BOQ** equals the estimate; no costs for you. As the project manager, **Handover**: record a meeting with an action (it becomes a task).
3. **Send for sign-off**; estimation, the commercial manager and the project director approve in the inbox (the director too above 50 crore). The pack shows signed off, intact; costs for those who see them; **Correct this baseline** starts a superseding pack.
4. **Baseline vs Current** shows the baseline by section and says why the current side is empty.

## 6. Known limitations
D-323 to D-330. A site with no planned post for a designation counts as outside the plan (the director also approves) until HR sets the plan.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As HR set the plan (`PUT /hr/site-plans`), open **New Deployment**, choose an employee, project, site, role on site and a site access role; the plan banner shows within or outside the plan; send for approval; the project manager approves.
2. Try a second primary deployment for the same dates: it is refused with the number of the first.
3. Open **Crews & Gangs**, make a crew, add a member (deployed at the site); open **Deployment Board**: the people sit in their crews against the plan; move one with "Move to crew": it waits for approval; Undo withdraws it.
4. Open **Transfer Request**, move an employee to another project: the current manager, the receiving manager and the HR manager approve; tick the handover; the old posting ends and the new one starts on the dates; **Employee 360 > Deployments** shows both.

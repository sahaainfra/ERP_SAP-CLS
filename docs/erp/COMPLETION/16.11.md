## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migration 245, seed only): flag, two navigation entries under the Stores group, page tracker. No table: every figure is worked out from the live ledgers and documents inside the data scope of the person.
- **KPIs** (FR-01): inventory value (company, project and store views, with the ledger sum at the same entry, the difference and a month-end trend), stock turns, items at or below reorder, receipts pending inspection (with late count), open material requests, issues today, transfers in transit, non-moving value and share, wastage against the allowed (16.10), reconciliation status of the previous month (16.10), and a procurement strip (15.10). Targets come from four parameters.
- **Lifecycle funnel** (FR-02): per material, quantity required (MRP of the latest run, else approved material requests), requisitioned, ordered, delivered, accepted (net of QC rejection), issued and consumed (net of returns), with the share of the previous stage and the widest gap; each figure opens its documents.
- **Work queues** (FR-03): material requests awaiting action, receipts pending inspection, returns pending (site returns and returns to vendor), counts due. Alerts list and "my pending actions".
- **Drill-down and export** (FR-04): every tile and queue opens the records it counts; CSV of the KPIs, any drill-down list or the funnel, formula-neutral, masked, audited.
- **Project 360**: the stores slot shows value, items below reorder, receipts pending inspection, open requests and transfers in transit of the project.
- **API**: 9 routes under `/api/v1/inv/materials` (3,005 routes in all). **Browser**: Materials Management Dashboard, Material Lifecycle Funnel, English and Hindi.

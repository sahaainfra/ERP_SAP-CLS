## 7. How to verify manually
1. As the estimator open an estimate, **Bid Price Finalisation**: give a discount of 3 % and its reason; the bid price changes as you type; above 2 % **Send the discount to the director**; the director approves it in the inbox.
2. **Estimate Review**, **Assumptions and exclusions**: list them. On the estimate, **Send for approval**.
3. As the commercial manager open **Estimate Review**: tick every item of the checklist and **Approve the review** (or return it with a comment). Look at **Sensitivity** (+10 % on steel) and **Unbalanced items**.
4. As the director approve the estimate in the inbox. The estimate, its bid price and its register are locked.
5. **Bid Price Finalisation**: download the priced BOQ in Excel or PDF, or print it, with the rates in words. Sign in as a quantity surveyor: the panel shows the bid price without costs. Switch to Hindi.

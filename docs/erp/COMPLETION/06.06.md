## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 106 and 107, nothing existing altered): five tables with the standard columns. Rates, amounts, quantities and percentages are integers in minor units. The seed adds the flag, five navigation entries and tracker rows, and the notification template (en, hi).
- **Calculation** (BR-01, ADR-270, ADR-271): two built-in formulas of the engine.
  - `ra.line_amount` = quantity × (1 + wastage %) × rate, rounded to paise.
  - `ra.unit_rate` = water and electricity on the direct cost, then overhead on cost, then profit on cost plus overhead, then contingency, insurance and escalation, each rounded to paise; the total ÷ the basis quantity; GST added when the analysis adds it.
  - Both have golden outputs and a hand-written twin the engine must match. The four earlier golden files are unchanged.
  - The service prices every save, recalculation and submit through the engine and stores the trace. A client total that differs is refused.
- **Resource rate library** (FR-01): a rate per item, by region (state) and/or project, from a date, with its source (quotation, market survey, DSR, last PO).
  - The rate in force is the project's, then the region's, then the general one.
  - A new rate ends the previous one the day before.
  - A rate that an analysis used is never corrected, only replaced.
  - Excel import `MDM_RESOURCE_RATE`.
- **Schedules of rates** (FR-02): books (CPWD, state PWD, client) in editions.
  - Items are pasted or imported (`MDM_SOR_ITEM`) into a draft edition, which is then put in use; the previous edition is superseded.
  - A new edition copies the current one's items.
  - Search covers the editions in use.
- **Rate analyses** (FR-03 to FR-06, BR-02, ADR-272 to 274):
  - **Starting point:** a template (M25 and M20 concrete, brickwork 1:6, 12 mm plaster, reinforcement, earthwork) matched to the company's items, or the work item's bill of resources; library rates filled in.
  - **Components:** material, labour, equipment, transport, subcontract or other, each an item or typed, with quantity per basis, wastage and rate.
  - **Add-ons and link:** water, overhead, profit, contingency, insurance, escalation, GST, and a link to an SOR item showing % above or below.
  - **Release:** `MDM_RATE_ANALYSIS` goes to the director for the standard library, the project manager of the project for a project analysis, and the commercial manager for a variation. Return, resubmit, withdraw (back to draft), cancel and reject work; the maker never approves.
  - **Locking and amendment:** a released version is locked; an amendment is a new version that supersedes it from its date.
  - **Versions:** compared side by side, with history, a printable page (audited) and a CSV export (audited).
  - **For other modules:** `releasedRate()` gives estimation, BOQ pricing, budgets and variations the rate in force.
- **Impact** (FR-05, T-02): every current analysis using a resource is recomputed at a new rate (or the library's), showing old and new unit rate, difference and %. Nothing is saved.
- **Integration:**
  - lookup `mdm/sor-items`;
  - object type `ra_analysis`;
  - four events;
  - approval-matrix defaults and three workflow-matrix bands;
  - evidence panel: lines priced by hand, % against the SOR.
- **Browser:** Resource Rate Library, Schedule of Rates, Rate Analyses (register), Rate Analysis (object page with a change form), Rate Change Impact (report with bars and totals).
- **Permissions** (`mdm.analyse.*` plus `mdm.rate.edit`):

  | Role | Rights |
  |---|---|
  | EST | create, edit, delete, submit, cancel, amend, print, export; keeps the library and the schedules |
  | QS, PCMGR, PD | view, print, export |
  | DIR | view, approve, cancel, print, export |
  | PM, COMMGR | view, approve, print, export |

  Project records are seen within the person's project grants. Anyone without `mdm.analyse.view` (for example a site engineer) sees no rates.
- **Documents:** ARCHITECTURE section 60, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, DECISIONS ADR-270 to 274, DEFECTS D-311 to D-314, openapi, PROGRESS.csv (row 54), screenshots in `docs/erp/screenshots/rates/`.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. **Build the library:** as the estimator open **Resource Rate Library** and add the rates of cement, sand, aggregate, mason, beldar, mixer and vibrator for Maharashtra, or import them from Excel ("Resource rates").
2. **Load a schedule:** open **Schedule of Rates**, create DSR-DL 2025 and paste its rows (item code, description, unit, rate), then **Put in use**.
3. **Prepare an analysis:** open **Rate Analyses**, choose "Reinforced cement concrete M25" and **New analysis**. The components come priced from the library. Choose the work item, link the SOR item and **Save and calculate**: ₹ 8,409.83 per cum, and the **Schedule of rates** tab shows % above or below.
4. **Release it:** **Submit for release**; the director approves it in **My Approvals**. The page now says released and locked, and **Change** is gone.
5. **Amend it:** give a reason and choose **Amend (new version)**. Change cement in the library, then **Recalculate from the library**, submit, and approve. Version 1 is superseded; **Versions › Compare with this** shows the difference.
6. **Check the impact:** open **Rate Change Impact**, choose cement and type 8000. Every analysis that uses cement shows its old and new unit rate and the change.

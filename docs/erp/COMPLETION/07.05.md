## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 116, 117; nothing existing altered): `att_punches`, `att_anomalies`, `att_visits`, `att_device_bindings`, `att_site_settings`; seed: flag, five navigation entries and tracker rows, a notification template (English and Hindi).
- **Service** (`punches.js`): the decision on a punch (deployment or visit, clock skew, mock location, accuracy, fence with tolerance, selfie rule, device), the ways in (phone, supervisor crew, kiosk badge, device interface, offline engine), anomalies (outside the fence, impossible travel, low and repeated low accuracy, mock location, shared and new phone, clock, late sync, out without an in) and their review, visits, the report of the day, site settings, retention.
- **Workflows**: `HR_ATT_VISIT` (reporting manager, then the project manager of the site), `HR_ATT_ANOMALY` (supervisor or project manager, then the HR manager; in the matrix).
- **API** `/api/v1/att/*` as asked (`POST /att/punches` with the Idempotency-Key as client id, `/punches/crew`, `GET /punches/today`, `GET /anomalies`, `POST /anomalies/:id/review`) and more (16 routes in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`); events `att.punch.recorded`, `att.anomaly.raised`, `att.anomaly.reviewed`; widget `att.anomalies_open`; job `att.punch_retention`.
- **Permissions**: `att.punch.{own,view,crew,kiosk,device,settings}`, `att.anomaly.{view,review}`. Every role holds `att.punch.own` (the self-service role EMP now has it, and only it); HRMGR all; PM, PD review at their sites; WSUP crew punch, kiosk and review; SE, CM, SECMGR kiosk and site view; ITADM the device interface.
- **Browser**: Punch In/Out (fence indicator, mini map of the fence and position, selfie, offline queue indicator, visit with a reason, last punches, privacy notice), Crew Punch (tick all, group photo), Kiosk Mode (QR where the browser can, typed code otherwise, photo, auto-reset after 5 s), Attendance Anomalies (worklist: severity then age with a colour, accept or reject with a note, bulk), Today's Attendance (by site, drill-down to the punches, export, per-site punch settings).
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 65, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, DECISIONS ADR-298 to 302, DEFECTS D-335 to D-339, openapi.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As HR open **Today's Attendance**, choose a site and set its punch settings (selfie, accuracy, tolerance).
2. As an employee open **Punch In/Out** on a phone at the site: **Check my location** says inside the fence; **Punch IN**. From outside the fence the same button is refused with the reason.
3. As the crew supervisor open **Crew Punch**: choose the crew, tick all, take the group photo, send; as the site engineer open **Kiosk Mode** and enter a badge code.
4. As HR open **Attendance Anomalies**: the outside-fence punch is there; accept it with a note and it counts; **Today's Attendance** shows it, and each count opens its punches.

## 4. Click-path
1. As ACCMGR: an imprest for the site accountant (limit ₹25,000); as FINEX a contra "Cash withdrawn from the bank" ₹20,000 → the accounts manager approves.
2. As FINEX: Finance → Bank Reconciliation → choose the statement file → **Import**: 3 new lines, 2 matched by themselves, 1 left. **Match**: the bank charges → post to an account, or set aside. **Reconciliation statement**: difference ₹0.00 → the accounts manager signs it off.
3. As SACC on the phone: Finance → Site Imprest: ₹1,850, Materials, Sri Rama Hardware, the bill photo → **Save and send**: cash in hand ₹18,150. Without a connection the voucher is kept on the phone.
4. As CFO: Daily Cash & Bank Position.

The Hindi chunk is 188.5 KB gzip, within the bundle budget.

# Part 23.07 completion: GST tax engine, e-invoice (IRN) and e-way bill

Part: 23.07 (step 173). Date: 2026-11-18. Module M23 (`fin`). Feature flag `ff.fin.p2307` (OFF by default; ON in development and test). Depends on 23.02 and 06.02 (with 166.8's GST 2.0 rows) and on the integration hub of 01.08.

Status: **Ready for review** — the tax resolver, GSTIN checks, the GST settings (dated, maker-checker), e-invoices and e-way bills through a GSP connector of 01.08 (a sandbox provider that plays the IRP and the e-way bill system; a generic GSP JSON provider) are built behind `ff.fin.p2307` and tested on desktop and phone. **Not done, with the part that closes it:** a certified production GSP adapter — token and payload encryption (D-731, 29.02; production mode is therefore not used yet), the QR on the tax invoice's own print template (D-732, 22.04), the tax engine inside the remaining documents' GST split (D-733), the concrete ticket taking the generated e-way bill number (D-734, 20.02); D-520 re-assigned to 15.06.

## 2. What was built
| Requirement | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 tax resolver | `src/server/modules/fin/gst.js` (`resolve`, `rateOn`, `timeOfSupply`, `taxOf`), `POST /fin/gst/resolve`, `platform/tax` | supplier/recipient by GSTIN or state; place of supply (given, the property's state for works contract, the recipient, export 96); B2B, B2C, SEZ, export, import, deemed export, with or without payment; reverse charge (given or from the HSN master); the rate of the dated masters on the s.14 date; integer arithmetic half up; PO rates through it |
| FR-02 GSTIN | `POST /fin/gst/gstin-check`, `gst_gstin_checks` | shape, state, check character, then the portal's status through the connector; the company's GSTINs on the settings page |
| FR-03 e-invoice | `gst_einvoices`, `fin/gsp.js` (sandbox, `gsp_http`), `/fin/einvoices*` | IRP schema 1.1 from a tax invoice (22.04) or a credit note (23.05) made by their events; heads checked against the resolver; IRN (SHA-256 of GSTIN, year, type, number), ack, signed QR handed back to the document; retries with back-off (hourly job) and by the finance executive; cancellation within 24 h by the accounts manager; the IRN-due worklist (alerts day 20 and 25, blocked after 30 days for AATO ≥ ₹10 crore); number unique per GSTIN, year and type in capitals; print with the QR (PDF) |
| FR-04 e-way bill | `gst_ewaybills`, `/fin/ewaybills*` | from a tax invoice, a stock transfer or a concrete delivery; Part A only or with the vehicle; validity by distance (200 km / 20 km ODC a day, to the end of the day); vehicle update; extension within 8 h of expiry, never beyond 360 days; cancellation within 24 h; expiry alerts and expiry marking; the transfer's dispatch takes the generated number |
| FR-05 compliance log, sandbox | `gst_compliance_log`, the `sandbox` provider | every request and answer per document, with the hub's log id and the correlation id |
| Settings (§7, §9) | `gst_settings`, workflow `FIN_GST_SETTINGS`, page **GST Settings** | versions from a date: turnover, switches, windows, alert days, limits, connector, mode; proposed by the accounts manager, approved by the CFO; history; export / import (JSON → a draft); the tax engine to try |
| §8 rights | `PERMISSIONS_23_07` / `ADDED_23_07` | ACCMGR manages; FINEX retries; CFO approves the settings; STOREMGR makes the e-way bill of a transfer; AUD reads; the system generates IRNs by itself |

## 7. How to verify manually
1. Integrations (01.08): a connector `GSP-SBX` of type GST provider, provider *sandbox*, approved.
2. As ACCMGR: Finance → GST Settings → **Propose a new version**: turnover ₹12 crore, e-invoicing and e-way bills on, connector GSP-SBX, sandbox, a reason → **Send to the CFO**. As CFO: open the version → **Approve**.
3. Issue a tax invoice (22.04): its IRN appears by itself; Finance → e-Invoice & e-Way Bill Register → the e-invoice: IRN, acknowledgement, the QR; **Print with QR**.
4. **IRN due** tab: an older invoice shows its days left (amber after day 20); **Generate IRN**.
5. **e-Way bills** tab → **New e-way bill**: the invoice, 720 km, vehicle TS09EA1234 → valid to the end of the fourth day; **Update the vehicle**; **Cancel** within 24 hours.
6. GST Settings → **Try the tax engine**: supplier in 36, recipient in 27, HSN 7214 → IGST 18 %; property state 36 → CGST + SGST.

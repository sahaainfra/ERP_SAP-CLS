## 2. What was built

- **Tables** (migration `20261228000431_ho_handover`): `ho_handovers`, `ho_checklist_items`, `ho_client_assets`.
- **Routes** under `/api/v1/close` (15 in the route policy): handovers, checklist items, client assets, dossier and exports.
  - FR-01 checklist: a new handover starts with as-builts, O&M manuals, warranties, statutory approvals, test certificates, commissioning
    certificates, spares, training, snags and keys; more items can be added. An item is done with its evidence — a file, an issued document
    revision of the project, or an issued commissioning certificate (required for a commissioning item); a mandatory item cannot be set aside;
    "not applicable" needs a reason.
  - FR-02 dossier: the index of the evidence by section (page and CSV); files download through the file service with its access checks.
  - FR-03 client asset register: tag, description, category, location, make, model, serial number, capacity, warranty, maintenance and the
    commissioning system; CSV export for the client's facility management.
  - FR-04 whole-project (one live per project) or area handover (a project location or a named area), with its defects liability months.
  - FR-05 certificate: requested by the project manager, approved by the project director through the workflow (`HO_CERTIFICATE`), then the
    project manager records the client's signatory, date and signed copy. Issue sets the effective date and publishes `ho.handover.issued`
    with the end of the defects liability period (for 26.03).
  - BR-01 readiness: every mandatory item done, no open or rectified class A snag of the project, site or location, and for a whole-project
    handover every top-level commissioning system at ready for start-up. Checked at request and at the client's signature. The checklist and
    assets are frozen while the handover is in approval or issued.
- **Permissions**: `ho.view`, `ho.manage`, `ho.request`, `ho.sign`, `ho.export` (PM all; PD view and export; DOC and QCMGR manage; QCE, CM,
  SE view; COMMGR, DIR, AUD view and export).
- **Pages**: Handovers (list and new), Handover (certificate and readiness, checklist with evidence upload, client assets, dossier CSV),
  Client Asset Register (with export). Text in the `close` area; Hindi and Telugu are drafts that need review.

## 6. Known limitations

- The client's decision is recorded by the project manager until the client portal (25.04, D-761) takes decisions; a portal share of the
  dossier to the client is not wired (25.01 shares remain manual).
- The dossier is an index with links to each file; a single bundled PDF or ZIP is not produced.
- The client asset register is not posted to the fixed asset register (it is the client's, not the company's).

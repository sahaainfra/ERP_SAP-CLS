## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 186, 187; nothing existing altered): `plan_baselines` (the picture of a schedule as JSON with its SHA-256, the number, kind of revision, reason, change summary, reference, the change against the baseline it revises, status, the current mark, approval) with a partial unique index for one current baseline per project and two triggers that refuse any change or removal of an approved baseline; `plan_imports` (one row per file read: name, hash, size, format, mapping, validation report, result). Seed: the flag, three navigation entries, three page-tracker rows, two notification templates in English and Hindi.
- **Baselines** (FR-01, BR-01, ADR-476, ADR-477, `modules/plan/baselines.js`): a baseline is taken of a calculated schedule in use; the picture holds calendars, the WBS nodes in use, activities with dates, durations and floats, links and resources; it is taken again when the baseline is sent, so what is approved is what was sent. Approval by the project manager and then the project director (`PLAN_BASELINE`); the planner holds no approval and the engine gives the originator no task. Approved, it is the current baseline of the project and frozen.
- **Revisions** (FR-02): a revision is a new baseline (extension of time, re-sequencing, change of scope, other) with its reason and reference; the change against the current baseline is worked out (finish, added, removed, changed, later, critical and later, links, the ten largest slips) and the planner's summary is required before it is sent; the current baseline changes only on approval; the earlier baselines stay approved and readable.
- **Comparison** (FR-03, ADR-478, `modules/plan/analysis.js`): start, finish and duration variance of every activity and milestone against the schedule in work, a named schedule or another baseline; the critical activities that slipped; filters, sort, pages, totals; export as XLSX, PDF and CSV; the Gantt of 13.02 draws the dates of the current baseline under its bars.
- **Import** (FR-04, BR-02, ADR-479, ADR-480, `modules/plan/exchange.js`): MS Project XML, Primavera P6 XML and XER are read into one model; step 1 reads and checks (nothing changes), step 2 changes the mapping (calendars, WBS nodes, resources, the field of the activity code, replace or merge, start, progress, resources) with the report worked out again, step 3 applies in one transaction and calculates. Errors stop the import; warnings count what is carried differently. Only a draft or returned schedule is a target.
- **Export** (FR-05): a schedule or a baseline as MS Project XML or P6 XML.
- **Quality check** (FR-06): fourteen checks in the manner of DCMA-14 (logic, leads, lags, relationship types, hard constraints, high float, negative float, long durations, invalid dates, resources, missed activities, critical path test, critical path length index, baseline execution index) with the limits as parameters, the drill-down of a check and the export.
- **API**: 28 routes under `/api/v1/plan`, in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`; optimistic locking on update; `Idempotency-Key` required on create, submit, amend and apply.
- **Permissions**: `plan.baseline.{view,create,edit,delete,submit,approve,cancel,amend,print,export}` and `plan.schedule.import`. PLAN prepares and imports; PM reviews; PD approves and may cancel; PCMGR and CM read, print and export; DIR reads and prints; AUD reads and exports; site and other project roles read.
- **Events and notifications**: `plan.baseline.approved`, `plan.schedule.imported`; the originator is told of an approval and of a rejection.
- **Browser**: Baselines (list; one baseline with its approval, change, comparison, the other baselines and history; revise), Import Schedule (the three-step wizard and the earlier imports), Variance and Quality (parameters, two charts and the table with totals, drill-down, export; the fourteen checks); baseline bars on the Gantt Planner; English and Hindi.
- **Performance**: `npm run erp:plan-baseline-perf` (50 projects, 100,020 baselines, 20 schedules of 5,000 activities): list first page p95 29 ms, by project and status 3 ms, search 145 ms; the baselines of a project 149 ms; one baseline 0.3 ms; the current baseline with 5,000 activities 51 ms; variance 236 ms; quality check 142 ms; baseline dates for the Gantt 46 ms (limits 500 ms, 300 ms for one record); a baseline taken of 5,000 activities 135 ms; the MS Project file written 136 ms and the P6 file 141 ms; a 4.9 MB file of 5,000 activities read and checked 279 ms, applied and calculated 349 ms (limit 3 s). The query plans are printed and use the indexes.
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 99, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, ADR-476 to ADR-480, D-476 and D-477, openapi.

## 6. Known limitations
D-476: the file of an import is not kept as an attachment (the files platform accepts no `.xml` or `.xer`); calendars of a file are matched, not created; constraints other than the two this schedule knows are carried as the nearer one or left out with a warning; whole days only. D-477: baseline, variance, quality and apply run in the request; the printed programme does not draw baseline bars yet; an XER file is read but not written; the limits of the quality check are not saved per company. The delegation and escalation of the approval are those of the workflow engine (04.01) and were not tested again here.

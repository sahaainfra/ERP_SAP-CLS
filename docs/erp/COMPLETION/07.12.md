## 2. What was built
- **Database** (migrations 130, 131; nothing existing altered): `exp_policies`, `exp_travel_requests`, `exp_advances`, `exp_claims`, `exp_claim_lines`, `exp_advance_recoveries`; seed: flag, seven navigation entries and tracker rows, notification templates (English, Hindi), a widget. Money in paise.
- **Service** (`expenses.js`): policies with maker-checker activation and the most specific policy by category, grade and city tier; the claim with per diem and mileage computed on the server, limits, receipt rule, GST rules, duplicate bill detection, old bills, flags and justifications; a preview of the same checks while typing; settlement against an advance (net payable or recoverable) applied at approval and reversed on cancel; advances (salary with instalments for payroll, travel, imprest), payment by accounts, recoveries by payroll; travel requests; the worklist; the analysis; the widget.
- **Workflows**: `HR_EXP_CLAIM` (manager, accounts manager above ₹25,000, CFO above ₹1 lakh; a deviation or an old bill moves it one level up), `HR_EXP_ADVANCE` (manager, HR manager for salary or accounts manager otherwise, CFO above the limit), `HR_EXP_TRAVEL` (manager, project manager or department head); the manager is the reporting manager (resolver `exp.manager`).
- **API**: 47 routes under `/api/v1/hr/{travel-requests,advances,claims,expenses}` in the route policy and `openapi.yaml`; exports of the three lists.
- **Permissions**: `hr.travel_request.*`, `hr.advance.*`, `hr.claim.*` (the ten of each), `hr.claim.pass`, `hr.advance.disburse`, `hr.advance.recover`, `hr.{claim,advance,travel_request}.view_all`, `hr.expense.{configure,config_approve,report}`.
- **Events**: `exp.travel.approved`, `exp.advance.{approved,disbursed,recovered,closed}`, `exp.claim.{approved,passed,paid}`.
- **Browser**: New Expense Claim (camera bill capture, live checks), Travel Request, Advance Request, My Claims & Advances, Claim Approvals (flags visible, bulk decisions, pass for payment, record payment), Expense Analysis, Expense Policies; English and Hindi (`exp` namespace, 209 keys).
- **Docs**: ARCHITECTURE section 72, DB_CHANGES, DB_BASELINE, ADR-329 to 332, D-365 to 369, openapi.

## 7. How to verify manually
1. As the accounts manager open **Expense Policies**: make a policy (grade G3, Lodging, limit ₹3,000, bill photo above ₹500) and a per-diem policy; the CFO activates them.
2. As an employee open **New Expense Claim** on a phone: add a hotel bill of ₹4,500 (it is flagged over the limit: write why), take the bill photo (save the draft first), add a per-diem line (the amount is computed), send it.
3. The manager opens **Claim Approvals**: the flag and the reason show; approve. Because of the deviation the accounts manager approves next; then passes the claim for payment and records the payment.
4. **Advance Request**: a salary advance in instalments goes through the manager and the HR manager; accounts record the payment; payroll reads the instalments due and records each recovery.
5. **Expense Analysis** shows the claims by project, category and employee. Switch to Hindi: headings and labels change.

## 1. Discovery summary
There was no organisation at all: `company_id` was a bare number on every table, with no company, branch, department, project or site record. Everything is new; no existing object was altered. The prerequisite 01.02 (database foundation, integrity registry) is in place and used; the event bus (01.04) carries every change; the approval engine is the interim one (D-022). Decision per requirement: `docs/erp/DISCOVERY/02.02.md`. Departures from the pack: the geofence editor draws on a metric grid with no base map (D-069); the organisation chart shows departments and heads, not person-level reporting lines (D-070); the shell switcher is by company until access grants exist (D-068).

## 2. What was built
- **Tables** (migrations `20261030000024` and `20261030000025`, up and down): `org_companies` (company 1 seeded), `org_branches` (regions and branches), `org_departments` (divisions and departments to any depth), `org_designations`, `org_projects`, `org_sites` (circle or polygon geofence, hours, in-charge), `org_locations`, `org_cost_centres` (cost and profit, hierarchical, mapped to a department, project or site) and `org_assignments` (effective-dated holders); flag `ff.iam.p0202` (OFF by default, ON in development and test).
- **Rules**: codes unique within the parent (BR-01); a site belongs to one project and cannot move; radius 25 m to 5 km, polygons closed and not self-crossing (BR-02); GSTIN, PAN and state checks; no loops in any hierarchy; deactivation blocked with every blocker listed (BR-03), through a registry that attendance, stock and billing will add to.
- **Approvals**: a new project, a new or inactive site become active only through PD then PCMGR (the requester cannot decide); a geofence change on a site that is not a draft goes to SYSADM with a mandatory reason. A direct edit of either is refused, so no route, import or job bypasses the chain. A draft site takes its geofence directly.
- **Effective dates**: the department head, project manager and site in-charge change from a date; a date of today or earlier takes effect at once, a later one waits for the daily job `iam.org_effective_apply`; history shows who held the role and when, and a waiting change can be cancelled.
- **API** (`/api/v1/iam/org`): list, create, read and change for the eight entities; `tree`, `context`, `lookups/users`, `descendants`, `blockers`, `history`, `requests`, `deactivate`, `activate`, `assignments`, project `status`, `activation-requests`, `geofence/change-requests`, `geofence/test` (saved site) and `geofence/test` (unsaved shape). Optimistic locking with `version`, `Idempotency-Key` on every post, one error envelope.
- **Pages** (behind `ff.iam.p0202`, permission `org.view`): Organisation (companies, branches and regions, departments, designations, locations), Projects & Sites (project and site object pages with details, geofence editor, approvals and timeline), Organisation Chart (search, expand and collapse, move by drag or by a dialog, undo, print or save as PDF), Cost & Profit Centres (list and hierarchy), and the project and site switcher in the shell. Lists have search, filters, sort, column chooser (remembered), 25/50/100 paging, status chips, CSV export of every matching row, empty, error (with correlation ID) and no-permission states.
- **Roles**: SYSADM and HRMGR maintain departments and designations; SYSADM companies, branches and locations; PM, PCMGR and PD projects and sites (PM so the maker-checker chain has a requester); ACCMGR cost centres; HRMGR and PM ask for geofence changes; every signed-in person reads their own context. HRMGR and PD are new role codes. The PAN of a company is masked for anyone without `org.company.manage`.
- Docs: `ARCHITECTURE.md` section 19, `DECISIONS.md` ADR-68 to ADR-73, `DEFECTS.md` D-068 to D-075, `DB_CHANGES.md`, `DB_BASELINE.md`, `openapi.yaml`; `schema/baseline.json` re-locked (205 objects).
- Shared code: the geofence arithmetic is `src/shared/geo.js`, used by the server and the browser editor alike (re-exported by `platform/org`).

## 7. How to verify manually
1. `npm run build`, then `AUTH_MODE=dev-headers NODE_ENV=development PORT=3000 DATABASE_PATH=data/dev.sqlite node src/server/index.js`, and open http://localhost:3000. The yellow bar at the top picks a development role; use company 1.
2. As **SYSADM**: Organisation > Branches & regions > New branch (state 27). Then **HRMGR**: Organisation > Departments, add a division and a department under it; try the same code twice; deactivate the division and read the blocker.
3. As **PM**: Projects & Sites > New project, open it, Request activation. As **PD** then **PCMGR** approve it under Approvals (the project page shows each step). Add a site, open it, Geofence tab: choose Polygon, type the site's latitude and longitude under "Go to", click four corners, Close shape, Save geofence; test a point by clicking the map or typing a coordinate.
4. Request activation for the site and approve it as PD then PCMGR. Back on the Geofence tab change the shape, Request geofence change with a reason, approve it as **SYSADM**; the old fence stays until then.
5. As **ACCMGR**: Cost & Profit Centres, add a profit centre and a cost centre under it mapped to the project; open the Hierarchy tab. As **HRMGR**: Organisation Chart, search, Move a department and Undo.
6. `NODE_ENV=test node --test tests/org-platform.test.js tests/org-api.test.js`; `npx playwright test e2e/organisation.spec.js`; `npm run erp:regression` runs everything.

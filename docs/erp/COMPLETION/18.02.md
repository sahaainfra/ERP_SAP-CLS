## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 264, 265): `qa_inspection_requests`; flag, three pages, tracker, templates. No existing table changed.
- **Request**: raised for a planned point (its activity, location, step and type come from it), or an activity or location; date wanted not in the past; consultant flag; one open request per point.
- **Decision**: the quality engineer (then the consultant when required) records findings (reason, comments, the checklist inspection) and decides; approved with comments when comments exist; a rejection returns the point to planned.
- **Gate** (BR-01, T-01): an unreleased hold point of an earlier activity fails the work authorisation check `HOLD_POINT` and makes a measurement line ineligible (`/hold-points/check`).
- **Re-inspection** (BR-01): a rejected point accepts only a request linked to the rejection, once.
- **Analysis**: ageing, overdue, first-time approval %, rejection reasons, rejections waiting for re-inspection.
- **API**: 17 routes; **browser**: Inspection Requests (register and object), Raise Inspection Request, IR Rejection Analysis; English and Hindi.

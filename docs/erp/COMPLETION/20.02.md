# Part 20.02 completion: Production, Dispatch & Delivery Tickets

Part: 20.02. Date: 2026-11-21. Module `rmc`, flag `ff.rmc.p2002` (default off; on in development and test). Depends on 20.01, 16.04 (stores issue), 18.05 (pour cards).

Status: **Ready for review, not accepted.** Open items D-645 in `DEFECTS.md`.

## What was built
- **Database** (migrations 292, 293): `bp_requisitions`, `bp_batches`, `bp_delivery_tickets`, `bp_silo_moves`; flag, six pages, tracker, templates.
- **Requisition**: raised by the site engineer from a pour card (grade checked), approved by the construction manager; the plant in-charge schedules it on a plant with an approved mix design in force (BR-01 of 20.01) with a capacity warning, or hands it to an external supplier; the board shows urgency and plant load.
- **Batches**: weights for every material of the mix, the theoretical weights and variance worked out on the server, a flag beyond the tolerance, scales in calibration, silos reduced through a ledger (a shortfall is refused unless the company allows it), cancel puts the materials back; plant PC rows are imported idempotently.
- **Delivery tickets**: ticket per truck with a QR code and e-way bill when leaving the premises, quantity limit of the requisition, receipt at site on a phone screen (times, slump flag, returned and wasted concrete), the net quantity goes to the pour card, a rejected load is returned in full, approval by the plant in-charge.
- **Consumption report** by material and grade with flagged batches and the returned and wasted concrete; CSV.
- **API**: 52 routes under `/api/v1/rmc`; **browser**: Concrete Requisitions, Production Schedule, Batch Records, Delivery Tickets, Ticket Receipt at Site, Theoretical vs Actual Consumption; English and Hindi.
